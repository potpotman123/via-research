A. Purchase Price
1. How does the vendor price their products, services,
and/or solutions?
i. Is pricing based on a discount from a manufacturer’s price list, catalog, fixed
price, or dynamic pricing? List which pricing mechanisms are being utilized as
part of the Offeror’s response.
Via’s pricing is derived from our not-to-exceed pricing catalog. We believe that the
discounts applied to our list price will provide members with a unique opportunity to
procure Via’s state-of-the-art operational capabilities and technology at below-market
prices. Each catalog line item can be purchased from Via individually unless otherwise
indicated.
Pricing is typically usage based, subject to a minimum. The unit prices are fixed for the 
contract term (sometimes subject to annual inflation escalators). Via will work together 
with each purchasing member to determine pricing on a case-by-case basis. When 
turnkey operations are being purchased, price can vary depending on service specifics 
such as location, hours of operation, vehicle type, etc.  
ii. Include price and product, service, and/or solutions lists with the following
information, if applicable: manufacturer or other part number, product/service
description, manufacturers suggested or other list price, and net price.
Our pricing catalog with part numbers, descriptions, and prices can be found in the
“Products and Pricing” attachment in Ion Wave.
iii. For dynamic pricing option, include the products, services, and/or solutions
being offered under dynamic pricing and a sample of the pricing, charges, and
any fees.
Via’s unit prices are fixed for the contract term. We therefore have not provided dynamic
pricing.
iv. Include any value added products, services, and/or solutions including any
rebate and/or incentive programs.
Via’s submitted pricing catalog includes tiers and ranges that reflect volume-based
discounts. In addition, participating agencies will receive the following benefits:

All Via solutions are sold directly through Via or a wholly-owned subsidiary; we do not sell

We have significant experience working with public agencies, as more than 90% of our

Our pricing catalog includes tiered pricing with volume-based discounts for Via software 
and Remix products. For Participating Public Agencies purchasing turnkey operations, we 
create a bespoke pricing proposal based on the service specifics such as operating 
hours, location, vehicle type, driver requirements, etc., and our quote will typically be 
more favorable for larger-scale purchases. 
6. What are the available ordering methods?
Via operates without distributors, dealers, or resellers. We rely on our highly specialized 
sales team in North America, led by Mike Vaccarino, Chief Revenue Officer. Mike brings 
extensive experience in launching new products and fostering partnerships with 
government and municipal clients.  
Mike and his team will be the primary contacts for all Region 4 and OMNIA participating 
entities, guiding them through every step of our tailored ordering process. They are 
backed by a dedicated launch team of service design and implementation specialists. 
This team manages critical aspects of the service launch, including driver recruitment, 
testing, dry runs, service configuration, localization, and ensuring a seamless transition 
for all partners. 
7. What is the invoicing process?
Via’s Finance team will align on invoicing cadence and format with each participating 
agency. Typically, Via bills our customers with Net 30 payment terms. The minimum fees, 
which are agreed upon on a case-by-case basis, are paid annually upfront. Any usage 
beyond the minimum is billed monthly in arrears. As part of our invoicing package, we 
provide details about usage during the prior month (e.g., active vehicles or vehicle hours).

We suggest periodic (annual) price adjustment, and we reserve the right to adjust prices
