I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Douglas County is seeking to provide microtransit services in the Highlands Ranch area. Staff has
prepared a Contract for Services between Douglas County and River North LLC, a subsidiary of Via
Transportation Inc. (Via), for microtransit services in the amount of $2,901,410.
II. REQUEST
Staff requests approval of the Contract for Services with River North LLC in the amount of
$2,901,410.
III. BACKGROUND
In 2025 the Board of County Commissioners (BCC) budgeted $3,000,000 in Capital Improvement
Plan funds to be designated for providing microtransit services in Douglas County. Staff was
directed to secure a bid for service through a Request for Proposal (RFP) process which was
initiated on January 24, 2025. Via responded to the RFP and proposed a microtransit service at an
annual estimated cost of $2,901,410. During a work session on March 11, 2025, the BCC directed
staff to prepare a contract for services with Via.
IV. DISCUSSION
Via is the service provider of microtransit in Lone Tree. Their turnkey service solution and existing
local partnerships provide a model for expansion into the Highlands Ranch area. Via has proposed
an all-inclusive solution for Highlands Ranch that includes their end-to-end software platform,
robust service design, planning and mobilization services, driver training, a call center for
multilingual customer service, vehicles and maintenance, marketing, and community engagement.
Via recommends launching microtransit service throughout all of Highlands Ranch with trips into
and out of Lone Tree.  The proposed hours of operation would be:
•
Monday thru Thursday from 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m.
•
Friday from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.
=== page 2 ===
•
Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.
To accommodate this service Via plans to have up to 15 vehicles available at peak hours and 
anticipates a ridership capacity between 2,200 and 2,400 rides per week. Additional service 
requirements include:  
•
Mobile app and phone-based system to book a ride
•
ADA accessible vehicles
•
Connections to commercial areas along Highlands Ranch Parkway, County Line Road, and
the Park Meadows Retail Resort for both employment and shopping
•
Connections to local schools, parks, and recreation centers
•
Connections to local hospitals and medical centers
•
Connections to RTD bus and light rail; including, the County Line and Littleton/Mineral
light rail stations
•
Access to real-time trip data to make system performance and improvement decisions
The proposed microtransit service will be scheduled to launch in the late Spring of 2025 and will 
be available to everyone.  
V. RECOMMENDED ACTION
The County Attorney’s Office has reviewed the contract for services, and it complies with all State
and local requirements. Staff recommends approval of the Contract for Services with River North
LLC in the amount of $2,901,410.
