directly threatens my ability to get back home in Pittsfield Township I use FlexRide 's late night service and
many friends are disabled and thus rely on FlexRide as our daily commute. In my experience, On-Demand
service alone is unreliable: most of the time Via's app simply tells people "Sorry, we couldn't find a ride" or
Please try again later' That level of uncertainty is incompatible with maintaining employment. The elimination
of advance bookings is not a minor inconvenience-it jeopardizes the job security. Further, the "no drivers
available" boilerplate line is typically patently false. FlexRide drivers have been complaining to riders for
months that passenger bookings are frequently very unevenly distributed across the available drivers: one
driver will typically get 20+ rides assigned to him over 1-2 hours and be forced to drive nonstop and unable to
stop for bathroom breaks, while simultaneously another driver will be stuck idling in a parking lot for 30+
minutes with no passengers assigned Riders have called FlexRide receptionists countless times, but they are
usually unable to help. Some receptionists can and do edit drivers' trip manifests to redistribute the rides, but
most other receptionists fall back on reading aloud the same false no drivers available boilerplate messages
a that the app shows. I am also concerned that this cut to next-day booking may not align with Via's contractual
obligations to AAATA/Pittsfield Township, and that the Trustees may not even be aware that this reduction is
happening. Similarly, every single FlexRide driver and FlexRide receptionist we've spoken to on Monday and
Tuesday has also been entirely unaware of this service cut. Further, this push to On-Demand service would
bring FlexRide even further out of alignment with its original goal: to replace the fixed-route #61 bus service
How can we expect commuters to get to work on time when advance scheduling is thrown out the window
entirely? We used to have predictable Route #61 timetables, now we'l have nothing Further, many riders are
unaware that this service change was even announced, because the only platform it was announced on was
a transient phone notification from Via's FlexRide app. It is unacceptable for Via to leave riders in the dark on
this. I respectfully ask that you exercise oversight of this issue. At minimum, I hope the Trustees can: Verify
whether Via is authorized to make this level of service reduction. Ensure that AAATA holds Via accountable for
reliable service delivery. Advocate for riders whose livelihoods depend on predictable transit, especially those
with disabilities. Require AAATA to properly announce this service change under TheR de's service alerts
webpage and on TheRide's social media. Require Via Transportation to pin their service announcement as a
pop-up message in the FlexRide app as they have done with less-important messages in the past-to ensure
all FlexRide riders are made aware that this service reduction is happening at all. Encourage AAATA to
conduct their own FlexRide user satisfaction surveys instead of relying on Via's possibly inflated metrics.
AAATA is claiming 95% of ride demands are met, but they are failing to account for "suppressed demand" or
"peak spreading" due to inadequate service. My coworker told me yesterday morning that "I never even try to
book on-demand rides after 4 pm anymore because I know it's impossible." I also encourage the Trustees to
see for yourselves how abysmal the on-demand FlexRide service currently is: create your own rider account,
and try taking FlexRide to and from Ahmo's on your lunch break for a few days or a week. Thank you so much
for your attention to this matter and for your continued public service. Sincerely, Latitude Brown
