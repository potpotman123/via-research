TITLE:    MET Transit Service Agreement for use of Cloud Based Route Planning and Scheduling
Software
PRESENTED BY:Rusty Logan
Department:
Transit
 
RECOMMENDATION
Staff recommends that the City Council approve the three-year agreement with Remix Technologies
LLC for $123,500.
BACKGROUND (Consistency with Adopted Plans and Policies, if applicable)
Since December of 2019, MET Transit has subscribed to the Remix Transit Planning software
platform for use in route and schedule planning and development; MET entered into an agreement
with the vendor on an annual basis for an initial pricing of $15,000 annually for the route planning
module.  This software was utilized extensively in the recently completed Transit Development Plan
that is expected to be implemented in the Summer of 2023. Recently, MET Transit has also
subscribed to an additional scheduling module available from Remix to support development of
vehicle block scheduling, run-cutting (daily operator work schedules), and rostering (weekly operator
schedules). Both of these modules have proven to be invaluable to MET as both utilize software
support to assist in development of routes, bus schedules, and employee schedules. Prior to
utilization of this software system, route and scheduling development was a tedious task utilizing
spreadsheets and manual calculations of hours and capabilities, which took months of staff work to
develop just a single scenario; by utilizing the Remix Planning Platform, staff can now develop
multiple scenarios in a matter of days, leading to development of more efficient options for utilizing
resources in execution of transit service.  Additional benefits of the system include quick and efficient
development of detours, integration with fixed-route technologies for easy import and export of route
and schedule data into Computer Aided Dispatch/Automated Vehicle Location (CAD/AVL) systems,
and tools for presenting developed scenarios to both the workforce and the public as needed. Due to
the addition of the scheduling module as well as cost increases seen since the initial subscription,
MET Transit is requesting to enter into a three-year services agreement with reduced pricing
offered from the vendor, decreasing costs from $48,200 annually, to an annual average cost of
$41,166. MET Transit has heavily utilized the planning platform and has numerous data sets already
developed and present in the Remix system and will rely on the system to support implementation of
the Transit Development Plan fixed-route system overhaul; hence, MET is requesting this agreement
be executed as a sole source option to maintain the time and work already invested into usage of the
system. Further, Remix Technologies offers features within their software platform not supported by
other vendors, further supporting justification for a sole source agreement. 
ALTERNATIVES
City Council may:
Approve the agreement with Remix Technologies LLC; or
Not approve the agreement and advise staff on how to proceed. 
FISCAL EFFECTS
The proposed cost of the subscription is $37,500 for the first year, $41,000 for year two, and $45,000
for year three.  Funds for this project are available in MET Transit's budget and will be expended using
a combination of both local and Federal funds; planning software is an eligible expense with MET
Transit's annual Federal Transit Administration section 5303 planning funding, which provides for an
80% Federal, 20% local match rate.  Year to year cost breakdowns are as follows:
