We are excited to propose our paratransit scheduling software system for Hillsborough County’s
Sunshine Line. Hillsborough County and Via share a similar vision: one where effective transit relies
on innovative technology that enables flexible service, enhanced rider experience, and meaningful
coordination across all local transit networks. As such, we leverage our modernized technology to
improve efficiency, rider experience, and accessibility, while providing intuitive scheduling and
dispatching tools, support, and reporting features to our partners. The result is more trips served
for eligible riders using fewer vehicles, with fewer miles traveled and fewer dollars spent.
Via is the world’s leading provider of TransitTech solutions, including paratransit, microtransit, and
senior transportation. We partner with more than 650 cities and transit agencies across the globe,
including over 25 in the State of Florida across urban, suburban, and rural communities. We have
significant on-the-ground experience across the State, including a unique partner success
approach that provides dedicated attention and support for partners like Hillsborough. Further, we
are the undisputed leader in successfully transitioning transit agencies, cities, and counties off of
the Routematch software with 15 transitions completed over the past 12 months making Via the
ideal partner to support Hillsborough County in the next phase of its paratransit software journey.
In the pages that follow, we provide detailed information about our proposed solution for
Hillsborough County. We hope this response demonstrates our enthusiasm to partner with the
County and welcome any future opportunities to further discuss our proposal.
