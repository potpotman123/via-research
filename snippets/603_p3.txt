GALVESTON ISLAND TRANSIT 
STAFF REPORT 
D.  
The platform pulls data from different sources that would include, census, demographics, 
demand modeling, American Community survey and transit cost structures in layers for 
transportation planning.  
 
E. 
Remix Technologies is the only software provider that pulls all the information into one 
accessible platform that allows a transit agency to conduct a route study/modeling project 
in-house without the added expense of an outside consultant.  
 
F.  
Remix Technologies offers an annual service option. Galveston Island Transit  has requested 
to a three-year renewal contract similar to the original contract. The total cost of the contract 
would be $58,500. Remix Technologies is a sole source provider (attached).  
 
 
 
Fiscal Impact Report 
 
The contract would be renewed for three (3) years and would be funded through federal/state 
formula funding. The matching requirement would be $29,500.  The matching requirement would 
come from state funding and from GIT other transportation generated revenue. i.e. Charters, Parking 
Fees, Advertising revenue.
