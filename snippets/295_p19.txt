10. Consulting and Transit Planning 
 
As the new High Valley Transit service is implemented, Customer may eventually consider an 
expansion in the scope or volume of fixed-route transit, on-demand, or paratransit services it 
offers; strategic partnerships with neighboring counties and agencies; public-private 
partnerships; and other initiatives.  To support these activities, as directed by Customer, Via’s 
planning and consulting team, Via Strategies, will carry out additional fixed-route, on-demand, 
and multimodal service planning; network optimization; equity analysis; and other strategic 
mobility consulting and planning services. The budget for these activities will not exceed 
$50,000, and any work performed will be billed on an hourly basis according to the following
