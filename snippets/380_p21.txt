Agreement for Access to Student and Employee Information 
 
 
Nomad Transit, LLC (hereafter referred to as “Contractor”) hereby acknowledges and 
agrees that the personally identifiable student and employee information (“Information”), 
disclosed to it by Tacoma School District No. 10 (“District”) is for the limited purpose of 
conducting work as described in the attached Consultant/Contractual Services agreement and will 
be used only for that purpose. 
 
 
Further, the Contractor is subject to compliance with all relevant laws and regulations 
pertaining to the possession and maintenance of such Information, including, but not limited to the 
Federal Family Education Rights and Privacy Act, 20 U.S.C. Section 1232g and 34 CFR Section

28A.605.030 pertaining to parental review of student education records and parental written 
consent for release of records and RCW 42.56.230, pertaining to personal information that is

been completed, all Information will be destroyed, and no copies or other retention of the 
personally identifiable Information will be maintained.

ATTACHMENT B 
SCOPE OF WORK 
 
The general objective of this Contract-DSA TSD-24-146 is to provide safe and reliable on-demand 
alternative vehicle transportation services for District students. In order to accomplish the general 
objective of this Contract-DSA, the District and Nomad Transit shall perform the specific duties 
outlined below. 
 
RESPONSIBILITIES OF NOMAD TRANSIT 
 
SAFETY 
 
A. Nomad Transit will be responsible for the safe and reliable vehicle transportation of District 
students at the District’s request. 
 
B. Nomad Transit will ensure that all services under this Contract will comply with current 
Washington State Division of Industrial Safety orders, OSHA, and any/all other applicable local, 
state, and federal safety laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the vehicles and vehicles for 
hire.  
 
C. Nomad Transit will ensure all appropriate license(s), permits to operate, and training are obtained 
and maintained for the compliant operation within the cities, counties, and state covered by this 
Contract, including but not limited to the Washington State Vehicle Code.  
 
1. Cell phones will not be used for personal purposes while students are in the vehicle. 
 
D. Pursuant to Revised Code of Washington (RCW) 28A.400.330, Nomad Transit will prohibit any 
employee from working under this Contract who has contact with children during the course of 
their employment and who has pled guilty to or been convicted of any felony crime involving the 
physical neglect of a child under Chapter 9A.32 or 9A.36 RCW (except motor vehicle violations 
under Chapter 46.61 RCW), sexual exploitation of a child under Chapter 9.68 RCW, sexual 
offenses under Chapter 9A.44 RCW where a minor is the victim, promoting prostitution of a 
minor under Chapter 9A.88 RCW, the sale or purchase of a minor child under RCW 9A.64.030, 
or violation of similar laws of another jurisdiction. Failure to comply with this section shall be 
grounds for the District to immediately terminate the Contract.   
 
E. Nomad Transit shall utilize for all potential drivers at the time of hire the background check 
procedures of the Washington State Patrol and potential drivers will be screened through a 
national FBI check. Fingerprinting will be used, with a complete Washington State Identification 
Fingerprint Card, to ensure positive identification as is the responsibility of Nomad Transit. All 
background checks, including fingerprinting, must be completed and demonstrated as “passed” 
prior to any potential driver or employee performs services for the District under this Contract. 
 
F. Nomad Transit drivers and staff which may come in to contact with students must be fit-for-duty 
and able to communicate effectively orally and in writing. All drivers will be clean, neat in 
personal appearance, and courteous.
