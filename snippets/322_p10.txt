Subject:Evaluation Summary and Recommendation for RFP-23-24177, Paratransit Scheduling Software
The purpose of Request for Proposals RFP-23-24177 was to solicit proposals from qualified firms to provide a
comprehensive cloud-based Paratransit Scheduling Software System to be used for all aspects of Hillsborough
County's Sunshine Line program including client eligibility, advance and real-time trip reservations, issuing bus
passes, manual and fully automated trip scheduling, dispatch, automatic vehicle tracking, billing, reporting, client-
facing applications and in-vehicle mobile data computers.
The RFP was advertised on May 17, 2023, and closed on July 20, 2023, with a total of five (5) responses received.
The evaluation committee was composed of three (3) voting members. The members were:
Karen Smith, Business & Compliance Coordinator, Sunshine Line (Committee Chair)
Leslye McNeil, Operations Manager, Sunshine Line
Iva Carter, IT Service Delivery Manager, IT Department
The committee convened on August 17, 2023, at 9:00 AM to evaluate, reach a consensus score, and rank the
proposals based on the following criteria:

The evaluation committee reached a preliminary consensus on the points awarded for each proposal and
requested demonstrations from the top three proposals based on the preliminary consensus scores.
Demonstrations were presented to the evaluation committee on August 25, 2023, by each proposer individually
at 12:30 PM (Ecolane USA, Inc.), 2:00 PM (Via Transportation Inc.), and 3:30 PM (Sparelabs) respectively. Each
session included a question and answer session. Following the presentations, the evaluation committee
reconvened at 5:00 PM on August 25, 2023 and reached consensus on final points for each proposal.
The final, completed, and signed Evaluation Criteria consensus scoring sheet is attached to this memo for
reference. With this, the department recommends that the firm that received the highest number of points, Via
Transportation Inc., be recommended for award of the contract pursuant to the terms listed in the RFP and
approval by the Hillsborough County Board of County Commissioners.
