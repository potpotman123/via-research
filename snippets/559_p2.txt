Background and Scoring Criteria 
 
On October 17, 2024, Passaic County Deputy Administrator, Marc Seemon, Supervisor of Roads, Kenny 
Simpson, and Director of Paratransit, Ahmet Akdag met to review and score responses to RFP-25-004: On-
Demand Micro-Transit Pilot Program (Passaic County MOVE), (hereafter “RFP”) in conformance with the 
requirements of N.J.S.A. 40A:11-4.1. Pursuant to the RFP, the proposals were scored using the following 
weighted scoring criteria: (1) Management, 40/100; (2) Technical 40/100; and (3) Cost, 20/100.  
Scoring of Respondents 
 
On October 10th, 2024, the County received two (2) responses to the RFP from qualified firms: (1) HBSS 
Connect Corp. and (2) River North Transit, LLC.  After reviewing the responses, the Review Committee scored 
the proposals and provided an average total score based upon Management (40%), Technical (40%), and Cost 
(20%) criteria. The Committee’s collective scores are follows:  
 
I. 
HBSS Connect Corp. 
a. Management: Provided a quality breakdown of needs assessments with experience working with 
large government organizations. (32/40) 
b. Technical: Provided a detailed scope of goals and objectives in relation to the RFP, however, 
their total number of vehicles to provide this type of service was significantly lower than the other 
responses. (27/40) 
c. Cost: Cost is in line with budgetary expectations.  (15/20) 
Total Score = 74/100 
II. 
River North Transit, LLC 
a. Management: Provided a quality breakdown of needs assessments with significant experience 
working with large New Jersey government organizations. (37/40) 
b. Technical: Provided a detailed scope of goals and objectives in relation to the RFP with an 
appropriate level of vehicles to provide the requested service. (40/40) 
c. Cost: While this was the highest bid received, the total number of vehicles provided by the other 
responses would not adequately meet the anticipated service goal.  Cost is in line with budgetary 
expectations for this service.  (15/20) 
Total Score = 92/100
