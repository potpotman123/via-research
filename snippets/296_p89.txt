Dear Region 4 ESC Purchasing, 
We are excited to propose adding Via’s innovative catalog of transportation products, 
technology, and associated services to the solutions available to the purchasing members 
of OMNIA Partners, as well as to other public agencies through Region 4. Many of our 
customers are active OMNIA members, and we are committed to deepening our support 
for their needs while expanding our reach to other members and public agencies, as well 
as bringing new members to OMNIA as purchasers. 
Our offerings encompass a diverse array of solutions from a single provider, including 
turnkey integrated software and operations; comprehensive technology-enabled 
management for student transit; microtransit, paratransit, senior transportation, and 
non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT) software; transit network planning 
software and services; and rider-facing tools for full network journey planning. These 
solutions allow our partners to seamlessly connect with existing transportation networks 
to improve mobility and provide communities with better access to education, jobs, 
healthcare, and essential services. 
Our proposal for Region 4 ESC is centered on:

across the services we power. Today, we directly operate more than 100 transit services 
around the world, with over 1,350 vehicles on the roads every day, and provide software 
solutions for more than 650 partners globally. We have significant experience working
