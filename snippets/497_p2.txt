Request for Approval to Exercise the Option to Renew Term and Authorize a 
Designated Purchase for On-Demand Transit Services 
This item was amended at the June 14, 2023 Transportation, Mobility and Planning Committee 
(TMPC) meeting to reduce the option to renew term from three years to 18 months and to make 
corresponding reductions to the various contract amounts and to direct the County Mayor or 
County Mayor’s designee to prepare and provide a cost analysis report detailing the ridership, 
cost per rider, and trip distance information for the on-demand transit services provided during 
the initial term of Contract No. RFP-01083 to River North Transit, LLC. 
Summary 
This item is recommending exercise of an 18-month option to renew term available under Contract No. 
RFP-01083, On-Demand Transit Services, with expenditure authority of $2,310,375 and approval of a 
designated purchase for $9,527,000 to cover expanded service zones. On July 21, 2020, the Board of 
County Commissioners (Board) approved an initial three-year term with an option to renew for up to three 
years subject to Board approval. Therefore, the Department of Transportation and Public Works (DTPW) 
is now requesting approval of the option for 18-month term, allowable under the original contract. During 
this time, a solicitation will be issued on behalf of DTPW for a subsequent competitively awarded contract. 
This item also approves a designated purchase to support the expansion of the on-demand transit 
program to other areas of Miami-Dade County. The implementation of new service zones will cover transit 
gaps in areas with no or low transit frequency, such as northern and southern areas of the County. This 
service will complement the implementation of the Better Bus Network (BBN).  Furthermore, this service 
will be expanded to assist riders with disabilities when elevators and escalators at Metromover and 
Metrorail stations are down. This innovative application will support the Country’s efforts to enhance 
accessibility and minimize service disruption. 
The GO Connect program is an on-demand transit service (commonly known as microtransit) that provides 
shared, on-demand, dynamically routed, near door-to-door rides (nearby pick-up/drop-off location) to and 
from transit facilities and popular destinations within the designated service zones. Although GO Connect 
provides rides to customers making essential and short trips, it has the unique capability to prioritize rides 
connecting with fixed transit services. GO Connect users can view, book, pay and track rides in real-time 
via smartphone/mobile devices, website, or phone call. 
The service is delivered through a proprietary digital platform that seamlessly integrates on-demand transit 
services into Miami-Dade’s transit system. Through the GO Connect program, DTPW has introduced an 
intermodal trip planning functionality to the rider mobile application which allows riders to plan a trip by 
combining both fixed-route and on-demand services. The intermodal trip planning application is uniquely 
positioned to ensure on-demand services do not take away ridership from the current transit network but 
rather enhances first- and last-mile connectivity.  To prevent service disruption and enable expansion to 
new zones, the 18-month extension and designated purchase are recommended.

Background 
DTPW uses this contract to provide shared, on-demand, dynamically routed, near door-to-door rides to 
and from transit facilities for customers connecting to rail or bus within designated service zones. Although 
the service provides rides to customers making essential and short trips within designated service zones, 
rides connecting to transit corridors are prioritized. The program provides customers with the ability to 
request a ride in real-time, pay and track the ride via smartphone/mobile devices, website, or phone call. 
The service includes real-time ride matching and dynamically routed service, meaning that the route to 
the destination can change based on real-time demand without affecting the customer’s travel time (in-
vehicle time) and wait time (pick-up time).  
The assignment of vehicles allows for the completion of one or more trips at a time to maximize vehicle 
occupancy and minimize vehicle miles traveled, while maintaining the desired level of service (LOS), with 
average wait time and in-vehicle time of no more than 15 minutes each. To maintain the desired LOS, the 
number of deviations to pick-up/drop-off passengers is limited. Finally, customers have the ability to 
request a wheelchair accessible vehicle. 
GO Connect has a multimodal trip planning app that allows customers to see directly in the mobile app, 
web portal or by phone call, all available transit options. It is designed to ensure that riders are offered 
traditional transit options first. Customers using the GO Connect app can easily and quickly access transit 
service information, book, pay and track their rides using a single app. This helps riders make informed 
choices, while enhancing their trip experience.  
The most significant benefits of the GO Connect intermodal trip planning application are described below: 
•
Increase Ridership: By providing all transit options in real-time and in a single app, GO Connect
can drive ridership to fixed-route transit corridors, strengthening the existing transit network and
helping to build ridership for the Better Bus Network (BBN) and South Dade Bus Rapid Transit
(BRT).
•
Increase Access and Mobility Equity: GO Connect uses a real-time on-demand and dynamically
routed technology to help cover areas near the BBN and BRT with low or no transit service,
allowing more residents to access BBN and BRT, thereby increasing access to underserved areas
in Miami-Dade. Additionally, the ability to easily and quickly make modifications to designated
service zones enables DTPW to assist riders with disabilities by providing GO Connect rides
between transit stations when there are elevator and escalator outages at Metromover and
Metrorail stations.
•
Improve Traveler Information Access: GO Connect riders can do it all:  booking, paying, and
tracking their rides using the GO Connect app. The GO Connect multimodal trip planning app
allows riders to seamlessly and quickly view and book fixed routes and GO Connect options in a
single app. This application functionality will expand to allow the integration of multiple mobility
providers. It provides quick and easy access to transit information in a single app, empowering
riders to make informed decisions.

Trip Prioritization and Prevention of Mobility Mode Competition: the technology used allows GO
Connect to prioritize rides connecting to transit. This recent offering is uniquely positioned to
ensure the on-demand services do not take away ridership from the current transit network but
rather enhances first- and last-mile connectivity. The logic used is primarily geared around target
wait-time, walking distance, and number of transfers among other parameters established by
DTPW. This unique feature of the GO Connect app allows the service to only act as a “feeder
system,” prioritizing rides connecting to transit corridors. The existing County transit app, GO
Miami-Dade Transit, does not have an intermodal booking integration and cannot prevent mode
competition.
Since launching on October 28, 2020, GO Connect has seen a massive growth in demand (despite the 
impacts of COVID-19). To date, the service has completed over 174,500 rides across the four service 
zones (Town of Cutler Bay, Dadeland/South Miami, West Kendall, and Civic Center) and provided rides 
to over 2,300 unique riders. GO Connect ridership has increased over 60% in the past year, reaching its 
highest ridership in March 2023, with over 20,000 ride requests and 11,983 completed rides. Currently, 
GO Connect provides over 500 rides during weekdays, contributing to traffic reduction while serving as a 
feeder service for the County’s fixed transit system. 
In March 2023, GO Connect received 9,165 more ride requests and served 4,633 more rides than in 
March 2022; this was a 63% increase in monthly ridership and 82% growth in monthly requests. This 
increasing trend is indicative of the significant continual growth observed since GO Connect launched, 
exceeding the existing scope of the contract. The service is under significant pressure to meet rider 
demand as it continues to grow, with over 10% of rides being denied on a daily basis due to high demand. 
This item as well as the future competitive solicitation for a replacement long-term award addresses the 
need to prevent service disruption in the current commuting and transit patterns. 
Additionally, GO Connect has provided more convenient and affordable service options while improving 
access to major transit corridors. According to a rider survey conducted in August 2022, about 93% of the 
responders would be disappointed if GO Connect were to no longer be an option. Survey data indicated 
that about 42% of survey respondents make under $25,000 a year, suggesting that GO Connect is 
providing an affordable transportation option for groups that would otherwise have limited access to 
mobility. According to the ridership data, about 30% of the rides are made to and from Metrorail and 
TransitWay stations, increasing access to major transit corridors while serving first- and last-mile trips. 
Additional expenditure authority is needed to support DTPW in establishing a billable hourly rate of $57.50 
for the hours of service that will enhance the scope defined in Appendix A, Section 2.3 of the contract – 
adding the cost of Dadeland weekend service; adding vehicles to meet growth in ridership and including 
pricing and budget for May Mobility Autonomous Vehicles and Transit app; and adding the budget for the

low transit frequency, such as northwestern and southern areas of the County. This service will
complement the Better Bus Network (BBN) and the South Dade Bus Rapid Transit (BRT).
3)
$3,500,000 for increased service capacity delivered through autonomous vehicles operating as
part of the GO Connect program. This service will increase contract capacity and enhance
