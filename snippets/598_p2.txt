2024 PTAB ANNUAL REPORT SUMMARY 
 
  In drafting the historical ‘Annual Report”, our city team collaborated on the focus, intent 
and value of this report.  Through our review we determined that with the transitions made 
in 2024, it has made several impacts on how this annual report is presented.  This recap 
will be the Public Transit Advisory Board’s version of the 2024 Annual Report. 
1/1/2024 - Management Contract Transition to a Turnkey Management Agreement 
Via Transportation was selected in the fall of 2023 as the turnkey management for Sioux 
Area Metro after approximately 35 years of management under First Transit.  The ‘turnkey’ 
model has provided a new perspective and opportunity to see the potential of our transit 
system through a new lens.  This has been a great learning experience for all departments 
within the city and the SAM team and has only been possible with incredible collaboration, 
dedication, trust, and hard work.  No major change in any project escapes without some 
frustration; the wins are the rewards.  We’ve had both.   
The focus of this transition was to ‘Reimagine Transit’ for Sioux Falls.  The roots of this 
transition plan began with the 2019 Study.  One result of the study was the goal of 
implementing On-Demand transit in Sioux Falls.  A pilot was executed.  Via Transportation’s 
concentration is On-Demand transportation.   This is how our 2024 transition plan was put 
into play: 
• Several Public Engagement Presentations, City Council & PTAB Communications 
o Continued Saturday On-Demand 
o Implemented Southeast On-Demand Service 
o Redesign of the Fixed Route System as suggested in the 2023 TDP 
o Retained 99% of the SAM employees 
o Union negations update 
o Hired new General Manager – October 2024 
• August 2024 – Implemented full-city On-Demand Service 
o To date includes 25 On-Demand accessible Vans 
o Comingle with Paratransit system by year end 
o Removed the grandfathered Paratransit coverage 
• September 2024 – Implemented Redesign of Fixed Route System 
o Reduced routes from 12 to nine 
o Increased frequency on a majority of routes

Via and the SAM team work together on statistical reporting.  This view is di`erent from 
what we have seen through previous management.  Change isn’t always easy, but 
characteristically good!  Keeping in mind the contract model is entirely di`erent, so will the 
reporting data be.  Our data point focus includes: 
• Ridership – to include ambulatory/non-ambulatory 
o Fixed Route 
o Paratransit 
o On-Demand 
• On-Time Performance 
• Safety 
• Customer Satisfaction 
• Revenue Hours 
• Revenue Miles 
• Cost Per Hour per mode of Service 
This report does not include the year-end statistics for 2024.  With the redesign of the Fixed 
Route system in September as well as the transition of the On-Demand to full 
implementation in August, the number will not be comparable to 2023.  Via is providing the 
City Council with an update in June 2025 that will include several reports.  This data will be 
provided to PTAB at the June 2025 meeting as well.  
2/2024 – FTA Triennial Review 
This federal compliance requirement came at a very busy time that included major 
management changes both at the City of Sioux Falls and SAM.  It is a three-year review of 
our federal funding activities and the compliance that is involved with receiving these 
funds.  It was humbling to work with the many departments within the city and SAM system 
to complete this review.  The ﬁnal review resulted in one minor ﬁnding.  However, the 
greatest outcome of this review was the greater understanding by all departments within 
the city of the transit program, it’s funding mechanisms, federal regulations, 
responsibilities and the role public transportation plays in our communities.  We should be 
proud.
