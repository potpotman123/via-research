Mobility Management 
In addition to the van transportation service listed above, MCTS has secured an FTA 
Section 5310 Grant for 2023-2024 to fund Mobility Management activities that support 
use of the transit system by older adults and persons with disabilities. These funds have 
also been requested for the 2025-2026 grant cycle.  The activities supported by this 
grant include individual and group travel training; community outreach and education; 
mobility device training; bus operator ADA sensitivity and passenger assistance training.   
 
Transit Plus Same Day Pilot 
In September 2023, Transit Plus ended a long-standing taxi program that served 
ambulatory Transit Plus riders due to the lack of wheelchair accessible vehicles (WAVs) 
and required random drug and alcohol testing for the drivers.  Knowing that this 
program was ending, the Milwaukee County Board of Supervisors set aside contingency
=== page 4 ===
funds in the 2024 budget to allow for the implementation of a replacement program.  
The Paratransit Taxi Task Force met throughout 2023 and 2024 to develop the service 
parameters for this replacement program in line with the Board’s stipulations.   
 
Service began on the Transit Plus Same Day Pilot at the end of August 2024 and is 
contracted with Tidewater Transit, dba Via, to run until September 2025 or until 
budgeted funds are expired.  Service is provided on a same day basis for 
health/medical or employment rides that could not have been scheduled the day before 
and thus could not have been scheduled on the existing paratransit vans.  Some 
examples of the use cases for this service would include a same day trip to urgent care, 
going to pick up a sick child, or being called into work.  All vehicles are wheelchair 
accessible, and drivers are subject to random drug and alcohol testing. Rides can be 
shared or private depending on demand.  The contracted rate for this service is $98.73 
per vehicle hour.   
 
Service runs from 7 AM to 5:59 PM, 7 days/week and is provided border to border 
within Milwaukee County.  The rider fare is $10 per one-way trip. A Personal Care 
Attendant (when required due to rider disability) is allowed to accompany the rider at no 
cost. One companion can ride with the client for an additional $10. Fare can be paid 
with a credit/debit card or a cash voucher code purchased at the MCTS Administration 
Building.  Trip booking can be done using a smartphone app or over the phone. The 
vehicle must arrive within one hour of booking the trip. 
 
There were 10 trips provided on this program in September and 10 trips in October. 
Transit Plus and the Task Force members continue efforts to educate potential riders 
about the availability of this service. 
 
SUMMARY 
The MCTS Paratransit Program, known as Transit Plus manages all aspects of ADA 
paratransit eligibility, service provision, reporting and compliance with state and federal 
requirements. In addition, Transit Plus manages a same day discretionary program that 
is offered by Milwaukee County to help offset the unmet need for same day 
transportation for persons with disabilities. All programs are closely monitored to 
maintain service efficiency and effectiveness. Complaints are carefully recorded, 
investigated and resolved. Efforts to enable persons with disabilities to be able to 
successfully use fixed route services in addition to, or instead of, paratransit services 
are on-going through mobility management.
