5. 
Contract Award – Stockless Office Supplies 
9:30 a.m.  A briefing paper was included in the meeting materials, requesting that the Audit, 
Finance and Administration Committee accept, and forward to the Board of Commissioners for 
approval, this request to authorize the President & CEO to renew the Sourcewell Participating 
Agreement with Staples Contract & Commercial LLC, who was awarded the contract as a result 
of a competitive procurement process and is most advantageous to BSD with price and other 
factors considered, for a five-year (2025-2029) timeframe, in a not-to-exceed amount of $1 
million.  William A. Dee, Vice President, Procurement and Inventory Management, gave an 
overview of this item.  He noted that in December 2011, the then President & CEO of BSD 
signed a contract modification, which allowed BSD to continue using Staples as BSD’s office 
supply vendor through joining a purchasing alliance called National Joint Powers Alliance 
(NJPA), and on June 6, 2018, the National Joint Powers Alliance (NJPA) changed its name to 
Sourcewell. Mr. Dee reported that, in February 2020, BSD’s Internal Audit conducted an audit of 
the stockless office supplies NJPA agreement and recommended Procurement to submit a 
briefing paper to obtain Committee and Board Approval every five (5) years to remain a member 
of the National Joint Powers Alliance (NJPA), now Sourcewell.   
 
Mr. Dee stated that Sourcewell was created by state law as a service cooperative to provide 
programs and services to education and government agencies, and it was established with the 
statutory purpose of assisting public agencies in meeting specific needs that are more efficiently 
delivered cooperatively than by an entity individually.  He reported that stockless office needs for 
the Agency are ordered via authorized BSD employees, through the Staples Advantage ordering 
platform. BSD’s past spending from 2022 through 2024 has been approximately $100,000 to 
$122,000 per year ($122,000 x 5 = $610,000); therefore, BSD anticipates the next five-year total 
spend to not exceed $1 million.  He stated that management is recommending that that the 
Finance, Audit and Administration Committee accept, and forward to the Board of 
Commissioners for approval, this request to authorize the President & CEO to renew the 
Sourcewell Participating Agreement with Staples Contract & Commercial LLC, who was 
awarded the contract as a result of a competitive procurement process and is most advantageous 
to BSD with price and other factors considered, for a five-year (2025-2029) timeframe, in a not-
to-exceed amount of $1 million. 
 
A motion to approve this item, as presented, was made by Commissioner Beach and seconded by 
Commissioner Johnson.   
 
The motion passed unanimously.

6. 
Pension Plans, 401(k) Retirement Savings Program and OPEB Trust Investment 
Performance Update as of December 31, 2024 
9:33 a.m.   A briefing paper was included in the meeting materials, to present to the Audit, 
Finance and Administration Committee, the Pension Plans, 401(k) Retirement Savings Program 
and OPEB Trust Investment Performance Update as of December 31, 2024.  Carmen Brothers, 
Retirement Plan Manager, provided an overview of this item noting the following: 
 
Year to date December 31, 2024, Pension Plan, 401(k) Retirement Savings Program and OPEB 
Investment Performance Reports were presented to the respective Trustees by CAPTRUST at the 
February 2025 round of trustee committee meetings. 
 
BSD 401(k) Retirement Savings Program 
 • 
Total 401(k) Program assets were $108.5M as of the end of December 2024. This is up 
$17.8M in combined contributions and investment earnings from calendar year end 2023 
which stood at $90.7M.   
 
Salaried Pension Plan  
 
• 
As of December 31, total Plan assets were $109.0M. The plan experienced $1.2 million 
in investment losses during the fourth quarter of 2024 and $11.4 million in investment 
gains for the year. Fourth quarter cash flows totaled -0.8M, with year-to-date cash flows 
of -$3.3M.   
 
IBEW Pension Plan   
 
• 
As of December 31, total Plan assets were $8.0M. With the exception of 2018 and 2022, 
Total Portfolio Market values have steadily increased over the past 11 years. In 2008 the 
market value fell to $1.1M, but has quadrupled since reaching the market bottom. Now 
that the Plan is fully-funded the cash flows into the Plan have moderated. 
 
ATU 788 Pension Plan 
 
• 
As of December 31, total Plan assets were $179.6M.  Fund flows over the past few years 
have been consistently negative and 2024 continued this trend. Fourth quarter cash flows 
totaled -1.6M. The Portfolio experienced investment losses of -3.5M for the quarter and 
$16.1M of gains for 2024. 
 
OPEB Trust  
 
• 
The OPEB Retirement Trust ended the year with a total Portfolio market value of 
$76.0M. The plan experienced $1.1 million in investment losses during the fourth quarter 
and $7.2 million in investment gains for 2024. 
 
This item was presented as information only.
