Background 
Metropolitan Transportation Services provides transit service using contracts with private 
companies. The microtransit service is a demand-responsive transit service where users 
request on-demand trips either using a mobile application or by calling a reservationist and 
trips are assigned to the next available vehicle for completion.  This contract will provide a 
fully hosted and maintained software solution with a secure combination of public-facing, 
ADA-compliant rider app, driver app, dispatch system, account management system, robust 
operations, monitoring, administrative and reporting functions to support Metro micro service 
in North Minneapolis, Woodbury Area, and Roseville Area with additional service zones to 
be added in 2025 through 2027. 
A Request for Proposals was issued on June 5, 2024. A pre-proposal meeting was hosted 
by Council staff that outlined the solicitation requirements, discussed project specifications, 
and responded to plan holder inquires. There were twenty-four registered plan holders 
including eleven prime proposers, two consultants, three subcontractors, five suppliers, 
three plan rooms, and nine plan holders identified as minority, woman, small, veteran, or 
disadvantaged business enterprises. On July 17, the Council received four proposals for 
consideration of award. 
An evaluation panel consisting of staff from Metropolitan Transportation Services, Metro 
Transit, and Minnesota Valley Transit Authority, evaluated the proposals using the following 
criteria: proposal quality, proposer’s qualifications and experience, proposer’s capabilities 
and ability to deliver the service, and proposal price. The evaluation panel recommends 
Spare Labs Inc., as having all the required attributes and to be the most advantageous to

Thrive Lens Analysis 
This item supports several Thrive Outcomes. Microtransit service, implemented through 
this software, is provided in portions of the region otherwise not served by all day transit, 
which furthers Stewardship by increasing the average number of jobs reachable by transit 
and Equity by connecting additional residents to opportunity. The Micro Transit service, 
implemented through this software, facilitates the use of additional local service in BRT and 
LRT corridors, which increases Livability and Equity for mobility-challenged individuals by 
providing added local stops. Financial Stewardship is also furthered because this contract 
provides a lower cost solution for a lower productivity transit service. 
 
Funding 
Funding for this service is included in the annual operating budget.
