CONDITIONS OF COMMENCEMENT OF PERFORMANCE 
AND 
SCHEDULE OF PERFORMANCE 
 
Nomad Transit shall not commence performance or be entitled to compensation or reimbursement 
for any services rendered, prior to the occurrence of each of the following conditions: (1) this 
Contract-DSA must be executed by an authorized representative of Nomad Transit and the District; 
and, if required, (2) this Contract-DSA must be approved by the Board of Directors.  
 
This Contract-DSA becomes effective upon signature by all parties and remains in effect through 
August 31, 2027, unless terminated prior to this date per the conditions herein.
