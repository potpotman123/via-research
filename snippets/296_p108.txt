7. As applicable to the products, services, and solutions
being offered, describe the accommodations the Offeror
can provide to passengers who have physical disabilities or
individuals with dietary restrictions.
Via’s on-demand transit services bring underserved individuals — particularly those 
without access to private transportation and those with mobility limitations — into the 
social fabric of their communities. We are committed to delivering public mobility 
solutions to riders of all abilities and means. In the services we operate, Via includes 
wheelchair-accessible vehicles (WAV) in the vehicle fleet and will ensure that all WAVs are 
equipped with accessibility features (including wheelchair ramps) to meet the diverse 
needs of our customer’s riders. By accommodating riders with mobility limitations, Via’s 
services empower these populations to travel freely throughout their communities. 
To ensure the reliability of these features, Via performs regular maintenance checks and 
inspections of wheelchair lifts and other vehicle accessibility features, confirming that all 
remain in proper working condition. 
Maintenance records document these checks, 
ensuring full transparency and availability to the 
FTA upon request. Should any accessibility 
feature become damaged or inoperative, Via 
acts swiftly to coordinate repairs and minimize 
service disruptions. Additionally, all drivers 
receive specialized training in the operation of

Via has significant experience helping partners meet Americans with Disability Act (ADA) 
requirements through the provision of on-demand services. Via is both a developer of 
paratransit software and direct operator of several ADA paratransit services ourselves, 
serving as the paratransit operator for Capital Area Transit System in Louisiana and Green 
Bay Metro in Wisconsin. We therefore have a detailed understanding of accessibility 
requirements for transit providers, and we are prepared to offer on-demand transportation 
that is truly accessible. In all of our turnkey services, we commit to the following: 
●
Deliver an equivalent quality of service for ambulatory and non-ambulatory
customers;
●
Ensure that all drivers are trained in the delivery of accessible service, including
the use of vehicle ramps and provision of door-to-door service; and
●
Ensure that all drivers have gone through sensitivity training and are prepared to
deliver exceptional service to all riders.
Moreover, Via’s on-demand solution is designed to offer support for riders with visual or 
hearing impairments, riders without smartphones, and riders who are unbanked. The Via 
Rider App takes full advantage of iOS and Android accessibility tools — including 
VoiceOver and Talkback, screen readers for iOS and Android devices respectively, 
adaptive font sizes and contrast, and switch control. Via relies on broadly-adopted and 
recognized accessibility standards — such as compliance with the Web Content 
Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) at the 2.0 AA level, as well as the American with 
Disabilities Act (ADA) — to ensure all riders can easily engage with all service 
communication.
