The proposed contract will use a fleet of six-passenger vehicles to continue to provide Via 
service throughout the city limits and to and from the CentrePort TRE Station and will add 
new service to the Trinity Metro bus stop located on Eastchase Parkway in Fort Worth. Under 
the proposed contract, at least fifty percent of the non-ADA fleet shall be hybrid or electric at 
contract start, and one hundred percent of the non-ADA fleet shall be hybrid or electric by 
the start of the second year of the contract term.
A Request for Proposals (RFP) was issued on January 19, 2022 to solicit responses from various 
firms experienced with providing transportation and demand response rideshare services, in 
order to continue the on-demand rideshare service. An evaluation team, comprised of City 
staff, evaluated and scored the submittals based on weighted criteria; including data collection 
and reporting requirements, vendors’ experience, quality assurance, customer service, 
performance monitoring, innovative private/public partnership, and overall cost. The following 
firms were evaluated:

The total cost for two years of standard on-demand rideshare service is $22,047,916. 
$17,064,760 of this cost will come from a mix of Federal Transit Administration (FTA) funding
(covering approximately 40 percent of the cost) and City funding (covering approximately 60 
percent of the cost). The remaining $4,983,156 will come from farebox and advertising
revenue that will go directly back into the service. 
In addition to the standard Via service costs listed above, three other related Via services are 
expected to be implemented through additional external and internal funding.  
The North Central Texas Council of Governments (NCTCOG) provided an additional $350,000 
in FTA funds for the Arlington Student Transportation Program at no additional cost to the 
City. The program currently provides free Via rides for eligible Tarrant County College and 
Arlington Independent School District students. As of October 31, 2022, $179,502 of the 
original $350,000 was available to cover the cost of free Via rides for eligible students. 
The City has also been awarded a $4,560,000 grant from NCTCOG to continue and expand 
the RAPID project. The RAPID project was initially a one-year deployment, which operated 
from March 23, 2021, to March 18, 2022, funded in part by a grant from the Federal Transit 
Administration. This project integrated a fleet of autonomous vehicles on the Via rideshare 
platform for operation in a set area in Downtown Arlington and on the University of Texas at 
Arlington (UTA) campus. Via partnered with May Mobility to provide the autonomous vehicles 
(AV) for the project. 
The forthcoming NCTCOG grant funding will continue the RAPID service for an additional two-
year deployment, as well as add new autonomous vehicles to the service and test technologies 
related to safely removing the human operator from the AVs. During the term of this contract, 
the City will pay Via up to $4,020,224 for services performed for deployment of the Arlington 
RAPID project. City funding in the amount of $100,000 will be used to continue the RAPID

service prior to the availability of the NCTCOG funding. Once available and if accepted by City

The City is currently considering opportunities to provide on-demand rideshare service into 
neighboring municipalities to serve additional travel needs of our residents and visitors. A 
maximum cost of $4,000,000 could be used during this contract term to provide some form 
of intercity service, if Council decides to move forward with intercity service. 
The Texas Attorney General has previously ruled that certain information in the Via contract 
is confidential and cannot be disclosed to the public, and therefore a redacted copy of the 
contract is included in the agenda packet.
