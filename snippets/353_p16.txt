Description of Vendor Entity and Vendor's Goods & Services
If awarded, this description of Vendor and Vendor's goods and services will appear on the TIPS website for
customer/public viewing.
Via offers an end-to-end transportation technology platform and a turnkey operating model that allows partners of
all sizes and levels of complexity to manage every aspect of public mobility. We offer innovative software and
services that include student transportation, microtransit, ADA paratransit, senior transportation, and integrated
trip planning and payment systems. To date, we have deployed transit solutions in partnership with over 700
communities in over 40 countries around the world, powering over 140 million rides. As the direct operator of over
75 transportation services around the globe, we deeply understand the day-to-day realities that public entities and
nonprofits, including school districts, cities, counties, and regional agencies, face in delivering public transit. This
first-hand experience directly informs our software products and operational models, allowing us to ensure that our
offering is tailored to meet the specific needs of our partners and their communities. ​ ​ Since Via’s founding in
2012, we have delivered exceptional service to our partners and have collaborated with them to improve
accessibility for their communities. Along the way, we have heard countless stories about the positive impact of our
services from Riders (“I am able to get to where I need, at a cost I can actually afford”), Drivers (“It feels good to be
able to provide a service and help people accomplish the things they need to during the day”), and Partners (“This
partnership with Via is the next generation of what public transportation can be”). Building upon our experience
delivering large-scale transportation services around the world, we are proud to have built a strong reputation for
providing transportation that is safe, reliable, and efficient, especially for the passengers that need it most.
