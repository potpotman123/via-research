12.  Advertising 
 
During the Contract term of the Service, Contractor can collect revenue by entering into 
advertising partnerships. Contractor will be solely responsible for coordinating the 
implementation of various advertising solutions which may be effected through third party 
service providers and include, but shall not be limited to, selling advertising space on the 
vehicles. In the event any services are effected through third party service providers, Contractor 
shall provide notice thereof to City.        
      
Nonetheless, in implementing advertising for the Service, Contractor shall comply with the 
Arlington On-Demand Rideshare Service Advertising Guidelines approved by Arlington City 
Council on October 13, 2020, as may be amended from time to time, which are incorporated 
herein as Exhibit D by reference as though written word for word. In the event City receives an 
appeal of Contractor’s decision not to run an advertisement, the City shall provide prior written 
notice (e-mail acceptable) to Contractor of the appeal and of its ultimate decision.  Contractor 
shall notify the City of the reason for the denial.   
      
Contractor will be responsible for all costs associated with the advertising program, including but 
not limited to installation, maintenance, repairs, customer service. All advertising revenue 
collected by Contractor will be credited to the City on the monthly statements. Contractor shall 
provide monthly reports to the City in respect of this program which reporting shall provide an
