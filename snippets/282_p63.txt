10. Arlington Student Transportation Program
The City has received additional federal funding on the condition that it implement an Arlington
Student Transportation Program, whereby eligible Tarrant County College (TCC) and Arlington
Independent School District (AISD) students can use the Services at no cost to the student. The
amount of additional federal funding received is equal to the amount of local funding necessary
to implement the Program and as a result the implementation has no financial impact on the City.
This program may be extended to other schools and/or colleges upon approval of funding.
Contractor shall provide City with promotional/discount codes ("Promo Codes") on an as needed
basis, beginning December 11, 2022. Each Promo Code will have a unique identifier, will cover
the full Ride Cost of any ride taken during a specific period of time, and will have an expiration
date to be determined by the City. When the user uses the Promo Code, that user will be able to
use the Service at no cost to the user. Contractor shall provide Promo Codes to the City on an as
needed basis, but no later than 14 calendar days after City sends Contractor the request.
11. Arlington RAPID
The City wishes to continue the Arlington RAPID (Rideshare, Automation, and Payment
Integration Demonstration). Through Arlington RAPID, Contractor will continue to collaborate
with the City to integrate a fleet of autonomous vehicles into the current vehicle fleet, including
at least one wheelchair accessible autonomous vehicle, to provide Services in a zone covering
Downtown Arlington and the University of Texas at Arlington (UTA) campus (shown in the
Autonomous Vehicle General Service Area Map in Exhibit B). Contractor will configure the
existing application to allow all riders the booking of autonomous vehicle trips in the proposed
zone depicted in the map included herein. The map may be amended from time to time upon the
written agreement between City and Contractor.

12.  Advertising 
 
During the Contract term of the Service, Contractor can collect revenue by entering into 
advertising partnerships. Contractor will be solely responsible for coordinating the 
implementation of various advertising solutions which may be effected through third party 
service providers and include, but shall not be limited to, selling advertising space on the 
vehicles. In the event any services are effected through third party service providers, Contractor 
shall provide notice thereof to City.        
      
Nonetheless, in implementing advertising for the Service, Contractor shall comply with the 
Arlington On-Demand Rideshare Service Advertising Guidelines approved by Arlington City 
Council on October 13, 2020, as may be amended from time to time, which are incorporated 
herein as Exhibit D by reference as though written word for word. In the event City receives an 
appeal of Contractor’s decision not to run an advertisement, the City shall provide prior written 
notice (e-mail acceptable) to Contractor of the appeal and of its ultimate decision.  Contractor 
shall notify the City of the reason for the denial.   
      
Contractor will be responsible for all costs associated with the advertising program, including but 
not limited to installation, maintenance, repairs, customer service. All advertising revenue 
collected by Contractor will be credited to the City on the monthly statements. Contractor shall 
provide monthly reports to the City in respect of this program which reporting shall provide an
