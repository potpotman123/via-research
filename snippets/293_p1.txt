Resolution No. __________ 
 
A resolution authorizing the execution of an Amendment 
#1 to the On-Demand Rideshare Contract with River 
North Transit, LLC., of New York, New York (“Via”) in 
an amount not to exceed $164,984, to include paratransit 
software services and creation of a Vietnamese language 
smart phone application 
 
 
WHEREAS, the City partnered with Via Transportation, Inc., to launch an on-demand 
rideshare service on December 11, 2017 to offer an additional mobility 
option for residents, employees and visitors; and 
 
WHEREAS, on November 29, 2022, City Council approved Resolution No. 22-335, 
authorizing the most recent contract for on-demand rideshare service with 
River North Transit, LLC., for an amount not to exceed $30,247,642; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City desires to amend the contract to include paratransit software 
services and creation of a Vietnamese language smart phone application; 
NOW THEREFORE 
 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF ARLINGTON, 
TEXAS: 
I. 
 
That all of the recitals contained in the preamble of this resolution are found to be 
true and are adopted as findings of fact by this governing body and as part of its official 
record. 
 
II. 
 
 
That the City Manager or his designee is hereby authorized to execute an 
Amendment #1 to the On-Demand Rideshare Contract with River North Transit, LLC., of 
New York, New York, to include paratransit software services and creation of a 
Vietnamese language smart phone application.  These amendments to the Contract 
represent a total of $164,984 and increase the amount of the Contract to an amount not to 
exceed $30,412,626.  Funds are available in the Transportation Department Grant Account 
No. CC418604-61000-SC0533-GR000018 and subject to FY2024 and FY2025 budget 
approval.
