Staff's Recommended Board Motion:
Award a five (5) year procurement to the highest-ranked and best qualified firm, Via Transportation,
Inc., d/b/a Via Mobility, LLC, and execute the related Deployment Service Order for the implementation,
administration, and support of a new paratransit scheduling system (RFP-23-24177) for the Sunshine
Line at a total cost of $860,825.00. Proposals were received from five (5) different proposers, all of
which were thoroughly reviewed, scored, and ranked by the tending evaluation committee. In this case,
the opportunity for proposers to receive five (5) bonus points by subcontracting a minimum of 10% of
the ultimate fees to Certified Disadvantaged Minority/Disadvantaged Women Business Enterprises
(DM/DWBE) was available; however, none of the proposers requested such points. Funding for this
procurement is available in the requesting department's approved budget, and purchase orders will only
be issued against approved budgets.
Financial Impact Statement:
Funding for this procurement is available in the requesting department's approved budget, and purchase
orders will only be issued against approved budgets.
Background:
The recommended award amount is approximately 31.1% ($389,175.00) lower than the requesting
department's annual estimate. Effective competition and transparency were achieved through the use of
competitive sealed proposals for this procurement and the requesting department determined the offered
pricing to be fair and reasonable. The recommended firm will install, implement, administer, and
maintain a new paratransit scheduling system, which will include managing client eligibility, advance
and real-time trip reservations, issuing bus passes, manual and fully automated trip scheduling,
dispatching, automatic vehicle tracking, billing, and reporting. The resulting contract will be made
available to the Hillsborough County Governmental Purchasing Council.
