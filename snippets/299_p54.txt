Rider App 
Via’s platform includes a white-labeled Rider App, a smartphone application compliant 
with WCAG 2.1 standards and available for free download in both the iOS and Android 
stores. For individuals without smartphones or internet access, or for those who simply 
prefer additional assistance over the phone, Via’s system allows riders to book rides by 
calling a customer support representative, or through a web version of our mobile 
application if desired by the customer. 
Our commingled app makes it seamless for riders to transition between 
microtransit and on-demand services, even when different zones are supported. 
Via’s simple and customer-friendly Rider App has been downloaded by millions of 
passengers and consistently receives glowing reviews. If a partner has configured their 
service to allow for real-time or pre-scheduled booking through the app, riders can easily 
toggle between both options (as shown in the graphic above). Riders can also make minor 
changes to their booked rides, pay for rides through a variety of payment options, 
indicate the need for a wheelchair, and plan multimodal journeys, including transfers to 
fixed route buses.

Driver App 
Our driver application has been developed to make drivers’ jobs safer and easier and can 
run on a variety of cellular enabled tablets and smartphones. The app provides a single 
interface where a driver can access the information they need before, during, and after 
their shifts. This includes turn-by-turn navigation, a dynamically updated rider manifest 
that integrates seamlessly with the Via Operations Center, automated notifications, and 
“one click” task acknowledgements. Real-time location data from the rider and driver 
apps feeds back into our algorithms, allowing for dynamic optimization of routing and trip 
assignments. 
Integration Capabilities 
Via's platform is designed for a high level of integration; our modular and open 
architecture is purpose-built for ease of integration with other platforms using our proven 
APIs. This system architecture reflects our company mission, which is to help our 
partners manage all transportation modes within a single holistic platform. This vision also 
animates our product and engineering team's focus on developing world class software 
products that connect effectively with other technology systems.
