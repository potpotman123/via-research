8. How will price updates be handled?
We suggest periodic (annual) price adjustment, and we reserve the right to adjust prices 
on an annual basis based upon the US BLS Consumer Price Index (we expect our price 
adjustments to generally align with CPI). We are also often working to expand our product 
offerings, so we may wish to add new SKUs from time to time so that our latest offerings 
are available to participating agencies.

B. Reputation of the Vendor and of the Vendor's
Goods and Services
1. What is the vendor's reputation in the industry?
Via Transportation, Inc. is the world's leading provider of TransitTech solutions, including
microtransit, ADA paratransit, senior transportation, school transportation, and
non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT). Via was founded in 2012, and in 2013 we
launched in New York City what was, as we understand it, the world's first marketplace
for on-demand shared rides. Our experience as operators of a large-scale urban demand
response transit service and as primary users of our own software, together with the data
from the tens of millions of rides we've delivered, has been critical for developing the
highly efficient, purpose-built algorithms and deep vertical technology stack that are the
foundation of our modern solution.
Today, we partner with more than 650 cities, transit agencies, and school districts
across the globe, providing operations and software to improve efficiency, rider
experience, and accessibility. The result is safer rides with fewer vehicle miles traveled
and less time spent in the vehicle.

2. Are the vendor’s products/services well-regarded?
Via’s products are recognized as the market leading solutions for transit technology and 
operations, used by cities and transit agencies worldwide to modernize their legacy 
systems into smart, efficient, data-driven networks. Our solutions are known for 
delivering industry-leading cost-efficiency, reliability, and service quality. Via’s platform 
powers a wide array of transportation modes—including microtransit, ADA-paratransit, 
student and senior transport, NEMT, AV and EV fleet management, and school bus 
routing—along with fixed-route and on-demand planning tools. Our reputation is built on 
proven performance: our proprietary algorithms and AI-driven routing reduce travel times, 
vehicle miles, and costs while increasing utilization and rider satisfaction. With a track 
record of innovation and measurable impact, Via’s software and services are consistently 
recognized as best-in-class across the global mobility landscape.
