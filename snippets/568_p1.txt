Background
Historically when developing, modifying, and evaluating trolley routes, schedules, stops, potential
passenger access, and costs, the Public Works staff has relied on a number of software programs including
Excel, Google Earth, and Census data to perform the analysis. In addition, the Public Works Department
(PWD) has also relied on the assistance of consultants to further refine the transit routes from a planning
concept to an implementable reality. However, this method of transit planning has become antiquated
with the development of Remix. Remix is a cloud-based software that provides a transit platform that
brings together the complete transportation picture into a single program. Remix's Transit Platform
provides quick bus route design and analysis capacities through an internet browser interface, including:
map-based route and stop planning, stop-based demographic analysis, operating cost calculations based on
both frequencies and timetables, and customizable accessibility visualizations and analytics.

The PWD is requesting to purchase the Remix Software license for a three (3) year period. Remix will
facilitate the development of transit alignment analysis, will help create public transportation routes, will
help project ridership, and develop schedules based on GPS roadway data. This software is needed to
refine the trolley alignments of the preferred alternative recommended in the Doral Trolley / SMART Plan
Coordination Study, and for the development of the new trolley route schedules. The total cost of the
software for the first year is $14,000.00, which includes a one-time setup fee of $2,000.00, a one-time
data fee of $1,000.00, and a $11,000.00 yearly fee. The first-year complete cost will be funded by the
Public Works Department Transportation Fund. From the second year on, the yearly fee ($11,000.00)
will be carried in the Information & Technology Department "Repair Maintenance Office Equipment
Other" Account, Account No. 001.22005.500464.
Procurement
Remix has provided documentation identifying themselves as a sole source for the only cloud-based transit
planning software that provides quick bus route design and analysis capacities through an internet browser
interface. Miami-Dade County and the City of Miami are also customers of Remix. Remix is a
subcontractor through a contract between WSP (a transportation consultant firm) and Miami-Dade
County for the County's Vision Zero project, which is a safety program with a goal of eliminating fatalities
due to vehicular crashes. The City of Miami has a two (2) year contract with Remix which was executed
on January 18, 2019 and was not competitively bid.
Additionally, the PWD performed market research on transit planning software to determine the
capabilities of other products in the market. There are several software's that exist that are similar to
Remix that assist agencies with the planning of new transit routes. However, the other softwares lack the
ability to provide service cost estimates, demographic data for Title VI requirements, or the number of
vehicles required for the transit service. Additionally, Remix would not require additional GIS software
to draw out the new potential alignment manually, based on multiple scenarios developed. The table
