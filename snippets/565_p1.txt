The Public Works Department (PWD) respectfully requests the Mayor and City Councilmembers to

Background
On August 12, 2020, the Mayor and City Councilmembers adopted Resolution No. 20-158 (approved 5-
0) authorizing the City Manager to enter into a three (3) year agreement with Remix Software, Inc. for
the transit platform in an amount not to exceed $ 14,000.00 for the first year and $11,000.00 in the seconc
and third year. The Public Works Department funded the first-year cost which included a one-time setup
fee of $2,000.00, the data fee of $1,000.00, and a $11,000.00 yearly fee. The subsequent second- and
third-year yearly fee was to be carried in the Information & Technology Department (IT) budget "Repair
Maintenance Office Equipment Other" Account, Account No. 001.22005.500464.
The IT budgeted $ 11,000.00 for Fiscal Year 2021-22 to cover the second-year cost of the Remix Software
and will budget funds to cover the cost for the third year, however when the item was originally presented
to the City Council for authorization, the PWD did not take into account the annual data fee of $1,000.00
in addition to the yearly $11,000.00 software fee, for a total fee of $ $12,000.00. As such, the PWD is
requesting to increase the approved contract amount with Remix for the second- and third-year contract

Procurement
Remix provided documentation identifying themselves as a sole source for the only cloud-based transit
planning software that provides quick bus route design and analysis capacities through an internet browser
interface. Miami-Dade County and the City of Miami are also customers of Remix. Remix is a
subcontractor through a contract between WSP (a transportation consultant firm) and Miami-Dade
County for the County's Vision Zero project, which is a safety program with a goal of eliminating fatalities
due to vehicular crashes. The City of Miami has a two (2) year contract with Remix which was executed
on January 18, 2019 and was not competitively bid.
Additionally, the PWD performed market research on transit planning software to determine the
capabilities of other products in the market. There are several software's that exist that are similar to
Remix that assist agencies with the planning of new transit routes. However, the other software lack the
ability to provide service cost estimates, demographic data for Title VI requirements, or the number of
vehicles required for the transit service. Additionally, Remix would not require additional GIS software
to draw out the new potential alignment manually, based on multiple scenarios developed. The table
