Exhibit B 
METHOD OF PAYMENT 
 
The Fees for the Service are outlined below.  
 
Western Zone 
The Contractor will require the County to issue ongoing monthly fees based on vehicle hours. All 
regulatory fees related to the service will be charged as a pass-through cost. The Project shall 
include a fleet of vehicles as described in Exhibit A, Service Parameters, subject to extension by 
mutual agreement of the parties on terms to be agreed, including any change in fees. The total 
contract amount for the Highlands Ranch service shall not exceed two million seven hundred 
ninety-two thousand ten dollars and zero cents ($2,792,010.00).  
 
 
   
Ongoing Invoice Fees  
The County shall be responsible for monthly ongoing fees of seventy dollars and nineteen cents 
($71.59) per vehicle hour (the “Monthly Subscription Fees”). A vehicle hour is defined as each 
hour during which a driver is paid on the Via platform. Notwithstanding the number of vehicle 
hours incurred per month, the County shall be responsible for a minimum Monthly Subscription 
Fee of fifty-five thousand dollars and zero cents ($55,000) per month (the “Minimum Monthly 
Fees”). The Minimum Monthly Fees shall be payable in advance on the first of the month provided 
that the first Minimum Monthly Fees shall be due upon execution of this Contract. If the Monthly 
Subscription Fees for any month exceed the Minimum Monthly Fees paid hereunder, the County 
shall be responsible for paying such difference (the “Monthly True-up”) on a monthly invoice 
basis. All fees set forth herein shall be payable by the County on a net fifteen basis. If the 
Contractor does not receive timely payment, the Contractor may charge the maximum monthly 
interest allowed by law or one percent, whichever is greater, suspend performance and seek cost 
of collection, including reasonable attorneys’ fees.

The Contractor shall provide to the County an invoice statement showing the calculation of the 
Monthly Subscription Fees incurred based on the actual number of vehicle hours that Driver 
Partners performed during that month in the form included at the bottom of Exhibit B. The County 
hereby agrees that the sample invoice form is satisfactory to the County, both in substance and 
format, and sufficient to process invoicing such that questions regarding invoice or invoice support 
format will not delay payment to the Contractor. If the County requires additional invoice support, 
the Contractor will use best efforts to provide it and additional costs may apply.   
 
If changes in applicable federal, state, or local law result in a significant change in the Contractor’s 
costs, the parties may opt to renegotiate the ongoing monthly fees. Should changes in applicable 
market dynamics result in an increase in the Contractor’s cost per hour of ten percent (10%) or 
more to any of the following categories, compared to costs at the time of contract signing, the 
parties may amend the ongoing on-demand vehicle hour rate to cover labor (driver pay), vehicle 
rental, and fuel.  
 
Should the Contractor elect to adjust the per vehicle hour rate, the Contractor shall provide 
supporting documentation of the relevant cost increases to the County, and the Contract and/or its 
exhibits will be amended to reflect the new Fee structure and per vehicle hour rate so that, at the 
discretion of the County, either:  
1. The total not-to-exceed contract value is adjusted such that the total not-to-exceed number 
of vehicle hours remains unchanged, or  
2. The total not-to-exceed number of vehicle hours is adjusted such that the total not-to-
exceed contract value remains unchanged.  
 
If the County requires additional WAV retrofits or vehicle wraps, the Contractor will bill the 
County at cost as a pass-through as incurred, at an estimated cost of seventeen thousand dollars 
and zero cents ($17,000.00) each for WAV retrofit, and vehicle wraps for the fleet at an estimated 
cost of three thousand dollars and zero cents ($3,000.00) each. 
 
 
Eastern Zone 
The Fees for the Service are outlined below. The Contractor will require the County to issue an 
upfront fee, and ongoing monthly fees based on vehicle hours. All regulatory fees related to the 
service will be charged as a pass-through cost. The Project shall include a fleet of vehicles as 
described in Exhibit A, Service Parameters, subject to extension by mutual agreement of the parties 
on terms to be agreed, including any change in fees. The total contract amount shall not exceed 
one million six hundred twelve thousand six hundred five dollars and zero cents ($1,612,605.00).
