The real issue is that for many people there are no alternatives available for driving and for the elderly or teens too young to drive, this
lifeline is only available for limited use. Going out to dinner and getting a ride back afterwards, weekend trips to see a movie, Sunday
morning going to church, all become problematic for someone who doesn’t drive.
Public transport is a service not a business. Getting people where they need to go and then getting back afterwards gives them a quality of
life they should be able to do. It also gives them independence not requiring a friend to pick them up. Uber and Lyft are too expensive to
use for anything more than a special occasion.
Providing transportation to schools would also keep traffic off the roads. We do have many children riding bikes to school, but so many
are driven everywhere by parents. School buses would make a huge difference to traffic at commute times. Since school is out for
summer, I am able to see a big difference when I am out and about in the morning commute time. It shows me that traffic is school
related.
If this Link system is stopped, what will be the alternative? Will the City provide an alternative? Can we bring back the shuttles and
improve them for areas of town which were not covered? Can this be done charging a small fare? Don’t take away a service that many
depend on which will effectively prevent them from going out.
