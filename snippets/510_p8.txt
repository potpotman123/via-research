VIA’S CREDENTIALS 
Austin’s Capital Metropolitan Transportation Authority’s Pickup service, a Via-powered 
microtransit offering, showcases how forward-thinking transit agencies can reengineer 
paratransit and dial-a-ride for the benefit of their communities. 
Before working with Via, Capital Metro operated a conventional deviated-fixed route service 
with low ridership. With the hands-on support of Via’s microtransit experts, in June 2017 
Capital Metro launched a flexible, on-demand service that utilizes its dispatchers, vehicles, and 
operators in an entirely new way. Pickup riders can now request rides anywhere within the 
service area directly from a custom-built app as well as through a dispatcher, and get picked 
up within minutes in wheelchair accessible cutaway buses. And to support the unbanked, 
Pickup was designed to accept cash fares. 
Pickup has quickly achieved previously unattainable levels of service quality for customers and 
operational efficiency for the Authority.
