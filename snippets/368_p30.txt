4.2.5 Other Specifications
We anticipate being able to meet several of the optional requirements defined in the RFP.
We would welcome further discussion regarding the scope of these requirements to
ensure that we meet all of Bay Transit’s needs.
Bay Transit Requirement
Via
Software customer interfaces for on demand (microtransit) and the demand response modules shall
be compatible with Via’s customer facing App.
Software shall be compatible and able to integrate with security camera footage.
Software shall allow comingling between on-demand (microtransit) and the demand response modules
and databases.
Software shall track and report rider’s method of fare payment and have fare reconciliation capability.
Software shall have capability to track and report the cost of no-shows and late pick-ups for demand
response and on demand trips.
Both text and IVR communication methods are preferred.
4.3 Hardware Compatibility
The Via platform is built to be flexible, and highly compatible with a wide range of
hardware solutions, including Android tablets. Through our background deploying
transportation systems around the world, we have gained experience integrating with a
number of other software and hardware platforms, ranging from local payment systems to
CAD/AVL providers. We would be happy to discuss any potential future integrations with
Bay Transit upon request.

Via uses a third-party controlled-access system for data storage. Via data centers are
hosted offsite by Amazon Web Services (AWS), which are highly secure facilities that do
not permit visitations. New data is submitted into the system through the user-facing
applications — the Rider and Driver Apps. Once received, the data is encrypted and
stored inside the AWS infrastructure, and analyzed and processed by the appropriate Via
teams. Via’s data is archived by means of Amazon Web Services (AWS) S3 backups. All
data is eventually expired and removed according to the Data Retention Policy, which Via
maintains in accordance with ISO27001.
Reporting is accessed through two main interfaces in the VOC: Service KPI Dashboards
and Data Generator Reports. In addition to our standard reporting features, we also offer
data access using our Direct Data Access (DDA) solution, which provides a machine-to-
machine connection to data tables, and expanded data visualization capabilities through
an integration with Google’s Looker Studio platform.
●
Service KPI Dashboards. These dashboards function as the central view for
service analysis and management. Through this interface, our partners can
organize and visualize the thousands of data points our system collects every
minute based on their unique needs and concerns, and leverage our filtering and
grouping tools to generate reports that range from neat and simple to highly
complex. These reports ensure that partners understand core service indicators,
such as total ridership and on-time performance, and can make data-driven
changes to service parameters such as maximum wait time, fleet size, and/or
vehicle supply throughout the day. Further, these tools allow partners to easily
measure the efficacy of marketing campaigns and rider engagement strategies to
understand what is drawing and keeping riders on the service.
