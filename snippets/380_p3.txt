DUTIES OF NOMAD TRANSIT 
 
A. The general objective of this Contract and Data Sharing Agreement TSD-24-146 (Contract-
DSA) is to provide safe and reliable on-demand alternative vehicle transportation services 
for District students, as outlined in the Scope of Work (Attachment B) under the General 
Terms and Conditions (Attachment A). 
 
B. In order to accomplish the general objective of this Contract-DSA, Nomad Transit shall 
perform the specific duties included in the Scope of Work (Attachment B), and as specified 
in its response to the District’s Request for BID # B24021C which is incorporated herein by 
reference, to the satisfaction of the District’s designee, Director of Transportation Services, 
under the General Terms and Conditions (Attachment A).

SCHEDULE OF PERFORMANCE 
 
Nomad Transit shall not commence performance or be entitled to compensation or reimbursement 
for any services rendered, prior to the occurrence of each of the following conditions: (1) this 
Contract-DSA must be executed by an authorized representative of Nomad Transit and the District; 
and, if required, (2) this Contract-DSA must be approved by the Board of Directors.  
 
This Contract-DSA becomes effective upon signature by all parties and remains in effect through 
August 31, 2027, unless terminated prior to this date per the conditions herein.
