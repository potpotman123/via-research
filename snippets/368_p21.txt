4.2.2 Dispatcher Interface
The Via Operations Center, or the VOC, is the central application designed for
administrators and dispatchers to manage every aspect of the service in real time. The
console features a set of sophisticated automations designed to minimize the amount of
manual intervention necessary by dispatchers, while still preserving the flexibility they
need to do their job. The VOC also provides streamlined reporting to help drive
continuous service optimization and cost efficiency. The VOC is cloud-hosted, accessible
on any browser and device from anywhere in the world, and allows multiple users to
access it at once. For more details regarding administrative features in the VOC, see
section 4.2.4 Administrator Interface.

Our software uses up-to-date mapping technology and leverages real-time speed and
location data to continuously optimize for on-time performance. Via’s mapping interfaces
are updated every quarter, and road closures are updated live. Bay Transit can also
update any closures manually in the VOC. If Bay Transit knows of any significant changes,
Via can push a map update. If the software recognizes that a vehicle is running late to a
pickup, and there is another vehicle available to reach the pickup quicker, it will
automatically reassign the trip and notify the riders and drivers. The VOC also allows for
specific parameter configurations, such as maximum wait times, to ensure high qualities
of service that are in line with Bay Transit’s vision.

Bay Transit Requirement
Via
Scheduling
The software shall be enabled for demand response and/or on-demand rides, including ride scheduling
and dispatch.
System shall provide configurable demand response service scheduling business rules, such as ride
distance or advanced ride date restrictions.
Software shall allow transit provider dispatchers to see all rides scheduled and dispatched.
Software shall allow dispatchers to reschedule, cancel and/or reassign rides, as necessary.
Software shall have the capabilities to flag riders that consistently no-show for rides, provide a
list/notification of these riders to Bay Transit, and limit these riders’ access and ability to use the system.
Software shall have the ability to identify operators’ and vehicles’ availability for scheduling (e.g.,
operators on break and vehicles not active due to vehicle service/maintenance), to reduce potential for
mis-scheduling a ride to an unavailable operator or vehicle.
Software shall allow dispatchers to aggregate rides provided riders have a similar pick-up location or
destination.
Software shall allow dispatchers to schedule rides for riders that have subscriptions (standing orders).
Software shall allow the ability to assign vehicles to multiple regions.
Software shall update estimated time of arrival as well as the rider manifest based on ride requests and
real-time travel information.
