Fixed-Route Transit Planning and Scheduling

able to quickly generate routes and understand costs, coverage, and demographics 
served by different route variations. We have a deep understanding that transit planning

partners strike an effective balance between the two.

Transit Operations and Management Expertise 
As a consultancy, a software company, and a transit operator, Via has highly specialized 
experience planning and delivering state-of-the-art technology with efficient day-to-day 
operations. We have developed playbooks to launch and manage fleets, drivers, customer 
support, and all day-to-day operations. 
Community and Stakeholder Engagement Expertise 
Via understands that effective, responsive public transportation must be designed to meet 
the needs of the entire public, including transit riders who are often not engaged during 
the planning and service design process. Our team both directly conducts public 
engagement processes and also regularly partners with specialized, local consultants to 
identify key stakeholders, build robust in-person and remote outreach and engagement 
strategies, and actively incorporate the input and ideas of all local riders into our work. 
Via has uniquely extensive experience planning multimodal networks in harder-to-serve 
areas, and we have completed over 80 transportation planning studies since 2019.
