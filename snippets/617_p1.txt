Dear Commissioners:
On December 27, 2023, the City of Fargo and Via Mobility LLC entered into an agreement through North
Dakota State Contract #283.
Via Mobility LLC provides paratransit software that allows The City of Fargo's Transit Department
(MATBUS) to provide paratransit services to the cities of Fargo, West Fargo, Moorhead and Dilworth.
The City of Fargo's Transit Department wishes to submit an additional Contractor Work Order Request
(WOR) through the state contract to continue to include post implementation software use through July
31, 2027 as well as transitioning Microtransit software service providers from the City's current
provider, Transloc, to Via. The intention is to begin microtransit services with Via and provide Transloc
with a notice of termination.
Recommended motion: Approve the attached State Contract Contractor Work Order Request with Via
Mobility LLC.
Sincerely,
