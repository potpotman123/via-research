RESOLUTION NO. _______ 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
CITY OF KEY WEST, FLORIDA, APPROVING THE 
ATTACHED “KEY WEST TRANSIT SOFTWARE AS A 
SERVICE DEPLOYMENT SERVICE ORDER” FOR ON-
DEMAND TRANSIT SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SaaS), 
IN AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $248,880.00 AS 
DESCRIBED IN THE ATTACHED PRICE QUOTE FROM VIA 
TRANSPORTATION, INC. PURSUANT TO SECTION 2-
797(4)(b) EXEMPT CONTRACTUAL SERVICES, BEST 
INTERESTS OF THE CITY; AUTHORIZING THE CITY 
MANAGER OR DESIGNEE TO EXECUTE ANY NECESSARY 
DOCUMENTS; PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE  
 
 
WHEREAS, City staff recommends the procurement of On-Demand 
Transit Software as a Service from Via Transportation, Inc. in the 
best interests of the City; and  
WHEREAS, Key West Code of Ordinances section 2-797(4) 
provides that if exceptional circumstances exist to exempt a 
purchase from competitive bid requirements for the best interests 
of the city, the City Manager shall place a resolution before the 
city commission for approval of such exemption; and  
WHEREAS, City staff anticipates the award of federal grant 
funding under the “Enhancing Mobility Innovation Program,” which 
would provide funding of up to $250,000 (City 20% match), which 
will cover up to 80% of the cost of this pilot project; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE 
CITY OF KEY WEST, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS: 
 
 
 
Section 1: 
That the attached “Key West Transit Software 
as a Service Deployment Service Order” between the City and Via 
Transportation, is hereby approved in an amount not to exceed 
$248,880.00, pursuant to Code of Ordinances Section 2-797(4), best 
interests of the City. 
 
 
Section 2: 
This purchase will be funded with anticipated 
Federal Grant Funding through the “Enhancing Mobility Innovation 
Program” in an amount of up to $250,000.00. Such funding will be 
matched up to 20% using the FY22 budget allocation for on-demand 
transit in the amount of $45,000.00, and supplemental funding as 
necessary from the Transit Alternative Fund. 
 
Section 3: 
That 
the 
City 
Manager 
or
