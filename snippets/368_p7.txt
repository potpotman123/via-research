Local Experience Powering Bay Transit Express
Via has a deep understanding of Bay Transit’s existing system, the
unique physical environment of the rural region, and the needs of
the local community through our history of successful partnership
with Bay Transit. In particular, our experience powering Bay Transit
Express since 2021 has given us a keen awareness of unique rider
preferences, demand patterns, and other metrics critical to
delivering a successful service in Bay Transit’s service area. In addition, we have
developed an embedded connection to the local community through partnerships with
organizations such as The Master Center and The American Addiction Treatment Center
in Gloucester. Leveraging our experience as the software provider for Bay Transit Express,
Via can offer multiple benefits across the system’s other services:
●
Familiar working relationships: Via and Bay Transit have a proven working
relationship from Bay Transit Express, including on-the-ground work sessions and
staff training in Gloucester. Bay Transit is able to contact Via project team
members whenever necessary — we have prioritized a personalized approach and
can continue to do so as our partnership expands. This ensures that Bay Transit is
able to maintain clear channels of communication regarding the platform, and
visibility into crucial components of their services across the region.
●
Award-winning technology: Via has already been selected as Bay Transit’s
on-demand technology provider based on our experience and capabilities,
contributing to Bay Transit being awarded the 2023 USAging’s Aging Innovations &
Achievement Award for Bay Transit Express. Selecting a proven solution already
leveraged by Bay Transit enables significant efficiencies and advantages at the
regional and local levels.
●
Local knowledge: Our local project teams understand how Bay Transit likes to
work, and what Bay Transit needs to successfully operate both local and regional
services. This critical historical knowledge and local familiarity that we have built
