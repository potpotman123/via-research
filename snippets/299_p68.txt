On-Demand (Microtransit and Paratransit) Service Planning 
Via’s proven service planning methodology guides our partners through a structured 
process, broad enough to ensure no use-case opportunity is missed while detailed 
enough to produce a clear path to deployment if desired. Our planning is driven by our 
proprietary simulation tool, a core component of our service planning offering, which 
allows Via to test permutations of potential services and clarify complex trade-offs 
between quality of service (QoS) and operational efficiency. Through simulations, we 
clarify complex trade-offs between the quality of service and eﬃciency. Some of the tool’s 
outputs include metrics of expected wait times, ﬂeet size to meet varying demand 
scenarios, ride aggregation predictions, and more. Via also has highly specialized 
experience planning and operating paratransit services. We understand many of the 
operational challenges of delivering paratransit services and the drivers of service costs, 
experience that informs our analysis and recommendations for paratransit feasibility and 
efficiency studies. We have particular expertise in developing recommendations for 
human services transportation and paratransit — programs that serve seniors, individuals 
with disabilities, and other vulnerable populations.

Fixed-Route Transit Planning and Scheduling 
By leveraging the world’s leading collaborative transit planning software, Remix, we are 
able to quickly generate routes and understand costs, coverage, and demographics 
served by different route variations. We have a deep understanding that transit planning 
requires tradeoffs between coverage-oriented and ridership-oriented services, and help 
partners strike an effective balance between the two.
