2023 Annual Report 
 
Technology and Rider Experience:  
Transit technology and the customer experience was the focus of the 2023 Sioux Area Metro, PTAB 
and City of Sioux Falls mission.  A technology plan was drafted to include the mission and vision of 
this focus.   
The IT Department, led by Kamie Clauson-Livermore, took inventory and assessment of the 
network, hardware and software utilized by SAM.  Improved system security, updating of hardware 
and implementation of technology to improve efficiencies was recommended and plans being put 
in place. 
CradlePoints to support WiFi and Real-Time GPS on the buses was approved. Software that is due 
for upgrading included Paratransit scheduling/dispatch systems. 
The COSF is updating the city’s website.  Review of SAM’s content and customer experience 
opportunities were considered in this update.  Further updates to this website have been tabled for 
the management transition, with all federally required information to remain on the city’s webpage, 
as well as any future website that may be developed.  Social medial platforms have been turned 
over to the SAM team for updating and content. 
Transit Development Plan: 
This document is updated on a five-year basis.  This update went through the PTAB review, 
community participation and stakeholder engagements throughout 2022.  The plan was approved 
by PTAB on 2/21/2023 and the City Council 2/22/2023. 
FTA 5310 Capital Grant Recommendations: 
The annual review of applications for the FTA 5310 funding was completed by PTAB, TCC, CAC, TAC 
and UDC.  The 2022 apportionment was $372,521, with $341,410 being awarded and a carryover of 
$31,111 will be applied to 2023 apportionment.  Grants were awarded to three agencies for 
operations, camera system, and two vehicles.  
Sam on Demand: 
SAM On-Demand Saturday Pilot continued to be monitored with the SAM Team and Pantonium’s 
support team.  The goal of implementing On-Demand service to six days per week was not possible 
due to the continued driver shortage. 
With the challenges put forth by the driver shortage, the overall concept and implementation of the 
On-Demand system was seeing growth and acceptance by the community and the SAM Team.

Service Reduction and Changes: 
Service reductions due to driver shortage has been SAM’s reality, along with the rest of the nation.  
SAM did experience a greater number of applicants in 2023, bringing the system closer to full driver 
capacity.   
The 41st Street and I-29 Interchange construction project will impact routes 10 and 11 for 
approximately two years.  Work was done to mitigate the impact for those traveling to Roosevelt 
High School. 
Management Agreement RFP: 
After considerable review and collaboration with several city departments, an RFP for the 
management services for Sioux Area Metro was released in July 2023.  J. Tschetter and J. 
Johannsen, PTAB members, served on the RFP selection process. 
First Transit (now TransDev) has provided management services for over 30 years.  The direction 
the RFP was focusing on was for a ‘turnkey’ operation, with a focus on technology and data-driven 
decision making.   
There were two proposals submitted under this RFP, TransDev and Via Transportation.  In-person 
interviews took place with the selection team and supporting city team members.  There was an 
opportunity for a joint venture, if either or both entities were interested, such as one company 
contracting with the Fixed Route system, the other with Paratransit and Microtransit services. 
The contract was awarded to Via Transportation as a turnkey operation in September 2023, for a 
management change on 1/1/2024.   Via Transportation choose to retain the entire system services 
under their management.  Contract negotiations were based on service hours per system (Fixed 
Route, Paratransit, and Microtransit), with KPI’s to be determined.

Site Selection Study: 
Through an RFP process, TSP of Sioux Falls was awarded the Site Selection project August 2022.  
This scope of work includes finding the best location for the future building site for the SAM facility.  
This study was completed, and draft results are being considered for further action.  Future sites 
decisions and action will be contingent on the city’s Downtown Plan, site availability that meets 
FTA and local requirements, as well as include it in the Capital Improvement Plan (CIP).    
Including this project in the CIP will assist with the future FTA funding request process. 
PTAB Bus Tour: 
June’s monthly meeting was held at Sioux Area Metro, with a brief business meeting held in their 
meeting room, followed by a bus tour of a route.  This was advertised and open to the public.  It 
gave great opportunity for board members to experience the transit system and was well received. 
Transit Budget: 
The 2023 transit budget process has been reviewed using the current management contract, with 
the future of this contract being subject to the RFP process.  Projected budget figures have been in 
alignment with the 2022 budget, with inflation factors considered.  No solid budget figures were 
presented as it was evident that the management selection completed September 2023 would 
dictate the 2024 budget.  
PASS It on Program:   
A delegation from Minnehaha County reported on this program collaboration with SAM.  There are 
40,000 rides allowed through this program provided to various nonprofit organizations for those 
they serve.  Most of these rides include employment, job searches, medical and family visits. 
This success of this program includes the ability to help launch and lift these passengers out of 
homelessness and into employment. Challenges include the inability to allow transfers, resulting 
in needing more passes. 
An estimated $472,000 in repaid debt from January through September of 2023 to the state of 
South Dakota has been seen due to access to employment through this transportation program, 
which includes fines, restitution, child support and other debt.
