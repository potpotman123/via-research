G. Nomad Transit will not allow any Nomad Transit employee (direct or contracted) whose conduct 
may in any way expose a student to any impropriety of word or conduct whatsoever for any 
reason. 
H. Nomad Transit will maintain the responsibility to determine which persons are to be hired and 
discharged within their respective organization. 
1. The District may, by written order to Nomad Transit, cause Nomad Transit to remove
from any or all jobs under this Contract any person who is deemed inappropriate to
perform services under this Contract.
I. Nomad Transit will demonstrate best efforts to hire drivers and other personnel with previous 
school transportation service experience with a demonstrated good aptitude for training. 
J. Nomad Transit will conduct drug testing of all new hires and Nomad Transit will conduct drug 
and alcohol testing following its hiring of employees as appropriate. 
1. Tests will be done at random, and/or when reasonable suspicion arises.
2. Following an incident under the following circumstances:
a.
Human fatality.
b. The employee being issued a citation, and one or more of the following:
i. Disabling damage to any motor vehicle requiring towing from the scene;
or
ii. Injury/immediate medical treatment necessary away from scene.
3. Nomad Transit’ drug testing program shall demonstrate compliance with appropriate
testing requirements, and in line with a robust company policy.
4. Should a Nomad Transit employee that is assigned to work under this Contract fail to
meet any of the above requirements, Nomad Transit will immediately notify the District’s
Director of Transportation Services in writing.
K. Nomad Transit will work with the District to develop a mutually agreed upon plan to ensure all 
drivers are continually and timely trained in the areas of student management and customer 
service as appropriate. 
1. Nomad Transit will report significant student management incidents and/or challenges to
the District immediately.
2. Drivers may not physically remove a student from a vehicle or force them in/out.

3. Should an unmanageable student behavior occur during a route, the driver will safely pull
over in an effort to maintain control of the situation.
a.
Every effort shall be made to safely transport the student(s) to their stop.
b. If necessary, the driver will call dispatch for assistance from a field supervisor,
and/or seek authorization to proceed to the nearest District school for assistance
by a building administrator.
L. Nomad Transit will provide the school and the District’s Director of Transportation Services or 
designee, as soon as possible, but no later than four (4) working hours, of student incident reports 
of lost students, behavioral, disciplinary, or health problems which arise during bus trips that may 
jeopardize the safety of others. 
1. Nomad Transit will immediately report to the District any student not accounted for that
was originally in a vehicle for transport.
2. Nomad Transit will make every effort in cooperation with the District to locate the student.
M. Nomad Transit will provide the school and the District’s Director of Transportation Services or 
designee immediately by telephone of any vehicle accident or injury, in accordance with District 
policies and procedures.   
1. Nomad Transit will forward within twenty-four (24) hours of each accident where an injury
is sustained a written report describing all details of such accident.
2. All other accident reports shall be submitted within five (5) days of each accident and filed
according to State law and regulations.
N. Nomad Transit drivers will provide consistent photo identification which will include the 
respective driver’s name and photo. Nomad Transit will provide this photo identification at their 
expense, an approved form of digital identification will be accepted. 
O. The District will maintain the right to stop all or a portion of a given service at any time should 
there be cause for a potential safety or other concern at no cost to the District. 
ROUTING – RELIABILITY 
A. Nomad Transit will be available to provide vehicle transportation services to District students 
during the hours of 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily, not including weekends or holidays, at the 
District’s request.  
B. Nomad Transit will be available to provide vehicle transportation services to District students

C. Nomad Transit will be capable of providing up to and as many as thirty (30) different vehicles at 
any time as needed for the vehicle transportation of District students. 
1. The District routing is based on enrollment and student need in real-time, with a
typical heavier volume in spring.
D. For those occasions with less than thirty (30) trips booked and confirmed for a given day, for any 
trips booked and confirmed more than twenty-four (24) hours in advance (one business day) that 
do not get covered by Nomad Transit as requested, Nomad Transit will be charged the cost the 
District incurs to make up for such. The District will provide documentation outlining how any 
such changes are calculated. 
E. Nomad Transit will be responsible for arriving on-time and no later than ten (10) minutes from 
the scheduled time. 
F. Nomad Transit will provide vehicles that are appropriately permitted, properly maintained and in 
good working order, clean, and have all required safety equipment and associated features. 
1. A file shall be kept available for each vehicle documenting such and will be made
immediately available to the District upon request.
2. Parents/Guardians of students that are required to use a car-seat under Washington
State Law will have child safety seat(s) available and as appropriate.
BILLING 
A. Invoices will be submitted to the District each month to reflect the prior month’s services. 
Payment will be made following receipt of Nomad Transit’s adequate and original invoices. For 
an invoice to be deemed adequate, the Purchase Order number must appear on the invoice as 
well as itemizations. Itemizations must include: 
1. Date of service.
2. Name of student.
3. Address of pick-up.
4. Address of drop-off.
5. Name of Nomad Transit staff.
6. Vehicle ID (e.g., license plate number).
7. Miles per trip (roundtrip if applicable).
8. Rate, and total.
B. Nomad Transit will ensure that invoices reflect the rates as provided within Attachment C, Rate 
Schedule.
