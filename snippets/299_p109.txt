8. Products, services, and solutions
i. Which category or categories do the products, services, and solutions being
offered fall under?
Via’s products and services fall under the Transportation category.
ii. Describe in detail the products, services, and solutions being offered.
We have reviewed the specifications included in RFP 25-08 and are confident that Via can
provide Participating Public Agencies with a variety of on-demand transportation
solutions that meet and exceed expectations. Our proposed solutions offer a
comprehensive suite of cutting-edge services tailored to meet a wide range of transit
needs, from microtransit and paratransit to non-emergency medical transport (NEMT). We
look forward to working individually with interested members to define and scope projects
and solutions that meet their unique needs.
Below, we have included an overview of the services, software, and transportation 
solutions that Via is able to provide. We have included full descriptions in the “Products 
and Pricing” attachment in Ion Wave. Our catalog includes: 
●
Turnkey Microtransit, Paratransit and Fixed-Route Transportation Services: We
offer the technology and the operational expertise to run transportation services
on behalf of our partners. This end-to-end solution handles everything from
software deployment to fleet management and driver operations, ensuring
seamless implementation for transit agencies.
●
Technology-Enabled Student Transportation Services: We operate alternate
student transportation services across the U.S., transporting thousands of
students every day across our specialized student transit services in the US. These
students consist of a wide range of demographics, including children and youth in
Foster care, McKinney-Vento students, students with IEP requirements, and
students with Special Needs.
●
Comprehensive Transit Network Management: Via offers comprehensive
managed services designed for transit agencies to optimize their transportation
operations. Our solutions encompass fleet management, featuring real-time
tracking, maintenance scheduling, driver monitoring, and route optimization to
ensure efficient service delivery. By leveraging our expertise, agencies can

9. Safety and Reliability
i. Describe any processes and procedures to ensure the safety of your
customer and reliability of your service including, but not limited to,
background checks, driver screening, rider/driver validation, drug & alcohol
testing, GPS tracking, vehicle standards & inspections, or any other verifiable
step that is taken to ensure passenger safety.
With Via’s drivers covering over 5.8 million miles each month, the safety of every trip is
paramount. We have established a dedicated Trust and Safety Team focused on
improving driving safety outcomes in order to maintain our reputation for delivering safe
rides. Our investment in tech-enabled operational advancements allows us to proactively
manage safety. This includes advanced recordkeeping that seamlessly complies with FTA
procedures in the United States and enables aggregate analysis of driver performance.
Our solutions include numerous processes and procedures to ensure safety and 
reliability, as described below. 
●
Driver Vetting: Via mandates background checks, including fingerprint screening,
drug and alcohol testing compliance, and driver and vehicle licensing according to
state and local laws.
●
Driver Onboarding: All drivers undergo Mandatory Safety Training, a series of
workshops on topics such as defensive driving and safely navigating roads;
vehicle and equipment operation; and following safety and emergency instructions,
including how to handle and report accidents and major incidents.
●
