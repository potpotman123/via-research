The evaluation committee reached a preliminary consensus on the points awarded for each proposal and
requested demonstrations from the top three proposals based on the preliminary consensus scores.
Demonstrations were presented to the evaluation committee on August 25, 2023, by each proposer individually
at 12:30 PM (Ecolane USA, Inc.), 2:00 PM (Via Transportation Inc.), and 3:30 PM (Sparelabs) respectively. Each
session included a question and answer session. Following the presentations, the evaluation committee
reconvened at 5:00 PM on August 25, 2023 and reached consensus on final points for each proposal.
The final, completed, and signed Evaluation Criteria consensus scoring sheet is attached to this memo for
reference. With this, the department recommends that the firm that received the highest number of points, Via
Transportation Inc., be recommended for award of the contract pursuant to the terms listed in the RFP and
approval by the Hillsborough County Board of County Commissioners.
