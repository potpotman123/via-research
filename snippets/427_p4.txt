Melinda reported that Pace launched another VanGo location at the Palatine Metra Station. 
VanGo is an approach to first mile/last mile transportation and has two other locations in 
Deerfield and Lake Forest. Pace is getting a lot of interest in this program. 
 
Melinda reported that recruitment efforts are going well. Pace hosted a Hire on the Spot event at 
North Division on February 27 and 28 that resulted in 25 job offers. Pace will host Hire on the 
Spot events for mechanics at UTI Technical Institute on March 2, and for all positions at 
Waubonsee Community College on March 9, Olive-Harvey College on March 14, Elgin 
Community College on March 16, and Pace Headquarters on March 25. Pace community 
relations staff is attending multiple municipal hiring events to spread the word about job 
openings at Pace. In total, Pace has 90 future employees in various stages of the 8-week training 
required before an employee can operate a bus. Pace particularly needs to fill vacancies at the 
Northwest garage to get Pulse Dempster up and running.  
 
Melinda reported that the $2 fare for the TAP and DuPage Uber Access programs will be 
reinstated on April 15. This implementation date was pushed back because over 10,000 TAP 
accounts were added during the pandemic when fares were temporarily waived, and these new 
users have never loaded money onto their cards. In addition, Pace changed the online platform 
that allows riders to put money on their TAP card. Pace needs time to distribute information and 
make sure customers understand the process. 
 
Melinda reported that Pace along with RTA, CTA, Metra, CMAP, Metropolitan Planning 
Council, Transit Alliance, and other groups were invited to offer testimony to the joint Trans-
portation and Public Safety/Infrastructure Appropriations Subject Matter Committee on the 
successes and challenges of public transportation in the RTA service area. Melinda believes Pace 
stood out due to the variety of our service offerings: express bus, On Demand, VanGo, and Dial-
A-Ride. The committee was very interested in how Pace incorporates TNCs. CMAP discussed 
the PART process. In response to Director Noak’s report earlier, Melinda said that despite the 
Pace Board of Directors and PART Steering Committee meeting at the same time, CMAP 
Executive Director Erin Aleman holds monthly briefings for service board executive directors.  
 
Melinda reported that six public hearings to solicit input on part-time VanPool fares and the 
expansion of Naperville-Aurora On Demand service will be held at the end of the month. She 
thanked the board members who volunteered as hearing officers. 
 
Melinda reported that February ridership is up 11% over 2022 ridership at the same time last 
year, with the system carrying about 1.4 million riders. About 10,000 TNC trips were taken in 
February, showing TNC program growth. Suburban service budget is favorable by $6 million 
while the ADA paratransit budget is favorable by $2.6 million.  
 
After Melinda’s report, Director Schielke encouraged all board members to reach out to 
members of the Illinois General Assembly ahead of CMAP’s PART report about reforming the 
transit system. He speculates that they want to reorganize the RTA and service boards under one 
agency with one board. He is proud of our work, believes the system works well as it is now, and

Action Items 
 
SBD #23-15 – Ordinance authorizing the award of a contract to River North Transit LLC, a 
wholly owned subsidiary of Via Transportation, Inc. for a Transportation Network Company 
(TNC) pilot project for late-night first mile/last mile rideshare services from Harvey to O’Hare in 
an amount not to exceed $1,362,055.00. 
 
Erik Llewellyn presented on the pilot, describing the service gaps in Routes 332 and 352 that the 
pilot addresses, connections to Pace and CTA, zone boundaries, and hours. Discussion ensued:  
• Chairman Kwasneski raised concerns about branding vans with the Pace logo if drivers 
are allowed to operate the vehicles for personal use. Director Smith agreed. 
• Director Marcucci asked staff to start thinking about how Pace could provide west 
suburban residents opportunity and access to jobs now that Western Access O’Hare 
project, which was created so the benefits of economic activity at O’Hare could spill out 
west, will finish next year. Erik said Pace would revisit a study conducted with several 
Pace partners to identify potential public transit opportunities in the 390 corridor.  
• Director Smith reminded everyone that Pace is routinely innovative. She applauded the 
pilot for filling important service gaps and helping the financial well-being of residents.  
• Director Wells said this is something that the Harvey area desperately needs. He asked 
for clarification on the implementation schedule. Melinda and Erik provided a range 
between 12-24 weeks but will push for summer at the request of Director Wells.  
• Director Arfa raised that maybe people who were not thinking about job opportunities in 
those areas may be interested in exploring opportunities now that Pace is providing

Issues/Discussion/Reports 
 
Chairman Kwasneski encouraged Directors to review the January 2023 budget reports and 
financial statements contained in their packets. He asked Melinda how ridership compares to 
2019 pre-pandemic numbers. Melinda reported that fixed route ridership is now at 60% of pre-
pandemic ridership. ADA has now recovered 100% of the ridership lost during the pandemic, 
showing that the service is necessary, but is operating with only 70% of the drivers. Melinda 
explained that taxi and TNC partnerships are necessary right now to meet service demand. 
Director Schielke commented about the 8 new senior living communities being built in or near 
Batavia, with approximately 800 units/building, illustrating how demand for services will only 
increase. He described Kane County’s award-winning partnership with Pace transporting seniors 
to the vaccination hub in the former Sam’s Club building during the pandemic. He concluded 
saying that the CMAP Steering Committee needs to be aware of facts like this.
