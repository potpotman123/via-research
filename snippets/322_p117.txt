3. Technical Approach

support to help enhance mobility and access for Hillsborough County’s Sunshine Line. For this
project, Via proposes our Software-as-a-Service ՄSaaSՅ solution: a comprehensive technology
system to power intuitive and user-friendly mobility services for riders. Our technology, in tandem
with our deeply involved partner support services and in-depth experience replacing
Routematch-powered services, will help seamlessly expand mobility across the county while
maintaining manageable operational and administrative costs.

to deliver a highly efficient, best-in-class mobility service to the County. In response to the

User-friendly reservations for advance, real-time, and subscription / recurring
trips paired with manual, computer-assisted, and fully automated trip scheduling.
Comprehensive client record and eligibility applications management features to
accommodate the County’s current application process logic and flow.
An extensive selection of service reports, as well as reporting configurability and
the complete data transparency of Direct Data Access ՄDDAՅ.
A custom-designed bus pass distribution solution that enables staff to continue to
