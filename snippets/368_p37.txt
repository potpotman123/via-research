4.6 Technical Support
From our experience as operators of transit services, we understand that consistent
service delivery is incredibly important for Bay Transit riders, drivers, and staff. Our
software has been designed to function with minimal intervention so that our partners can
focus more on innovating their transit systems and less on solving technical problems. To
deliver on this promise, our global staff includes over 500 software engineers who are
committed to building reliable, user-friendly software. The result is a highly available
and consistent platform for our partners, delivering 99.9% uptime. In the event that Bay
Transit riders, drivers, and staff need support, we have developed robust materials and
processes to ensure prompt incident response and resolution:
4.6.1 Written Materials and Guides
As a part of the implementation and training
process, Via works with our partners to
build a suite of tailored materials regarding
the use of the platform and various features
(for more details, see section 4.5.3 Training
Plan). Bay Transit will continue to have easy
access to these materials afterwards, so
that staff may quickly reference them at any
time when they have questions.
4.6.2 24/7 Technical Support
Bay Transit will have access to 24/7 technical assistance through our support platform. A
rotating team of support engineers is on call at all times to resolve any problems that may
arise. Incidents are escalated using a predefined alert protocol, which instantly notifies
the on-call member of the team. The technical team member will immediately examine
the issue to begin the process of issue resolution and resource allocation. All issues will
be resolved based on their level of severity and associated response times, and Via will
provide Bay Transit with regular status updates on resolution.
4.6.3 Partner Success Support
