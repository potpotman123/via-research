I. Any Other Relevant Factor
1. Provide any other relevant information.
Over the past twelve years, we have developed the world’s most advanced transportation 
technology system, offering a unique and fully integrated end-to-end public transit 
solution for Region 4 and OMNIA participating entities. Our comprehensive software and 
services package includes advanced route planning algorithms, a fleet management 
module, and user-friendly applications for dispatchers, drivers, and riders, as well as all 
necessary operating services (such as drivers, vehicles, maintenance, and system 
management). 
What sets us apart in the industry is our ability to seamlessly integrate a wide range of 
services into a cohesive ecosystem. While each of our solutions—microtransit, 
paratransit, turnkey operations, fixed route, NEMT, street design, transit planning, 
consulting, and Citymapper (mobility-as-a-service)—can be implemented individually, our 
unique advantage is the ability to combine all of these components into a single, fully 
integrated transit network unlike anything else on offer in the market. This is a 
transformative innovation for transit agencies, offering several key benefits: 
●
Unified Platform for Diverse Transit Modes: Microtransit, fixed route, paratransit,
and NEMT services all operate seamlessly under a single platform, giving agencies
real-time visibility and centralized control over their entire network. This unified
system reduces operational silos and streamlines management, enabling agencies
to balance resources efficiently across various service types.
●
Unified Technology and Operations: Via offers an all-in-one solution that
combines advanced software with full operational support, eliminating the need for
multiple providers. By managing both technology and day-to-day operations, we
create a streamlined, highly efficient experience. This integrated approach
provides consistent performance, rapid implementation, and simplified
management across all transit modes.
●
Real-Time Data Sharing Across Services: Our solution allows for real-time data
sharing between all service types. For example, insights from fixed-route
schedules can inform microtransit and paratransit operations, enabling better
coordination between these services. This enhances efficiency by minimizing
service gaps, reducing wait times, and offering passengers a smoother transfer
between different modes of transport.

●
Dynamic Route Optimization: With advanced AI and machine learning algorithms,
our platform constantly optimizes routes across microtransit, NEMT, and
paratransit services based on live demand. This ensures that resources are
deployed efficiently, and ride times are minimized, helping agencies to cut costs
and improve service delivery.
●
Integrated Planning and Scheduling: Our transit planning software works in
tandem with fixed route scheduling, on-demand planning, and street design tools,
allowing agencies to design, adjust, and implement route networks that align with
real-world demand and urban development. The integration of planning software
with operational tools ensures that transit systems are not only well-planned but
are also responsive to changes in rider demand, traffic conditions, or other
external factors.
●
Intermodal MaaS Capabilities: With our Citymapper MaaS platform, riders can
easily navigate between different modes of transport—whether it is public transit,
microtransit, bike-sharing, or ride-hailing—all from a single app. This integrated
mobility approach ensures that riders have maximum flexibility and convenience,
while agencies benefit from increased ridership and better data on multimodal
travel patterns.
●
Efficient Resource Allocation: The platform enables transit agencies to
dynamically allocate vehicles and staff between different service types. For
instance, during off-peak times, vehicles typically used for fixed routes could be
redeployed to serve on-demand microtransit or NEMT trips, maximizing fleet
utilization and ensuring that no vehicles sit idle.
●
Single Point of Accountability: Since all services and software tools are integrated
under one platform, there’s a single point of accountability for operations,
reporting, and troubleshooting. This eliminates the challenges that often arise from
managing multiple vendors or systems, reducing operational complexity and
ensuring smooth communication across all services.
Via’s software solutions’ level of interoperability transforms how transit agencies manage 
their operations, offering unparalleled coordination, improved operational efficiency, 
reduced costs, and enhanced user experiences for riders, drivers, and agency staff. Our 
exceptional track record in recruiting and retaining qualified drivers, combined with 
flexible vehicle sourcing options, enables rapid deployment while ensuring high quality 
and safety standards. Ultimately, our unique combination of technologies and services
