Hillsborough County intends to update the award amount of a formerly published Notice of
Intent to Award (NOITA) of a previously awarded five (5) year contract with Ecolane USA,
Inc., for a new paratransit scheduling software system and associated maintenance (RFP-
23-24177) for the Sunshine Line Department at a total cost of $1,026,877.16. On July 25, 2024,
a NOITA was published; however, the NOITA incorrectly stated a contract amount of
$1,027,877.16. During a recent contract review and update to Blanket Purchase Agreement
No. 224300867 an inconsistency of $1,000.00 was discovered to the award amount. In an
effort for full transparency to the public, this NOITA accurately reflects the quoted price and
contract amount. In addition, the identified discrepancy did not change the order of the
