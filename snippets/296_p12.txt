Describe Offeror's customer service/problem resolution process. Include hours of operation, number
of services, etc.
Delivering an exceptional customer experience is core to Via’s success. We uphold the highest standards of
service and integrate customer feedback across our operations. Each customer is assigned a dedicated Partner
Success Manager (PSM), who acts as the main point of contact, supporting daily operations, driving service
improvements, and coordinating with Via’s specialist teams as needed. PSMs are regionally assigned to ensure
familiarity with local needs, and reachable by email and phone during business hours. Customers also receive
regular updates, feature announcements, and invitations to regional and national events. Additionally, Via
provides 24/7 technical support through an online platform, with on-call support engineers addressing and
escalating issues per a set alert protocol. Incidents are resolved based on severity, and customers receive
ongoing updates throughout the resolution process.

Describe Offeror's invoicing process. Include payment terms and acceptable methods of payments.
Offerors shall describe any associated fees pertaining to credit cards/p-cards.
Via’s Finance team will align on invoicing cadence and format with each participating agency. Typically, Via bills our
customers with Net 30 payment terms. The minimum fees, which are agreed upon on a case-by-case basis, are
paid annually upfront. Any usage beyond the minimum is billed monthly in arrears. As part of our invoicing
package, we provide details about usage during the prior month (e.g., active vehicles or vehicle hours). Payment
via Wire or ACH is preferred, but payment by check is also permitted. At this time, payment by credit card or
procurement card (P-card) is not accepted.
