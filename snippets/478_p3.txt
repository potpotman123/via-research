WHEREAS, the Evaluation Committee recommends that a contract be awarded 
to River North Transit, LLC, 10 Crosby St Fl 2 New York, New York, 10013, for Design 
and Implementation of a Transportation On-Demand Micro-Transit or Similar 
Transportation Service Pilot Program for Middlesex County, in the City of New 
Brunswick,  New Jersey, as required by the Middlesex County Department of 
Transportation for the period of October 1, 2023 through December 31, 2024, in the 
total amount of $1,137,951.00 (CC-23-934); and 
 
 
WHEREAS, the Evaluation Committee advises that the contract shall commence 
on or about October 1, 2023 for a period of 15 months. This will include a three (3) 
month design and rollout period. The County shall have the option to extend the term of 
the contract for two (2) additional twelve (12) months terms, pending the availability of 
funding and the approval of the Middlesex County Board of County Commissioners.  
Any services ordered or made within thirty (30) days after the Contract expires shall be 
under the same terms and conditions specified in the bid and contract prices submitted 
by the vendor, unless said vendor indicates, in writing, thirty (30) days prior to the 
contract expiration, that it shall not be bound by said terms, conditions and contract 
prices; 
 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners 
of the County of Middlesex that pursuant to N.J.S.A. 40A:11-4, the Purchasing Agent 
shall be and is hereby authorized to award a contract to River North Transit, LLC for 
Design and Implementation of a Transportation On-Demand Micro-Transit or Similar 
Transportation Service Pilot Program for Middlesex County, in the City of New 
Brunswick,  New Jersey, as required by the Middlesex County Department of 
Transportation for the period of October 1, 2023 through December 31, 2024, in the 
total amount of $1,137,951.00 (CC-23-934); and 
 
 
 
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the contract shall commence from on or about 
October 1, 2023 for a period of 15 months. This will include a three (3) month design 
and rollout period. The County shall have the option to extend the term of the contract 
for two (2) additional twelve (12) months terms, pending the availability of funding and 
the approval of the Middlesex County Board of County Commissioners.  Any services 
ordered or made within thirty (30) days after the Contract expires shall be under the
