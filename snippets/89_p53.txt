Revenue Share 
Cupertino shall pay to Via $1.50 for each completed ride (the "Revenue Share"), to be invoiced 
monthly by Via, starting upon launch of the Deployment. The City of Cupertino shall receive a 
monthly invoice credit in an amount equal to the net farebox revenue collected, less the 
Revenue Share retained by Via in that same previous calendar month.
