Our software uses up-to-date mapping technology and leverages real-time speed and
location data to continuously optimize for on-time performance. Via’s mapping interfaces
are updated every quarter, and road closures are updated live. Bay Transit can also
update any closures manually in the VOC. If Bay Transit knows of any significant changes,
Via can push a map update. If the software recognizes that a vehicle is running late to a
pickup, and there is another vehicle available to reach the pickup quicker, it will
automatically reassign the trip and notify the riders and drivers. The VOC also allows for
specific parameter configurations, such as maximum wait times, to ensure high qualities
of service that are in line with Bay Transit’s vision.
Ride Booking
The Via Operations Center has a
streamlined dispatcher interface
when manual ride booking is
required. Designed to support riders
who do not have smartphones or
access to a web browser, or who
simply prefer to book over the phone,
the Booking Module enables

driver. Once all trip information is entered, dispatchers receive a set of trip proposals,
which they can discuss with the rider before confirming the booking, and if required the
dispatcher can automatically book a return trip.

Bay Transit Requirement
Via
Scheduling
The software shall be enabled for demand response and/or on-demand rides, including ride scheduling
and dispatch.
System shall provide configurable demand response service scheduling business rules, such as ride
distance or advanced ride date restrictions.
Software shall allow transit provider dispatchers to see all rides scheduled and dispatched.
Software shall allow dispatchers to reschedule, cancel and/or reassign rides, as necessary.
Software shall have the capabilities to flag riders that consistently no-show for rides, provide a
list/notification of these riders to Bay Transit, and limit these riders’ access and ability to use the system.
Software shall have the ability to identify operators’ and vehicles’ availability for scheduling (e.g.,
operators on break and vehicles not active due to vehicle service/maintenance), to reduce potential for
mis-scheduling a ride to an unavailable operator or vehicle.
Software shall allow dispatchers to aggregate rides provided riders have a similar pick-up location or
destination.
Software shall allow dispatchers to schedule rides for riders that have subscriptions (standing orders).
Software shall allow the ability to assign vehicles to multiple regions.
Software shall update estimated time of arrival as well as the rider manifest based on ride requests and
real-time travel information.

Real-Time Information: Demand Response and/or On-Demand Service
The software shall provide real-time demand response and on-demand ride scheduling dispatched
information.
Software shall offer live vehicle tracking, route optimization, and passenger information.
Software shall offer two-way messaging capabilities to communicate with drivers regarding route
changes or passenger requests.
Software shall allow the dispatcher to identify any incidents along the route in real-time to create
accurate recorded data for reporting.
Software shall provide a real time up to date interactive map including every vehicle in the system and
vehicles’ current location.
Vendors should identify any optional software enhancements or capabilities for a real-time dashboard
where dispatchers can easily identify rides in real-time.
Daily Management: Demand Response and/or On-Demand Service Capabilities
The software shall allow dispatchers to make required adjustments while vehicles are in service and will
provide communication tools for interacting with drivers and passengers.
Remove vehicles from the daily fleet roster on demand.
Adjust different service times for different service areas.
Field supervision interface to monitor and make changes to service plans for vehicles currently in
operation.
Spatial Capabilities (GIS features) including heat maps of pick-ups and drop offs to preposition vehicles.
Play-back capability for trips and any vehicle up to 30 days.
Software must allow dispatchers the ability to easily print rider manifest in case the electronic manifest is
disabled.
Software must allow dispatchers to record incidents and produce incident reports.
