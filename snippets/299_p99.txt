Via Solution
In 2019, Trinity Metro partnered with Via to launch 
ZIPZONE, one of the nationʼs fastest-growing and most 
cost-effective microtransit services. A key part of the 
agencyʼs strategy to further reduce operating costs, 
increase efficiency, and improve quality of service 
throughout the city was to offer a fully integrated “smartˮ 
network, using Viaʼs software to match each trip request 
to the most efficient vehicle, driver, and mode of 
transportation to serve that trip.
In February 2024, Via was awarded the contract for a 
commingled ADA-paratransit and microtransit solution 
that will leverage a single unified software platform and 
Viaʼs best-in-class dispatch capabilities to drive 
significant cost savings by allowing eligible riders of both 
services to be served by a unified pool of vehicles and 
drivers. Despite a complex operational setup involving 
multiple vendors, extensive stakeholder change 
management, and a robust data migration from Trapeze, 
the service was launched on time in autumn 2024.
Via's integrated transit functionality will 
allow our riders to effortlessly travel 
across their communities, offering three 
advantages: availability, optionality, 
and simplicity.

Via Solution
In October 2020, Via partnered with Miami-Dade County 
to launch MetroConnect, an on-demand microtransit 
service that complements the existing transit 
infrastructure and encourages wider transit usage. 
MetroConnect provides shared, wheelchair accessible, 
first-and-last mile rides to and from Metrorail and bus 
stations within the service zones.
In 2023, the County leveraged Viaʼs Remix planning suite 
to conceptualize, solicit feedback on, and implement the 
Better Bus Network BBN - the first redesign of the 
Miami network of bus routes in 40 years. With more than 
130+ routes designed on Viaʼs Remix platform, the County 
continues to improve transit outcomes for residents, 
growing ridership while lowering cost per ride.
Via and Miami Dade County continue to innovate with the 
launch of autonomous vehicle shuttle service in 2024 and 
the roll out of Citymapper MaaS journey planning 
solutions in early 2025.
