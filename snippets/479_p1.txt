2. 
MINIMUM NEED: 
 
The Office of Contracting and Procurement (OCP), on behalf of the District Department 
of Transportation (DDOT) seeks a contractor to provide a cloud-based multimodal street 
design software for use by engineering and non-technical professionals, that combines 
bus route priority, cycling infrastructure, pedestrian initiatives, geospatial analysis, and 
stakeholder engagement, to design safer streets and to manage shared mobility to support 
the District’s Vision Zero initiative to end traffic fatalities by 2025. The software must 
create corridor-level street designs that integrate planning-level cross sections to aid the 
design of safer streets.
