3.2.3 Integrated Overflow Capabilities for Taxis and Other Providers
Our platform is also capable of utilizing additional vehicle supply to minimize trip rejections by
leveraging our trip brokering specialized functionalities. We understand that the County dispatches
a limited number of rides to a local taxi company. Via can provide overflow dispatch capabilities for
any local taxi companies that the County chooses to partner with to help streamline overflow
dispatching and we would be happy to work with the County to evaluate whether a TNC overflow
partnership would be helpful to the service.
Illustratively, across North America we manage integrations with several paratransit customers,
including in Alexandria, Virginia; Hampton Roads, Virginia; and St. John’s, Canada. In Hampton
Roads, we launched a trip brokering service to send overflow trips to independent contractor
drivers, which now provide more than 20% of total trips and have helped to enable a 15% drop in
per-ride costs.
Via also has a partnership with Uber through which we provide
software integration into an Uber API, which allows for overflow rides
to be dispatched to the platform’s drivers. We would be happy to
discuss this solution further if it is of interest to the County.

3.3 Dispatching and Operations Management
The Via Operations Center ՄVOCՅ is the central application designed to manage every aspect of an
operator's daily workflow. The console features a set of sophisticated automations designed to
minimize the amount of manual intervention necessary by dispatchers, while still preserving the
flexibility they need to do their job. The VOC provides customers with best-in-class solutions for:
3.3.1 Trip Scheduling and Real-time Performance Management
The Via Hub and Ride Plan provides Hillsborough County staff a map-based interface to monitor
and control their deployment in real-time. This interface displays real-time vehicle location, vehicle
load, schedule adherence, driver status, and vehicle status. Notifications alert dispatchers to
pending issues giving them the option to let the software automatically resolve the issue or to
manually intervene.
Our software leverages real-time speed and location data to continuously optimize for on-time
performance. If the software recognizes that a vehicle is running late to a pickup, and there is
another vehicle available to reach the pickup quicker, it will automatically reassign the trip and
notify the riders and drivers.
If the dispatcher wants to make a manual intervention, the tool provides a simple interface to
communicate with riders and vehicle operators, access more information about a rider or trip, and
adjust trip parameters as necessary. As an alternative to preset response options between the
dispatcher and vehicle operator, the VOC also offers flexible and custom communication features
that enables all necessary conversation while ensuring the safety of the vehicle’s operator while in
motion.

3.3.2. Profile & Eligibility Management
Our extensive suite of rider, driver, and fleet management features allows administrators to
manage the workflow of their operations including rider management (such as manual account
creation when necessary), driver management, shift scheduling, and vehicle tracking. Dispatchers
can also monitor shift status, visualize shifts across dates and times, and add or edit shifts.
Client Profile Management: The VOC clearly displays rider/client profiles (called “Rider Profiles” in
the Via System), each including the rider’s status (active, inactive, suspended), trip history, future
reserved trips, accessibility and eligibility details, mobility equipment specifications, payment and
contact information, and other relevant profile data. We can also configure custom fields based on
the County’s needs, including Social Security Number, client ID, bus swipe card number, and other
information not already included in the Rider Profile. Rider Profiles also allow administrators to see
which programs a rider is eligible for and track rider-specific accommodations or “driver notes.”
Authorized staff can edit information in the customer database or add relevant comments to Rider
Profiles at any time.
Eligibility Management: Via’s eligibility management module is designed to help partners increase
application certification speed and volume, reduce review costs, and provide a user-friendly and
transparent application experience for riders. Via’s platform provides staff with a workflow to
review applications, track their status, and move applicants to the next step in the review process.
Through these eligibility capabilities, riders can only request rides for which they are eligible. In
addition, we are deeply committed to having the most advanced eligibility product for our
customers and have plans to continue to invest in enhancing our product and adding new features
in the coming months. We also incorporate feedback from our customers through our partner
success program.
