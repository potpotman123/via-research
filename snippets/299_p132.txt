Agreement without further formal solicitation, there may be circumstances where
Public Agencies will issue their own solicitations. The following options are available
when responding to a solicitation for Products covered under the Master Agreement.
i. Respond with Master Agreement pricing (Contract Sales reported to OMNIA
Partners).
ii. If competitive conditions require pricing lower than the standard Master
Agreement not-to-exceed pricing, Supplier may respond with lower pricing
through the Master Agreement. If Supplier is awarded the contract, the sales are
reported as Contract Sales to OMNIA Partners under the Master Agreement.
iii. Respond with pricing higher than Master Agreement only in the unlikely event
that the Public Agency refuses to utilize Master Agreement (Contract Sales are
not reported to OMNIA Partners).

than Master Agreement, and include Master Agreement as the alternate or
additional proposal.

Marketing Plan 
Via has extensive experience marketing to the government sector broadly, including 
within the education space: business-to-government (B2G) accounts generate nearly all 
of our revenue. We rely on a multi-channel approach ranging from paid media/newsletter 
sponsorships to demand generation outbound email campaigns, to hosting webinars and 
other thought leadership, and extensive conference attendance. To engage Participating 
Agencies, we will work with the Region 4 and OMNIA teams to develop a customized 
campaign strategy and accompanying branded materials and sell sheets that we can use 
for distribution to the nationwide network, in addition to hosting specialized information 
sessions such as webinars and direct sales calls with our team. If OMNIA is able to 
provide us with contact information for all relevant participating entities, this is preferable; 
alternately, we are able to leverage our data enrichment partners to allow us to identify a 
targeted list for sales outreach.  
Distribution Plan 
Via has implemented complex transit services in over 650 communities globally, and will 
leverage this experience to ensure seamless distribution of services to public agencies 
nationwide. Via understands that exceptional project management is key to the delivery of 
a successful on-demand transportation service. For each solution we deploy, our 
dedicated launch team utilizes a proven launch process and takes an agile approach to 
service development and project governance. During this process, we rapidly iterate 
system design, including any desired integrations; communicate frequently with the 
customer; and quickly incorporate feedback to mitigate the risk of delays. Through 
productive communication and project management, our team completes each task within 
agreed-upon timelines while maintaining the flexibility to adapt as necessary.
