Moving the pick-up pin beyond a valid service zone grays out the map and displays an
“Out of Zone” message, so riders always know where service is available. If a rider wishes
to add or subtract passengers to their group, they simply tap the “+” or “-” icon within the
app. Via can also configure the service so that riders may book a ride by entering a place
name (such as a library, school, store, or service) as well as
by entering an address. The booking page can also suggest
Points of Interest (POIs) that are defined by Bay Transit and
easily searchable by all riders.
Upon a trip request, the Via algorithm matches the requested
ride with available seats across the fleet, assigning the rider
to a vehicle for a convenient personal trip that also optimizes
for efficiency across the entire service. Once a rider accepts
a ride proposal, the ride is automatically added to the ride
plan, integrating directly with both the Driver App, and the
VOC. This guarantees reliable service, such as a high rate of
on-time performance and customer satisfaction, for riders.
The rider app will send the rider automated trip updates. In
the event of service delays or disruptions, Bay Transit will
also be able to send announcements directly to riders
through the Rider App.

Real-Time Information: Demand Response and/or On-Demand Service
The software shall provide real time demand response and/or on-demand ride scheduling dispatched
information to customers. This would include scheduled date/time, pick-up location,
scheduled/anticipated pick-up time, actual pick-up time, origin and destination locations, route
distance, fare payment information (if applicable), drop-off location, drop-off date/time, and real-time
updates for each ride.
Software shall provide such information via both customer facing app and web-based portal for riders
without smartphones.
Software shall provide real-time navigation from rider location (based on browser IP/mobile app
geolocation) to pick-up location for rides.
Software shall have the ability to provide riders with alerts/notifications in real-time, and these shall be
accessible to non-English speakers (prioritizing Spanish speakers).
Software shall provide alerts/notifications in real-time on waiting time and no shows tracking for riders,
as these can result in penalties and fees.
Software shall consider peak periods and available traffic information when reporting real-time route
information, such as estimated time of arrival (ETA).
Fare Payment
The software shall allow demand response and/or on-demand riders to pay electronically in advance for
any scheduled trips and/or subscriptions.
Software shall allow a cash payment option for riders without credit card or other online payment
methods.
Software shall indicate to the rider any applicable fare(s) to be paid by their Care Agency and/or any
companions.
Title VI Requirements
Software’s customer facing interface for all service types shall be accessible to non-English speakers.
