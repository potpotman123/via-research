Platform Functionality in Rural Service Zones
Via’s platform is designed to contend with limited or lost cellular service, a situation that
may be more likely to occur in rural areas. In the event of lost connectivity, the Driver App
will continue to operate using cached data, and administrators and dispatchers will be
able to identify when vehicles have lost connectivity. Drivers will no longer receive new
bookings, but will continue to be presented with turn-by-turn navigation and directions to
their current pickup and drop-off tasks. When connectivity is regained, the Driver App will
immediately sync up with the rest of the system and be updated with any relevant ride or
route changes. In the meantime, our “Offline Mode” eliminates any negative impact of
limited connectivity on riders, drivers, and dispatchers.
