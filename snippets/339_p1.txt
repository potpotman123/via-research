NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF 
THE CITY OF KEY WEST, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS: 
 
Section 1: 
That the attached “Amendment #1” to the 
Service Order,” amending the term of the service order from 
one year to three years with two option years in an amount 
not to exceed $270,000 over a five year period is hereby 
approved. 
 
Section 2: 
That adequate funding for this project 
has been allocated through the FDOT Transit Service 
Development Grant Program for FY 24 and other transit revenue 
programs. 
 
Section 3: 
That the City Manager, upon the advice 
and consent of the City Attorney, is authorized to execute 
any necessary documents. 
 
Section 4:  That this Resolution shall go into effect 
immediately upon its passage and adoption and authentication 
by the signature of the Presiding Officer and the Clerk of 
the Commission.
