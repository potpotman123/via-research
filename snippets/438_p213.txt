The OWNER and SERVICE PROVIDER agree as set forth below: 
1.1 
ENGAGEMENT 
1.11  
The OWNER hereby engages Port City Transit, LLC  (the “SERVICE PROVIDER”) as an 
independent contractor to provide full and comprehensive “turnkey” management, operations, 
maintenance, logistics, marketing and promotion, innovation planning and implementation, and 
the provision and integration of end-to-end technology platforms  for the Mobile Transit System, 
together with an integrated ride-share program, encompassing fixed-route, paratransit, and  
microtransit services within the corporate limits of, and all annexed areas to, the City of Mobile, 
in accordance with this Agreement and Exhibit A. The Request for Proposals (RFP) dated June 
20, 2025, and (with addenda) is incorporated by reference for descriptive/background purposes 
only; in the event of conflict, this Agreement controls. 
1.2 
DESIRED RESULT 
1.2.1 
Establish a long-term engagement with the SERVICE PROVIDER that: (a) optimizes the  
management, operation, and costs of the Mobile Transit System, including but not limited to 
capital overhead and operational based expenses; (b) plans and implements service and 
technology innovations that increase ridership, improve service quality, and reduces operational 
costs; (c) delivers services at competitive, fair, and reasonable pricing within the City’s, pre-
approved annual budget; and (d) provides ongoing technical support to City staff and elected 
officials to ensure a compliant, accessible, and inclusive transit.

1.3 
SCOPE OF SERVICES 
1.3.1 
Scope and Compensation. The SERVICE PROVIDER’s services are those set forth in this Agreement, 
Exhibit A and Exhibit B, and further described in the SERVICE PROVIDER’s Proposal dated July 
25, 2025, which is incorporated for descriptive purposes only. As consideration, and subject to annual 
City of Mobile appropriation, the OWNER shall pay the SERVICE PROVIDER an annual not-to-
exceed amount of Eight Million and 00/100 Dollars ($8,000,000.00). Additionally, subject to annual 
Federal Transit Administration appropriation awarded to the City of Mobile, the OWNER shall pay 
the SERVICE PROVIDER an annual not-to-exceed amount of approximately Four Million One 
Hundred Thousand and 00/100 Dollars ($4,100,000.00) (the combined totals constituting the “Annual 
NTE”). The Annual NTE includes the Monthly Fee; which includes Operating Expenses as defined in 
§6.1. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall not invoice costs in excess of the Annual NTE without the 
OWNER’s prior written approval. In the event of any conflict among the Proposal, this Agreement, 
and the RFP (including addenda), this Agreement controls. 
1.3.2 
The SERVICE PROVIDER’s services are as set forth in Exhibit A (Scope of Services). The Fee 
Proposal dated July 25, 2025 is incorporated solely to establish fees, rates, and payment terms and has 
been included as a part of the contract document as Exhibit B. In the event of any conflict between the 
Fee Proposal and this Agreement (including Exhibit A and the RFP with addenda), this Agreement 
controls.      
1.3.3 
The Operations Support; Planning and Reporting. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide those 
transit services reasonably required to assist the OWNER in the efficient operation of the Mobile 
Transit System. At no additional cost, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall furnish periodic written reports 
(at least monthly) and data-driven recommendations concerning service extensions, route planning, 
service policies, and fare structures, with the objective of increasing ridership and reducing cost per 
passenger to the maximum extent feasible and within the OWNER’s approved budget. All services 
and recommendations shall conform to the policies, standards, and procedures established by the 
OWNER and to all applicable Federal, State, and local laws and regulations (including Title VI, ADA, 
and FTA requirements). The OWNER retains sole authority to approve or implement any service or 
fare changes. 
1.3.4 
The SERVICE PROVIDER represents and warrants that it is duly organized, qualified to do business 
in Alabama, and properly licensed to perform the services required by this Agreement, and it shall 
obtain and maintain all required registrations, permits, and approvals throughout the Term. If services 
are performed through a local operator entity, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall ensure such entity is 
an appropriately licensed entity formed prior to Notice to Proceed and contractually bound to this 
Agreement; the SERVICE PROVIDER remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of any 
such entity. 
1.3.5

the SERVICE PROVIDER shall submit a detailed, line-item annual operating budget (the “Annual

levels (fixed-route, paratransit, and microtransit), labor and fringe, fuel and energy assumptions, 
insurance, maintenance, parts, technology/platform costs, and other anticipated Operating Expenses.

may not be exceeded without a written amendment.
