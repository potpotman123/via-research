1. Introduction 
The City of Spartanburg is seeking proposals from qualiﬁed ﬁrms to provide a pilot microtransit 
service program. The purpose of this pilot is to explore the feasibility and efectiveness of on- 
demand transit services to meet local transportation needs. This pilot program will utilize three (3) 
vehicles already owned by the City and will need to operate with existing branding that has already 
been developed. Upon successful completion of the pilot, the City is interested in the potential to 
expand the program. 
The selected ﬁrm will be responsible for full management of the microtransit services, including but 
not limited to driver hiring and management, vehicle maintenance, service operations, and 
ensuring that the service meets the community’s transportation needs. Proposals should be 
scalable, with room for growth beyond the initial pilot phase. 
 
2. Background 
The City of Spartanburg is focused on improving mobility and reducing trafic congestion by 
providing innovative transportation options for its residents. Microtransit services, ofering ﬂexible 
and on-demand rides, will help meet the diverse needs of residents who may not have access to 
traditional transit options or need transportation for short distances or non-commute purposes. 
This pilot program will start with a limited service area and three (3) City-owned vehicles. The City has 
designed speciﬁc branding to be used across the service. This RFP outlines the requirements for 
the management and operation of the service during the pilot phase. 
 
3. Scope of Work 
The successful proposer will be required to perform the following tasks: 
3.1 Microtransit Service Operations 
• 
Service Design and Implementation: Develop an operational plan for the pilot 
microtransit service with City Staf. 
• 
Technology Platform: Implement and manage a user-friendly mobile app or web-based 
platform for riders to book and pay for rides. 
• 
Vehicle Operations: Manage the day-to-day operation of the City-owned vehicles, 
including coordination of schedules, driver assignments, and route optimization. 
• 
Provide Exceptional Customer Service and Operations: 
3.2 Management of Drivers 
• 
Driver Hiring and Training: Hire qualiﬁed drivers to operate the microtransit vehicles. This 
includes conducting background checks, providing driver training, and ensuring compliance 
with all local, state, and federal regulations.

• 
Driver Scheduling and Supervision: Establish and maintain a system for scheduling and 
supervising drivers, ensuring that all shifts are covered and vehicles are properly stafed. 
Hiring local drivers as much as feasible. 
3.3 Vehicle Management 
• 
Vehicle Maintenance: Take over 3 new City vehicle for the pilot. Ensure the vehicles are 
well-maintained, safe, and in compliance with all applicable safety standards. This includes 
routine inspections, repairs, and the provision of necessary equipment for the vehicles. As 
the program expands, vendor will work with City staf to select and purchase new vehicles. 
• 
Branding and Exterior Design: Ensure that the current and new City-owned vehicles are 
branded with the City’s existing designs, and that the branding is consistently applied 
across the ﬂeet. 
3.4 Customer Service and Support 
• 
Customer Support: Provide customer service support for riders to address issues such as 
booking problems, scheduling inquiries, and feedback collection. 
• 
Collect and Share Data: share collected data with City staf to understand trends, patterns 
and help make informed programmatic decisions. 
• 
Alterative Scheduling Options: Vendor will provide options for those without smart phones 
to book rides via phone calls. 
• 
Community Outreach: Work with City Team to conduct outreach to the Spartanburg 
community to raise awareness of the microtransit service and encourage participation in 
the pilot program. Also to train citizens as required and appropriate, and provide 
appropriate training and market material. 
3.5 Reporting and Evaluation 
• 
Performance Metrics: Track key performance indicators (KPIs) related to service eficiency, 
customer satisfaction, ridership, and ﬁnancial performance. 
• 
Regular Reporting: Provide monthly and quarterly reports to the City of Spartanburg on 
service performance, challenges, and recommendations for improvement. 
• 
Quick response to service issues: Quick and through communication is expected through 
out the program to keep a maximum operational up time. 
 
4. Pilot Program Duration 
• 
Start Date: The program is expected to launch in the Summer of 2025. 
• 
Pilot Program Duration: The pilot phase will last for 12 months, after which the City will 
evaluate the program and determine the next steps, which may include expansion or 
contract extension.

5. Proposal Submission Requirements 
Proposals must include, at a minimum, the following: 
5.1 Executive Summary 
A brief overview of the proposer’s qualiﬁcations, experience, and approach to the microtransit pilot 
program. 
5.2 Service Plan 
Detailed description of the proposed microtransit service, including: 
• 
Operational plan and service area coverage 
• 
Proposed technology platform for booking and payments 
• 
Plans for vehicle management, including maintenance and branding 
• 
Driver recruitment, training, and scheduling processes 
• 
Customer support and community outreach strategies 
5.3 Company Proﬁle 
Information about the proposer’s company, including: 
• 
Company history and relevant experience in providing microtransit or related services 
• 
Demonstrated ability to manage transportation services of similar scope 
• 
Proposed staf and their qualiﬁcations 
• 
References from past clients with similar transportation projects 
5.4 Financial Proposal 
A breakdown of the ﬁnancial aspects of the proposal, including: 
• 
Estimated costs for the pilot program 
• 
Costs associated with vehicle maintenance, driver management, and technology platform 
implementation 
• 
Proposed fare structure and revenue projections 
• 
Payment schedule and any potential contingencies 
5.5 Scalability Plan 
Outline the ﬁrm’s vision for expanding the program after the pilot, including potential growth in 
service area, ﬂeet, or other considerations.
