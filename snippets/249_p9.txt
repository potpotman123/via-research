Appendix B: 
Deployment Parameters 
 
The Parties agree to the following Deployment Parameters. Upon mutual agreement, the Parties 
may agree to subsequent modifications of these Parameters during the term of this Agreement. 
 
x Deployment Service Zone: Approximately 77.2 square miles coverage zone in North Utah 
County, Utah.  
x Service Days/Hours: The Deployment’s operating hours shall be Monday through 
Saturday, 6:00am to 9:00pm in North Utah County.  
x Vehicle Fleet: For North Utah County, Via’s Vehicle Partner will offer a fleet of up to 
sixteen (16) branded, licensed, and insured vehicles, eight (8) of which will be Americans 
with Disabilities Act (ADA)-compliant Wheelchair Accessible Vehicles (WAV), to be 
made available to independent contractor Driver Partners, who will be able to gain access 
to these vehicles after being registered onto the Platform. Of the sixteen (16) total vehicles, 
four (4) vehicles shall be treated by Via as spares in the event that a regular service vehicle 
requires maintenance or replacement. Fleet size and composition of WAVs and non-WAVs 
may be adjusted based upon mutual agreement of the Parties as the service grows. As 
electric charging infrastructure becomes available, Via agrees to transition to a mix of 
electric or hybrid vehicles, with all associated vehicle costs included in the current hourly 
rate service fees. 
x Distance Caps: UTA may implement distance caps, which limit how far a rider can 
travel, to optimize the service based on parameters set at its discretion. 
x 
TNC Overflow: UTA may direct ride requests to brokered TNC services based on 
parameters set at its discretion.

Appendix C:
Service Fees
The table below outlines the payment structure, in which UTA would be charged a price per driver
hour. Any new regulatory fees imposed by a governmental entity related to the service will be
charged as a pass-through cost contingent on UTA advance agreement provided such agreement
will not be unreasonably withheld. UTA will have the option of decreasing other services or
expenses in order to off-set these additional fees. The Deployment shall include a fleet of vehicles
as described in Appendix B, subject to extension by mutual agreement of the parties on terms to
be agreed (including any change in fees). The total contract value shall not exceed that listed in
this Appendix C, "Not-to-Exceed Fees," of $3,463,509 including contingency and marketing for
the 11-month duration of this Service Order (through June 30, 2027).
