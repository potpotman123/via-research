Moreover, we create internal case studies highlighting successful cooperative 
engagements, regularly sharing these insights with our commercial team. This fosters a 
culture of continuous learning and improvement, keeping us agile and informed in our 
cooperative strategies. At Via, we treat cooperatives as true partners in our mission. Our 
dedicated points of contact engage in collaborative brainstorming sessions with various 
cooperatives to identify innovative ways to enhance our partnerships and better serve our 
customers. 
Impact-oriented company and mission-driven products 
Public transportation serves as crucial infrastructure that underlies our collective health, 
education, and prosperity. Ready access to transportation drives outcomes of many 
important social indicators: better access to hospitals and medical facilities increases 
care and improves patient outcomes; improved pupil transportation maximizes school 
attendance and alertness in class; and greater transportation coverage increases 
economic opportunity and yields higher rates of employment. 
Delivering meaningful social and environmental impact is core to our TransitTech solution. 
Our transit planning and service performance analytics allow partners to analyze the 
impact of their transportation projects across demographic data such as race, poverty, 
housing, and education. Our user applications are designed with accessibility at the 
forefront and can accommodate a wide range of user needs.
