8.6. 
FARES AND FARE HANDLING 
  
Fares will be determined by the City. The City expects the Vendor to collect all service revenue, both cash and 
online payments on the rider app. Revenue shall be returned to City as a credit on monthly invoices within 30 
days of the end of the billing cycle during which revenue was received. The proposal shall clearly confirm this 
understanding and explain how revenue will be returned to the City.  
8.7. 
PROJECT MANAGEMENT 
Project management will be a key responsibility of the Proposer and a continuous function. The Proposer's 
Project Manager assigned to the project shall have the authority to make commitments and decisions that are 
binding on the proposer. The City will designate a project manager to coordinate all project activities. All 
communications between the City and the proposer shall be coordinated through their respective Project

8.8. 
TECHNOLOGY & EQUIPMENT 
• Proposer shall provide all hardware, software, installation, training, technical assistance, and equipment 
required to deploy and manage the Microtransit rideshare service.  The City imagines a technology 
platform that operates in real-time, combining passengers traveling within the service area zone in a way 
that optimizes vehicle utilization while maintaining excellent customer service quality and on-time 
performance goals.  
• The technology platform should support fully automated scheduling, dispatch, and reservations allowing 
riders to schedule trips on-demand in real-time via a telephone or a smartphone application. Passengers 
should also be able to see where their assigned vehicle is located and watch its approach to their pick-up 
location. This should be accessible to designated City staff via a standard web browser and it should 
allow for report generation and operational data extraction for analysis at any time that the service is in 
operation.  
• Proposer will be responsible for coordinating payment and fare integration within the technology 
platform and/or across other City applications, as necessary. The City envisions that the payment system 
will be highly accessible and may include access through a smartphone app, online, or by phone and 
would accept multiple forms of payment to accommodate residents that are unbanked, do not own a 
smart phone, or have low technology literacy.  
• The Proposer shall provide all necessary hardware required to fully deploy the transportation service. If 
any transfer of equipment is required between vehicles, transfer should be simple and efficient. The 
operator user interface should be safe and easy to use, and it should include audible and clear messaging 
indicating passenger updates and stop changes. Driving instructions should be available in list, map, and 
turn-by-turn voice instructions to limit driver distraction and guarantee safe vehicle operation.  
8.9. 
VEHICLE ACQUISITION & OPERATIONS 
The Proposer shall supply and maintain all necessary vehicles for the service. If Proposer does not own the 
necessary vehicles for the service, vehicle acquisition by way of purchase, leasing, or other agreement will be 
required by the Proposer. The selected Proposer shall work with the City to develop a vehicle acquisition plan 
that clearly outlines the fleet size, type, fixed and variable costs, operations, cleaning schedule, and maintenance 
needs. The City desires vehicles that accommodate mobility limited users and bicycle storage, as feasible. 
Vehicles shall be no more than six (6) years old and maintained regularly to ensure they are in good working 
order, and a plan must be in place to provide regular vehicle inspection and maintenance records to the City. 
Vehicles must meet all EPA standards. All of the non-ADA fleet should be plug-in electric at contract start. A 
portion of the fleet must be ADA accessible vehicles, must be hybrid-electric if no fully electric vehicles are 
available, and all relevant FTA requirements must be met with the provision of ADA compliant service.  
Selected Proposer is responsible for ensuring that any owned or leased vehicles used in the performance of this 
Pilot Project are covered by sufficient automobile liability insurance. Selected Proposer will be required to 
provide to the City copies of all applicable insurance certificates and policies.
