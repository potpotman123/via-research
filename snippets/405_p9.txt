Appendix B: 
Deployment Parameters 
 
The Parties agree to the following Deployment Parameters for the UTA On Demand Deployment 
in Southern Davis County. Upon mutual agreement, the Parties may agree to subsequent 
modifications of these Parameters during the term of this Agreement. 
 
 Deployment Service Zone: Approximately 28.2 square miles coverage zone in Southern 
Davis County, Utah. See Appendix A for a map of the service zone boundary. 
 Service Days/Hours: The Deployment’s operating hours shall be Monday through Friday, 
6:00am to 9:00pm. 
 Vehicle Fleet: Via’s Vehicle Partner will offer a fleet of up to eight (8) branded, licensed, 
and insured vehicles, including two (2) Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)-compliant 
Wheelchair Accessible Vehicles (WAV) to be made available to independent contractor 
Driver Partners, who will be able to gain access to these vehicles after being registered 
onto the Platform. Of the eight total vehicles, two (2) vehicles shall be treated by Via as 
spares in the event that a regular service vehicle requires maintenance or replacement. Fleet 
size and composition of WAVs and non-WAVs may be adjusted based upon mutual 
agreement of the Parties.

Appendix C: 
Service Fees 
 
The table below outlines the payment structure, in which UTA would be charged a price per driver 
hour and a fixed monthly price for Technology and IT Operations. Any new regulatory fees 
imposed by a governmental entity related to the service will be charged as a pass-through cost 
contingent on UTA advance agreement provided such agreement will not be unreasonably 
withheld. UTA will have the option of decreasing other services or expenses in order to off-set 
these additional fees. The Deployment shall include a fleet of vehicles as described in the Service 
Order in Section 4, Service Parameters, subject to extension by mutual agreement of the parties on 
terms to be agreed (including any change in fees). The total contract value shall not exceed that 
listed in the Service Order’s Appendix C, “Not-to-Exceed Fees,” of $2,282,200 for the 17-month 
duration of this Service Order (through December 31, 2023).
