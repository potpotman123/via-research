Background  
In 2019, the Council executed 19M025, a 2-year SAAS (software as a service) contract with Remix. 
The agreement included two products: 1) Transit, a map-based bus route planner and 2) Streets, 
a map-based street design platform for creating and enhancing road features and facilities. 
The use of these services grew significantly and in 2021 a two-year renewal for the same products 
was approved with a new Contract 21M006. The same vendor began operating under the name 
Remix Technologies LLC after being purchased by Via Transportation. 
Metro Transit’s internal Remix user base continued to expand and in 2023 a second contract 
renewal was executed as a new Contract 23M006 with an expiration date of 3/31/2025. This 
contract was later amended to add a third feature to the Remix portfolio. This product, called On-
Demand Planning (OdP), is helping the agency grow its micro transit services. 
Procurement and Service Development are currently advancing a Request for Proposal (RFP) to 
procure similar transit planning tools through an open and competitive process. Proposals were due 
on 12/30/2024. Given the time constraints and RFP schedule, we need to extend the current 
contract to ensure continuity of service until the next contract is awarded. Therefore, we are 
requesting authorization for a three-month extension with Remix, through 6/30/2025. 
Without an amendment, delays in work could be expected for bus network analysis for Network 
Now, transit advantage planning, and micro transit service planning.

Rationale 
The execution of professional service contracts exceeding $500,000 requires Council approval. The 
current contract value of $473,250, as amended through the first CAA, plus the $65,625 proposed 
in this second CAA, will total $538,875. 
Thrive Lens Analysis 
Stewardship: Investment is transit planning tools provides us an opportunity to reinvest and grow 
the capacity of our communities, with more inclusive development and intentional investment.  
Equity: Transit planning tools help the agency create connections with the Metro Transit network 
which will bring more people to jobs and bring more jobs to communities. This enriches 
communities across the region and helps balance opportunities. 
Livability: Transit planning tools help create a network which allows users quicker and safer 
access to jobs, services, amenities and entertainment 
Prosperity: Transit planning tools reflect a strategic investment in regional infrastructure which 
promotes economic competitiveness and creates prosperity for the region. 
Sustainability: Transit planning tools will lead to a better transit system. A robust transportation 
system with more choices and mobility will transport more people efficiently with fewer external 
costs (wear-and-tear, pollution, accidents) to the region. 
Funding 
Funding for this amendment is available through local sources provided under Metro Transit’s 
operating budget. 
Small Business Inclusion 
No small business goal was established on the initial CIM. There are no impacts with the proposed 
action. When the new RFP is ready to route, the Office of Equity and Equal Opportunity will review 
small business opportunities at that time.
