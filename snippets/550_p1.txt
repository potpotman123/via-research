RESOLUTION NO. 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF 
COMMERCE, 
CALIFORNIA, 
AWARDING 
A 
PROFESSIONAL 
SERVICES AGREEMENT TO VIA MOBILITY, LLC., TO PROVIDE 
ON-DEMAND TRANSIT SOFTWARE TECHNOLOGY TO IMPROVE 
TRANSIT SERVICE AT THE CITY’S METROLINK STATION AND 
DIAL-A-RIDE PROGRAM 
 
WHEREAS, on January 12, 2021, the City Council approved the Commerce in 
Motion Comprehensive Operational Analysis (COA) final report; and authorized City 
staff to implement the preferred alternative fixed-route service recommendations. 
Part of the service recommendation included implementing a new on-demand service 
to replace low performing fixed-route service in the City. The recommended on-
demand technology will reduce the amount of time passengers spend waiting for a 
connection at Commerce Metrolink Station and can be specifically targeted by 
geographic location or time of day to control operational costs, constrain service to 
Commerce Station, or expand service to new areas as demand and financial 
considerations 
warrant. 
Additionally, 
the 
on-demand 
feature 
will 
aid 
in 
accommodating existing passengers that may be impacted due to transit’s new route 
changes that take effect December 2021, and 
 
WHEREAS, the on-demand transit software technology RFP was advertised, 
and the opening of the proposals was conducted on June 14, 2021. Six (6) firms 
submitted proposals, and 
 
WHEREAS, all proposals received were first evaluated and ranked on the 
technical evaluation responses. Proposals that were determined technically 
acceptable were reevaluated with cost as a consideration. After using both technical 
and cost evaluations, the proposals were ranked to establish a competitive range, 
and  
 
WHEREAS, based on the proposal results, Via Mobility, LLC, is the most 
qualified professional consultant that also provided the most efficient and cost-
effective approach. Additionally, they provided references and the required Federal 
certification documentation. The City staff was able to negotiate a cost of $121,700 
for this project, and 
 
WHEREAS, the cost of the on-demand transit software technology shall be 
covered 100% with Federal Grant Funds (5307 Funds) and have no impact on the 
City's operating budget. This project has been approved by the Southern California 
Association of Governments (SCAG), Los Angeles County Metropolitan Authority 
(Metro), and the Federal Transportation Administration (FTA); and included as part of 
the regional Federal Transportation Improvement Plan (FTIP - Project ID: 
LAOG1295).  
 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF COMMERCE 
DOES HEREBY RESOLVE, DECLARE AND DETERMINE AS FOLLOWS:
=== page 2 ===
Section 1. 
The City Council of the City of Commerce hereby finds and 
declares that the foregoing recitals are true and correct, and incorporates them 
herein as findings and as a substantive part of this Resolution. 
 
Section 2. 
Award and Approve the Professional Services Agreement to Via 
Mobility, LLC., to provide on-demand transit software technology, maintenance and 
support in the amount of $121,700 and authorize a 2.7% contingency of $3,300 for a

PASSED, APPROVED, and ADOPTED this 20th day of July 2021.
