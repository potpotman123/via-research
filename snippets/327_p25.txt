10. 
Contract Modification - Testing of Fire Protection System & Fire Extinguishers 
Maintenance Services 
 
8:51 a.m.  A briefing paper was included in the meeting materials, presenting to the Operations 
Committee, for discussion, acceptance, and referral to the Board of Commissioners for approval, 
a request to authorize the President and CEO to execute a contract modification with Gateway 
Fire Protection Systems, Inc., for Testing of the Fire Protection System & Fire Extinguisher 
Maintenance Services, in the amount of $198,623.222, increasing the total contract not-to-exceed 
amount to $681,959.04.  Chuck Stewart, Executive Vice President and Chief Operation Officer – 
Metro Transit, provided an overview of this item, noting that on March 3, 2021, Bi-State 
Development (BSD) entered into a contract with Gateway Fire Protection to provide required 
services for testing of the agency’s fire protection system and fire extinguisher maintenance 
services, in the amount of $313,097.03, for a performance period of five (5) base years.  He 
reported that the scope of work included optional services; however, since the time of the initial 
contract execution, BSD’s Maintenance Division confirmed that these optional services are 
required.  Mr. Stewart noted that two (2) contract modifications, totaling $170,238.80, have 
already been made to the original base contract, which caused the cumulative cost to increase to 
$483,335.82, and a third modification is now being requested, which adds funds to Base Year 4, 
plus $100,000 in anticipated needs for Base Year 5.  He reported that the Project Team, which 
includes members of the Maintenance and Safety Departments, feels the modification will 
adequately address the required maintenance services for the agency’s Fire Protection System, 
and the proposed increased cost has been reviewed by staff and was found to be fair and 
reasonable 
 
 
A motion to approve this agenda item, as presented, was made by Commissioner Johnson and 
seconded by Commissioner Jackson-Jennings.   
 
 
The motion passed unanimously. 
 
11. 
Contract Authorization: Cervello Security Information and Management Solution 
 
8:53 a.m.  A briefing paper was included in the meeting materials, presenting to the Operations 
Committee, for discussion, acceptance, and referral to the Board of Commissioners for approval, 
a recommendation that Bi-State Development execute a three-year enterprise license agreement 
with Cervello, through World Wide Technology’s OMNIA contract, in the not-to-exceed amount 
of $690,000.  Jyotsna Gupta, Vice President and Chief Information Officer, Information 
Technology, gave an overview of this item, noting that on February 22, 2023, the Bi-State Safety 
Oversight Agency approved Corrective Action Plan 202110-SS-04, due to an identified hazard of 
insufficient event logging of the MetroLink SCADA system, found after an external 
cybersecurity audit.  She stated that management desires to close State Safety Oversight (SSO) 
Corrective Action Plan 202110-SS04, by monitoring SCADA system usage through deployment 
of the Cervello Security Information and Even Management tool.  Ms. Gupta stated that OMNIA 
Partners is a state and local government cooperative purchasing entity and has nationally 
solicited, evaluated and awarded contracts through a competitive bidding process, on behalf of its 
members, and management is requesting that Bi-State obtain the Cervello Security Information 
and Even Management tool, through the Agency’s partnership contract with OMNIA. She noted 
that the proposals for the Cervello system were evaluated by an evaluation committee, using the

12. 
Contract Modification: Microtransit Project Contract Extension 
 
8:55 a.m.  A briefing paper was included in the meeting materials, presenting to the Operations 
Committee, for discussion, acceptance, and referral to the Board of Commissioners for approval, 
a recommendation that the President and CEO be authorized to approve a contract modification 
with Via, for the continued operation of microtransit service, for a one-year extension (through 
May 31, 2026), in an amount not to exceed $6,344,000. Chuck Stewart, Executive Vice President 
and Chief Operation Officer – Metro Transit, provided an overview of this item, stating that Via 
has been instrumental, serving as a safety net for Metro’s fixed route system.  He noted that Via 
serves to supplement the paratransit system, as demand continues to increase.  Mr. Stewart 
reported an increase from 5,000 rides per week to 8,000 rides per week.  He noted that, currently 
Metro has drivers available; however, there is a need for vans.  He stated that there is still a need 
for additional mechanics, and Via is helping fill the void during this period.  Mr. Stewart noted 
that the Agency is working on a future plan for microtransit, and will be working on a request for 
proposals for this in the future.  He noted that currently, management is requesting an extension 
of the current Via contract, through May 2026.       
 
 
A motion to approve this agenda item, as presented, was made by Commissioner Johnson and 
seconded by Commissioner Golliday.   
 
 
The motion passed.

14. 
Operations Report 
 
9:00 a.m.  An operations report for Metro Transit was included in the Committee packet.  Charles 
Stewart, Executive Vice President and Chief Operation Officer – Metro Transit, provided a 
summary of the report for the Committee.  He noted continued success in building the workforce, 
but noted a continued need for mechanics.  He stated that the $5,000 sign on bonus is still in 
effect for mechanics, and he noted an upcoming hiring event.  Mr. Stewart noted a zero-denial 
rate for Call-A-Ride, and stated that the Agency continues to improve the phone software for this 
service, along with on-line requests for rides.  He noted that an event for Transit Worker 
Appreciation Day was held at the Illinois Garage, where a resolution was presented.  He noted 
that the MetroBus Operators are the face of Metro, and that they do a great job representing the 
Agency.  Mr. Stewart reported that Community Connect events continue to be held, with the goal 
of transparency to the community and public input regarding service.  He stated that the Agency 
is holding negotiations with Local 788 regarding clerical, and the goal is to have an agreement in 
place by June 30th.  Mr. Stewart stated that Metro staff will be wearing Cardinal’s jerseys on 
Thursday, March 27th, for St. Louis Cardinals’ Opening Day.  He also noted that a Bus Road-e-o

15. 
President/CEO Report  
9:06 a.m.  Bi-State Development President/CEO Taulby Roach addressed the committee noting 
that the budget approval process is moving forward, gaining approval from the Agency’s
