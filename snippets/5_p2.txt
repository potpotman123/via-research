(Commission District 1)
Recommendation
Staff recommends the City Commission approve an event agreement and road closure
requests with Historic Downtowner Saloon, LLC., in substantially the form attached, and
authorize execution of the agreements by the City Manager.
Background
On June 21, 2024, Historic Downtowner Saloon, LLC. submitted a special event
application for the Craftober Festival to be held at Historic Downtowner Saloon. The
event is scheduled to take place on Saturday, September 28, 2024, which is less than the
90-day application deadline, therefore the special event application fee is $1,000. The
event impacts are limited to Saturday, September 28, 2024, which includes the setup
period, event period, and breakdown period.
The event organizers attended the August , 2024, special events meeting to review the
event details with City staff including the Parks & Recreation Department, Transportation
and Mobility Department, Police Department, and Fire Department. The special events
meetings focus on the operational logistics, cross departmental coordination, and event
organizer requirements.
This event organizer is requesting the following special permissions that require City
Commission approval:
Road Closures:
S. New River Drive. (from the Andrews Bridge to the circle)
City staff invited the surrounding civic associations to the special events meeting as well
as sent a copy of the application and site plan. Upon approval, the City's online events
calendar will be updated to reflect this event.
