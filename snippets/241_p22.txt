Fixed Upfront Costs   
The County shall pay to the Contractor a one-time non-refundable installation fee of eighty 
one thousand dollars ($81,000.00), which will be payable in full upon signing of this Agreement. 
In addition to technology localization costs, installation fee includes 3 Wheelchair Access Vehicle 
(WAV) retrofits at a cost of $17,000.00 each and 10 vehicle wraps for the fleet at a cost of 
$3,000.00 each. If the County requires additional WAV retrofits or vehicle wraps, the Contractor 
will bill the County as incurred. Replacements of the 3 WAVs during the Additional Term for fleet 
refresh will also be billed as a pass-through. The estimated cost of each WAV retrofit is $17,000.00 
and the estimated cost of each vehicle wrap is $3,000.00.   
 
Ongoing Invoice Fees   
The County shall be responsible for monthly ongoing fees of $67.00 per vehicle hour (the 
“Monthly Subscription Fees”). A vehicle hour is defined as each hour during which a driver is 
paid on the Via platform. Notwithstanding the number of vehicle hours incurred per month, the 
County shall be responsible for a minimum Monthly Subscription Fee of $25,000.00 per month 
(the “Minimum Monthly Fees”). The Minimum Monthly Fees shall be payable in advance on the 
first of the month provided that the first Minimum Monthly Fees shall be due upon execution of 
this Contract. If the Monthly Subscription Fees for any month exceed the Minimum Monthly Fees 
paid hereunder, the County shall be responsible for paying such difference (the “Monthly True-
up”) on a monthly invoice basis. All fees set forth herein shall be payable by the County on a net 
fifteen basis. If the Contractor does not receive timely payment, the Contractor may charge the

The Contractor shall provide to the County an invoice statement showing the calculation of the 
Monthly Subscription Fees incurred based on the actual number of vehicle hours that Driver

format will not delay payment to the Contractor. If the County requires additional invoice support, 
the Contractor will use best efforts to provide it and additional costs may apply.    
 
If changes in applicable federal, state, or local law result in a significant change in the Contractor’s 
costs, the parties may opt to renegotiate the ongoing monthly fees. Should changes in applicable 
market dynamics result in an increase in the Contractor’s cost per hour of ten percent (10%) or 
more to any of the following categories, compared to costs at the time of contract signing, the 
parties may amend the ongoing on-demand vehicle hour rate to cover labor (driver pay), vehicle 
rental, and fuel.   
 
Should the Contractor elect to adjust the per vehicle hour rate, the Contractor shall provide 
supporting documentation of the relevant cost increases to the County, and the Contract and/or its 
exhibits will be amended to reflect the new Fee structure and per vehicle hour rate so that, at the 
discretion of the County, either:   
1. The total not-to-exceed contract value is adjusted such that the total not-to-exceed number 
of vehicle hours remains unchanged, or   
2. The total not-to-exceed number of vehicle hours is adjusted such that the total not-to-
exceed contract value remains unchanged.   
 
Marketing Materials   
The County shall reimburse the Contractor for promotional or print marketing materials at cost, 
on a pass-through basis, for an amount not to exceed $3,000 for the 1-year Term.  
 
 
Invoicing: Western and Eastern Zones   
The Contractor shall provide monthly invoices on or before the 15th of each month and by email 
to transit@douglas.co.us with line item break out maintenance, operating, and administration costs 
that make up the contracted hourly cost.
