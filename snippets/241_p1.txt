I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Douglas County intends to continue providing Link on Demand (Link) regional rideshare service in
Highlands Ranch and expand east through portions of unincorporated Douglas County into the
Town of Parker in 2026. The request is for approval of the 2026 Public Contract of Service (PCS)
between the Douglas County Board of County Commissioners (BCC) and River North Transit LLC, a
subsidiary of Via Transportation Inc. (Via), to provide regional rideshare in the amount of
$4,404,615.
II. REQUEST
Staff requests approval of the 2026 PCS between the Board of County Commissioners and River
North Transit LLC in the amount of $4,404,615.
III. BACKGROUND
The Douglas County Integrated Transit and Multimodal Study (Study), completed in 2025,
recommended microtransit service in northern Douglas County as a strategy to meet the transit
and mobility needs of the community. The County partnered with the City of Lone Tree to expand
the City’s popular and successful Link service into Highlands Ranch. River North Transit LLC, a
wholly owned subsidiary of Via, was selected as a Preferred Vendor to provide the recommended
service and a PCS was executed to operate Link through March 31, 2026. Link was launched in
Highlands Ranch on May 27, 2025, and has provided over 72,000 trips, consistently receiving a 4.9
out of 5 star rider rating.
IV. DISCUSSION
Via currently operates regional rideshare service in Lone Tree, Highlands Ranch, and Meridian. The
2026 PCS maintains service in the Western Zone, including Highlands Ranch and Acres Green, and
supports an expanded Pilot Project (Pilot) to the east as recommended in the Study.

The Pilot, referred to as the Eastern Zone, will add service into the northwest and central portions 
of Parker and the unincorporated communities of Meridian Village, Grand View Estates, Stepping 
Stone, and Stonegate. Service includes regional connections to shopping, employment, and 
healthcare centers, ADA accessibility, and integration with the Regional Transportation District 
(RTD). An additional ten vehicles will be added to support the Eastern Zone, increasing total 
ridership capacity by over 1,300 rides per week. The Eastern Zone will launch in April 2026 and 
serves the general public.

The PCS will be funded through Road and Bridge Sales and Use Tax revenue, State grants and a 
contribution of $250,000 from the Town of Parker in support of the County’s provision of regional 
rideshare in the Eastern Zone. 
V. RECOMMENDED ACTION 
The County Attorney’s Office has reviewed the Contract, and it complies with all State and local 
requirements. Staff recommends approval of the 2026 PCS between the Board of County 
Commissioners and River North Transit LLC in the amount of $4,404,615.
