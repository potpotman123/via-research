Every transit agency has to decide how best to allocate its scarce resources
-- there are, after all, never enough buses and drivers to provide high-
quality service everywhere. With Via, transit agencies can reduce costs by
providing real-time demand-responsive service using smaller vehicles in
low-density areas, while freeing up bus capacity for high-demand corridors.
