2. Are the vendor’s products/services well-regarded?
Via’s products are recognized as the market leading solutions for transit technology and 
operations, used by cities and transit agencies worldwide to modernize their legacy 
systems into smart, efficient, data-driven networks. Our solutions are known for 
delivering industry-leading cost-efficiency, reliability, and service quality. Via’s platform 
powers a wide array of transportation modes—including microtransit, ADA-paratransit, 
student and senior transport, NEMT, AV and EV fleet management, and school bus 
routing—along with fixed-route and on-demand planning tools. Our reputation is built on 
proven performance: our proprietary algorithms and AI-driven routing reduce travel times, 
vehicle miles, and costs while increasing utilization and rider satisfaction. With a track 
record of innovation and measurable impact, Via’s software and services are consistently 
recognized as best-in-class across the global mobility landscape.
