1.18 
Operations Headquarters 
CONTRACTOR shall establish, furnish, and maintain an office within the operations facility as 
described under Section 3.4 herein below.   
 
The CONTRACTOR will be required to supply all administrative office furniture, computer 
hardware, and software associated with this contract, including that needed to manage the 
website and mobile applications discussed in Section 1.21.  CONTRACTOR will be proficient in 
the use of video software currently in use by CITY. 
 
The CITY-owned facility will include appropriate parking space for CONTRACTOR employees as 
well as the CITY-owned transit vehicles. 
 
1.19 
City-Owned Vehicles 
CITY will provide all revenue service vehicles.  The CONTRACTOR shall be initially provided air 
conditioned, wheelchair accessible vehicles. An adequate number of vehicles will be made 
available to the Contractor to assure at least a twenty percent spare ratio. CONTRACTOR may 
utilize CITY-owned vehicles for driver training.  See Attachment 14 for Fleet Roster. 
 
CITY shall pay for all repairs unless the repairs arise/result from Contractor negligence.  
 
CITY reserves the right to add/subtract or substitute vehicles for those described herein. 
 
CITY will not provide CONTRACTOR with any non-revenue vehicles to conduct daily non-revenue 
service tasks. CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for providing any non-revenue vehicles that 
may be desired for running of errands, field review of operations, shuttling of drivers, etc. 
 
At the initiation of the contract, CITY and incoming CONTRACTOR will jointly inspect each vehicle 
and sign off on the inspection sheet.  
 
1.20 
Performance Specifications 
All performance specifications will be strictly adhered to in order to provide the highest level of 
service possible. CITY reserves the right to monitor the CONTRACTOR in its performance of the 
Contract to ensure all performance specifications are adhered to.  
 
To receive full compensation, the CONTRACTOR is required to meet or exceed the following 
standards of performance on a monthly basis:  
 
Operating Performance Standards - Vehicles shall be operated with due regard for the 
safety, comfort, and convenience of passengers and the public.  Service shall be provided 
as scheduled or according to any adjusted schedule established by CITY, including route 
modifications required because of a declared emergency. The CONTRACTOR shall strive 
to maintain on-time performance, however, CONTRACTOR shall not be held responsible 
for the failure to provide on-time service due to weather, unavoidable vehicle malfunctions, 
or naturally occurring disasters, if sufficient documentation is provided to CITY. 
 
Personnel Performance Standards - Regularly assigned drivers must be available at all 
times of transit operation to ensure consistent and reliable service under the Contract.

1.21 
Telephone and Technology 
1.21.1 Telephone Numbers 
The CITY will provide the CONTRACTOR with the Customer Service and reservation numbers 
for the ADA paratransit service as well as the general public on-demand service.  
 
CONTRACTOR shall provide customer information service to the public during all hours of service 
operation. CONTRACTOR shall also have the ability to accept calls from hearing-impaired 
persons as well as persons with limited English proficiency. 
 
All phone lines associated with this contract shall be used solely for the purpose of providing 
customer information and accepting ride reservations, and shall not be used by the 
CONTRACTOR for any other purpose. 
 
Upon termination of this agreement the CONTRACTOR shall release such phone numbers to the 
CITY.  
 
1.21.2 Software Backend Characteristics 
Software must provide for scheduling, dispatch, reservation, user interface, and data collection. 
Bidders proposing to leverage existing technology and utilize proprietary software must indicate 
how it meets these requirements. The system must offer a call-in reservation option as well as a 
self-booking option through a mobile app and website. Advance reservations must be available 
for the ADA paratransit service and general public on-demand service. Riders will have the option 
to pay online, via a mobile app, or onboard the vehicle. 
 
The technology must have the ability to limit trip requests based on geography (within an 
established service area) and time (within established service hours). It must also be able to 
schedule curb-to-curb service (ADA paratransit) and designate safe and appropriate “virtual 
stops” for corner-to-corner service (general public on-demand service). 
 
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for creating the GTFS “feed” and providing and integrating 
any software and hardware necessary to provide the real-time service information and functions 
required below, to work seamlessly with the customer webpage and mobile apps, as well as with 
Google Transit.  The real-time software shall integrate with the CITY’s existing vehicle telematics 
hardware and software already in use, and/or any upgrades to CITY hardware and software as 
needed.  The CONTRACTOR shall deploy the real-time software, support the real-time software, 
and update the real-time software.  CONTRACTOR shall include in its proposal an explanation of 
its cybersecurity plan and proposed framework of system and operation.

1.21.3 Website and Mobile Application Characteristics 
CONTRACTOR shall provide a fully functional and fully supported transit website and mobile 
application on behalf of the CITY’s public transit program. The scope and content of these 
technologies will include all transit programs operated by the CITY. 
 
The transit website and mobile application must be free and publicly available through common 
web browsers and as native mobile apps for iPhone and Android that are accessible for ADA 
compliance. It shall include, at a minimum, the capabilities stated above under “Software Backend 
Characteristics,” and the following features:  
 
• 
On-demand and scheduled trip booking. 
• 
Mobile fare payment via debit/credit cards as well as an on-vehicle cash payment option. 
• 
Ability for riders to request/book wheelchair-accessible vehicles. 
• 
Real-time vehicle locations and arrival and departure times for scheduled rides. 
• 
Ability to identify a Personal Care Attendant (PCA). 
• 
Maps which provide an overview of the service area, streets, important points of interest, 
and through zooming, sufficient detail to see routes, transit stops, real-time transit vehicle 
locations, and the ability to click on the real-time transit vehicle icons to view service 
options and link to purchase trips. 
• 
A concise set of navigation links which repeat on each page, including descriptions of 
each transit service, service maps, schedules in html and downloadable pdf formats, fare 
information, etc. 
• 
Tools to create and manage secure intuitive customer accounts which allow the user to 
purchase, modify, and cancel trips and multi-trip passes; schedule repeating trips and one-
time trips; arrange notifications, integrate trips into personal calendars; manage and view 
past and future trip payments, manage account preferences, ask questions and report 
concerns to the CONTRACTOR and to the CITY transit staff, and related features. 
• 
User accounts shall allow for the CITY and the CONTRACTOR to easily communicate 
with and serve the customers as well as access and manage the customer accounts. 
• 
Customers shall receive clear and accurate automated notifications through their choice 
of email, text, call, and/or message through their account app of service alerts and account 
notifications, including impacts or schedule changes to specific trips purchased by the 
user, trip reminders, and other notifications they choose from a menu of options. 
• 
User-friendly tools which seamlessly integrate and allow users to progress from one action 
to any other action without losing their data entry or selections, such as:  trip planning, 
purchasing, modification of trip(s), cancellation of trip(s), report concerns, and secure 
account management. 
• 
Trip planning component shall provide search filters for and offer a range of the most 
reasonable COLT service options to meet the riders’ travel needs, including but not limited 
to cost, departure time and location, arrival time and location, vehicle type, and other 
relevant options.
