Why work with Via?
Via is the world's leading developer
and operator of microtransit systems —
over the past five years, we've provided more
than twenty-five million microtransit rides
to over a million customers. Every day, our
technology powers tens of thousands of rides
around the world.
We partner with some of the smartest
transit agencies, cities, and transportation
contractors in the world, including the Land
Transport Authority in Singapore, the
Capital Metropolitan Transportation
Authority in Austin, the Los Angeles
Metropolitan Transportation Authority, the
cities of Arlington, Texas and West
Sacramento, California, Transport for New
South Wales in Australia, the public transit
agency in Berlin, Germany (the BVG) through
our joint-venture with Mercedes-Benz, Arriva
in the United Kingdom, Keolis in France, and
Go Bus in New Zealand.
