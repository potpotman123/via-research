In addition, the parties agree as follows: 
 
A. 
Data Sharing and Dashboard Services 
THAT the CONTRACTOR and CITY, as outlined in the attached Scope of Services, shall 
co-own all customer data input by end users into the technology solution for purposes of 
the Services and that CONTRACTOR shall make data available to the CITY as set forth 
in the Scope of Services. Notwithstanding the foregoing, CONTRACTOR shall own all 
de-identified, anonymized and/or aggregated data, which it may use for any purpose, 
including to improve its product and services offering, including the Services. For the 
avoidance of doubt, (1) all data shared pursuant to the Contract Documents (including co-
owned data) shall be subject to the confidentiality provisions set forth herein and (2) 
CONTRACTOR agrees that it will not sell any data to third parties that contains personally 
identifiable information. 
 
B. 
Performance Monitoring 
THAT the CONTRACTOR shall, as outlined in the attached Scope of Services, provide 
Weekly Staff Briefings (reporting the service data, performance standards, safety data, and 
customer service data set forth in Section 9 of the Scope of Services on a weekly basis), 
shall provide Monthly Business Reviews (reporting the content set forth in Section 9 of the 
Scope of Services on a monthly basis) within twenty (20) business days of the last day of 
the month for service during the previous month, shall provide Quarterly Business Reviews 
(reporting the content set forth in Section 9 of the Scope of Services on a quarterly basis) 
within twenty (20) business days of the last day of the month after every three (3) months 
of service, and shall provide Annual Business Reviews (reporting the content set forth in 
Section 9 of the Scope of Services on an annual basis) within twenty (20) business days of 
the last day of the month after every twelve (12) months of service. Upon request by CITY, 
CONTRACTOR shall provide daily ridership information.
