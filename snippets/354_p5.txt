RESOLUTION NO. 25-17
RESOLUTION RATIFYING TWO CONTRACT RENEWALS FOR REMAINING OPTION YEARS AND
AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO INCREASE THE EXISTING CONTRACT AMOUNTS WITH
VIA TRANSPORTATION, INC. AND URBAN TRANSPORTATION ASSOCIATES INC. FOR TRANSIT
RELATED SOFTWARE PLATFORMS
WHEREAS, the City of Asheville has authority, pursuant to Article 2 of Chapter 160A of the North
Carolina General Statues, to enter into contracts for services with other entities; and
WHEREAS, the City Council may, pursuant to Rule 18 of the Asheville City Council Rules of
Procedure, ratify actions taken on its behalf but without prior approval; and
WHEREAS, the City of Asheville wishes to ratify execution of the available option years for two
contracts whose original term has expired on December 31, 2024, and to add funds to each contract as
follows:
• $26,000 in funds to the contract with Via Transportation Inc., for planning & schedule
software, for a total contract amount of $115,500; and
• $37,128 in funds to the contract with Urban Transportation Associates Inc., for automatic
passenger counter software, for a total contract amount of $94,979
WHEREAS, Via Transportation Inc. acquired Remix Technologies in 2021, with whom the
initial/original contract was executed and was subsequently reassigned to Via Transportation Inc. for the
remaining two years of the base contract and two one-year options; and
WHEREAS, the annual amounts under the Via Transportation Inc. contract are as follows:
• Year 1 (with Remix Technologies) - $19,500
•
Year 2 - $22,000
• Year 3 - $22,000
Year 4 (1st option year) - $26,000
• Year 5 (2nd option year and this action) - $26,000
Total = $115,500
WHEREAS, the annual amounts under the Urban Transportation Associates Inc. contract are as
follows:
Year 1 - $10,787
• Year 2 - $10,787
Year 3 - $10,787
Year 4 - $10,787
Year 5 - $14,703 (increase associated with transition to cloud-based platform, Amd. 1)
Year 6 (1st option year, included in this action) - $18,200
• Year 7 (2nd option year, included in this action) - $18,928
Total = $94,979
WHEREAS, the original contracts terms have expired, but staff wish to exercise the remaining
option periods under the terms and conditions of the original contracts; and
WHEREAS, in order to avoid a lapse in service and to pay for services rendered, it is necessary
to ratify the additional available option periods and authorize funding for both contracts, adding $26,000,
for a total contract amount of $115,500 to the contract with VIA Transportation Inc; and adding $37,128,
for a total contract amount of $94,979
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF ASHEVILLE
THAT:
=== page 5 ===
Approves a resolution ratifying two contract renewals for remaining option years and authorizing
the City Manager to increase the existing contract amounts as follows:
• With Via Transportation Inc. for Transit Scheduling and Planning Software by $26,000 for
a total of contract amount of $115,500 for the final option year; and
With Urban Transportation Associates, Inc. for Automatic Passenger Counter Software by
$37,128 for a total contract amount of $94,979 for the final two option years.
Read, approved, and adopted this the 28th day of January 2025.

RESOLUTION NO. 25-18
RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO AMEND THE EXISTING CONTRACT
BETWEEN THE CITY OF ASHEVILLE AND HAGERTY CONSULTING, INC.
WHEREAS, the City of Asheville has authority pursuant to Article 3 of Chapter 160A of
the North Carolina General Statutes to enter into contracts for services with other entities; and
WHEREAS, the City of Asheville is currently under contract with Hagerty Consulting, Inc.
to provide recovery management services; and
WHEREAS, the amendment to this contract is intended to ensure continuous operations;
and
WHEREAS, the amendment to this contract will support maximization of reimbursement
for City funded recovery efforts through various federal and state funding sources.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF
ASHEVILLE THAT:
The City Manager is authorized to execute a contract amendment with Hagerty
Consulting, Inc. to add an additional $4,994,885 to this contract, bringing the total amount to
$5,994,885.
Read, approved, and adopted this 28th day of January, 2025.
Magdalen Paulson
City Clerk
