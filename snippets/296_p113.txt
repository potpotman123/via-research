9. Safety and Reliability
i. Describe any processes and procedures to ensure the safety of your
customer and reliability of your service including, but not limited to,
background checks, driver screening, rider/driver validation, drug & alcohol
testing, GPS tracking, vehicle standards & inspections, or any other verifiable
step that is taken to ensure passenger safety.
With Via’s drivers covering over 5.8 million miles each month, the safety of every trip is
paramount. We have established a dedicated Trust and Safety Team focused on
improving driving safety outcomes in order to maintain our reputation for delivering safe
rides. Our investment in tech-enabled operational advancements allows us to proactively
manage safety. This includes advanced recordkeeping that seamlessly complies with FTA
procedures in the United States and enables aggregate analysis of driver performance.
Our solutions include numerous processes and procedures to ensure safety and 
reliability, as described below. 
●
Driver Vetting: Via mandates background checks, including fingerprint screening,
drug and alcohol testing compliance, and driver and vehicle licensing according to
state and local laws.
●
Driver Onboarding: All drivers undergo Mandatory Safety Training, a series of
workshops on topics such as defensive driving and safely navigating roads;
vehicle and equipment operation; and following safety and emergency instructions,
including how to handle and report accidents and major incidents.
●
Verification: Our system includes tools for both passenger and driver verification.
When a rider boards a vehicle, the driver verifies the passenger’s first name and
must confirm the pick-up in the Driver App (see image on following page). Riders
are provided with clear details of their pick-up location and vehicle information,

Monitoring: We regularly monitor driver performance, rider feedback, and vehicle
condition to ensure a safe and secure environment for both drivers and
passengers. Via agents will evaluate and take appropriate action to address any
risk to driver and passenger security. We utilize in-vehicle video recording with
auto-save features to document any traffic violations or near-misses, ensuring
clarity in our safety processes. Additionally, our preventative tools leverage AI
technology to detect potentially risky in-vehicle behaviors, complemented by audio
alerts that help drivers avoid accidents before they occur.
●
Vehicle Inspections and Maintenance: Via trains driver
partners to complete pre-trip inspections, schedules
maintenance checks through provided software, and
ensures that all performed maintenance is recorded.
Preventative maintenance is scheduled to avoid service
disruption, and vehicles are removed from service if they
sustain damage that impairs safe operation.
●
Emergency Response Procedures: We have established
comprehensive plans and procedures for live operations
accident response, including handling emergency equipment, managing road
emergencies, addressing customer issues, conducting site evaluations, and
responding to adverse weather and biological hazards. These proactive measures
ensure we are prepared for any situation, reinforcing our commitment to safety in
every aspect of our operations.
