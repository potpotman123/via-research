Software Training 
Via will ensure Commerce staﬀ are prepared to successfully utilize the on-demand transit 
system with a comprehensive training program. Via has trained hundreds of system 
operators and tens of thousands of drivers across our global deployments. Leveraging this 
experience, we will curate a customized training program to ensure that all relevant 
personnel, including Commerce system administrators, dispatchers, and drivers are 
equipped to use the Via platform and operate the service successfully. 
 
Via’s hands-on workshops will allow each trainee the opportunity to interact with all relevant 
tools, run through scenarios, and ask questions. We will also provide a library of online and 
oﬄine resources, including printed manuals, online troubleshooting guides, FAQs, and high-
quality tutorial videos and webinars. Our training program will ensure that drivers can safely 
operate the service using Via’s platform, and that Commerce staﬀ can expertly monitor 
service operations, access data, and develop reports as needed. 
 
Via typically spreads our live training program over two days, with dedicated Via team 
members to lead the sessions. We are happy to adjust the timing and structure of the training 
program upon request. Our hands-on training workshops include sessions on the following 
topics: 
 
• Systems Training: workshops for using the Driver App and Rider App (2 courses) 
• Dispatch and Customer Service Training: workshop for all administrative 
tools in Via’s Operations Center (2 courses) 
• Advanced Administrative Tools: training for advanced administrative 
functions, such as inputting road closure notices (1 course) 
• Data Dashboards Training: workshop for understanding and leveraging 
Via’s data dashboards and reports (1.5 courses) 
• Marketing Training: consultation on service marketing strategies (2 courses) 
 
We can also oﬀer two types of web-based training sessions: 
• Operations Workshop: high-level overview of the Rider App, Driver App, and 
administrative tools 
• Service Optimization Workshop: overview of Via’s data dashboards and 
reports, and best practices for marketing and growing the service 
 
Please ﬁnd additional information regarding our training plan in proposal section 5.B.4., pp. 
77). 
 
3. Ongoing Support 
For the life of our partnership, Commerce will have access to a dedicated Partner Success 
Manager who will serve as Commerce’s primary point of contact, coordinating resources 
within Via as necessary to provide analytical, consultative, and technical support to 
ensure the ongoing optimization of the service. Our Partner Success Team will be 
committed to the successful implementation of Commerce’s SaaS platform through the 
following support services: 
 
• Service success management. The Partner Success Manager will organize and 
lead regular check-ins with Commerce to review performance, collect feedback, 
and align on next steps for service growth and optimization. 
• Ongoing service monitoring.

• The Partner Success Manager will monitor and analyze service performance

• Product updates and bespoke features. The Partner Success Manager will

• Service growth and expansions. The Partner Success Manager will coordinate 
internal Via resources needed to plan and execute on service expansions, such 
as ﬂeet size increases or zone extensions. 
 
Beyond day-to-day support, the Via team will oﬀer ongoing consultation based on the 
operational best practices we have accumulated through over 200 global services. 
Experts from our Partner Operations team will continuously analyze performance data, 
providing advice on how Commerce can improve service quality and optimize ﬂeet 
productivity. They will also use performance data from other Via services in order to 
benchmark Commerce’s service against deployments of similar size and scope. These 
data-driven insights will equip Commerce with a comparative understanding of 
performance and strategies to grow the service. 
 
Performance Evaluation 
Via will give Commerce comprehensive visibility into all aspects of service delivery, 
enabling the City to track agreed-upon milestones and performance standards. 
Speciﬁcally, Via will provide: 
 
• In-depth reporting. Via will provide comprehensive data reports to evaluate the 
performance of Commerce’s microtransit program on a daily, weekly, and/or 
monthly basis as requested by Commerce. Authorized users will be able access 
data on a 24/7 basis directly through the Via Operation Center (VOC), our web-
based administrative console, using their assigned credentials. All data from the 
VOC can be exported into CSV and Excel formats for further analysis or to easily 
upload data into separate systems as needed. Please ﬁnd additional information 
regarding Via’s reporting suite in proposal section Appendix 5.A. Software 
Overview. 
• Quarterly business reviews. We will conduct in-depth, data-driven Quarterly 
Business Reviews (QBRs) that analyze the service’s long-term performance along 
speciﬁc, mutually-deﬁned key performance indicators (“KPIs”). Supported by Via’s 
in-house Operations and Data Science teams, the Partner Success Manager will 
schedule these quarterly review meetings with City stakeholders to assess system 
performance and trends. 
• Partner success services. In addition to regular reports and QBRs, Via will provide 
performance evaluation on an ongoing basis through the Partner Success services 
described in proposal section 3.A.2. Professional and Technical Qualiﬁcations.
