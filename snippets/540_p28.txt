ACTION AGENDA  
 
1. FS 2025-016, Authorize the Chief Executive Officer to Increase the Contract 
Amount with Via Transportation, Inc., (Contract No. 1750) in the Amount of 
$3,200,000 to provide Runner Services in Puyallup, Spanaway, Tideflats, 
Ruston, and Gig Harbor for a new total not to exceed contract spending 
authority amount of $7,100,000 
 
Specialized Transportation Administrator Sarah Streiffert provided an overview of 
how the Runner Service is going, reporting that ridership has grown since inception 
of the service and ridership has stabilized since Q4 of 2024. She reviewed the 
Runner zones and advised the commissioners that Pierce Transit will be 
restructuring its Runner operations to make the system more efficient and to reduce 
wait times. This change may mean that customers may not necessarily be taken to 
point to point locations, but instead be taken to or connected with the closest fixed 
route location.

2. Update on Hardship Assistance Response Team (HART) Program 
 
Emergency Management Coordinator Rodney Chandler provided a status update on 
the roll out of the HART program. He advised that Pierce Transit will be utilizing a 
Designated Crisis Responder (DCR) contracted through Multicare to assist those 
experiencing a crisis in the Pierce Transit system. He advised the DCR is in the 
process of being onboarded. In addition to working out in the Pierce Transit system, 
the DCR will provide training to Pierce Transit staff so they can recognize the signs 
of a person in crisis and teach de-escalation skills.  
 
Mr. Chandler advised that the HART vehicle is operationalized and that the program 
will not serve the City of Tacoma as they have their own behavioral health tax. He 
advised this program is a new concept for transit and staff is working hard to 
operationalize it.
