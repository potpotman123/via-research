4.1.4 
LABOR RELATIONS; EMPLOYMENT RESPONSIBILITIES 
  
 
(a) Employer of Record. The SERVICE PROVIDER (and/or the Local Operator) is the sole employer 
of all personnel performing the services and is exclusively responsible for wages, benefits, 
withholdings, and compliance with all applicable laws (including FLSA, Title VII, ADA, FMLA, 
NLRA, OSHA, E-Verify/immigration, and—if applicable—CDL/FMCSR, FTA Drug & Alcohol, 49 
C.F.R. Part 655, and PTASP/SMS, 49 C.F.R. Part 673). 
(b) Collective Bargaining / Employment Agreements. Any proposed collective bargaining 
agreement, employment agreement, side letter, MOA, or amendment that materially affects cost, 
staffing, service levels, or compliance shall be submitted to the OWNER for prior written approval 
before execution. No such agreement may (i) bind or name the City as a party, (ii) create City 
obligations or third-party beneficiary rights, or (iii) restrict the City’s rights to procure, transition, or 
operate the Transit System, except to the extent required by applicable law (including 49 U.S.C. § 
5333(b)). 
(c) Cost Control. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall not agree to any term that would increase costs 
beyond the approved budget or Annual Not-to-Exceed (NTE) without the OWNER’s prior written 
amendment. Any unapproved increase is at the SERVICE PROVIDER’s sole expense. 
(d) Notice and Contingency Planning. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall immediately, and in no 
event later than twenty-four (24) hours, notify the OWNER in writing upon learning of any actual or 
threatened labor dispute, work stoppage/slowdown, picketing, strike, lockout, or grievance that may 
delay or disrupt performance. Within forty-eight (48) hours (or sooner if requested), the SERVICE 
PROVIDER shall provide a written contingency plan acceptable to the OWNER to maintain required 
service levels. 
(e) Subcontractors. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall flow down the requirements of this Agreement 
to all tiers of subcontractors and remains fully responsible for their compliance. 
(f) Indemnity. To the extent caused by the acts or omissions of the SERVICE PROVIDER or its 
subcontractors, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the City from 
claims, penalties, and liabilities arising out of employment or labor matters, except to the extent caused 
by the City’s negligence or willful misconduct.

(a) Notice and Meeting. If the OWNER identifies performance issues with the General Manager  
  
(“GM”), the OWNER may notify the SERVICE PROVIDER in writing. Within five (5) business days  
  
of notice, the SERVICE PROVIDER shall meet with the OWNER’s representative and, within ten  
  
(10) business days, submit a written Corrective Action Plan (CAP) with specific measures and  
  
deadlines. 
 
  
(b) Immediate Removal for Cause. For issues involving safety, security, harassment, fraud, willful  
  
misconduct, or similar material concerns, the OWNER may require immediate removal of the GM. 
 
  
(c) Interim and Permanent Replacement. If removal is required or the CAP is rejected or not met,

4.2         OWNER/SERVICE PROVIDER REPRESENTATIVES 
4.2.1 
OWNER’s Representative. The Executive Director of Finance (or successor title) is the OWNER’s 
authorized representative for this Agreement (the “OWNER’s Representative”). The OWNER may 
change the OWNER’s Representative by written notice to the SERVICE PROVIDER. The OWNER’s 
Representative is the primary point of contact and may issue written directions, approvals, and 
determinations required under this Agreement; however, the OWNER’s Representative has no 
authority to modify this Agreement, increase compensation, extend time, or issue change orders except 
as expressly authorized in writing by the OWNER. All notices, submittals, and requests for approval 
shall be directed to the OWNER’s Representative (with copies as the OWNER may designate). In an 
operational emergency, email authorization from the OWNER’s Representative may be relied upon to 
proceed up to a stated not-to-exceed amount, subject to formal confirmation within ten (10) business 
days.

4.2.3 
PERSONNEL QUALIFICATIONS; KEY PERSONNEL CHANGES 
(a) The SERVICE PROVIDER shall ensure that all personnel (including subcontractor personnel)
assigned to this Agreement are properly qualified, licensed/certified, trained, and capable of
performing their assigned duties in compliance with this Agreement and all applicable laws and
regulations.
(b) Key Personnel. The SERVICE PROVIDER’s Key Personnel (as identified in the accepted
Proposal) are subject to the OWNER’s prior written approval.
(c) Changes/Substitutions. The SERVICE PROVIDER shall not reassign, replace, or substitute any
Key Personnel without the OWNER’s prior written approval (not unreasonably withheld). For
planned changes, provide at least fifteen (15) days’ prior written notice with résumés and
qualifications of proposed replacements. For unplanned separations, provide immediate written
notice, assign a qualified interim within ten (10) business days, and propose a permanent
replacement within sixty (60) days.
(d) Background/Safety Compliance. All safety-sensitive personnel, as defined by FTA regulations
and in §2.1(Q), shall satisfy background screening, motor-vehicle record checks, and pre-
employment and ongoing drug and alcohol testing consistent with 49 C.F.R. Part 655. The
