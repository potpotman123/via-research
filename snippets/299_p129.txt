E. Describe any green or environmental initiatives or policies.
At Via, our commitment to sustainability is deeply embedded in our technology solutions
and operational practices. Our powerful algorithms and operator tools empower partners
to create efficient, data-driven transit networks that significantly reduce vehicle miles
traveled (VMT) through smarter routing and better aggregation of passengers. These
optimizations not only decrease reliance on high carbon-emitting vehicle trips but also
expand access to essential mobility services. We also support the integration of electric
and hybrid vehicles into transit fleets, further promoting cleaner transportation options.
Moreover, our integrated platforms, including Citymapper and Remix, significantly 
enhance our environmental initiatives. Citymapper offers users real-time data on public 
transit, cycling, and walking routes, encouraging greener travel choices. Meanwhile, 
Remix supports transit planning and service optimization, allowing agencies to design 
routes that maximize ridership while minimizing environmental impact. Together, these 
integrated solutions create a comprehensive approach to sustainable mobility that 
benefits both communities and the environment.

transportation technology system, offering a unique and fully integrated end-to-end
public transit solution for OMNIA participating entities. Our comprehensive software and
