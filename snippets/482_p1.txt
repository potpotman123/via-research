SECTION B:  CONTRACT TYPE, SUPPLIES OR SERVICES AND 
PRICE/COST 
B.1
The Office of Contracting and Procurement (OCP), on behalf of the District Department of 
Transportation (DDOT), seeks a Contractor to provide a cloud-based multimodal street design 
software for use by engineering and non-technical professionals, that combines bus route 
priority, cycling infrastructure, pedestrian initiatives, geospatial analysis, and stakeholder 
engagement, to design safer streets and to manage shared mobility to support the District’s 
Vision Zero initiative to end traffic fatalities by 2025.  The software must create corridor-level 
street designs that integrate planning-level cross sections to aid the design of safer streets. 
B.2
The District contemplates award of a Fixed Firm Price in accordance with Title 27 DCMR 
Section 2402.1 
B.3
AGGREGATE GROUP OR INDIVIDUAL ITEM

by “Aggregate Award Group” herein. Bidder must quote unit prices on each item within each

basis.
