O. 
Subcontractors 
 
For the avoidance of doubt, none of the following shall be deemed to constitute 
subcontractors or subcontractors of CONTRACTOR hereunder: (i) independent 
contractor driver partners of CONTRACTOR; (ii) any vehicle supply partner of 
CONTRACTOR offering vehicle leasing options to such driver partners; or (iii) any 
third-party technology vendor offering solutions integrated by CONTRACTOR into its 
technology solution or otherwise used by CONTRACTOR. 
 
P. 
Termination for Payment Default 
 
If the CITY fails to make a payment to CONTRACTOR when due under the Scope of 
Services, the CONTRACTOR may terminate this Contract for default if the CITY has 
failed to cure such default for at least 15 days after being notified of such default by the 
CONTRACTOR.  
 
If the Services have reached the CITY’s budget, as set forth in Section II below, and no 
additional funding has been obtained, CONTRACTOR may immediately cease all 
Services and terminate this Contract by written notice to the CITY. 
 
Q. 
Cooperative Contracting (Assignability Clause) 
 
In the Proposal, Contractor agreed to cooperative contracting.  Any public agency (i.e., 
city, district, public authority, public agency, municipality, and other political subdivision 
or any FTA-funded entity) in Texas shall have the option of participating in this Contract 
at the same prices, terms, and conditions, except that the Parties may negotiate the scope 
of work and service parameters and other contract terms, other than unit pricing, unless 
service parameters and scope of work are determined to be materially different by 
Contractor),     as may be agreed upon by the participating public agency and Contractor.  
CITY reserves the right to assign all or any portion of the services awarded under this 
Contract including option quantities to other public agencies under this provision.  Once 
assigned the participating public agency will enter into its own contract with Contractor 
and will be solely responsible to Contractor.  City’s right of assignment will remain in 
force until completion of the Contract to include options, whichever occurs first.  City 
shall incur no financial responsibility in connection with contracts issued by another 
public agency.  The public agency shall accept sole responsibility for placing orders or 
payments to Contractor.   
 
II. 
COMPENSATION TO CONTRACTOR

CITY agrees to pay CONTRACTOR the amounts set forth in the Scope of Services, not to 
exceed $26,247,642 (i.e., the total amount of funding obtained by the City as of the date 
hereof) in the aggregate for the initial two-year period of this Contract, subject to the

B. 
Additional Funding 
 
CITY anticipates it may receive or provide additional funding for the Services to be used 
to expand the service area into neighboring municipalities.  CITY also anticipates that 
CONTRACTOR will collect additional service revenue pursuant to Section IV(C), below.  
If the CITY enters into an interlocal agreement with other municipalities to expand the 
service area into those municipalities, and the City receives or provides additional funding 
to be used for such service area expansion, it may use those funds and any service revenue 
collected pursuant to Section IV(C), to purchase on-demand service and/or operating hours 
in accordance with the pricing set forth in Exhibit A. The total cost of the contract, 
including the additional on-demand service and/or operating hours used to expand the 
service area, shall not exceed $4,000,000 for the two-year term of this Contract.  Additional 
on-demand service hours and/or operating hours shall not be added to the service pursuant 
to this paragraph until the City Manager or his designee provides written approval of 
additional hours and funding for service area expansion, and the addition of on-demand 
service hours and/or operating hours and funding is approved by the Arlington City Council 
if required by the Federal, State, or Private funding source. 
 
C. 
Service Revenue 
 
CITY and CONTRACTOR covenant and agree that service revenue collected at the time 
of service shall be collected by the CONTRACTOR through a separate payment processor 
subaccount to be held in trust for the benefit of the CITY’s On-Demand Rideshare Service.  
CONTRACTOR shall provide monthly statements detailing how much service revenue 
was collected for the month, how the service revenue is being applied to the project and 
any expenditures made and providing the remaining balance of the subaccount.  During the 
term of the Renewal and Modification, the following distributions shall be made: 
 
100% of all service revenue collected shall be retained by the CONTRACTOR and used 
as a funding source for the Initial Contract Amount in Section II.A.  Service revenue shall 
be applied towards Contractor fees, defined in Exhibit “A” Section 3, on a monthly basis.  
Provided however, CITY shall have final determination on how the service revenue is used. 
Service revenue collected and retained by the CONTRACTOR shall not be used or 
distributed without written approval of the City Manager or his designee.  
 
CONTRACTOR estimates that it will collect $4,263,964 in service revenue, specifically for 
the Standard Via Service, during the two-year term of this Contract, which will be retained 
by the CONTRACTOR for the purposes of growing the On-Demand Rideshare Service.

D. 
Advertising Revenue 
 
CONTRACTOR shall provide monthly reports detailing the amount of advertising 
revenue collected for the month and any fees and/or expenses due in respect of the 
advertising solution procured as further detailed in Exhibit A.  During the term of the 
Contract, the following distributions shall be made: 
 
100% of all advertising revenue collected net of fees and expenses due in respect of the 
advertising solution procured shall be distributed to the CITY on a monthly basis as a 
credit to any amounts owed to CONTRACTOR; provided that 1) the CITY 
acknowledges that advertising revenue collected (net of fees and expenses) in a particular 
month shall be credited to the CITY in the invoice for the month in which 
CONTRACTOR is paid by any third-party advertising platform and 2) all advertising 
revenue credited to the CITY shall offset the not to exceed Initial Contract Amount by 
the same amount.       
 
III. 
PARTNERSHIPS IN THE SERVICE AREA 
 
From time to time, CONTRACTOR may enter into partnerships with organizations in the 
Service Area to enable such organization’s clients or customer to book trips through the 
same rider application used for the Service.  The CONTRACTOR shall be allowed to 
enter into such partnerships so long as the CITY provides prior written approval (e-mail 
being sufficient).  Any fee collected from the client or customer in respect of the rides 
(whether per hour or per ride) will be treated as Service Revenue. 
 
IV. 
TERM 
 
 
This Contract shall commence on December 11, 2022, upon receipt of written 
Notice to Proceed from the City, unless otherwise mutually agreed by both parties, and 
shall continue to December 31, 2024, unless terminated earlier in accordance herewith.  
Upon City Council approval and mutual agreement of the parties, this Contract may be 
extended for up to four (4) two-year periods, not to exceed a total of ten (10) years of 
service overall, at payment terms to be mutually agreed. 
 
V. 
INDEMNIFICATION BY CITY
