Proposed action 
That the Metropolitan Council authorize the Regional Administrator to negotiate and execute 
Contract 24P233 with Remix Technologies, LLC, for transit planning tools in the amount not to 
exceed $1,160,897. 
Background 
Metro Transit Service Development and other departments use specialized software to plan 
scenarios for fixed route transit, micro transit, and streetscape design.  
A  Request for Proposals was issued November 8, 2024. A pre-proposal meeting was held on 
November 20, 2024 that outlined the solicitation requirements, discussed project specifications and 
responded to plan holder inquires. There were fifteen registered plan holders for this project, 
twelve prime proposers, three plan rooms, and four plan holders identified as minority, woman, 
veteran, small or disadvantaged business enterprises. The Council received three proposals on  
December 30, 2024 to be evaluated for consideration of award. 
In the review of the proposals, six criteria were used in descending order of importance: Quality of 
the proposal; Qualifications/Experience of the proposer; Solution fit and technical approach; Key 
personnel; Responsiveness; and Price. Price was approximately equal in importance to a 
combination of all other criteria.   
The evaluation panel reached consensus that the proposal submitted by Remix Technologies, 
LLC, was most advantageous to the Council and recommend for award. 
Rationale 
The execution of a contract for professional and technical services exceeding $500,000 requires 
Council approval.

Thrive lens analysis 
On Feb. 12, 2025, the Council adopted Imagine 2050, which builds on policy direction in Thrive 
MSP 2040. This contract supports the Thrive outcome of Stewardship by providing tools that 
Planners can use to model future transit investments and estimate resource costs, thereby 
exercising good stewardship over public resources. This contract also supports the Thrive outcome 
of Equity by providing tools to help Planners measure the benefits and impacts of service changes 
on historically disadvantaged communities. 
Funding 
Funding for this contract will be provided under Service Development’s operating budget. 
Small business inclusion 
The Office of Equity and Equal Opportunity (OEEO) thoroughly reviewed this procurement for 
Metropolitan Council Underutilized Business (MCUB) opportunities in accordance with federal and 
state laws and regulations as well as contract specifications.  Upon conclusion of OEEO’s research 
and analysis, no MCUB goal was set.
