Description of good or service requested:
Contract services to extend the TART Connect Micro Transit pilot program for a third
and final year.
Unique features/qualifications required that are not available from another supplier:
Tahoe Downtowner has been operating the program for the first two years of the pilot
program. The TART Board decided to extend the pilot program by an additional year to
gather more information before a competitive solicitation is released.
Were other options sought?
• If yes, why were they unacceptable? If no, why were alternatives not reviewed?
The original contract was not competitively bid because Placer County, the primary
contract manager did not pursue a competitve bid. After the final year of pilot program,
the TART plans to competitively bid the program with jursidictions included.
What efforts were made to determine there was no competition for the service or good and how was it
determined the price for the purchase is fair and reasonable?
The price was determined to be fair and reasonable due to the FY23/24 program
estimated based on ridership in Zone 3 of the program space. The increased costs
associated with the year 2 hours extension and additional stops is divided among
funders and apportioned by population.
