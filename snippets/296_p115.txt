Via has trained tens of thousands of system
users and drivers across our global deployments,
and each of our customers receives customized
training to suit individual needs. These trainings
include live workshops and hands-on
experiences in the classroom and in the field, as
well as suites of written documentation and
online / virtual sessions. Our training is always included as standard in all our offerings,
and training is provided by Via employees. No additional costs apply in order to access
our training program, and we are always happy to provide refresher training, new-hire
training, and other updates to our customers free of charge. Below
we have outlined the details of our training programs for all
relevant staff who interact with our platform.
Driver Training 
All Via drivers are taken through a rigorous training program to 
ensure that they are fully prepared to deliver safe and high quality 
on-demand transit service. Our structured driver and staff training 
program ensures compliance with relevant safety regulations and 
customer performance expectations. We offer multiple classroom 
programs to cover the essentials of providing safe and reliable 
service, such as comprehensive training regarding the Driver App,
