NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE
CITY OF KEY WEST, FLORIDA, AS FOLLOWS:
Section 1:
That the attached "Key West Transit Software
as a Service Deployment Service Order" between the City and Via
Transportation, is hereby approved in an amount not to exceed
$248,880.00, pursuant to Code of Ordinances Section 2-797(4), best
interests of the City.
Section 2:
This purchase will be funded with anticipated
Federal Grant Funding through the "Enhancing Mobility Innovation
Program" in an amount of up to $250,000.00. Such funding will be
matched up to 20% using the FY22 budget allocation for on-demand
transit in the amount of $45,000.00, and supplemental funding as
necessary from the Transit Alternative Fund.
