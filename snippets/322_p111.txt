1.5 Understanding of Project and Requirements
We understand that Hillsborough County is seeking a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service ՄSaaSՅ
Paratransit Scheduling Software System for their Sunshine Line service. This software system will
include client-facing applications and in-vehicle mobile data computers ՄMDCs) and encompass
various functionalities, including client eligibility management, trip reservations (both advance and
real-time), bus pass issuance, manual and fully automated trip scheduling, dispatch, automatic
vehicle tracking ՄAVLՅ, billing, and reporting. Through this system, the County aims to replace their
existing Routematch scheduling software and MDCs, convert existing client and trip data to this
new platform, and benefit from proposed process and technology enhancements that align with
the County’s objectives.
Based on the RFP’s described project and envisioned system, we are excited to propose our
advanced software solution, configured to meet the Sunshine Line’s operational goals,
accompanied by comprehensive partner support to ensure the long-term success of the Sunshine
Line. Throughout this proposal, we draw on our vast experience performing seamless Routematch
software replacements and serving large-scale paratransit and Transportation Disadvantaged
program partners in Florida and beyond.

2. Experience
2.1 Steps to Ensure Project Completion
We at Via have a deep understanding and appreciation for the operational and human
considerations key to implementing paratransit scheduling software. Through our on-the-ground
experience working with riders, drivers, and dispatchers, we have come to understand both the
intricate and holistic needs of transit software users. We have delved into every component of our
partners’ transportation systems, digitizing solutions and integrations for paratransit, on-demand,
social services, and fixed-route transportation. In doing so, we have gradually built up a software
solution for integrated mobility and a one-stop-shop for service planning and delivery.
To ensure the successful completion of this project in a timely manner with RFP requirements met,
we will employ our tried and tested implementation practices (please refer to Section 3.7ր
Implementation Plan for details) based on our detailed scoping of all requirements (each addressed
throughout Section 3ր Technical Approach).
Additionally, in both software configuration and project implementation, Via’s teams take a
comprehensive approach to risk management, putting various checks in place to ensure we deliver
high-quality, on-time deliverables for each component of the project. Below, we outline five
common risks associated with projects similar to the County’s own paratransit
Routematch-replacement service and highlight our proven risk mitigation strategies for each:
●
Shift to updated technology. Via recognizes that the introduction of new technology may
be unfamiliar and confusing for users who are accustomed to the previous Routematch
system. Via has accumulated specific expertise, both virtually and in-person, in helping
administrators and members of the public acclimate to the intuitive Via platform. For
example, in Montana, Via transitioned Blackfeet Transit’s service from Routematch and
grew ridership by 162%, with over half of the service’s ridership being accessibility
riders. Our success with the transition and expansion of services like this one goes to
show our proficiency in evolving services from Routematch and other legacy providers to
the Via system.
●
Data collection and consistency. Risks related to Via’s standard methods of data
collection are quite low — potential risks come with the acquisition and use of data from
existing software providers and other third-party sources. These risks can be mitigated by
understanding the quality of available data and the speed at which it can be collected early
in the project schedule. We have significant experience migrating data from Routematch
and, by working on the ground with the County, we will seek to gain an understanding of
the quality of the County’s data early on and develop a plan for mitigating any data
collection and migration risks.
