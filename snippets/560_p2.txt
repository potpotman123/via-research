Department:  
Transportation 
 
 
 
 
 
Title: 2023-963 Request for Proposals Award for Mobility-On-Demand Software 
App (B) 
 
 
Department: Transportation 
 
Description: Request for the City Commission to award the Request for Proposal to the 
top ranked firm, Via Transportation, Inc., for Mobility-On-Demand Software App, which 
will assist the City in improving the quality of the Mobility-On-Demand service. 
 
Fiscal Note: This project’s implementation and first year costs are funded in the 
Federal Transit Administration (FTA) FY22 Supergrant; the remaining four years will be 
budgeted in the RTS Planning Contractual Services budget.  The five-year project cost 
is $139,000.00.   
 
 
Explanation: 
On April 3, 2023, the Department of Financial Services - Procurement Division solicited 
a Request for Proposal (RFP) for a Mobility-On-Demand Software App. The Mobility-
On-Demand Software App will assist the City in enhancing the quality of services for the 
microtransit Mobility On-Demand Service currently operating in east Gainesville. The 
RFP process was implemented in compliance with, and as required by, Federal Transit 
Administration (FTA), Florida Statutes and City of Gainesville policies. 
 
The City received ten (10) responses to the RFP but determined one to be non-
responsive.  An evaluation team consisting of City staff evaluated and ranked the 
remaining nine (9) proposals as follows:  
 
1. *Via Transportation, Inc. 
2. *Spare Labs, Inc. 
3. *HBSS Connect Corp. 
4. *RideCo, Inc. 
5. *TransLoc, Inc.
=== page 2 ===
6. Downtowner Holdings LLC 
7. Moovit, Inc. 
8. Pantonium, Inc. 
9. Software Staffers 
 
Five bidders (indicated with*) submitted redacted proposals. The redacted information in 
the submittals is claimed by bidder to be confidential and/or proprietary and cannot be 
made public. 
 
The top ranked firm selected by the Evaluation Committee is Via Transportation, Inc.

☒ Goal 3: A Great Place to Live and Experience 
☐ Goal 4: Resilient Local Economy 
☒ Goal 5: “Best in Class” Neighbor Services 
 
Recommendation: 
The City Commission: 1) approve the Request for Proposal for a Mobility-On-Demand 
Software App rankings, 2) award the Request for Proposal to Via Transportation, Inc., 
and 3) authorize the City Manager or designee to negotiate and execute a contract with 
the top ranked firm, subject to approval by the City Attorney as to form and legality.
