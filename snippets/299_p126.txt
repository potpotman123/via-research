Via’s Incident Management Operations (IMO) team, which is staffed 24/7, completes all 
investigations promptly. When an L3 or L4 incident occurs, the IMO or the Project Lead 
will notify the customer within 24 hours and will subsequently provide an in-depth 
readout after the investigation is completed. Following each investigation, an IMO team 
member presents a recommendation for next steps, and a team leader reviews and either 
approves or adjusts that recommendation. Via uses a points-based system to evaluate a 
driver’s safety and customer service record. Each incident has an associated point and 
drivers are subject to refresher training and disciplinary action when they reach certain 
points thresholds. Disciplinary action could range from a formal warning to termination.

12. Account Management Features
i. Describe options on how individuals will be billed for the products, services,
and solutions being offered. Is this individually or monthly?
Individuals can pay for their trips directly through the Rider App or over the phone using a
variety of payment methods, including credit or debit cards, digital transit passes,
subscription passes, cash cards, or vouchers. Via will handle all management of data, the
implementation of a Participating Agency’s desired fare structure, and validation of
payments and reporting.
Typically, Via suggests implementing a fare structure for on-demand transit services that 
aligns with the Participating Agency’s fixed-route fare. Matching the existing fare helps 
maintain consistency and affordability for riders while 
promoting seamless integration with the broader network. We 
are happy to work with customers to refine the fare level as 
needed based on local feedback and ridership trends. 
We support a variety of pricing models and fare structures that 
can be tailored to meet our customer’s needs. Examples of 
potential configurations include: 
●
Discounted Fares: Via can charge different fares for
individuals eligible for customer-specific discounts,
such as students, seniors, and riders with disabilities,

ii. Describe receipt retrieval.
Individual riders are able to retrieve receipts by accessing their Rider Profile directly in the
Rider App. This unique Profile contains details about Trip History, including payment
method and amount.
For customers that require insight into fare revenue, Via’s fare system is designed to 
ensure accurate accounting and provide full transparency. All fare revenue is tracked 
within Via’s system, which maintains detailed records for each transaction, including 
payments from credit cards, cash-based vouchers, and promotional credits. Via employs 
strict internal controls to collect and secure fares, ensuring compliance with industry 
standards. Fare revenue can be credited directly back to customers, typically on a 
monthly basis, and all fare records will be readily available for audit by the customer upon 
request.
