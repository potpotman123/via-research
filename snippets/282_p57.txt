B. Invoicing 
 
The Fees shown above will vary month-to-month and be invoiced on a cost-per-hour basis (for 
on-demand service hours actually provided), or otherwise as outlined in subsection A above. 
 
Contractor shall invoice the City monthly on or about the 15th of each month, starting on or about 
January 15, 2023, to include fees for the previous calendar month.  
 
Invoices shall include: 
● Costs of services provided during previous calendar month; 
● Dates of service work;  
● Any necessary reconciliations of charges or payments from previous calendar 
month(s), if applicable; 
● Amount of any Service Revenue and Advertising Revenue collected during the 
invoice period; and 
● Amount of Service Revenue and Advertising Revenue applied to invoice balance. 
 
The following information should be submitted with each invoice as supporting documentation: 
● Documentation of any charges on the invoice, including but not limited to amount 
of driver tips, record of van hours, and cleaning costs for the month; 
● Detailed list of all rides taken with any promotional campaign or other special 
ride program for the month;  
● The following information regarding the Arlington Student Transportation 
Program, as described in Section 10: 
○ The total number of rides taken using Student Promo Codes each month; 
○ The total Ride Cost, as defined in section 5(c), of all rides taken using 
Student Promo Codes during the prior month; 
○ Other information regarding rides taken using Student Promo Codes, as 
agreed upon by Contractor and City, provided that such request shall not 
exceed the data sharing parameters set forth in Section 9. 
● The following information regarding the Arlington RAPID service, as described 
in Section 11: 
○ Itemized list of tasks worked on in the previous calendar month, cost of 
work performed for each task, and percent completed for each task; and 
○ Value of Via’s and May Mobility’s in-kind contribution associated with 
each task.  
● The following information regarding the Advertising services, as described in 
Section 12: 
○ Total revenue collected during the reporting period from Advertising 
services, broken out by type of advertising solution; 
○ List of expenses and fees incurred during the reporting period; and 
○ Total revenue net of fees and expenses.

4. Project Team
Contractor will take responsibility for the project management and integration of all relevant
elements of the on-demand rideshare service on a continuous basis during the course of such
service and will designate a "General Manager"
Contractor will
subsequently designate a "Partner Success Manager"
The City will designate a Project Manager to be the primary point of contact with Contractor
throughout the duration. The Contractor General Manager will be empowered to enact decisions
related to the project on the Contractor's behalf and will serve as the primary point of contact
with the City's Project Manager on an ongoing basis.
During the course of the Service, Contractor's Project Team, led by its General Manager and
Partner Success Manager, will be closely engaged with the City in order to ensure that key
deliverables are provided in a timely way, and that learnings from the Service are incorporated
into its optimization.
5. Service Scope Parameters
On an ongoing basis, Contractor shall set service scope parameters that yield a successful,
sustainable, and efficient Service.
The service scope parameters will be updated from time to time, and ultimately be determined by
both learnings generated about demand patterns and economics over time, as well as the level of
City financial resources available for the Service over the year. Based on these factors, the
Contractor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to operate a service that delivers upon the
City's goals and makes the most of the resources available for the project.
During the Contract term, the Contractor shall use commercially reasonable efforts so that the
service scope parameters be as follows:
A. Service Days/Hours: Monday through Saturday excluding holidays, for which a mutual
decision whether the service should operate during such days will be made between the
City and Contractor. The service will operate from 6am to 9pm, Monday through Friday,
and 9am to 9pm on Saturdays. Service Days and Hours may be changed or extended at
City's request and mutual agreement between the City and Contractor.
B. Geographic Coverage: The service area for the Contract term will be the area noted as the
Service Area in Section I(G) of the Contract. The service area may be revised upon
request of the City and mutual agreement between the City and Contractor.
