We initially limited our outreach to firms physically located in the Mobile area; if no Mobile-based
firms existed for a particular scope of work, we widened our search radius to include the whole
state of Alabama. We began sending these solicitation emails on July 9. We sent follow up emails
to all firms that did not respond to our initial solicitation within 2 days. After 4 days, if we did not
receive responses, we expanded our search to include firms based in other states. A list of firms
that we contacted about these opportunities can be found in Exhibit 4.
Follow up With Interested Firms
Of the 28 firms contacted about this procurement, we received responses from 9 interested
firms. We responded to all firms that indicated interest in the project via email. We sent over
scopes of work to firms that we had pre-existing relationships with, and scheduled introductory
conversations with firms that were new to us. Following these conversations, firms that were still
interested in the project were sent scopes of work, and asked to provide price estimates.
Provision of Adequate Information
Interested firms were sent the scopes of work attached in Exhibit 5. Firms that requested it were
also sent copies of the RFP and addenda. Any questions the firms had about these documents
were answered promptly over email.
Good Faith Effort Negotiation
We asked all interested firms to provide information on their pricing, as well as some background
materials on their firm. We received these materials from 6 firms - 3 for community engagement,
1 for uniform provision, 1 for janitorial services, and 1 for printing. Through evaluation of these
materials, and through subsequent negotiations with interested firms, we were able to secure
partnerships with 4 highly qualified firms that we believe are exceptionally well-suited to
carrying out these scopes of work effectively.
1. JCM & Associates - Uniform Provision
Blue Goose Uniforms is an AL UCP-certified DBE firm that specializes in providing
uniforms and promotional products for a range of industries. Blue Goose has more than
30 years of experience helping partners enhance their brand identity and company
culture by providing high-quality branded uniform apparel, backed by an unmatched
commitment to service and personal care.
2. C.E.R.M. - Community Engagement
C.E.R.M. is one of the largest minority-owned, professionally licensed, environmental,
engineering, and program management firms in the Southeastern United States. Carla
Davis, the project lead, has many years of experience in providing community
engagement services in the Mobile area, and excels in building strong community
relationships, translating technical information into accessible materials, and supporting
clients with detail-oriented, solutions-driven work.
3. Fine Printing - Printing Services
Fine Printing is a woman-owned AL DOT certified DBE that has been delivering quality
products to satisfied customers for over 25 years. They specialize in providing full color
printing as well as graphic art and design services.

industry. Specializing in providing commercial and industrial services to customers
