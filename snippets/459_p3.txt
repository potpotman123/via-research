METRO MICRO SERVICES
ISSUE
The previous MicroTransit Pilot Program – Part B was awarded in 2020 and expired 
on March 31, 2025. 
On March 16, 2025, Metro Micro (formerly MicroTransit) service operations 
transitioned to new contractors; Crosby Street Transit LLC (aka Via) as the 
operations contractor and Spare Labs, Inc. as the software provider under a new
operating model.
=== page 4 ===
TRANSITION SUMMARY
Employee Impacts:
•
All 145 Metro Micro Operators successfully transitioned to the new 
Contractor or became Part-Time Bus Operators.
•
All 16 Supervisors were transferred to Bus operations.
Rider Impacts – No Disruption
•
Seamless service transition with no disruption.
•
Over 10,000 registered riders were migrated to the new software, 
ensuring a smooth and continuous user experience.
