We are happy to offer a 2% discount for every item in our catalog to Participating Public

3. Are shipping charges, freight charges, return fees, or
restocking fees applicable?
Shipping charges, freight charges, return fees, and restocking fees are not applicable to 
Via’s solutions. 
4. How does the vendor ensure contract pricing?
i. How can customers verify that they are getting the agreed-upon contract
prices?
All Via solutions are sold directly through Via or a wholly-owned subsidiary; we do not sell
through third parties (i.e. resellers, distributors). The prices listed will be available
immediately to purchasing members should Via be awarded a contract as the result of the
On-Demand Transportation RFP.
All invoices automatically go through an approval workflow within our invoicing system 
before they are sent to customers. Approvers will review the pricing within the contracts 
to verify consistency with the agreed-upon contract prices before approving in the 
system and submitting for payment. 
ii. Describe your pricing for each service and how it will be auditable by
Participating Public Agencies.
We have significant experience working with public agencies, as more than 90% of our
revenue currently comes from public sector customers. We take a transparent approach
to ensure that our customers have confidence that our pricing is fair and reasonable. For

5. Are there additional discounts or rebates for larger orders
or other factors?
Our pricing catalog includes tiered pricing with volume-based discounts for Via software 
and Remix products. For Participating Public Agencies purchasing turnkey operations, we 
create a bespoke pricing proposal based on the service specifics such as operating 
hours, location, vehicle type, driver requirements, etc., and our quote will typically be 
more favorable for larger-scale purchases. 
6. What are the available ordering methods?
Via operates without distributors, dealers, or resellers. We rely on our highly specialized 
sales team in North America, led by Mike Vaccarino, Chief Revenue Officer. Mike brings 
extensive experience in launching new products and fostering partnerships with 
government and municipal clients.  
Mike and his team will be the primary contacts for all Region 4 and OMNIA participating 
entities, guiding them through every step of our tailored ordering process. They are 
backed by a dedicated launch team of service design and implementation specialists. 
This team manages critical aspects of the service launch, including driver recruitment, 
testing, dry runs, service configuration, localization, and ensuring a seamless transition 
for all partners. 
7. What is the invoicing process?
Via’s Finance team will align on invoicing cadence and format with each participating 
agency. Typically, Via bills our customers with Net 30 payment terms. The minimum fees, 
which are agreed upon on a case-by-case basis, are paid annually upfront. Any usage 
beyond the minimum is billed monthly in arrears. As part of our invoicing package, we 
provide details about usage during the prior month (e.g., active vehicles or vehicle hours).

We suggest periodic (annual) price adjustment, and we reserve the right to adjust prices
