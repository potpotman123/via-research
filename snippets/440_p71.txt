8) Co-Branding with Service Provider
• The City Marks are primary on all rider-facing materials. The Service Provider's mark may appear as
secondary and smaller (≤50% of the width of THE WAVE wordmark) and must not dominate.
• Approved mockups (if any) will be provided by the City. No new mockups without approval.
9) Vehicles, Facilities, and Field Items
• Buses/Paratransit: City-approved livery templates; logo placement on sides and rear; fleet numbers clear of
the City Marks.
• Stops/Shelters: Consistent header band and route identification; ADA signage and tactile elements as
applicable.
• Uniforms/Badges: City-approved embroidery/patch; no unapproved colorways.
• Printed Materials: Use approved templates for schedules, rider alerts, and maps.
10) Digital, Domains, and Social
• Websites/Apps: Titles and store listings must identify THE WAVE as a service of the City of Mobile.
• Accounts: All domains, social handles, and app store accounts including the City Marks must be registered
to and owned by the City. Access may be delegated to the Service Provider.
• Email/SMS Templates: Use approved signatures and headers. Follow opt-out and record-retention rules.
11) Accessibility & Legibility
• Meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios for text and essential graphics on digital and printed
rider-facing materials.
• Ensure large-format signage can be read at typical approach distances (e.g., 1 inch of letter height per 25
feet).
12) Trademark Notices & Attribution
• Use TM/® as directed by the City (registration status may vary).
• Include ownership notice where practical: "THE WAVE is a service of the City of Mobile."
13) Approvals & Quality Control
• Pre-approval required for: vehicle livery, facility signage, uniforms, website/app branding, press releases,
and new domains/handles.
• Routine rider notices consistent with approved templates are deemed approved.
• The City may require correction of non-conforming uses within 5 business days or immediate takedown for
safety/reputation concerns.
14) Asset Requests & Version Control
• Asset requests and approvals sent to: [brand@cityofmobile.org] (or designee).
• Maintain a single source of truth for artwork in a City-owned repository. Use only current "Approved" files.
15) Transition & Wind-Down
• On expiration/termination: cease new use; remove/de-identify Marks within 30 days; return native design
files; transfer credentials and control of all City-branded accounts; withdraw any DBA filings using THE
WAVE; and follow rider-notification instructions provided by the City.
16) Legal Cross-Reference
These Guidelines supplement and are governed by the Agreement's trademark license (Marks clause) and IP
provisions. In case of conflict, the Agreement controls.

Dear Mr. Laurence,
Via is committed to achieving the City of Mobile, AL's diversity goals as outlined in their RFP. We
understand and agree with the importance of creating opportunities for economic growth for
disadvantaged business communities.
Via specializes in providing full-service transit solutions for our partners, and our offering
includes the entirety of the City's stated scope of work. Because of this, we are very intentional
when choosing to partner with third parties, as subcontracting services that we would normally
handle in-house can significantly increase costs for our partners. Additionally, whenever
possible, our preference is to work with local subcontractors, as it allows us to learn from their
local expertise, and to provide business opportunities in the communities that we are serving.
The fact that there were very few DBEs in the mobile area that performed services that we would
typically consider subcontracting presented an additional challenge.
However, we recognize the importance of ensuring opportunity for historically disadvantaged
businesses, and understand that DBE participation in this project is a priority for the City. At the
time of submission, we are able to meet 1.19% of the City's 3.6% DBE goal. In order to facilitate
DBE participation in this project, we conducted a significant identification, outreach, and mutual
planning effort to enlist DBEs for this project. A summary of these efforts can be found below:
Select Portions of Scope of Work to be Subcontracted
Upon receiving this solicitation, we performed a comprehensive review of the scope of work as
outlined in the RFP to identify portions of the work that could be effectively subcontracted.
Through this review, we identified cleaning of service vehicles and facilities, tire provision,
community engagement, vehicle wrapping, uniform provision, and printing as scopes of work
where DBE participation would be both cost effective and add meaningful value to our delivery
of the service.
We also sought to separate scopes into smaller units to facilitate DBE participation - we'd
typically perform vehicle and facilities cleaning in-house with utilities staff, but separated these
scopes to create an opportunity for DBE janitorial firms to provide cleaning services for the
service facilities.
Attendance at Prebid Meeting
As there was no prebid meeting for this procurement, Via did not send anyone to attend.
