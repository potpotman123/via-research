A ttac h executive summary if more space needed : In September 2020, the City and County of Denver initiated a planning study to discover where a neighborhood transit should be operated to serve community needs and improve mobility for its residents. Following a thorough review backed by community engagement and in coordination with RTD, Denver began with Montbello as the first neighborhood to pilot the Connector. In 2021, DOTI began a pilot program with Northeast Transportation Connections (NETC), a nonprofit Transportation Management Association, to provide microtransit services to the Montbello neighborhood for a 12-month fare-free period. The objective of the pilot was to provide a dynamically routed, technology enabled, fare-free microtransit service within the Montbello neighborhood. The service provided service for English and Spanish riders while utilizing a mixed fleet of electric vehicles, plug-in hybrid vehicles, and wheelchair accessible vehicles. Due to the success of the initial pilot, in 2022, a contract amendment was approved to expand microtransit services to the Gateway neoghborhood . This amendment also provided for expansion of the Connector into its second service zone in North Denver; serving Globeville, Elyria, and Swansea (GES) neighborhoods with an additional 24 months added to the original contract. The initial pilot contract &amp; amendment with NETC terms on 12/31/24. Through this new contract with Montbello Downtowner LLC, the Denver Connector Microtransit P rogram will continue in Montbello , Gateway, Globeville, Elyria-Swansea and expand services into west Denver that City staff is currently finalizing plans with the community to define. With the w est Denver launch to begin with the execution of this agreement, the Connector Program moves out of a pilot phase and into maturity with three additional years of operations contracted via this agreement. 6 . Denver Connector Microtransit is phone app driven, on-demand, point-to-point, shared ride and neighborhood- scale transit service currently operating in and serving the residents of Denver neighborhoods of Montbello , Gateway, Globeville and Elyria-Swansea. In addition to assuming the existing operations of Connector in those communities within 2 existing service zones, the contracted operator will launch a third Connector service zone within neighborhoods in West Denver upon the execution of this agreement. The Connector is free to use, with trips capable of being booked in English and Spanish languages both by phone app and via a call center. Currently, Connector is a weekday service with operating hours from 6am to 8pm, Monday through Friday. The Contractor shall provide a fully turnkey, ADA accessible service, operated and managed by its staff with a mixed fleet of gas powered and electric vehicles which are furnished, fueled and maintained by the Contractor. Additionally, the Contractor shall provide marketing and engagement to support building resident awareness of the West Denver service area.
