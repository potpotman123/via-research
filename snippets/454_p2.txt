Proprietary and Confidential Information
Via Mobility, LLC (“Via”) response and all supporting documentation, including associated exhibits
and appendices, contain confidential information exempt from disclosure under the Kansas
Statutes Annotated (“K.S.A.”) § 45-215 (the “Kansas Open Records Act”). The confidential materials
include, but are not limited to, information relating to the pricing of Via's services, back-end
application processes, proprietary algorithms, unique business methodologies, entity officer and
member details, market positioning, third party reference letters, compliance efforts, and sensitive
information on key performance indicators. This information is exempt from disclosure under
several provisions of the Kansas Open Records Act, including but not limited to K.S.A. § 45-221(33)
(as a sealed bid and information relating to such a bid); K.S.A. § 45-221(33) (as financial
information submitted by a potential contractor); K.S.A. § 45-221(18) (as proprietary plans,
designs, drawings, or specifications for a transit solution); K.S.A. § 45-221(30) (as personal
information); and K.S.A. § 45-221(1) (as information that qualifies as trade secret under K.S.A. §
60-3320(4) and similar federal laws, the disclosure of which is prohibited or restricted by federal
law, state statute or rule of the Kansas supreme court). Accordingly, we request that your office
maintain the confidentiality of Via’s response and provide Via with timely notice of any third party’s
request for these materials prior to production by contacting compliance@ridewithvia.com.
