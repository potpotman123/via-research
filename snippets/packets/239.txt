CITY OF FORT LAUDERDALE
OUTDOOR EVENT AGREEMENT
THIS AGREEMENT is made by and between:
CITY OF FORT LAUDERDALE, a municipal corporation of
the State of Florida, referred to hereinafter as "City",
and
HISTORIC DOWNTOWNER SALOON, LLC, a Florida limited
liability company, whose principal place of business is 10 S. New
River Drive East, Fort Lauderdale, Florida 33301, and who is
referred to hereinafter as "Applicant" or "Sponsor".
WHEREAS, the Applicant wishes to hold an outdoor event and has submitted an
application pursuant to the requirements of Section 15-182 of the Code of Ordinances
of the City of Fort Lauderdale, Florida; and
WHEREAS, the Applicant is willing to obtain the requisite insurance, and is
willing to indemnify and hold harmless the City of Fort Lauderdale for any damage to
persons or property that might occur during or as a result of the outdoor event; and
WHEREAS, on February 19, 2019, by Motion, the City Commission of the City of
Fort Lauderdale authorized the proper City Officials to execute this Agreement.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises made herein, the
parties agree as follows:
The foregoing recitals are true and correct, and:
Effective Date.
The Effective Date of this Agreement is the date upon which City Commission approval
is granted.
Outdoor Event.
The Applicant is permitted to operate or sponsor the "CRAB FEST" (referred to
ereinafter as the "Event") outdoors only at the location(s) and time(s) set forth in th
Hached Outdoor Event Agreement Schedule One and Site Map (if applicable). which i
attached hereto and made a part hereof.
