Dear Mayor Lesser, 
 
 
At the annual Downtown Manhattan Beach BID meeting on April 27, 2017, the Downtown BID Board of Directors 
voted unanimously to support the extension of the Downtowner pilot program and also to encourage City Council to expand the 
program service area to serve a greater cross-section of Manhattan Beach residents. The BID Board of Directors encourages the 
City to expedite the expansion of this program so that a larger service area is realized by August 2017. The BID also directed the 
DBPA to continue the financial sponsorship of one Downtowner cart throughout the BID’s fiscal year of 2017-2018. 
The Downtown BID strongly encourages City Council to expand this program and to explore funding sources that will 
enable the expansion of the service area and to operate with a suitable fleet that can reach Sepulveda Boulevard. The Advisory 
Board also requests that the City Council consider a pilot program for service east of Sepulveda Blvd. With regards to advertising, 
the BID encourages the City Council to allow alternative advertisers and to require a  “responsibility” tagline in the messaging. 
 
This Business Improvement District has been in existence since April of 1969 under the authority of the “Parking and 
Business Improvement Area Law of 1965”.  This law was restrictive with respect to the use of funds.  In 1989 the State Legislature 
adopted Senate Bill 1424, “Parking and Business Improvement Area Law of 1989”.  In 1998 a group of concerned merchants and 
a growing coalition of downtown stakeholders, developed the proposal to establish a new Downtown Manhattan Beach Business 
Improvement District (BID) under the new legislation.  In October 1998, that legislation was approved and adopted as City 
Ordinance No. 1989.  In January 1999, the BID contracted with the Downtown Manhattan Beach Business & Professional 
Association (DBPA), a 501 (c) 6 not-for-profit corporation established in 1985, to provide specific benefits to the members of the 
BID. Parking and transportation has been, and remains, the biggest concern as well as a major focus for the Downtown Manhattan 
Beach BID. 
 
Thank you for your consideration.
