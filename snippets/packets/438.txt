The OWNER and SERVICE PROVIDER agree as set forth below: 
1.1 
ENGAGEMENT 
1.11  
The OWNER hereby engages Port City Transit, LLC  (the “SERVICE PROVIDER”) as an 
independent contractor to provide full and comprehensive “turnkey” management, operations, 
maintenance, logistics, marketing and promotion, innovation planning and implementation, and 
the provision and integration of end-to-end technology platforms  for the Mobile Transit System, 
together with an integrated ride-share program, encompassing fixed-route, paratransit, and  
microtransit  services within the corporate limits of, and all annexed areas to, the City of Mobile, 
in accordance with this Agreement and Exhibit A. The Request for Proposals (RFP) dated June 
20, 2025, and (with addenda) is incorporated by reference for descriptive/background purposes 
only; in the event of conflict, this Agreement controls. 
1.2 
DESIRED RESULT 
1.2.1 
Establish a long-term engagement with the SERVICE PROVIDER that: (a) optimizes the  
management and operation of the Mobile Transit System; (b) plans and implements service and 
technology innovations that increase ridership and improve service quality; (c) delivers services 
at competitive, fair, and reasonable pricing within the City’s, pre-approved annual budget; and 
(d) provides ongoing technical support to City staff and elected officials to ensure a compliant, 
accessible, and inclusive transit.
