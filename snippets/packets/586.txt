PART I
CITY OF LARGO, FLORIDA
REQUIREMENTS, CONDITIONS and EVALUATION CRITERIA
FOR SOFTWARE, HARDWARE AND IMPLEMENTATION FOR A
ROUTING, OPTIMIZATION AND CUSTOMER SERVICE SYSTEM
RFP # 19-P-688
Purpose of Request for Proposal
The City is seeking proposals from qualified and experienced vendors to provide and maintain a cloud-based, 
real-time routing, optimization and customer service system. The technology will be used on the City’s solid 
waste vehicle fleet to:
a)
Monitor the City's Solid Waste Divisions collections operations in order to further increase operational 
efficiency;
b)
Provide route information and direction to City drivers;
c)
Optimize collections operations, routing and establish integrated systems (GIS mapping and route 
optimization);
d)
Monitor fleet and truck performance;
e)
Provide “real-time” collection data via a remote management tool;
f)
Improve customer service and driver safety; and
g)
Provide data insights to advance the City’s solid waste and diversion goals.
The data collected through the information system should lead to better forecasts for budgets, assist in 
identifying current and future routing and equipment needs, and simplify the production of monthly, quarterly, 
and annual reports. The software must be easy to deploy and able to integrate effectively with other software 
utilized by the City.
While the technology will be installed in the City’s fleet to give drivers the ability to report and flag issues in the 
field, direct interaction from drivers should not be required for most data collection. The City should be able to 
automatically collect data with little interference in day-to-day truck operations.
Proposals should include sufficient information to assess the proposing vendor’s capability to meet the 
requirements for this project.
Background
The City’s Solid Waste Division (also referred to in this RFP as the “Division”) provides full-service, automated 
municipal solid waste (MSW), automated recycling,  claw truck bulk waste, rear-load yard waste collection for 
an estimated 16,200 residential homes, front load commercial MSW and recyling service for an estimated 1861
businesses and rolloff MSW, construction and demolition (C&D), and recycling service to 220 customers . This 
is completed through a network of 121 routes that utilize 30 trucks every week. Currently, the City utilizes a 
routing and optimization system provided by WM Logistics LLC. for all residential and commercial front load 
work.  Routes are currently developed for a ten (10) hour day as drivers work four (4) days per week.  Rolloff 
routes are developed manually each day. The Solid Waste Division operates out of the City’s Public Works 
Complex located at 1000 2nd Street S.E., Largo, Florida 33779.  The Solid Waste Division also conducts 
container repair and container storage activity at 1551 Starkey Rd, Largo, Florida 33771.
In fiscal year (FY) 2018, the Solid Waste Division collected 86,196 tons of MSW, bulk waste and yard debris 
and 7,760 tons of recyclables. The Solid Waste Division employs 57 full-time employees to include drivers, 
supervisors, office support and managers. The Division’s total approved budget for FY 2019 is $14,088,400. 
Tables 1 and  2 provide an overview of the Division’s current operations and service levels.
Since 1997, the City has developed and pursued an aggressive annexation strategy to create logical borders 
and to increase service efficiencies.  This progressive growth strategy requires fundamental and dynamic 
planning tools for evaluation and re-deployment of resources and services. The Division realizes the

Collections Fleet
The solid waste collection fleet consists of:
Sixteen (16) automated residential side loaders;
Eleven (11) commercial front loaders;
Seven (7) roll offs;
Two (2) rearloaders; and
Six (6) claw trucks.
Vehicle maintenance and repairs are typically performed in-house by the City, with some work contracted out 
as needed. Communication with and among drivers and other staff occurs via two way radio system. There is 
one (1) driver per one (1) vehicle with the exception of rear loaders with one (1) driver and one (1) helper and 
one (1) commercial frontload route with one (1) driver and one (1) spotter.
Collections Service
Residential. Residential MSW collection occurs twice per week for each service address (Monday – Thursday 
or Tuesday - Friday). Residential recycling, bulk waste and yard waste collection occur once per week 
(Monday, Tuesday,  Thursday and Friday). The City offers two (2) cart sizes for residential MSW and recycling 
service (64 gallon or 95 gallon).  The 95 gallon is the predominate cart.  Bulk waste and yard waste is left at the
curb and collected as is.
The City uses RFID software to track activity and location of residential MSW and recycling carts.  RFID 
hardware and software is provided by Rehrig Pacific Company.
Commercial Front Load. Commercial front load MSW collection occurs up to six (6) days a week (Monday -
Saturday). Commercial front load recycling collection occurs up to five (5) days a week (Monday - Friday. There
are four (4) container sizes for commercial front load service:
Two (2) cubic yard;
Four (4) cubic yard;
Six (6) cubic yard; and
Eight (8) cubic yard.
Commercial Roll Off.  Commercial roll off collection occurs up to five (5) days per week (Monday – Friday).  
This work is a mix of compactors and open top containers.  The work is fifty (50) percent permanently 
scheduled and fifty (50) percent call in.  Container sizes are as follows:
Ten (10) cubic yard open top;   
Twenty (20) cubic yard open top;
Thirty (30) cubic yard open top;
Forty (40) cubic yard open top;
Ten (10) cubic yard compactor;
Twenty (20) cubic yard compactor;
Thirty (30) cubic yard compactor;
Thirty-five (35) cubic yard compactor; and
Forty (40) cubic yard compactor.
