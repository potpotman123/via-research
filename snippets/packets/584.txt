PERFORMANCE BOND
(a) 
The Contractor shall provide a Performance Bond in an amount equal to fifty percent (50%) of the contract 
amount.  The Contractor shall be required to submit the required bond to the Contracting Officer within ten (10) days 
from the date of Contract Award Date. The surety company providing the bond must be listed in the latest United 
States Treasury Department Circular 570, be authorized to do business in Texas and have an underwriting limitation 
equal to or greater than the penal sum of the bond. If any surety upon any bond furnished in connection with the 
Contract becomes insolvent, or otherwise not authorized to do business in the State, the Contractor shall promptly 
furnish equivalent security to protect the interest of the Authority and of persons supplying labor, materials and/or 
equipment in the prosecution of the Work. 
(b) 
The bond shall be accompanied by a valid Power-of-Attorney, issued by the surety company and attached, 
signed and sealed, with the corporate embossed seal, to the bond, authorizing the agent who signs the bond to 
commit the surety company to the terms of the bond, and stating on the face of the Power-of-Attorney the limit, if any, 
in the total amount for which he/she is empowered to issue a single bond. 
(c) 
A surety bond rider increasing the dollar amount of any payment and performance bond will be required for 
any Change Order that increases the contract amount. 
(d) 
In addition, the Authority may request a surety bond increasing the dollar amount if: 
(1)

OTHER WORK TASKS/SERVICES 
- 
Vendor will provide on-boarding/Training Sessions for all staff who plan to actively use the program 
and maintain a user account either on-site or via webinar/video conference. On-boarding/Training 
sessions will be provided upon request for new staff. Full user documentation will be provided by the 
vendor. 
- 
Vendor will aid in the updating of underlying demographic data and transit route/stop data within the 
software as a service platform. Demographic data, drawing from most up-to-date annual American 
Community Survey (ACS) data, should be updated by vendor automatically as the data becomes 
available online. GTFS or other data format feeding the transit route/stop data should be updated with 
each triennial service change (January, June and August), by request of Capital Metro staff. 
- 
Vendor will aid in the uploading of custom data layers to the software as a service platform 
- 
Vendor will be available to help troubleshoot problems with the platform and/or data outputs throughout 
the time that Capital Metro maintains a subscription license during business hours (Monday-Friday, 
9am-5pm CST). Service response and resolution time during these hours should be less than 48 
hours. 
- 
Vendor will consider custom software changes or updates based on customer needs/requests. 
- 
For setting user logins, Capital Metro prefers an option to have user authentication integration with 
Microsoft Active Directory. Capital Metro will evaluate if this implementation is feasible with given 
security needs. 
- 
Vendor will provide secure web access to hosted system 24x7x365 except for agreed upon periods of 
maintenance. 
- 
Describe the safeguards in place to prevent unauthorized access to the domain, software, and data. 
- 
Vendor might perform interviews of Capital Metro IT personnel to determine business requirements 
and address all business requirements in the proposed specifications. 
- 
Describe the process and procedures for regular backups of analysis/maps/data output created by 
Capital Metro accounts.
