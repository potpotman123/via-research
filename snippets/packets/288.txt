ON-DEMAND RIDESHARE CONTRACT 
 
 
River North Transit LLC  (“Contractor” or “Via”) and City of Arlington, TX (“Customer” or “City” and, 
together with Via, the “Parties”) have entered into that certain agreement titled On-Demand Rideshare 
Contract, dated November 29, 2022 (the “Agreement”). This Amendment #1 to the On-Demand Rideshare 
Contract (the “Amendment”) is entered into on this 23rd day of May, 2023.  Upon execution of this 
Amendment #1 (the “Amendment”), the Parties agree to modify the Agreement as follows: 
 
1. 
Amendment.  
 
Section II. A. of the Agreement is modified to read as follows: 
 
CITY agrees to pay CONTRACTOR the amounts set forth in the Scope of Services, not to 
exceed $30,412,626 (i.e., the total amount of funding obtained by the City as of the date hereof) 
in the aggregate for the initial two-year period of this Contract, subject to the following 
provisions. This total of $26,367,762 includes a maximum of $22,047,916 for the standard Via 
service cost, $179,502 for the Arlington Student Transportation Program, $4,020,224 for the 
Arlington RAPID service, and $139,984 for the Handitran paratransit software, and $25,000 for 
the translation of the rider app in Vietnamese.   
 
In addition, all other references to the “not to exceed” amount shall be increased accordingly to 
$30,412,626,  
 
2. Amendment. Appendix E is added as an additional scope of work to the Agreement for Via software and 
related services for City’s Handitran paratransit services.   
 
3. Conflicts, Use of Terms, Governing Law. Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the 
meanings set forth in the Agreement. Except as expressly provided herein, the terms and conditions of the 
Agreement remain unchanged. This Amendment #1 will be governed by the same law as the Agreement 
and is effective as of May 23, 2023.
