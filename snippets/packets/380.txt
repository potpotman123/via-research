ATTACHMENT B 
SCOPE OF WORK 
 
The general objective of this Contract-DSA TSD-24-146 is to provide safe and reliable on-demand 
alternative vehicle transportation services for District students. In order to accomplish the general 
objective of this Contract-DSA, the District and Nomad Transit shall perform the specific duties 
outlined below. 
 
RESPONSIBILITIES OF NOMAD TRANSIT 
 
SAFETY 
 
A. Nomad Transit will be responsible for the safe and reliable vehicle transportation of District 
students at the District’s request. 
 
B. Nomad Transit will ensure that all services under this Contract will comply with current 
Washington State Division of Industrial Safety orders, OSHA, and any/all other applicable local, 
state, and federal safety laws, ordinances, and regulations governing the vehicles and vehicles for 
hire.  
 
C. Nomad Transit will ensure all appropriate license(s), permits to operate, and training are obtained 
and maintained for the compliant operation within the cities, counties, and state covered by this 
Contract, including but not limited to the Washington State Vehicle Code.  
 
1. Cell phones will not be used for personal purposes while students are in the vehicle. 
 
D. Pursuant to Revised Code of Washington (RCW) 28A.400.330, Nomad Transit will prohibit any 
employee from working under this Contract who has contact with children during the course of 
their employment and who has pled guilty to or been convicted of any felony crime involving the 
physical neglect of a child under Chapter 9A.32 or 9A.36 RCW (except motor vehicle violations 
under Chapter 46.61 RCW), sexual exploitation of a child under Chapter 9.68 RCW, sexual 
offenses under Chapter 9A.44 RCW where a minor is the victim, promoting prostitution of a 
minor under Chapter 9A.88 RCW, the sale or purchase of a minor child under RCW 9A.64.030, 
or violation of similar laws of another jurisdiction. Failure to comply with this section shall be 
grounds for the District to immediately terminate the Contract.   
 
E. Nomad Transit shall utilize for all potential drivers at the time of hire the background check 
procedures of the Washington State Patrol and potential drivers will be screened through a 
national FBI check. Fingerprinting will be used, with a complete Washington State Identification 
Fingerprint Card, to ensure positive identification as is the responsibility of Nomad Transit. All 
background checks, including fingerprinting, must be completed and demonstrated as “passed” 
prior to any potential driver or employee performs services for the District under this Contract. 
 
F. Nomad Transit drivers and staff which may come in to contact with students must be fit-for-duty 
and able to communicate effectively orally and in writing. All drivers will be clean, neat in 
personal appearance, and courteous.

C. Nomad Transit will be capable of providing up to and as many as thirty (30) different vehicles at 
any time as needed for the vehicle transportation of District students. 
1. The District routing is based on enrollment and student need in real-time, with a
typical heavier volume in spring.
D. For those occasions with less than thirty (30) trips booked and confirmed for a given day, for any 
trips booked and confirmed more than twenty-four (24) hours in advance (one business day) that 
do not get covered by Nomad Transit as requested, Nomad Transit will be charged the cost the 
District incurs to make up for such. The District will provide documentation outlining how any 
such changes are calculated. 
E. Nomad Transit will be responsible for arriving on-time and no later than ten (10) minutes from 
the scheduled time. 
F. Nomad Transit will provide vehicles that are appropriately permitted, properly maintained and in 
good working order, clean, and have all required safety equipment and associated features. 
1. A file shall be kept available for each vehicle documenting such and will be made
immediately available to the District upon request.
2. Parents/Guardians of students that are required to use a car-seat under Washington
State Law will have child safety seat(s) available and as appropriate.
BILLING 
A. Invoices will be submitted to the District each month to reflect the prior month’s services. 
Payment will be made following receipt of Nomad Transit’s adequate and original invoices. For 
an invoice to be deemed adequate, the Purchase Order number must appear on the invoice as 
well as itemizations. Itemizations must include: 
1. Date of service.
2. Name of student.
3. Address of pick-up.
4. Address of drop-off.
5. Name of Nomad Transit staff.
6. Vehicle ID (e.g., license plate number).
7. Miles per trip (roundtrip if applicable).
8. Rate, and total.
B. Nomad Transit will ensure that invoices reflect the rates as provided within Attachment C, Rate 
Schedule.

E. NONDISCRIMINATION: Both the District and Nomad Transit agree that no individual shall be 
excluded from participation in, denied the benefits of, subjected to discrimination under, or denied 
employment in the administration of or in connection with any aspect of this DSA because of sex, 
race, creed, religion, color, national origin, age, honorably discharged veteran or military status, 
sexual orientation including  gender expression or identity, the presence of any sensory, mental or 
physical disability, or the use of trained dog guide or service animal by a person with a disability. 
Harassment on the basis of any of the foregoing conditions is strictly prohibited.  The Parties agree 
to abide by the standards of responsibility toward the disabled as specified by the Americans with 
Disabilities Act and Washington Law Against Discrimination. In the event that one of the Parties 
hereto refused to comply with the above provision, this DSA may be canceled, terminated, or 
suspended in whole or in part by the other Party. 
F. ASSIGNMENT: Neither Party shall assign its rights or responsibilities under this DSA without the 
written authorization of all the other Parties. 
G. PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE: This DSA becomes effective upon signatures by all parties and 
remains in effect through August 31, 2027, unless terminated prior to this date per the conditions 
herein.  
H. DSA TERMINATION: The District may immediately terminate this DSA at any time for its own 
convenience, and for any reason, including, without limitation, violations of the Confidentiality 
provisions identified within, followed by written notice provided to Nomad Transit at the time of the 
decision to terminate the DSA, or at a practical time soon thereafter. Nomad Transit may terminate 
this Agreement for any reason by providing the District with 30 calendar days’ written notice of 
Nomad Transit intent to terminate.   
I. SEVERABILITY: If any term of this DSA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of the 
DSA will not be affected but continue in full force. 
J. INTEGRATION: This writing contains all terms and conditions of the DSA. It replaces all prior 
negotiations and agreements regarding the terms and conditions in this DSA. Any modifications to 
this DSA must be made by way of formal amendment/addendum.
