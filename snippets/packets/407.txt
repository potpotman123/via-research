RECOMMENDATION:
Approve Amendment No. 4 to Service Order No. 1 to the On Demand Technologies Master Service Agreement
and authorize UTA’s Executive Director to execute the agreement and associated disbursements with River
North Transit, LLC (Via) in the amount of $7,290,360 for renewal of the UTA On-Demand service in the Salt
Lake City (SLC) Westside zone.
BACKGROUND:
UTA has contracted with River North Transit, LLC (Via) through a Master Services Agreement (MSA) which was
approved by the Board on September 25, 2021 to expand microtransit services and programs (UTA Contract 20
-03399).  As approved by the Board on October 13, 2021, Via and UTA initiated Service Order No. 1 to deploy
microtransit service in the SLC Westside zone. Microtransit, branded as UTA On Demand, has emerged as a
cost-effective coverage solution.
This 4th Amendment to Via’s Service Order 1 would fund UTA On Demand microtransit service in the SLC
Westside zone through the end of UTA-Via’s MSA term in December 2026.  This growth was contemplated in
the initial RFP and the contract and has been determined by UTA legal to be within scope.
DISCUSSION:
This amendment provides ongoing microtransit service per the Five-Year Service Plan. UTA’s current service
order for Via to operate the SLC Westside zone expires on June 30, 2024. Instead of requiring annual renewals,

UTA desired a multi-year agreement to facilitate budgeting and service planning. The proposed new
amendment renews the contract for 30 months to align with the Via Master Service Agreement (MSA) term.
The cost is approximately $7.3 million over 30 months including 2% annual adjustments for inflation. This
amendment assumes zero growth in ridership and service hours. Innovative Mobility Solutions department
will submit operating budgets annually, and if needed, new service orders per the Five-Year Service Plan for
additional hours and vehicles.
