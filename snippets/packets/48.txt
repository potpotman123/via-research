PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT 
 
 
 
This agreement ("Agreement") is made as of July 20, 2021 (“Effective Date”), by and 
between the City of Commerce, a California municipal corporation ("City"), and Via Mobility a 
New York limited liability Corporation ("Consultant").  City and Consultant are sometimes 
hereinafter individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively referred to as the "Parties." 
 
 
R E C I T A L S  
 
 
WHEREAS, City desires to utilize the services of Consultant as an independent contractor 
providing the services outlined in the Scope of Services attached hereto as Exhibit A; and 
 
 
WHEREAS, the Consultant represents that it is fully qualified to perform such consulting 
services by virtue of its experience and the training, education, and expertise of its principals and 
employees. 
 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the performance by the parties of the covenants 
and conditions herein contained, the parties hereto agree as follows: 
 
1. 
Company's Scope of Services.  The nature and scope of the specific services to be 
performed by the Consultant are as described in Exhibit A. 
 
2. 
Term of Agreement.  This agreement shall commence on July 20, 2021 (the 
"Commencement Date") and shall remain and continue in effect until tasks described in Exhibit 
A is completed, but in no event later than July 20, 2024, unless sooner terminated pursuant to the 
provisions of this agreement.   
 
3. 
Compensation. 
 
 
A.  City agrees to compensate Consultant for services under this Agreement in compliance 
with the schedule outlined in Exhibit A.  Consultant shall submit invoices in the form and manner 
specified by City.  Each invoice shall include a breakdown of all monthly services performed 
together with the hours spent on each service.  Consultant shall maintain appropriate and 
necessary documentation supporting the invoices detailing the type of service provided.  It shall 
be available for review by City at all reasonable times upon request.   
 
 
B.  Total payment to Consultant pursuant to this agreement shall not exceed One Hundred 
Twenty-One Thousand Seven Hundred Dollars ($121,700).  
 
 
C.  If at the request of City, Consultant is required to incur out of pocket expenses (including 
but not limited to, out-of-town travel and lodging) which are above and beyond the ordinary costs 
associated with the performance of this Agreement, Consultant shall be entitled to reimbursement 
of such expenses.   
 
Consultant shall only be reimbursed for those expenses which: (I) appear on Consultant's monthly 
invoices; (II) are accompanied by a copy of City's written authorization for Consultant to incur such 
expenses; and (III) receipts documenting such expenses.

1. Solution Overview 
For Commerce, Via Mobility LLC (or “Via”) proposes to deliver our Software-as-a-Service 
(SaaS) solution, a comprehensive On-Demand Transit platform that meets or exceeds 
every requirement outlined in the City’s Request for Proposal (“RFP – On Demand 
Transit Software Technology”). Powered by our backend algorithms for scheduling 
shared rides and dynamically routing vehicles, our solution will deliver an exceptional 
user experience for riders, drivers, and system administrators. Please ﬁnd additional 
detail on each system component in our RFP. At a high level, Via’s SaaS solution includes 
all of the features and support services outlined in the RFP: 
 
 
2. Implementation Plan 
From designing and launching over 200 on-demand transit services, Via understands 
that exceptional project management is key to the success of any deployment. We 
take an agile approach to both product development and project governance: we 
rapidly test and iterate ideas, communicate frequently with our partners, and 
incorporate feedback without causing downstream delays. Through productive 
partner communication and nimble project management, our team meets deliverables 
within agreed-upon timelines while maintaining the ﬂexibility to course-correct as 
necessary. Backed by our team of 350 algorithm experts, product managers, systems 
administrators and developers, along with 350 data scientists, operations and growth 
marketing experts, and transportation planners, Via will provide Commerce with an
