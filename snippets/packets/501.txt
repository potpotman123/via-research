Recommendation 
It is recommended that the Board of County Commissioners (Board) approve this request for award of 
Designated Purchase Contract No. BW-10126, Transit Planning Data Tool Software, to Remix 
Technologies, LLC (Remix) in the amount of $960,000 for the Department of Transportation and Public 
Works (DTPW).  Approval of a designated purchase is requested, pursuant to Section 2-8.1(b)(3) of the 
Miami-Dade County Code, by a two-thirds vote of the Board members present, for the purchase of transit 
planning data tool software.  
The contract provides for a web-based software tool to rapidly analyze and evaluate transit concepts. It 
will be used by County Engineers and Planners to review mobility data and inventories, evaluate transit 
planning scenarios, sketch ideas and log stakeholder feedback, integrate all required functions sought 
by the County , and will be supported by cloud access from any work location and computer.  
Additionally, the software has the capability for transit planning, conceptual street design, and mobility 
management, which will, in part: 1) facilitate sketch planning, provide instant demographic impact 
analysis, and provide instant cost estimates; 2) sketch conceptual roadway designs to be used in 
connection with transit demonstration projects, sketch out multiple lane types including vehicle, turn 
lanes, bike lanes, bus lanes, pedestrian infrastructure, crosswalks, and support public engagement and 
share features for stakeholder input; and 3) generate vehicle location ground truth and metrics, view 
dockless vehicle location, identify the most popular spots in the County for rider drop-off and deployment, 
and display trip path visualization by segment across the County.  
DTPW, working with the Information Technology Department (ITD), experienced access to the Remix 
tool both through an ITD procured demo license during fiscal year 2019 and the Vision Zero Program, 
currently under development. The Vision Zero Program set the goal of zero pedestrian and bicycle 
deaths and implements new safety strategies using technology. Remix’s software tool provided data 
analytical services that supported this program. 
DTPW Engineers and Planner’s interaction with the Remix tool has been extremely positive, as it has 
yielded higher productivity, time savings and better cooperation among divisions. Before the Remix tool 
was available, DTPW divisions spent a significant amount of time searching data from several sources, 
and creating maps to visualize proposed routes among other efforts.  Previous to the use of Remix’s 
tool, the required software functionality was completed through rudimentary processes.  The use of the 
requested planning functionality promotes cooperation through a holistic transit analysis approach which 
incorporates conceptual street design and traffic analysis features.  
Comprehensive market research was conducted, including an NIGP query to research other public entity 
contracts, national search of past bids in BidSync, a Google search, and issuance of a Request for 
Information (RFI). The RFI yielded three firms offering software tools, including Remix.  Only Remix 
offered a tool which will support the full requirements of the County. 
Competition for the provision of the software is not practicable at this time because market research has 
shown that other commercial off-the-shelf software lacks the capability to integrate all required system 
functionality into a single platform that can quickly, effectively, and seamlessly share and analyze data.
