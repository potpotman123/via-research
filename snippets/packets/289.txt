Renewal and Modification 
to the 
ON-DEMAND RIDERSHARE CONTRACT 
 
 
River North Transit LLC (“Via” or “Contractor”) and City of Arlington, TX (“City” or “Customer” and, 
together with Via, the “Parties”) have entered into that certain agreement titled On-Demand Rideshare 
Contract, dated November 29, 2022, and modified by Amendment #1 dated May 23, 2023 (together, the 
“Contract”). The Contract, which is set to expire December 31, 2024, provides for up to four (4) two-year 
renewals.  The Parties desire to renew the Contract and modify it in certain respects.  Upon execution of 
this Renewal and Modification to the On-Demand Rideshare Contract (the “Renewal”), the Parties agree 
to renew and modify the Contract as follows: 
 
1. Service Period Extension. The Parties hereby agree to renew the Contract for an additional 
period of 24 months, starting on January 1, 2025 and ending on December 31, 2026 (inclusive), 
unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of the Contract.

EXHIBIT A 
RIDESHARE SERVICES SCOPE OF SERVICES 
 
1. Purpose; Scope 
Contractor will provide rideshare services as described below (the “Service”) for the City and its residents 
and visitors. Contractor's Services include: 
 Access to Via's fully localized dynamic vehicle routing and real-time passenger aggregation 
system; 
 Establishing relationship with vehicle rental company ("Vehicle Partner") to provide access to 
Vehicles on a rental basis to independent contractor driver partners; 
 Accompanying technical and operational support services in order to provide a turnkey solution; 
 A series of service development meetings to refine service scope for a supplemental microtransit 
service that operates during peak hours to maximize efficiency across modes and improve quality 
of service (the “Dynamic Direct Route”); 
 Data sharing and reporting as described under Section 9 below; 
 Access to the Via mobile application for users to book rides from a smartphone; 
 Integration of the Arlington Student Transportation Program as described under Section 10 
below; 
 Integration of the Arlington RAPID service as described under Section 11 below; and 
 Advertising services as described under Section 12 
 
2. Duration 
The Project Scope will begin on January 1, 2025, unless otherwise mutually agreed by both parties. The 
Service shall operate through December 31, 2026, with the option to renew in accordance with the 
Contract. 
3. Fees 
A. Contractor Fees 
 
The City agrees to pay Contractor the amounts below, not to exceed $20,791,648.32 in the aggregate for 
the term of this Contract. 
 
The fees for rideshare services shall be on a cost per on-demand service hour basis, corresponding with 
the tasks outlined in the Contractor's response to the RFP, as outlined below. The fees for the Dynamic 
Direct Route portion of the Services shall be on fixed costs and a cost per on-demand service hour basis, 
corresponding with the tasks outlined below.

B. Wheelchair Accessible Vehicle (WAV) Fleet 
  
Contractor will contract with a vehicle provider that shall develop and maintain a WAV fleet plan that 
satisfies relevant FTA ADA requirements - including the equal provision of service to potential riders of 
all physical means. Contractor shall procure through its partnership with May Mobility at least one (1) 
autonomous vehicle to its WAV Fleet, which will operate under the Arlington RAPID program, as 
described in Section 11.  
  
To fulfill this goal, Contractor shall cause to be deployed for the Service appropriately-qualified drivers 
operating existing City vehicles, taxi or livery vehicles, or dedicated vehicles specifically deployed for the 
Service.  
 
7. Customer and Driver Support 
While its platform is highly automated, and requires minimal human intervention to operate, Contractor 
shall provide high-quality customer service to both customers and driver partners during the course of the 
Service. If an issue arises for a customer or driver before, during, or after a ride, these parties will be able 
to reach customer service staff by in-app form submission, by submitting an email ticket, or by calling in, 
which will be replied to promptly by such staff. 
  
Upon request by the City, the Contractor shall provide to the City a periodic reporting system of customer 
service events, in order to maximize the City’s learnings about the Service.
