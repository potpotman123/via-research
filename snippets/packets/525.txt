Van Service 
Accessible van service is required by the Americans with Disability Act (ADA). Its 
purpose is to provide accessible service to persons with disabilities that mirrors the 
service provided by fixed route buses. This program serves Milwaukee County residents 
and visitors who are prevented from using fixed route buses some or all of the time due 
to a disability. Client eligibility is determined via doctor’s certification and an in-person 
functional assessment conducted by Transit Plus staff. 
 
Per ADA regulations, service must be provided by accessible vehicles within a ¾ mile 
zone around all fixed routes operated by MCTS. Currently, Milwaukee County provides 
border to border service within the County plus the required ¾ mile buffer around fixed 
routes that extend into other counties. This is a shared ride service available with a 
reservation made one to 7 days in advance of a ride. Clients can travel anywhere in the 
county-wide service area for any purpose. Service is provided from first exterior door to 
first exterior door during the same hours and days as fixed route bus operation. 
 
ADA van service drivers participate in random drug/alcohol testing required by the 
Federal Transit Administration (FTA) and provide door to door assistance to clients. The 
fare is capped by the FTA at no more than double the fixed route bus fare. The current 
fare is $4 per one-way trip for private pay riders or $35.00 per one-way trip for those 
enrolled in Medicaid funded long term care programs. 
 
Van service is provided through a contract with First Group (a wholly owned subsidiary 
of Transdev North America). MCTS pays a negotiated rate per service hour provided.  
The current van service contract began in October 2023 and ends in October 2030. 
Under this contract, the vendor is responsible for all aspects of service provision 
including maintaining appropriate insurance; hiring, training and managing staff; drug 
testing, scheduling, dispatching and providing rides; accident/incident investigation; 
vehicle acquisition; and equipment maintenance.   
 
The contract includes performance standards in the areas of on-time performance, ride 
duration, service availability, appointment drop-offs, compliance with federal 
requirements and service productivity. Incentives and penalties are applied as 
warranted.

The Paratransit Taxi Task Force met throughout 2023 and 2024 to develop the service

Service began on the Transit Plus Same Day Pilot at the end of August 2024 and is 
contracted with Tidewater Transit, dba Via, to run until September 2025 or until 
budgeted funds are expired.  Service is provided on a same day basis for 
health/medical or employment rides that could not have been scheduled the day before 
and thus could not have been scheduled on the existing paratransit vans.  Some 
examples of the use cases for this service would include a same day trip to urgent care, 
going to pick up a sick child, or being called into work.  All vehicles are wheelchair 
accessible, and drivers are subject to random drug and alcohol testing. Rides can be 
shared or private depending on demand.  The contracted rate for this service is $98.73 
per vehicle hour.   
 
Service runs from 7 AM to 5:59 PM, 7 days/week and is provided border to border 
within Milwaukee County.  The rider fare is $10 per one-way trip. A Personal Care 
Attendant (when required due to rider disability) is allowed to accompany the rider at no 
cost. One companion can ride with the client for an additional $10. Fare can be paid 
with a credit/debit card or a cash voucher code purchased at the MCTS Administration 
Building.  Trip booking can be done using a smartphone app or over the phone. The 
vehicle must arrive within one hour of booking the trip. 
 
There were 10 trips provided on this program in September and 10 trips in October. 
Transit Plus and the Task Force members continue efforts to educate potential riders 
about the availability of this service. 
 
SUMMARY 
The MCTS Paratransit Program, known as Transit Plus manages all aspects of ADA 
paratransit eligibility, service provision, reporting and compliance with state and federal 
requirements. In addition, Transit Plus manages a same day discretionary program that 
is offered by Milwaukee County to help offset the unmet need for same day 
transportation for persons with disabilities. All programs are closely monitored to 
maintain service efficiency and effectiveness. Complaints are carefully recorded, 
investigated and resolved. Efforts to enable persons with disabilities to be able to 
successfully use fixed route services in addition to, or instead of, paratransit services 
are on-going through mobility management.
