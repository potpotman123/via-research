Resolution No. __________ 
 
A resolution authorizing the execution of a two-year On-
Demand Rideshare Service Contract, with four optional 
two-year renewals, for a demand response rideshare 
service with River North Transit, LLC., of New York, 
New York, for an amount not to exceed $30,247,642 
 
 
WHEREAS, the City partnered with Via Transportation, Inc., to launch an on-demand 
rideshare service on December 11, 2017 to offer an additional mobility 
option for residents, employees and visitors; and 
 
WHEREAS, the contract was renewed annually, and the current contract provides service 
through December 10, 2022; and 
 
WHEREAS, on November 9, 2021, City Council approved Resolution No. 21-282, 
authorizing the competitive sealed proposal procurement method for 
demand response rideshare service; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Transportation Department and the Finance Department have 
determined that it is in the City’s best interest to execute the two-year on-
demand rideshare contract; NOW THEREFORE 
 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF ARLINGTON, 
TEXAS: 
I. 
 
That all of the recitals contained in the preamble of this resolution are found to be 
true and are adopted as findings of fact by this governing body and as part of its official 
record. 
 
II. 
 
 
That the City Manager or his designee is hereby authorized to execute a two-year 
On-Demand Rideshare Service Contract, with four optional two-year renewals, for a 
demand response rideshare service with River North Transit, LLC. of New York, New 
York, for an amount not to exceed $30,247,642, which includes $4,000,000 in additional 
funding for intercity services that may be added at a later date.  Any expenditures in excess 
of $26,247,642 are subject to approval by the City Manager or his designee.  Funds are 
available in the Transportation Department Grant Account No. CC418604-61000-SC0533-
GR000018.
