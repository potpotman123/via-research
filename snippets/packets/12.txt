Extension and Amendment of the Contract for Professional Services 
 
1. Purpose; Scope 
 
Each of Via Mobility, LLC a Delaware company with its principal office located at 114 5th Avenue, Floor 
16 and 17, New York, New York 10011 (“Via”), and Baldwin County Commission (“Customer”), and, 
hereinafter  the “Parties”  entered into an agreement titled Contract for Professional Services (the 
"Contract") and into a service order titled Baldwin County Deployment Service Order each dated June 18, 
2020  and modified by the Extension and Amendment Agreement dated September 21, 2021, and modified 
by the Extension and Amendment Agreement dated September 20, 2022, and modified by the Extension 
and Amendment Agreement dated September 19, 2023 (together, the “Agreement”). By this Extension and 
Amendment Agreement ( “Extension”), the parties agree towards continued collaboration of operation of 
the services provided under the Agreement. 
 
Amendment 
 
2. Duration  
 
This Extension shall extend the period of performance of the Agreement by a period of twelve months, 
from October 1, 2024 until September 30, 2025 (inclusive) (the "Additional Term"). The Effective Date 
of this Extension shall be October 1, 2024. 
 
3. Product Enhancements 
 
Via commits to make all commercially reasonable efforts to deliver the following capabilities within its 
software platform within the first 6 months of the Additional Term. Via agrees to communicate with 
Customer on an ongoing basis regarding the timeline for these capabilities, and to collaborate with 
Customer regarding the prioritization of these capabilities as relevant.  
 
• 
Correct scheduling error in  rider app for BayLinc specialty tag. Rider should be restricted to 
scheduling trips from Baldwin County to Mobile to 3 set departure locations and 3 specific 
departure times. Since mid-June passengers are able to schedule a trip from Baldwin County to 
Mobile from any location at any time. 
• 
Correct issue of randomly not presenting completed trips for payment processing.  These are not 
FAILED payments. When a payment fails, it appears on the Braintree system and in the Pricing 
and Payment Report as a failed payment. The payments that are not processed at all do not appear 
in the Pricing and Payment Report , in Braintree, or on the Rider Management Page.  The rides 
appear on the Ride Plan and Ride Request Report as completed. This issue was reported with the 
rollout of Via 2.0 on 6/1/2023. 
 
If the above capabilities are not delivered by April 1, 2025, Customer may terminate  the Agreement in 
accordance with Clause 19 and Exhibit B of the Contract. 
 
4. Remix Pilot.

=== page 2 of 2 ===
Baldwin County Commission may activate a 6-month pilot of Remix On-Demand Planning at no additional 
cost during the Additional Term. Use of the Remix Solution  is subject to the Remix service terms 
at https://www.remix.com/service-terms. 
 
5. Conflicts of Terms.  
 
Unless otherwise stated, all terms and conditions contained in the Original Order are also applicable to this 
Extension. If a term contained in this Extension is in conflict with the terms of the Original Order, the 
specific term in this Extension shall take precedence. Any term or condition in the Original Order, including 
all Appendices not affected by this Extension, shall remain in full force and effect.
