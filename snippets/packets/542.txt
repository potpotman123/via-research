BACKGROUND:   
 
Since 2021, Pierce Transit has been providing ADA-compliant Runner service via a third-party carrier to areas where 
fixed-route bus service is limited or infeasible, allowing customers access to public transit on-demand by calling in 
or using a smartphone application. The service was initially launched with one zone – Ruston, and has since 
expanded to Tideflats / Port of Tacoma, Spanaway-Parkland-Midland, Puyallup, and most recently Gig Harbor.  
 
In 2023 the Pierce Transit Board authorized the current multi-year contract with Via Transportation, Inc., to provide 
Runner services to Ruston, Tideflats/Port of Tacoma, Spanaway-Parkland-Midland, and Puyallup. The contract also 
included provisions to expand zones and flexibility to transition some Runner service to direct agency operations 
using in-house drivers and vehicles.   
 
Since the initial launch of Runner service and PostCovid, the customer demand for this type of service has been 
increasing as product awareness develops in communities and zones are expanded. In March 2024 the Tideflats 
zone was expanded and a new zone in Gig Harbor was added. Boardings in 2023 increased from 13,536 to 50,482 
in 2024.  
 
Due to increased demand for Runner service and the expansion of zones, additional funds are needed to continue 
this service through the end of the contract term of June 2026.  Without the increase, funds are estimated to run out 
in June 2025.  
 
RECOMMENDATION:  
 
Staff requests authority to increase the contract amount with Via Transportation, Inc., in the amount of $3,200,000 
to provide Runner services in Puyallup, Spanaway, Tideflats, Ruston, and Gig Harbor through June 2026, for a new 
not to exceed contract authority amount of $7,100,000.   
 
ALTERNATIVES:  
 
1. Do not authorize increasing the contract amount as presented. This is not recommended as this service 
provides a viable option where fixed route service is limited, and Runner service would then end June 
2025. 
2. Do not authorize the contract amount as presented and direct staff to transition the Runner service to be 
directly operated by Pierce Transit after the contract ends in June 2025. This is not recommended as the 
cost of operating the service in house would be significantly higher.  
 
PROPOSED MOTION: 
 
Move to: Authorize the Chief Executive Officer to increase the contract amount with Via Transportation, Inc., 
(Contract No. 1750) in the amount of $3,200,000 to provide Runner services in Puyallup, Spanaway, Tideflats, 
Ruston, and Gig Harbor for a new total not to exceed contract spending authority amount of $7,100,000.
