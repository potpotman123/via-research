6. Explain why the product or service requested is the only one that can satisfy your
requirements, as well as why alternatives are unacceptable. Be specific with regard
to specifications. Attach additional pages if necessary.
Remix is the only company that can provide a product (Transitmix) that meets DCTA's
needs: Transitmix is the only product designed to:
• help transit agencies sketch transit scenarios
• estimate costs in real-time
• upload routes via GTFS data format
• overlay geospatial data
• allow for rapid evaluation and comparison
• be delivered according to a Software as a Service (SaaS) model: users receive access
to an easy-to-use online platform without the worry of maintenance, hosting, or software
updates. Remix Software, Inc. maintains the technology infrastructure and enhances
Transitmix over time.
• whereas transit planning often requires a time-intensive, multi-step process with many
disparate tools, the technology in Transitmix enables planners to understand the impact
of multiple planning options immediately all in a visual, easy to use interface -- saving
valuable time and money for the
DCTA.
Currently DCTA uses Microsoft Excel spreadsheets for developing and analyzing route
schedules and ArcGIS to create and modify routes, and conduct demographic analysis.
The current process is time consuming and prone to errors.
