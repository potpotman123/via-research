Public Comment*
Dear Board of Trustees, I am writing as a disabled FlexRide rider with urgent concerns about a disastrous
upcoming change to the FlexRide service, operated by Via under contract with AAATA and Pittsfield Tw
Riders were notified Sunday afternoon that, beginning September 8th, FlexRide will be limited to On-Demand
only trips, eliminating the ability to book rides the day before. This change is alarming and directly threatens
my ability to get to my job at NOAA GLERL in Pittsfield Township. I am disabled and thus rely on FlexRide as
my daily commute. I always book as far in advance as possible. In my experience, On-Demand service alone
is unreliable: 8 out of 10 times when I attempt a same-day ride-such as returning to work after a doctor's
appointment of uncertain length-Via's app tells me "Sorry, we couldn't find a ride" or 'Please try again later.'
That level of uncertainty is incompatible with maintaining employment. The elimination of advance bookings is
not a minor inconvenience it jeopardizes my job security as a worker in the Township. Further, the "no
drivers available" boilerplate line is typically patently false. FlexRide drivers have been complaining to us for
months that passenger bookings are frequently very unevenly distributed across the available drivers: one
driver will get 20+ rides assigned to him over 1-2 hours and be forced to drive nonstop and unable to stop for
bathroom breaks, while simultaneously another driver will be stuck idling in a parking lot for 30+ minutes with
no passengers assigned | have called FlexRide receptionists on the phone about this a handful of times in
the past, but they are usually unable to help me. Some receptionists can and do edit drivers' trip manifests to
redistribute the rides, but most other receptionists fall back on reading aloud to us the same false 'no drivers
available' boilerplate messages that the app shows us. I am also concerned that this cut to next-day booking
may not align with Via's contractual obligations to AAATA/Pittsfield Township, and that the Trustees may not
even be aware that this reduction is happening. Simlarly, every single FlexRide driver and FlexRide
receptionist we've spoken to this Monday and Tuesday has also been entirely unaware of this service cut.
Further, this push to On-Demand service would bring FlexRide even further out of alignment with its original
goal. to replace the fixed-route #61 bus service. How can we expect commuters to get to work on time when
advance scheduling is thrown out the window entirely? We used to have predictable Route #61 timetables,
now we'll have nothing. Additionally, every rider I have spoken to between Monday and Tuesday has been
fully unaware that this service change was even announced, because the only platform it was announced on
was a transient phone notification from Via's FlexRide app. It is unacceptable for Via to leave riders in the
dark on this. I respectfully ask that you exercise oversight of this issue. At minimum, I hope the Trustees can:
1. Verify whether Via is authorized to make this level of service reduction. 2. Ensure that AAATA holds Via
accountable for reliable service delivery. 3 Advocate for riders whose livelihoods depend on predictable
transit, especially those with disabilities. 4. Require AAATA to properly announce this service change, such as
under TheRide's service alerts webpage and on TheRide's social media. 5. Require Via Transportation to pin
their service announcement as a pop-up message in the FlexRide app as they have done with less-important
messages in the past-to ensure all FlexRide riders are made aware that this service reduction is happening
at all. 6. Encourage AAATA to conduct their own FlexRide user satisfaction surveys instead of relying on Via's
possibly inflated metrics. AAATA's Mobility and Planning departments are failing to account for "suppressed
demand" or "peak spreading" due to inadequate service. My coworker told me yesterday morning that "I never
even try to book on-demand rides after 4 pm anymore because I know it's impossible." I also encourage the
Trustees to see for yourselves before September 8th how abysmal the on-demand FlexRide service currently
is create your own rider account, install the app, and try taking FlexRide on-demand to and from Ahmo's on
your lunch break for a few days or a week. I'll also be submitting a complaint through AAATA's general
complaint form, but as I have not heard back from them in the past on similar matters, I feel strongly that this
requires Trustee oversight to ensure accountability. Thank you so much for your attention to this matter and
for your continued public service. Sincerely, Elliot Phillips
