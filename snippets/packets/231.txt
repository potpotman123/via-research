AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO THE LICENSE AGREEMENT FOR SHUTTLE 
SERVICE- PILOT PROGRAM BETWEEN THE CITY OF MANHATTAN 
BEACH  
AND ALLSOP AND KNAPP, LLC, (dba THE DOWNTOWNER) 
This First Amendment (“Amendment No. 1”) to the License Agreement by and 
between the City of Manhattan Beach, a California municipal corporation (“City”) 
and Allsop and Knapp, LLC, a California limited liability company (“Contractor”) 
(collectively, the “Parties”) is hereby entered into on July 18, 2017 (“Effective 
Date”). 
RECITALS 
A. 
On October 18, 2016, the City and Contractor entered into a 
“License Agreement Shuttle Service – Pilot Program” agreement 
for 
professional 
services 
for 
the 
Contractor 
to 
provide 
transportation services as more particularly described therein 
(“License Agreement”); 
B. 
The Parties now desire to extend the term of the License 
Agreement to July 31, 2018.  
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Parties’ performance of the 
promises, covenants, and conditions stated herein, the Parties hereby amend 
the Agreement as follows: 
Section 1. 
The term of the License Agreement is hereby 
extended until July 31, 2018, unless earlier terminated. 
Section 2. 
Except as specifically amended by this Amendment 
No. 1, all other provisions of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect. 
 
IN WITNESS THEREOF, the Parties hereto have executed this 
Amendment No. 1 on the day and year first shown above.
