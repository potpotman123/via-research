RESOLUTION NO. 17-____ A RESOLUTION OF THE TOWN OF ERIE, COLORADO AUTHORIZING PAYMENTS TO VIA MOBILITY SERVICES FOR TRANSPORTATION SERVICES TO ERIE RESIDENTS IN 2017 ; AND SETTING FORTH DETAILS IN RELATION THERETO. WHEREAS, the 201 7 operating budget includes funds in the amount of $10,4 00.00 for VIA Mobility Services to provide transportation services to Erie residents ; and WHEREAS, the Board of Trustees of the Town of Erie believes it is in t he best interest of the Town to authorize such payment ; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE TOWN OF ERIE, COLORADO, AS FOLLOWS: Section 1. That the transportation services offered by VIA Mobility Services provide a valuable resource to residents of the Town of Erie ; Section 2. That the Town of Erie be and is hereby authorized and directed to make payment to VIA Mobility Services in the amou nt of $30,000.00 for such services; Section 3. That authorizing payment for these transportation services are found to be in the best interest of the Town of Erie and necessary for the preservation of the public health and safety. ADOPTED AND APPROVED THIS 10 th DAY OF JANUARY, 2017 BY THE BOARD OF TRUSTEES OF THE TOWN OF ERIE, COLORADO.
