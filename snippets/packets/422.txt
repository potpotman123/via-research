Exhibit A - UTA On Demand Microtransit and Other Innovative Mobility Services Scope of Work CONTINUING SERVICE: TRANSPORATION AS A SERVICE (TaaS) ON-DEMAND AND MICROTRANSIT The Contractor must plan, design, operate, and continuously improve on-demand and microtransit services utilizing TaaS models and other on-demand services as determined by UTA to support UTA’s Five-Year Service Plan . TaaS solutions must include a full turn-key solution which supports all aspects of a services including, but not limited to, contractor provided technology solutions (mobile app called UTA On Demand App), vehicles, vehicle maintenance and cleaning, drivers, and customer service capabilities. A service may be implemented as a pilot for testing concepts and various aspects of the service. The Contractor must provide the following services as part of a TaaS solution. Personnel The Contractor must provide all necessary personnel, including but not limited to, drivers, mechanics, administrative, customer service agents, service managers, and others to successfully operate a TaaS service and that meet the requirements itemized below. The Contractor must provide qualified drivers with good customer service skills, who are experienced in serving people with disabilities whether or not they use mobility devices, and who are trained as required by law for safe vehicle operations. The Contractor’s qualified and trained drivers must instill in customers confidence in the quality and safety of the service. The Contractor must establish minimum standards and driver qualification requirements. The Contractor must conduct a Utah Bureau of Criminal Identification (BCI) background check on all drivers, who must pass such check prior to their performing work on UTA contract services. The Contractor must implement a comprehensive drug and alcohol testing program for all drivers. This program must cover recruitment, hiring, and ongoing employment, ensuring full compliance with all applicable Federal Transit Administration (FTA) requirements. Key elements include but are not limited to adoption of clear policies and procedures, mandatory training for relevant personnel, regular random drug and alcohol testing, post-incident/accident drug and alcohol testing, and reasonable suspicion drug and alcohol testing. The Contractor is solely responsible for the independent implementation and management of its drug and alcohol program. UTA requires the ability to contact appropriate Contractor personnel regarding the drug and alcohol program. The Contractor must identify a Designated Employer Representative (DER) and/or a Drug &amp; Alcohol Program Manager (DAPM) for effective communication and service oversight. The Contractor must provide all technical training and support required for the provision of service that meets the requirements and standards of this contract, as well as any training required by UTA, and state and federal laws and regulations. The Contractor may provide some services with subcontractors. E.g., maintenance contracts. Such subcontractors and their qualifications must be included in this proposal, except that a subcontractor may be proposed during the contract term for UTA review and approval consistent with the section governing Addition of Subcontractors. The Contractor agrees to provide sufficient access to UTA and its contractors to inspect and audit records and information related to performance of the Contract as reasonably may be required. Vehicles The Contractor must provide all vehicles that are necessary to perform the requirements of this contract, such vehicles must meet all legal requirements, including but not limited to registration and emission inspections. The Contractor must be able to supply vehicles with specific fuel requirements as specified for each service order by UTA, including gasoline fueled vehicles, hybrid vehicles, and electric vehicles. Contractor must provide vehicles that meet ridership demand, service parameters, and that perform safely in weather conditions common to the Wasatch Front Region. The Contractor must be able to increase or reduce the number of vehicles to perform contract services as UTA determines requirements to meet changes in service demand or expansion. UTA will provide reasonable notice of such changes whenever possible. The Contractor must be able to temporarily shift vehicles across zones as needed to balance supply. All vehicles dedicated exclusively to UTA’s services must be Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) compliant wheelchair accessible vehicles (WAV). The Contractor is solely responsible for the maintenance of the service vehicles. Contractor must maintain service vehicles in a state of good repair that enables contractor to provide highly reliable service. The Contractor must regularly clean the vehicles to provide a pleasant environment for customers that is free of offensive odors and substances. The Contractor must register all permanent vehicles in Utah, pay Utah state registration fees, and affix Utah plates. As needed, the Contractor to provide enhanced cleaning procedures to ensure customer health and safety (e.g., support COVID-19 cleaning, and safety protocols as recommended by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). Parking / Vehicle Storage Facilities UTA will collaborate with the Contractor to identify appropriate parking lots or vehicle storage locations which UTA may own or lease for contractors use for this contract. In the event UTA and the Contractor are unable to identify an appropriate UTA controlled vehicle storage location, the Contractor will have sole responsibility to identify and lease or otherwise obtain use of an appropriate vehicle storage facility. Fuel The Contractor must include fuel costs in any TaaS pricing models. The Contractor must provide all fuel and charge the cost of fuel to UTA as a passthrough cost. The Contractor must report on fuel purchase and use by zone to enable National Transit Database (NTD) reporting and to fulfill UTA zone-specific billing requirements including but not limited to cost, gallons used, sales tax, and fuel spend without sales tax. Electric Vehicle Charging If the Contractor operates a service under this contract using an electric vehicle or fleet, UTA will collaborate with the Contractor to identify appropriate vehicle charging locations. Expense Responsibility: UTA is currently covering all electric vehicle charging costs because all designated charging locations utilize UTA-owned infrastructure. Contractors should not include electric charging expenses in their bids. The responsibility for payment of electric vehicle charging expenses will be determined on a case-by-case basis for any charging that occurs outside of UTA's designated infrastructure. Ride Booking Technologies The Contractor must provide a single customer-facing smartphone application (UTA On Demand Customer App) for a fully automated scheduling, dispatching and reserving demand responsive transit service as described in the section governing Software as a Service (SaaS) Solutions. All TaaS solutions must be managed through the UTA On Demand App that integrates all service options. This platform must be connected to UTA’s preferred trip planner, Transit App, for trip discovery. Customer Support Services The Contractor is required to develop and implement comprehensive Customer Support Services that provide to On Demand customers timely and effective information and actions such as may reasonably expected for the criticality and urgency of the services being provided. On Demand customers include IMZ clients such as Tooele County. The Contractor must provide Customer Support Services to respond to and to document customer feedback through a ticketing system. The Contractor must have a regular Customer Support Services Plan. The regular support plan must include: A critical issue escalation path and procedure for ad-hoc UTA support through the UTA Customer Service Department with processes for working with UTA for prompt and effective resolution when such coordination is required. A complaint resolution target timeline (e.g., 4 working days). Management of lost and found services. The ability to collect granular data regarding customer experience and satisfaction. This includes trend analysis of customer-provided quality ratings for the support they receive. The ability to audio record and review customer support calls. The Contractor must maintain ADA compliance and ability to communicate with, book on-demand, and pay for ride services for customers without a smartphone through telephone. The Contractor must provide customers access to the Contractor’s customer support services to book on-demand and to pay for ride services through telephone or web-based services for customers without a smartphone. Insurance The Contractor shall maintain insurance for all contract services and provide proof of said insurance to UTA. Insurance requirements and types are described in Part 4 of this RFP. Regulations and Compliance The Contractor is required to comply with all applicable state and federal laws and regulations, regardless of the allocation of federal funds, including but not limited to: FTA, Title VI, Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) regulations, FTA Drug and Alcohol Testing, FTA Mechanical Failure reporting, Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO), NTD reporting (e.g., NTD S-10 report, ridership data report, etc. Any legal requirements for safely operating a transportation service in the State of Utah and Federal Transit Authority (FTA). Any FTA data requirements need to be fulfilled by the Contractor. Any additional relevant regulation or requirements from the FTA Contractor Manual, 2025 (e.g. FTA Safety Management System (SMS), NTD Safety &amp; Security (SS40, SS50), Transit Asset Management (TAM), etc. as needed). In case of a manual update by FTA, the Contractor must be compliant with the current/latest adopted FTA guide/manual. Operating Plans and Policies The Contractor is required to develop and implement all plans and policies that are necessary for the efficient and effective performance of this contract. Such plans and policies must be updated from time to time as changing conditions require and must be available for UTA review upon request. The Contractor must develop and provide to UTA service implementation plans for all services provided. It will consist of the provider’s technical proposal and any additional information that may be needed to develop a complete and detailed scope of services, and any change orders, prior to the implementation of such services. The Contractor must develop and provide to UTA: All standard operating procedures, Safety plans and procedures, and Service evaluation plans. Policies and procedures must include provisions necessary to transport minors traveling with or without an adult as per UTA policy. Marketing and Communications UTA marketing personnel and marketing consultants will generally lead the creation of a marketing strategy and plan, customer acquisition plan, and any related marketing collateral. The Contractor shall collaborate in these efforts to ensure marketing material is on-brand, following best practices, and to share lessons learned from previous experience. UTA brand will be the default brand for the contract services and related vehicles, equipment, and other materials. UTA will consider proposals for a co-branded service or other branding and must approve any such proposals before implementation. CONTINUING SERVICE: SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE (SaaS) SOLUTIONS The Contractor must provide SaaS solutions including software, technologies, UTA On Demand App, mobile app solutions, UTA service support, and customer support services for TaaS solutions as described including on-demand and microtransit services, TNC operations, shuttles, and seat reservation services , and other services detailed in this Scope of Work. The customer facing technologies must be limited to a single app, except where UTA has explicitly approved an exception in writing, to provide a unified transportation system and experience which is simple and easy to use for the customer. The Contractor provided customer facing technology solution will be called the “UTA On Demand App” within this scope of services and associated contract. The Contractor must provide the itemized services as part of an integrated SaaS solution for UTA's contracted TaaS services, and not as stand-alone software. The Contractor will also provide SaaS licenses for Tooele County's independent use of the on-demand software. The licenses will include additional services used only by Tooele County, including dispatching and scheduling. In addition to the provision of the SaaS services, the Contractor must provide customers access to the Contractor’s customer support services to book on-demand and to pay for ride services through telephone or web-based services for customers without a smartphone. The Contractor must provide the following services as part of a SaaS solution. Transit App Integration The Contractor must design the UTA On Demand App in such a manner that ensures that the Transit App platform can clearly display the Contractor’s estimated trip cost and travel time to help customers make informed travel decisions. It is acceptable to use deep-linking from the Transit App, requiring the customer to download the native service app to request a ride and complete the booking process.
