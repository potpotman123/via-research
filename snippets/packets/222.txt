Extension And Amendment Of The Contract For Professional Services Purpose; Scope Each of Via Mobility LLC, a Delaware company with its principal office located at 10 Crosby Street, Floor 2 , New York, New York 10013 (“ Via ”), and Baldwin County Commission (“ Customer ”), hereinafter the “ Parties ,” entered into an agreement titled Contract for Professional Services (the “ Agreement ”) and a service order titled Baldwin County Deployment Service Order (the “ Original Order ”), dated June 18, 2020. By this Extension and Amendment Agreement (“ Extension ”) the parties agree towards continued collaboration of operation of the services provided under the Original Order. Amendment Duration This Extension shall extend the period of performance of the Agreement by a period of twelve months, from October 1, 2023 , until September 30, 2024 (inclusive). The Effective Date of this Extension shall be October 1, 2023. Conflict of Terms. Unless otherwise stated, all terms and conditions contained in the Original Order are also applicable to this Extension. If a term contained in this Extension is in conflict with the terms of the Original Order, the specific term in this Extension shall take precedence. Any term or condition in the Original Order, including all Appendices not affected by this Extension, shall remain in full force and effect. IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties hereto have caused this Extension and its accompanying Exhibits to be executed in duplicate as of the Effective Date.
