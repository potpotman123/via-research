CITY COUNCIL STAFF REPORT 
Meeting: August 20, 2019 
 
Subject 
Agreement with Nomad Transit LLC (Via Transportation Inc.) for the 18-month On-
Demand Community Shuttle Pilot Program. 
 
Recommended Action 
Authorize the City Manager to execute an agreement with Nomad Transit LLC (Via 
Transportation Inc.) for the 18-month On-Demand Community Shuttle Pilot Program 
with a not-to-exceed cost of $1,750,000. 
 
Discussion 
On June 18, 2019, as part of the Fiscal Year 2019-2020 Operating Budget, the City Council 
approved a budget request for implementation of an 18-month on-demand community 
shuttle with Via. City Council approved elimination of the formal competitive bid process 
for this contract since Nomad Transit LLC is the only known provider of on-demand, 
turn-key community shuttle services (transit vehicles, staffing, and management by one 
vendor). 
On June 18, 2019 three alternatives were presented to Council and Alternative A was 
approved, setting a base fare of $5, and a not-to-exceed amount of $1,750,000 for the 18-
month pilot program. Alternative A includes the use of ten transit vehicles, with hours of 
operation from 6am to 8pm Monday through Friday, and 9am to 5pm Saturday.  
 
Contract Negotiations 
Contract negotiations were conducted with Via shortly after Council approval in June 
2019. Primary negotiation points included indemnification language, limitation of 
liability, and City insurance requirements. The City’s project-specific insurance 
requirements are included as Exhibit D of the agreement, Attachment A.  
 
Next Steps 
Upon authorization of the proposed agreement Via will order the transit vehicles, a 
process estimated to take between six to eight weeks. Via will also begin vetting and hiring 
local drivers, and working with City staff on system planning details. City staff will work

Sustainability Impact 
An on-demand community shuttle service may lead to a reduction in Single Occupant 
Vehicle (SOV) miles traveled in Cupertino. A reduction in SOV miles traveled would 
reduce transportation-related greenhouse gas emissions (GHG), furthering the goals of 
the City’s Climate Action Plan. A community shuttle will make it easier for residents and 
employees to access the growing Bay Area transportation network, enabling more 
commuters to access Cupertino by public transit. Via vans will feature bicycle racks to 
help bicyclists make connections and also provide an alternative mode of transportation 
during inclement weather. Some of the Via fleet will be ADA vans, making it easier for 
mobility-impaired individuals to travel freely, and improving their quality of life.  
 
Fiscal Impact 
A Budget Adjustment of $1,750,000 for this program was approved with the Operating 
Budget for Fiscal Year 2019-2020.  No additional funding is needed.
