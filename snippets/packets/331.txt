Remix Proposal for Lexington, KY 
Prepared for Scott Thompson. By Matthew Guichard, Remix (matthew.guichard@remix.com).  Date: 16 Dec 2020  
What do I get 
with a Remix 
Mobility 
License? 
 
A full mobility management platform for your entire city staff. It’s an 
annual subscription for unlimited users, and includes: 
● 
Vehicle location ground truth and metrics: view dockless vehicle location and filter 
based on vehicle status, service provider, vehicle type, and geographic zone  
● 
Fleet distribution and trends via heatmap views  
● 
Understand the most popular spots in your city rider dropoff and deployment to 
inform parking zones  
● 
Set fleet cap and equity zone regulation and compliance rules to monitor over the 
course of your mobility program  
● 
Geofence policy zone creation: create zones to restrict or encourage 
parking/riding in specific geographic areas within your city 
● 
Trip path visualization by segment across your city based on the average daily 
usage based on a monthly average  
● 
Bike network analysis: Compare trip paths compared to current bike infrastructure 
to quickly identify key corridors of risk  
● 
Trip origin and destination: Understand where dockless trips start and end, based 
on custom geographic areas  
● 
Overall program metrics: utilization, total trips per day, average distance, average 
duration  
● 
Docked bikeshare support and key station metrics  
● 
Exports to CSV for further analysis
