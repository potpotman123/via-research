Remix Software Budget Increase
Subject:
Introduction
The Public Works Department (PWD) respectfully requests the Mayor and City Councilmembers to
authorize the City Manager to increase the second- and third-year contract amount with Remix Software,
Inc. (Remix) from $11,000.00 to $ 12,000.00.
Background
On August 12, 2020, the Mayor and City Councilmembers adopted Resolution No. 20-158 (approved 5-
0) authorizing the City Manager to enter into a three (3) year agreement with Remix Software, Inc. for
the transit platform in an amount not to exceed $ 14,000.00 for the first year and $11,000.00 in the seconc
and third year. The Public Works Department funded the first-year cost which included a one-time setup
fee of $2,000.00, the data fee of $1,000.00, and a $11,000.00 yearly fee. The subsequent second- and
third-year yearly fee was to be carried in the Information & Technology Department (IT) budget "Repair
Maintenance Office Equipment Other" Account, Account No. 001.22005.500464.
The IT budgeted $ 11,000.00 for Fiscal Year 2021-22 to cover the second-year cost of the Remix Software
and will budget funds to cover the cost for the third year, however when the item was originally presented
to the City Council for authorization, the PWD did not take into account the annual data fee of $1,000.00
in addition to the yearly $11,000.00 software fee, for a total fee of $ $12,000.00. As such, the PWD is
requesting to increase the approved contract amount with Remix for the second- and third-year contract
by a $1,000.00 per year to cover the cost of the annual data fee, for a total not to exceed amount of
