
DBPA and City introduced the Neighborhood Electric Vehicle service in 2015

Service offers free rides in vehicles wrapped in advertising

Council approved six-month pilot program on June 21, 2016
◦
Directed City Manager to select vendor; research Measure R as potential funding source
◦
Service area to Sepulveda (later Pacific); north-south to city limits

Vehicles stored and charged at Metlox Garage

City Manager’s office selected Downtowner to be vendor; contract signed in 
October of 2016

Pilot program launched on Feb. 1, 2017 with six vehicles operating out of the 
Metlox garage with hours from 11 a.m. – 11 p.m.

Program set to expire on July 31, 2017
