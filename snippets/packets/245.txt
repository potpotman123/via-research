Additional Backup Information:
This contract will provide unlimited licenses for the cloud-based street design software, Remix Street. Remix
Street is a planning platform for public transit that allows for collaboration between teams, provides tools to
develop new street design concepts, make data-driven decisions, and move early-stage infrastructure projects
forward. Some of Remix Street Technology’s unique specifications include initial concepts pulling in
OpenStreetMap data to auto-populate existing road conditions, opacity controls to clearly see satellite imagery
while designing, and advanced editing tools to create curves, tapers, and complicated intersections with
precision.
Via Transportation Inc./Remix Technologies LLC d/b/a Remix Technologies is the manufacturer and sole
distributor of Remix Street Technology.
