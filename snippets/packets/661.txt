Chandler City Council approves Chandler
Flex contract renewal
Home |  News Center
May 1, 2025
CHANDLER, Ariz – The Chandler City Council has approved a two-year contract
renewal for Chandler Flex, the city’s on-demand, shared transportation service.
The renewed agreement with River North Transit, LLC (Via) will extend service
from July 1, 2025, through June 30, 2027, at a cost not to exceed $3.315 million.
Chandler Flex was originally launched in July 2022 to provide residents with
convenient and flexible transit options.
