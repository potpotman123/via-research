Board agenda cont. 03/26/24
#10.
Untable Res#24-182, adopted March 15, 2024 Approve 2024 General Fund Budget
911:
#11
Approve a Letter of Understanding between Lorain County Board of Commissioners and Fraternal Order of 
Police/Ohio Labor Council (FOP-OLC) for the purpose of addressing Holiday Time use for employees at Lorain 
County 911
Community Development:
#12.
Implementing Sections 3735.65 – 3735.70 of the ORC establishing and describing the boundaries of Community 
Reinvestment Area in Elyria Township. Designate a housing officer to administer the program and create a 
Community Reinvestment Housing Council and a Tax Incentive Review Council.
Crime Lab:
#13.
Enter into a subgrant award Agreement with Ohio Department of Public Safety Ohio American Rescue 
Plan Funding 2022 Grant Program 2022-AR-CLB-911A in amount of $425,000.00. This will be applied 
towards new equipment to improve laboratory efficiency and decrease the backlog of testing for the 
Crime/Drug Lab. Authorize Administrator to execute document and will be paid from Acct#Public Safety-
Equipment-Crime Lab.
Job & Family Services:
#14
Amend Resolution No. 23-734, adopted October 17, 2023 authorizing Lorain County Safe Harbor to provide 
shelter for domestic violence victims and Domestic violence victim families in Lorain County using TANF and 
Title XX Funds. Said amendment is to increase the contract by $320,000.00 for the total contract amount not to 
exceed $512,500 and will be paid from Acct#TANF
Transit:
#15
Approve & enter into an agreement with River North Transit, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Via 
Transportation, Inc in the amount of $2,124,500 to provide a 1-year microtransit pilot project within the cities of 
Elyria and Lorain. The microtransit services will be provided within the cities of Elyria and Lorain, Monday 
through Friday from 6:30am to 6:30pm, effective July 8, 2024 through July 7, 2025 and will be paid from 
Acct#vehicle repair
#16.
Approve & enter into an agreement with Via Mobility, LLC for Software in the amount of $194,840 as a service 
license for Demand/Response scheduling and dispatching for a 3-year term. This software will provide riders with 
the ability to book their own trips through an app for scheduling Dial-A-Ride trips and will be paid from 
Acct#computer software
#17.
Approve & enter into an agreement with Remix Technologies, LLC in the amount of $68,000 for Software as a 
service license Route Planning & Scheduling Software for a 3-year term.  This software will allow restructure of 
its fixed route services as data is collected during the microtransit pilot project and will be paid from 
Acct#computer software
