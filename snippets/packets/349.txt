Introduction 
Ben Franklin Transit, (hereinafter referred to as BFT), a municipal corporation of the State of 
Washington, located at 1000 Columbia Park Trail, Richland, WA 99352, is soliciting proposals from 
qualified firms to provide the following services and requests listed below. 
BFT is soliciting sealed electronic proposals in response to Request for Proposal (RFP) 25-21 Rider 
Information & Trip Planning Platform Solution. Interested Contractors shall create a Free account with 
OpenGov Procurement by signing up at: procurement.opengov.com . Once you have completed account 
registration, browse back to: https://procurement.opengov.com/portal/bft and click on "Submit 
Response", then follow the instructions to submit the electronic proposal by the proposal due date. 
Proposals are due on Tuesday, December 9, 2025 no later than 2:00 pm Pacific Daylight Time (PDT). If a 
Contractor would like to submit a proposal via sealed hard copy, please send your request to the 
Procurement Manager and a pdf version of the RFP will be emailed to you in order to then submit your 
sealed proposal via mail or in person. It is the Contractors responsibility to make aware to the 
Procurement Manager that they prefer these notifications via email. 
1.1. Summary 
The selected vendor will provide a digital platform to improve the public transit rider experience by 
delivering real-time service information, trip planning, and two-way communication. The platform must 
offer mobile (iOS and Android) and web access, integrate with existing transit data systems, support 
agency planning needs, and provide tools for managing live service updates, including detours. Future 
integration with mobile ticketing is expected. 
1.2. Background 
BFT provides public transportation services in a 625 square mile area located in Benton County and 
Franklin County since 1981. This area includes the cities of Kennewick, Pasco, Richland, West Richland, 
Benton City, Prosser, and Finley, serving a population of approximately 285,000 residents. BFT sees 
roughly 12,000 fixed route boardings a day, and BFT's current trip planning solution has +5,000 active 
users. 
Current Service Profile 
BFT operates three modes of service: 
A. Fixed Route Service: Approximately 160 bus operators, 70 low-floor buses, ~55 Vehicles 
Operated in Maximum Service (VOMS), and an average of 12,000 unlinked passenger trips per 
day. Service span is currently 6 AM – 10 PM weekdays, 7 AM to 10 PM Saturdays, and 8 AM to 6 
PM on Sundays. 
B. ADA Paratransit Service: Dial-A-Ride service for individuals unable to utilize regular fixed-route 
bus services due to a disability or condition. 95 lift-equipped cutaway vehicles with an average 
of 54 live daily routes and 1000 daily passenger trips.
