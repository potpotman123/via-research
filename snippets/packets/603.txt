Request: Consider for the approval of a three (3) year contract renewal for transportation planning 
software with Remix Technologies LLC. for a total of $58,500 and authorize the City Managers staff 
to execute the agreement upon final approval by the City Attorney.    
 
Prior Council Action 
On September 9, 2021, City Council approved the original three (3) year contract with Remix 
Technologies LLC, at $17,500 per year for a total of $52,500. 
 
Background 
 
A. 
Remix Technologies is a software platform that has multiple functions and is used for transit 
providers. The platform was designed by transit professionals for transit administrations.   
 
B. 
Remix Technologies platform is a planning software that assists Galveston Island Transit in 
route design/alterations, modeling projects and the cost effects of each route, and Title VI 
data collection and analysis. Remix Technologies was a vital data source in GIT’s funding 
analysis with TxDOT split-letter process.  
 
C. 
The platform allows transit agencies to conduct several modeling projects and see the effects 
of each route, each bus stop has in terms of costs, demand, headways and efficiencies of the 
route itself.

D.  
The platform pulls data from different sources that would include, census, demographics, 
demand modeling, American Community survey and transit cost structures in layers for 
transportation planning.  
 
E. 
Remix Technologies is the only software provider that pulls all the information into one 
accessible platform that allows a transit agency to conduct a route study/modeling project 
in-house without the added expense of an outside consultant.  
 
F.  
Remix Technologies offers an annual service option. Galveston Island Transit  has requested 
to a three-year renewal contract similar to the original contract. The total cost of the contract 
would be $58,500. Remix Technologies is a sole source provider (attached).  
 
 
 
Fiscal Impact Report 
 
The contract would be renewed for three (3) years and would be funded through federal/state 
formula funding. The matching requirement would be $29,500.  The matching requirement would 
come from state funding and from GIT other transportation generated revenue. i.e. Charters, Parking 
Fees, Advertising revenue.
