Our demand response software delivers an exceptional experience for all users — riders,
drivers, and backend staff — and the necessary flexibility to meet Bay Transit’s evolving
transit needs. Our dedicated project team and expert advisors will continually work with
Bay Transit to achieve its goal of streamlining transit operations and enhancing the user

Our proposal for Bay Transit is centered on:
A Single End-to-End Platform for On-Demand and Demand Response Services
Via’s suite of familiar and user-friendly tools will provide Bay Transit with a single unified
interface to manage all of its on-demand and demand response services, eliminating the
​need for Bay Transit administrators to learn an entirely new system. Our software suite,
which currently powers Bay Transit Express in the Gloucester Courthouse area, includes
three core components: our white-labeled, fully automated Rider App, designed with user
experience and accessibility at the forefront; our Driver App, which provides real-time
turn-by-turn navigation and a hands-free display; and the Via Operations Center, a
highly automated administrative platform that Bay Transit staff will use to manage
services in real time. Through Via’s software, administrators will be able to seamlessly
visualize and analyze the services thanks to a suite of fully integrated dashboards,
reports and data tables.

Bay Transit Requirement
Via
Scheduling
The software shall be enabled for demand response and/or on-demand rides, including ride scheduling
and dispatch.
System shall provide configurable demand response service scheduling business rules, such as ride
distance or advanced ride date restrictions.
Software shall allow transit provider dispatchers to see all rides scheduled and dispatched.
Software shall allow dispatchers to reschedule, cancel and/or reassign rides, as necessary.
Software shall have the capabilities to flag riders that consistently no-show for rides, provide a
list/notification of these riders to Bay Transit, and limit these riders’ access and ability to use the system.
Software shall have the ability to identify operators’ and vehicles’ availability for scheduling (e.g.,
operators on break and vehicles not active due to vehicle service/maintenance), to reduce potential for
mis-scheduling a ride to an unavailable operator or vehicle.
Software shall allow dispatchers to aggregate rides provided riders have a similar pick-up location or
destination.
Software shall allow dispatchers to schedule rides for riders that have subscriptions (standing orders).
Software shall allow the ability to assign vehicles to multiple regions.
Software shall update estimated time of arrival as well as the rider manifest based on ride requests and
real-time travel information.

Service Planning and Design: Each community has unique goals and
passenger needs, so our launch process is anchored around a
collaborative input gathering stage that includes simulation studies, a
review of Bay Transit’s technical requirements, and alignment on
performance and reporting goals. The result is a service design plan that is completely
customized to suit Bay Transit’s needs, and which allows Via's implementation specialists
to configure the Via system according to Bay Transit’s goals.
Testing and Training: In advance of launch, Via's launch team will install
Via's Driver App, test with drivers in vehicles, and conduct training
workshops for Bay Transit staff to ensure they are comfortable using Via's
software from day one. Via-provided marketing materials can also support
the Bay Transit’s pre-launch community outreach and marketing efforts to
ensure passengers are aware of the service transition.
Launch Support: We understand how challenging it can be to transition
software providers, and we also recognize the importance of face-to-face
conversations to facilitate trust in the new system. Via is pleased to commit
to on-the-ground support for Bay Transit’s staff during the immediate
launch period. This in-person support will be supplemented by our robust partner support
team, including a dedicated Partner Support Manager and 24/7 technical support.
4.5.2 Data Conversion from Routematch to Via
As part of our launch process, we will migrate all data from Bay Transit’s existing systems
into Via’s platform in order to enable access through the VOC for report and data
generation. Via’s in-house Data Science team will work directly with staff to conduct a
proven, four-step data migration process:
1.
Knowledge sharing & data scoping
2. Data evaluation
3. Initial data migration, quality assurance (QA), and validation
4. Full migration, QA, validation, and field testing
This process will ensure a successful migration of data to the Via Platform, and enable us
to convert Bay Transit’s service with minimal disruptions.

4.6 Technical Support
From our experience as operators of transit services, we understand that consistent
service delivery is incredibly important for Bay Transit riders, drivers, and staff. Our
software has been designed to function with minimal intervention so that our partners can
focus more on innovating their transit systems and less on solving technical problems. To
deliver on this promise, our global staff includes over 500 software engineers who are
committed to building reliable, user-friendly software. The result is a highly available
and consistent platform for our partners, delivering 99.9% uptime. In the event that Bay
Transit riders, drivers, and staff need support, we have developed robust materials and
processes to ensure prompt incident response and resolution:
4.6.1 Written Materials and Guides
As a part of the implementation and training
process, Via works with our partners to
build a suite of tailored materials regarding
the use of the platform and various features
(for more details, see section 4.5.3 Training
Plan). Bay Transit will continue to have easy
access to these materials afterwards, so
that staff may quickly reference them at any
time when they have questions.
4.6.2 24/7 Technical Support
Bay Transit will have access to 24/7 technical assistance through our support platform. A
rotating team of support engineers is on call at all times to resolve any problems that may
arise. Incidents are escalated using a predefined alert protocol, which instantly notifies
the on-call member of the team. The technical team member will immediately examine
the issue to begin the process of issue resolution and resource allocation. All issues will
be resolved based on their level of severity and associated response times, and Via will
provide Bay Transit with regular status updates on resolution.
