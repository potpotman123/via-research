Introduction
This memorandum provides staff's recommendation for the Mayor and the City Council-Members to
authorize the City Manager to enter into a Contract Agreement with Remix Software, Inc. (Remix) for a
three (3) year period in an amount not to exceed the Departmental budgeted funds. The Remix Software
will facilitate the development of transit alignment analysis, will help create public transportation routes,
will help project ridership, and develop schedules based on GPS roadway data. Additionally, the Public
Works Department (PWD) is requesting to transfer $14,000.00 from Transportation Fund "Construction

Background
Historically when developing, modifying, and evaluating trolley routes, schedules, stops, potential
passenger access, and costs, the Public Works staff has relied on a number of software programs including
Excel, Google Earth, and Census data to perform the analysis. In addition, the Public Works Department
(PWD) has also relied on the assistance of consultants to further refine the transit routes from a planning
concept to an implementable reality. However, this method of transit planning has become antiquated
with the development of Remix. Remix is a cloud-based software that provides a transit platform that
brings together the complete transportation picture into a single program. Remix's Transit Platform
provides quick bus route design and analysis capacities through an internet browser interface, including:
map-based route and stop planning, stop-based demographic analysis, operating cost calculations based on
both frequencies and timetables, and customizable accessibility visualizations and analytics.
