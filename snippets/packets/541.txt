EXPLANATION: 
This amendment will be for up to three additional 
years. The annual costs, inclusive of sales tax, are 
detailed as follows: $54,725.00 for 2024, 
$58,520.00 for 2025, and $62,590 for 2026.  
 
BACKGROUND:  
 
In 2018 the Chief Executing Officer authorized the sole-source selection of the Remix software (now provided by 
Via Software). 
 
The Pierce Transit Planning Department uses Remix for fixed route scenario planning and testing of potential routes.  
It allows planning to plan a route, and then identify predictive schedule and fleet requirements. This software is 
also used for long range planning and coordination with partner agencies. Another module that is part of this 
agreement is the Remix on Demand Planning License.  This allows planning to predict use of potential new on-
demand service areas as well as manage existing Runner Zones.  This contract will combine both maintenance 
agreements together under one contract.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION: 
 
Staff recommends increasing the contract amount by $175,835.00 to extend the years of service on this contract 
so that the agency can continue to conduct essential fixed route and on demand planning scenarios of existing and 
potential routes.  
 
ALTERNATIVES: 
 
Do not authorize the contract extension and stop using Remix.  This is not recommended as it would remove access 
to a tool that our planning department relies upon. 
 
PROPOSED MOTION: 
 
Move to: Authorize the Chief Executive Officer to increase to the contract spending authority by $175,835 with Via 
Transportation, Contract No. 1214, to extend the service years of the contract for a total contract spending authority 
of $321,631.50.
