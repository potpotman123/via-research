Approval of Consent Agenda 
 
SBD #23-14 – Ordinance authorizing Change Order No. 5 to Contract No. 233878  with WEX, 
Inc. for third party administration (TPA) of health benefit plans.  This change order extends the 
contract for six months, and increases the contract total from $920,381.50 to $997,942.50, an 
increase of $77,561.00. 
 
Director McLeod moved, and Director Schielke seconded a motion to approve the Consent 
Agenda. The motion passed with the following roll call vote: Directors Arfa, Canning, Carr, 
Guerin, Hastings, Marcucci, McLeod, Noak, Schielke, Smith, Soto, Wells, and Chairman 
Kwasneski. All directors voted aye. 
 
Action Items 
 
SBD #23-15 – Ordinance authorizing the award of a contract to River North Transit LLC, a 
wholly owned subsidiary of Via Transportation, Inc. for a Transportation Network Company 
(TNC) pilot project for late-night first mile/last mile rideshare services from Harvey to O’Hare in 
an amount not to exceed $1,362,055.00. 
 
Erik Llewellyn presented on the pilot, describing the service gaps in Routes 332 and 352 that the 
pilot addresses, connections to Pace and CTA, zone boundaries, and hours. Discussion ensued:  
• Chairman Kwasneski raised concerns about branding vans with the Pace logo if drivers 
are allowed to operate the vehicles for personal use. Director Smith agreed. 
• Director Marcucci asked staff to start thinking about how Pace could provide west 
suburban residents opportunity and access to jobs now that Western Access O’Hare 
project, which was created so the benefits of economic activity at O’Hare could spill out 
west, will finish next year. Erik said Pace would revisit a study conducted with several 
Pace partners to identify potential public transit opportunities in the 390 corridor.  
• Director Smith reminded everyone that Pace is routinely innovative. She applauded the 
pilot for filling important service gaps and helping the financial well-being of residents.  
• Director Wells said this is something that the Harvey area desperately needs. He asked 
for clarification on the implementation schedule. Melinda and Erik provided a range 
between 12-24 weeks but will push for summer at the request of Director Wells.
