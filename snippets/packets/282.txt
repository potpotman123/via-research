EXHIBIT A 
 
ON-DEMAND RIDESHARE SERVICE SCOPE OF SERVICES 
 
 
1. Purpose; Scope 
 
Contractor will provide on-demand rideshare services as described below (the “Service”) for the 
City and its residents and visitors. Contractor’s Services include: 
 
● Access to Via’s fully localized dynamic vehicle routing and real-time passenger 
aggregation system; 
● Establishing relationship with vehicle rental company (“Vehicle Partner”) to provide 
access to Vehicles on a rental basis to independent contractor driver partners; 
● Accompanying technical and operational support services in order to provide a turnkey 
solution; 
● Data sharing and reporting as described under Section 9 below;  
● Access to the Via mobile application for users to book rides from a smartphone;  
● Integration of the Arlington Student Transportation Program as described under Section 
10 below;  
● Integration of the Arlington RAPID service as described under Section 11 below; and 
● Advertising services as described under Section 12. 
  
2. Duration 
 
The Service will begin on December 11, 2022, unless otherwise mutually agreed by both parties. 
The Service shall operate until December 31, 2024, with the option of four 2-year renewals, 
subject to earlier termination in accordance with the Contract.

6. Fleet 
 
A. General Fleet Plan 
 
Contractor will cause a fleet of a sufficient fleet of vans to be deployed for the Service, and to be 
custom branded using magnets or vehicle decals so as to be easily recognizable. The number of 
vans included in the fleet may be increased or decreased upon request by the City and agreement 
of both Parties.  Vehicle types must be approved by the City prior to being added to the fleet. 
Qualified and accredited independent contractor drivers will be able to gain access to these 
vehicles after being registered onto the Via system, enabling these individuals to sign up for 
specific daily driving sessions or longer periods of work.  
 
As necessary, Contractor may also establish a means for independent contractor drivers to drive 
on the Via platform using their own qualified vehicles, for instance, during times of high demand 
when the fleet cannot meet total demand. The decision to begin utilizing independent contractor 
driver’s personal vehicles must be approved by City. 
 
For all drivers on the Service, Contractor shall ensure that driver registration and certification 
protocols are compliant with relevant City and FTA guidelines (as outlined in Sections I, J and N 
of the Contract), including drug & alcohol regulations. Contractor shall create processes 
designed to ensure that any vehicles used in the delivery of service during the Service are kept 
clean and service-ready and meet the City guidelines outlined in Section I of the Contract. 
 
City and Contractor anticipate adding autonomous vehicles to the Service fleet in a limited area 
pursuant to Section 11, below.  Contractor will ensure that any operators, whether in person or 
remote, are qualified and accredited, and comply with all relevant City and FTA guidelines, 
including any drug & alcohol regulations.  All autonomous vehicles added to the fleet must meet 
at least the same standards as non-autonomous vehicles in the current Via fleet, to the extent 
applicable taking into consideration the nature of such autonomous vehicles.

shall provide notice thereof to City.
