CONTRACT AMENDMENT 
Paratransit and/or Fixed-Transit Scheduling and Dispatch Software # 283 
 
AMENDMENT NUMBER: 1 
 
This amendment is made to Paratransit and/or Fixed-Transit Scheduling and Dispatch Software # 283 
(Contract) between the State of North Dakota, acting through its Office of Management and Budget 
(STATE), and Via Mobility LLC (CONTRACTOR). 
 
The parties agree to the following terms and conditions and expressly agree that if any of the following 
terms and conditions conflict with any of the terms and conditions of the Contract, then, notwithstanding 
any term in the Contract, the following terms and conditions govern and control the rights and 
obligations of the parties.   
 
The parties agree to amend the Contract as follows:  
 
Renewal  
 
Contract Section 7, Term of Contract. The Contract commenced on its effective date and ends on July 
31, 2025 with up to four (4) options to renew the contract for a period of 12 months each. 
 
The parties have agreed to renew the Contract for an additional 12 months; therefore, the Contract is 
amended to change the expiration date to July 31st, 2026. The Contract has 3 renewals remaining. 
The Contract can be extended for an additional 12 months. 
 
The parties agree that the terms and conditions of the Contract and this Amendment govern and control 
the rights and obligations of the parties.   
 
All other terms of the Contract remain in effect. This Contract Amendment is not effective until fully 
executed by all parties.
