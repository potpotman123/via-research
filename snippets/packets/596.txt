2023 Public Transit Advisory Board Annual Report 
 
 
Service Reduction and Changes: 
Service reductions due to driver shortage has been SAM’s reality, along with the rest of the nation.  
SAM did experience a greater number of applicants in 2023, bringing the system closer to full driver 
capacity.   
The 41st Street and I-29 Interchange construction project will impact routes 10 and 11 for 
approximately two years.  Work was done to mitigate the impact for those traveling to Roosevelt 
High School. 
Management Agreement RFP: 
After considerable review and collaboration with several city departments, an RFP for the 
management services for Sioux Area Metro was released in July 2023.  J. Tschetter and J. 
Johannsen, PTAB members, served on the RFP selection process. 
First Transit (now TransDev) has provided management services for over 30 years.  The direction 
the RFP was focusing on was for a ‘turnkey’ operation, with a focus on technology and data-driven 
decision making.   
There were two proposals submitted under this RFP, TransDev and Via Transportation.  In-person 
interviews took place with the selection team and supporting city team members.  There was an 
opportunity for a joint venture, if either or both entities were interested, such as one company 
contracting with the Fixed Route system, the other with Paratransit and Microtransit services. 
The contract was awarded to Via Transportation as a turnkey operation in September 2023, for a 
management change on 1/1/2024.   Via Transportation choose to retain the entire system services 
under their management.  Contract negotiations were based on service hours per system (Fixed 
Route, Paratransit, and Microtransit), with KPI’s to be determined.

2023 Public Transit Advisory Board Annual Report 
 
SAM Reimagined 
The Sioux Falls transit system has operated under the management of First 
Transit/TransDev for approximately 30 years with the management contract due for renewal 
on 1/1/2024.  This business model includes First Transit as the management company for 
the deployment of the transit system, with the General Manager being an employee of First 
Transit, and the balance of the operations team as subsidiary corporation, Su Tran.  Day-to-
day operations, safety, security, and federal guidelines are their responsibility.   
Under this model, the City of Sioux Falls (COSF) Planning and Development team has 
played a very direct and involved role in budget planning, route planning, statistical 
analysis, system improvements and opportunities, including introducing microtransit to 
Sioux Falls.  Limited staff time, financial constraints, and understanding there was 
community demand for an improved system prompted the city of Sioux Falls to use this 
contract renewal opportunity to “Reimagine Transit”.   
Through the RFP process, two proposals were submitted: First Transit/TransDev and Via 
Transportation.  The RFP asked the proposers to think outside of the box, with a focus on 
improved technology, opportunity, and efficiencies. 
This is an exciting journey to revamp COSF’s transit system with SAM Reimagined. Via 
Transportation ‘s turnkey proposal, focusing on microtransit integration and leveraging 
technology, presents a promising shift towards more efficient and accessible public 
transportation. 
Retaining the existing SAM team demonstrates a commitment to continuity while 
embracing change.  The addition of ADA vans for Phase 1 of Microtransit service shows a 
dedication to inclusivity and accessibility, crucial aspects of any modern transit system. 
The Fixed Route Redesign, with its emphasis on more direct routes, increases frequency, 
and the integration of GIS technology through the SAM app, is a significant step towards 
improving the rider’s experience.  Renaming routes with a color theme and updating 
signage/maps will enhance clarity and ease of use for passengers.  This process has 
followed the required public engagement, implementation of feedback and will be fully 
deployed by fall of 2024. 
Looking ahead to the Fall 2024 Plan, the full implementation of the Fixed Route Redesign 
and the integration of Microtransit across the city will further expand transit accessibility 
and convenience for residents.  Diversifying the bus fleet to accommodate these changes 
reflects a forward-thinking approach to meeting evolving transportation needs. 
Overall, SAM Reimagined holds great promise for enhancing public transportation in Sioux 
Falls, fostering community connectivity, and contributing to the city’s overall quality of life.
