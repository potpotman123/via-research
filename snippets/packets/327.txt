12. 
Contract Modification: Microtransit Project Contract Extension 
 
8:55 a.m.  A briefing paper was included in the meeting materials, presenting to the Operations 
Committee, for discussion, acceptance, and referral to the Board of Commissioners for approval, 
a recommendation that the President and CEO be authorized to approve a contract modification 
with Via, for the continued operation of microtransit service, for a one-year extension (through 
May 31, 2026), in an amount not to exceed $6,344,000. Chuck Stewart, Executive Vice President 
and Chief Operation Officer – Metro Transit, provided an overview of this item, stating that Via 
has been instrumental, serving as a safety net for Metro’s fixed route system.  He noted that Via 
serves to supplement the paratransit system, as demand continues to increase.  Mr. Stewart 
reported an increase from 5,000 rides per week to 8,000 rides per week.  He noted that, currently 
Metro has drivers available; however, there is a need for vans.  He stated that there is still a need 
for additional mechanics, and Via is helping fill the void during this period.  Mr. Stewart noted 
that the Agency is working on a future plan for microtransit, and will be working on a request for 
proposals for this in the future.  He noted that currently, management is requesting an extension 
of the current Via contract, through May 2026.       
 
 
A motion to approve this agenda item, as presented, was made by Commissioner Johnson and 
seconded by Commissioner Golliday.   
 
 
The motion passed.

5. 
Contract Award – Stockless Office Supplies 
9:30 a.m.  A briefing paper was included in the meeting materials, requesting that the Audit, 
Finance and Administration Committee accept, and forward to the Board of Commissioners for 
approval, this request to authorize the President & CEO to renew the Sourcewell Participating 
Agreement with Staples Contract & Commercial LLC, who was awarded the contract as a result 
of a competitive procurement process and is most advantageous to BSD with price and other 
factors considered, for a five-year (2025-2029) timeframe, in a not-to-exceed amount of $1 
million.  William A. Dee, Vice President, Procurement and Inventory Management, gave an 
overview of this item.  He noted that in December 2011, the then President & CEO of BSD 
signed a contract modification, which allowed BSD to continue using Staples as BSD’s office 
supply vendor through joining a purchasing alliance called National Joint Powers Alliance 
(NJPA), and on June 6, 2018, the National Joint Powers Alliance (NJPA) changed its name to 
Sourcewell. Mr. Dee reported that, in February 2020, BSD’s Internal Audit conducted an audit of 
the stockless office supplies NJPA agreement and recommended Procurement to submit a 
briefing paper to obtain Committee and Board Approval every five (5) years to remain a member 
of the National Joint Powers Alliance (NJPA), now Sourcewell.   
 
Mr. Dee stated that Sourcewell was created by state law as a service cooperative to provide 
programs and services to education and government agencies, and it was established with the 
statutory purpose of assisting public agencies in meeting specific needs that are more efficiently 
delivered cooperatively than by an entity individually.  He reported that stockless office needs for 
the Agency are ordered via authorized BSD employees, through the Staples Advantage ordering 
platform. BSD’s past spending from 2022 through 2024 has been approximately $100,000 to 
$122,000 per year ($122,000 x 5 = $610,000); therefore, BSD anticipates the next five-year total 
spend to not exceed $1 million.  He stated that management is recommending that that the 
Finance, Audit and Administration Committee accept, and forward to the Board of 
Commissioners for approval, this request to authorize the President & CEO to renew the 
Sourcewell Participating Agreement with Staples Contract & Commercial LLC, who was 
awarded the contract as a result of a competitive procurement process and is most advantageous 
to BSD with price and other factors considered, for a five-year (2025-2029) timeframe, in a not-
to-exceed amount of $1 million. 
 
A motion to approve this item, as presented, was made by Commissioner Beach and seconded by 
Commissioner Johnson.   
 
The motion passed unanimously.

A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS  
OF THE BI-STATE DEVELOPMENT AGENCY  
OF THE MISSOURI-ILLINOIS METROPOLITAN DISTRICT  
MODIFYING A CONTRACT WITH RIVER NORTH TRANSIT, LLC  
D/B/A VIA TRANSPORTATION, INC FOR MICROTRANSIT SERVICES   
 
 
 
PREAMBLES: 
 
Whereas, The Bi-State Development Agency of the Missouri-Illinois Metropolitan District (the “Agency”/ 
“BSD”) is a body corporate and politic, created by an interstate compact between the States of Missouri and 
Illinois, acting by and through its Board of Commissioners (the “Board of Commissioners”); and  
 
Whereas, the Agency is authorized by Mo. Rev. Stat. § 70.370 et seq. and 45 Ill. Comp. Stat. 100/1 et seq. 
(jointly referred to herein as the “Compact”) to plan, construct, maintain, own and operate passenger 
transportation facilities, and to perform all other necessary and incidental functions, and to disburse funds for 
its lawful activities, to adopt rules and regulations for the proper operation of its passenger transportation 
facilities and conveyances, to contract and to be contracted with; and  
 
Whereas, Board Policy Chapter 50.010, Section G. Purchasing. requires the Board of Commissioners to 
approve modifications which modify the approved contract; and  
Whereas, Metro Transit has continued to rely on microtransit to provide transit coverage in areas MetroBus 
could not operate, and provide a safety net for vulnerable communities at risk for significantly reduced 
transportation services; and  
Whereas, while bus operator hiring and retention increased successfully through 2024, the availability of 
vehicle mechanics has not grown at the same rate; and 
Whereas, Via has proved to be a nimble and cost-effective way to meet transit demand in these areas where 
Metro does not have enough resources to sustain its service; and  
Whereas, Via’s flexible service model has provided the opportunity to explore commingling general 
microtransit customer trips with paratransit customer trips that exceed Call-A-Ride’s current operating 
capacity; and  
Whereas, it is feasible, necessary and in the public interest for the Agency for the Board of Commissioners 
to authorize the President and CEO to execute a contract modification with River North Transit, LLC, dba 
Via Transportation, Inc. for the continued operation of microtransit service, for a one-year extension (through 
May 31, 2026), in an amount not to exceed $6,344,000, in accordance with the terms and conditions described 
herein. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF THE BI-STATE DEVELOPMENT 
AGENCY OF THE MISSOURI-ILLINOIS METROPOLITAN DISTRICT DOES HEREBY RESOLVE, 
DETERMINE AND ORDER AS FOLLOWS:  
 
Section 1. 
Findings.  The Board of Commissioners hereby finds and determines those matters set forth 
in the preambles hereof as fully and completely as if set out in full in this Section 1.  
 
Section 2. 
Approval of the Modification of the Contract.  The Board of Commissioners hereby 
authorizes the President and CEO to execute a contract modification with River North Transit, LLC, dba Via 
Transportation, Inc. for the continued operation of microtransit service, for a one-year extension (through May 31, 
2026), in an amount not to exceed $6,344,000, under and pursuant to this Resolution and the Compact for the 
authorized Agency purposes set forth in the preambles hereof and subject to the conditions hereinafter provided.
