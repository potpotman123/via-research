Transit:
#26
Approve & enter into an agreement with River North Transit, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Via 
Transportation, Inc in the amount not to exceed $2,200,000 to extend micro transit service for 1-year within the 
cities of Elyria and Lorain . Transportation will run Monday through Friday from 6:30am to 6:30pm, effective 
July 15, 2025 through July 31, 2026 and will be paid from Acct#contractual/purchased services and vehicle repair
& maintenance.  
#27
Approve & enter into an agreement on behalf of Lorain County Transit with Via Mobility LLC in amount not to 
exceed $48,000 to Assist in the Development of a Transportation Development Plan. TDP addresses the needs of 
the public while allowing Lorain County Transit to sustain service and strategically expand its services. The plan 
will evaluate program operations and recommend realistic, fiscally responsible strategies to be implemented over 
the short term. This will be paid from Acct#professional services.
#28.
Approve & enter into a MOU on behalf of Lorain County Transit with NOACA, the Metropolitan Planning 
Organization. This agreement outlines the planning activities to be performed in conjunction with NOACA, Ohio 
Department of Transportation (ODOT) and Lorain County Transit.
