RESOLUTION NO.  25-45
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF NORWALK 
ADOPTING THE BUDGET AMENDMENT TO SUPPORT SERVICES FOR 
THE MICROTRANSIT/ON-DEMAND PILOT PROGRAM
WHEREAS, the City of Norwalk is committed to providing productive and quality 
service on all of its transit routes; and
WHEREAS, the City of Norwalk aims to reduce traffic congestion, lower emissions, 
and improve equitable access to transportation; and
WHEREAS, the City of Norwalk seeks to expand public transit to prioritize older 
adults and community members living with a disability while providing service to the 
general public; and 
WHEREAS, a Microtransit/On-Demand service is a cost-effective alternative to 
fixed-route buses in low-density zones that solves first-mile, last-mile requirements; and 
WHEREAS, the Microtransit/On-Demand fare policy will remain the same as 
Norwalk Transit System’s Fixed Route service adult base fare of $1.25 for duration of the 
pilot program. 
NOW, THEREFORE, THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF NORWALK 
HEREBY DETERMINES, FINDS, AND RESOLVES AS FOLLOWS:
