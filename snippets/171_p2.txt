ACTION STATEMENT: 
Approve the agreement for On-Demand Transit Software as a Service (SaaS) 
with Via Transportation, Inc. in an amount not to exceed $248,880 pursuant to 
City of Key West Code of Ordinances Sections 2-797 (4) b.  exempt 
contractual services and commodities. 
 
BACKGROUND: 
The FY 2021-22 Transit Budget allocated $45,000 for On-Demand Transit 
service for a pilot program.  In June of 2021, City staff identified a potential 
vendor; however, during the vetting process, several issues were unresolvable.   
In November of 2021, a Federal Transit Administration (FTA) Notice of Funding 
Opportunity (NOFO) for Enhancing Mobility Innovation was issued.  City staff 
determined this potential funding source could accelerate the On-Demand 
Transit project timeline with more beneficial cost share of 80% Federal and 20% 
City, rather than the 50% FDOT and 50% City.  A grant application was 
submitted to FTA. 
 
PURPOSE AND JUSTIFICATION: 
Given the short duration of the pilot program and the need to make prompt, 
extensive transit improvements for the riding public, staff is requesting a 
competitive bid exemption per Section 2-797(4)b.  On-Demand Transit allows 
for the City of Key West to use its transit resources more efficiently.  Transit 
vehicles within the city area will eventually operate only with trip requests 
thus eliminating empty or near empty vehicles from continuous circling of set 
routes.  Fuel savings, better personnel utilization given a 55% shortage of 
CDL drivers, and reduction in carbon emissions are a few of the anticipated 
positive impacts.  Developing new efficient fixed-route, circulator, and/or 
express service based on the On-Demand Transit usage analysis is another 
potential benefit.

City staff identified and successfully vetted Via Transportation, Inc. as an On-Demand Transit 
service vendor.  Via Transportation, Inc. is able to provide a one-year pilot program with all the 
City’s requirements such as installation, training, marketing, call center, and trip analytics.  Via 
Transportation, Inc is the parent company of a subsidiary the City of Key West currently uses 
for transit planning Software as a Service (SaaS). 
 
Staff has contacted other Florida transit agencies who currently use Via Transportation, Inc. 
and received positive recommendations of the On-Demand services.  Those entities include 
Miami-Dade County-Department of Transportation and Public Works, Palm Beach County- 
PalmTran, and St. Lucie County- Area Regional Transit.
