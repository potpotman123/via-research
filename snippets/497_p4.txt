Trip Prioritization and Prevention of Mobility Mode Competition: the technology used allows GO
Connect to prioritize rides connecting to transit. This recent offering is uniquely positioned to
ensure the on-demand services do not take away ridership from the current transit network but
rather enhances first- and last-mile connectivity. The logic used is primarily geared around target
wait-time, walking distance, and number of transfers among other parameters established by
DTPW. This unique feature of the GO Connect app allows the service to only act as a “feeder
system,” prioritizing rides connecting to transit corridors. The existing County transit app, GO
Miami-Dade Transit, does not have an intermodal booking integration and cannot prevent mode
competition.
Since launching on October 28, 2020, GO Connect has seen a massive growth in demand (despite the 
impacts of COVID-19). To date, the service has completed over 174,500 rides across the four service 
zones (Town of Cutler Bay, Dadeland/South Miami, West Kendall, and Civic Center) and provided rides 
to over 2,300 unique riders. GO Connect ridership has increased over 60% in the past year, reaching its 
highest ridership in March 2023, with over 20,000 ride requests and 11,983 completed rides. Currently, 
GO Connect provides over 500 rides during weekdays, contributing to traffic reduction while serving as a 
feeder service for the County’s fixed transit system. 
In March 2023, GO Connect received 9,165 more ride requests and served 4,633 more rides than in 
March 2022; this was a 63% increase in monthly ridership and 82% growth in monthly requests. This 
increasing trend is indicative of the significant continual growth observed since GO Connect launched, 
exceeding the existing scope of the contract. The service is under significant pressure to meet rider 
demand as it continues to grow, with over 10% of rides being denied on a daily basis due to high demand. 
This item as well as the future competitive solicitation for a replacement long-term award addresses the 
need to prevent service disruption in the current commuting and transit patterns. 
Additionally, GO Connect has provided more convenient and affordable service options while improving 
access to major transit corridors. According to a rider survey conducted in August 2022, about 93% of the 
responders would be disappointed if GO Connect were to no longer be an option. Survey data indicated 
that about 42% of survey respondents make under $25,000 a year, suggesting that GO Connect is 
providing an affordable transportation option for groups that would otherwise have limited access to 
mobility. According to the ridership data, about 30% of the rides are made to and from Metrorail and 
TransitWay stations, increasing access to major transit corridors while serving first- and last-mile trips. 
Additional expenditure authority is needed to support DTPW in establishing a billable hourly rate of $57.50 
for the hours of service that will enhance the scope defined in Appendix A, Section 2.3 of the contract – 
adding the cost of Dadeland weekend service; adding vehicles to meet growth in ridership and including 
pricing and budget for May Mobility Autonomous Vehicles and Transit app; and adding the budget for the 
deployment of new service zones. These changes require amending the contract allocation for 18-month 
option to renew term in the total amount of $9,527,000 with specific increases as follows: 
1) $2,500,000 for continued estimated demand growth in the 18-month option to renew term.
2) $3,500,000 for the implementation of new service zones to cover transit gaps in areas with no or
low transit frequency, such as northwestern and southern areas of the County. This service will
complement the Better Bus Network (BBN) and the South Dade Bus Rapid Transit (BRT).

This turnkey project has allowed the County to evaluate the new service delivery model. Since the 
community is now familiar with the model, it is believed that it will also support and accept the autonomous 
vehicle implementation in zones where GO Connect is already operating. The GO Connect service will be 
expanded to new zones across the County. Preliminary areas include the northwestern and southern parts 
of the County where transit gaps or low transit frequency are anticipated after the implementation of the 
BBN and South Dade BRT. GO Connect will complement the bus network system, facilitating access and 
connection to transit corridors. The designated purchase provides contract capacity to accommodate new 
service zones within municipalities and the unincorporated municipal service area and accessible service 
at Metromover and Metrorail stations during elevator and escalator outages. 
Competition for the scope of services is not practicable at this time since the County has a turnkey project 
contract with River North Transit, LLC which has been successfully operating GO Connect in four County 
zones, increasing service ridership by more than 60% in the past year. In essence, on-demand transit 
solutions have been built to maximize the efficiency of how a dedicated fleet of vehicles is dispatched to 
provide a service within a designated area.  Due to the nature of the service, applications are proprietary 
and not compatible with others. This is currently the standard in the industry.  A competitive bidding 
process for the option term would result in significant service disruption if a new vendor were to be awarded 
given the time needed to phase in and acclimate any new provider.  Moreover, a contractual process with 
another vendor would be cost prohibitive for that term. Continuing services with current vendor will save 
the County costs associated with: 
•
Vehicle Sourcing: GO Connect program operates with a fleet of 19 minivans and plans to acquire
an additional 20 to 24 minivans (including wheelchair accessible vehicles) needed to meet demand
and expand the service across the County. The fleet is managed by River North Transit, who
ensures the vehicles are wrapped and the necessary equipment is installed.
•
Driver Recruitment & Onboarding: GO Connect has recruited a pool of 60 drivers needed to
operate the current service and is well placed to support the planned expansion with additional
drivers. Over the course of the contract, River North Transit has developed this pool of drivers into
a strong community of advocates for the GO Connect service through significant training and
engagement efforts. Recruiting, training and maintaining an entirely new pool of drivers would be
expensive and negatively impact the experience of riders who have come to rely on GO Connect
drivers as knowledgeable transit advocates for Miami-Dade County.
•
Service Operations: Existing service zones (4) have been mapped and geo-fenced. Points of
interest and pickup/drop off locations have been mapped facilitating pick-up and drop-off of
passengers without impacting other transit modes.
•
Rider App (Multimodal Trip Planning App), Web Portal & Driver App: GO Connect has an
intermodal trip planning application that allows customers to view, book, pay and track their rides
using a single app; a web portal that allows the County to easily access all service performance
analytics and stats by date and zone (groups); and driver app for navigation. The web portal and
the multimodal trip application that allows GO Connect to prioritize rides connecting to transit and
prevent mode competition are not compatible with other platforms.
•
Dispatch & Customer Service: Go Connect counts with a call center operated by River North
Transit, LLC. The call center allows customers to book and pay for a trip via phone; assists
customers with information regarding the GO Connect service; and helps customers that need

Experience Servicing Large Service Zones: River North Transit, LLC is operating a service that is
similar to Miami-Dade’s in King County, Washington and Jersey City, New Jersey. It has a long
track record as the industry leader of providing turnkey microtransit services for municipalities at
scale.
Most importantly, the communities in the current service zones are already familiar with the program and 
mobile app. Splitting providers can lead to a poor rider experience as riders have to learn multiple 
technologies and switch between fleets as they pass through the County. River North Transit, LLC is 
uniquely positioned to support the upcoming deployment of the Better Bus Network and South Dade Bus 
Rapid Transit, improving the overall rider experience. Furthermore, it is the only microtransit service 
vendor that offers the ability to deploy on-demand autonomous vehicles, with successful AV deployments 
in both Arlington, Texas and Grand Rapids, Michigan. Accordingly, it is in the County’s best interest to 
approve this designated purchase pursuant to Section 2-8.1(b)(3) of the Code. Pursuant to Resolution No. 
R-391-17, while competition is not feasible at this time, the contract contains all current Board mandated
terms.
Scope 
The impact of this item is countywide in nature. 
Delegated Authority 
The County Mayor or the County Mayor’s designee will have the authority to execute Supplemental 
Agreement No. 3 and exercise all provisions of the contract, including any renewals, cancellation or 
extensions, pursuant to Section 2-8.1 of the County Code and Implementing Order 3-38. 
Fiscal Impact/Funding Source 
The fiscal impact is $11,837,375 for the 18-month option to renew term and associated designated 
purchase. The current contract term expires on August 31, 2023, and the option to renew term will 
commence on September 1, 2023. The contract has a current cumulative allocation of $5,596,200. If this 
request is approved, the contract will have a modified cumulative allocation of $17,433,575 and will expire 
on March 31, 2025.  The additional expenditure being requested was approved by the Board as part of 
the Fiscal Year 2022-23 County budget. The cost for operating GO Connect within the municipal area of 
the Town of Cutler Bay will be fully reimbursed by the Town of Cutler Bay.
