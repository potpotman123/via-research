Describe how Supplier will transition any existing Public Agency customers’ accounts to the Master
Agreement available nationally through OMNIA Partners.
Include a list of current cooperative contracts (regional and national) Offeror holds and describe how the Master
Agreement will be positioned among the other cooperative agreements.
Through hundreds of sales conversations every week, we know that many of our current and prospective
customers are interested in utilizing a cooperative purchasing vehicle, notably OMNIA Partners. We will integrate a
Region 4-awarded contract into our sales and contracting process by introducing this procurement vehicle early in
our discovery process with our prospective customers and our contract renewal conversations with current
customers. We closely track renewals, upsell, and cross-sell opportunities for our current partners, all of which
present an excellent opportunity to leverage OMNIA. Throughout our conversations with partners and prospects,
we will actively promote the benefits of using a cooperative purchasing program, highlighting their effectiveness
and the streamlined procurement process they offer. This ensures that current and potential customers
understand the value and convenience of leveraging the Master Agreement when continuing or choosing our
services. ​ ​ We hold several state and cooperative purchasing agreements, including Neoride, a state-level
purchasing agreement with the Virginia Department of Rail & Public Transportation (DRPT), the California Inter
County Public Transportation Authority (ICPTA), the Oregon Department of Transportation (ODOT), and the 791
Cooperative Purchasing Agreement. We also work with prominent cooperatives such as the League of Oregon
Cities, the National Association of State Procurement Officials (NASPO), the San Diego Association of
Governments (SANDAG), and the Mohave Cooperative, and have familiarity with OMNIA Partners processes. Over
the past three years, these agreements have collectively generated annual revenue ranging from $5 million to $10
million, highlighting the effectiveness of cooperative purchasing in enhancing our ability to deliver accessible and
efficient transit solutions across various states.​ ​ We look forward to partnering closely with OMNIA and eligible
partners to both introduce OMNIA’s cooperative purchasing program and discuss a seamless and productive
transition.

Describe in detail how Supplier’s national sales force is structured, including contact information for
the highest-level executive in charge of the sales team.
Via’s national sales force is led by Chief Revenue Officer Mike Vaccarino (mike.vaccarino@ridewithvia.com). The
sales team is organized by region, with dedicated Territory Leads overseeing sales in the following geographies:
Southeast, Pacific South, Pacific Northwest, Pacific Mountain, New England, Midwest, Mid-Atlantic South, Mid-
Atlantic North, Heartland South, and Central Plains. There is also a Territory Lead responsible for Student Transit
sales. Each Territory Lead manages a team of Partnership Leads and Partner Success Managers, who focus on
customer acquisition and retention respectively.

Explain in detail how the sales teams will work with the OMNIA Partners team to implement, grow and
service the national program.
Via is dedicated to ensuring the successful implementation, growth, and service of the national program in
partnership with OMNIA Partners. Our approach is structured around close collaboration, strategic alignment, and
exceptional service delivery, and includes:​ ​ Dedicated Points of Contact: To facilitate seamless communication and
service delivery, Via will assign dedicated account managers to work directly with the OMNIA Partners team. These
account managers will serve as the primary points of contact for all matters related to the national program,
ensuring prompt and effective resolution of any issues that arise.​ ​ Collaborative Planning and Implementation: Our
sales teams will work hand-in-hand with the OMNIA Partners team to understand their specific goals, and the
unique challenges of serving a national portfolio of public and educational agencies. This involves joint planning
sessions to align our sales strategies with OMNIA’s objectives, ensuring that our services are tailored to meet the
diverse needs of OMNIA’s members. We would be happy to work around your schedules.​ ​ Training and Support:
We will provide comprehensive training to our sales teams to ensure a deep understanding of our shared offering:
both the services and customer support protocols. This ensures that every stakeholder is well-equipped to convey
the benefits of our services to participating public agencies effectively.​ ​ Growth and Service Optimization: Via will
regularly review performance metrics with OMNIA Partners to identify opportunities for program growth and service
enhancements. Our team will leverage data-driven insights to refine our approach, ensuring that we continuously
adapt to changing needs and capitalize on opportunities to expand the program’s reach and impact.

Explain in detail how Offeror will manage the overall national program throughout the term of the
Master Agreement, including ongoing coordination of marketing and sales efforts, timely new
Participating Public Agency account set-up, timely contract
administration, etc.
Via’s sales team is extremely well-prepared to support cooperative purchasing agreements, and we tailor our sales
cycle to match each cooperative partner’s unique structure. Our scalable, technology-enabled organization
provides seamless support for all stakeholders, with a particular focus on ensuring that purchasing cooperative
members benefit from an efficient and responsive procurement process.​ ​ Our North America sales team,
composed of over 40 experienced professionals, receives consistent training on cooperative vehicles, and we
strive to stay up-to-date with purchasing protocols, processes, and new opportunities. Through established
processes, we integrate cooperative agreements directly into our go-to-market strategy, collaborating across
departments — including business development, strategy, marketing, customer success, launch, and consulting —
to ensure smooth onboarding for new partners. With a cooperative-focused sales cycle, we efficiently manage
every stage from opportunity creation to contract signing, maximizing these partnerships to deliver accessible
transit solutions across regions.​ ​ Our approach includes the following strategies:​ ​ Strategic Coordination of
Marketing and Sales: Via will work closely with OMNIA Partners to coordinate marketing and sales efforts, ensuring
that our outreach is aligned with the needs of participating public agencies. This includes joint marketing
campaigns, targeted sales initiatives, and collaborative participation in relevant events and conferences to raise
awareness of the program.​ ​ Efficient Account Set-Up and Contract Administration: We are committed to
streamlining the process for new Participating Public Agency account set-up and ensuring timely contract
administration. Via will implement efficient procedures and leverage our technology platform to expedite account
onboarding, contract execution, and compliance monitoring, minimizing administrative burdens and enabling quick
start-up times for new participants.​ ​ Ongoing Support and Engagement: Via will provide ongoing support to OMNIA
Partners and participating agencies throughout the contract term. This includes regular check-ins, performance
reviews, and the provision of customer support to address any operational challenges promptly. Our goal is to
foster a strong, supportive partnership that ensures the long-term success and satisfaction of all stakeholders.
