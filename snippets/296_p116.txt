Via Staff Training 
Every member of Via’s Operations team benefits from our robust in-house training 
resources, including a rigorous two-week onboarding program upon joining Via. During 
onboarding, Via employees receive comprehensive instruction on the full range of Via’s 
products and services as well as the functions performed by each team within Via to 
make every deployment a success. Our operational staff in particular receive targeted 
training to ensure excellence in the following areas: 
●
Using the Via Operations Center: An introduction to Via’s proprietary transit
management software, including scheduling, dispatch, and shift management.
●
Field Operations and Service Delivery: In-depth training regarding the needs of
day-to-day field operations, including the execution of daily vehicle and driver
checklists, fleet management, accident reporting, and operational safety.
●
Supply Planning: Extensive training on vehicle supply planning for active services
using Via’s proprietary Supply Forecast Tool and Supply Health Dashboard.
●
Driver Communication: Training modules on the standard operating procedures
and tools that Via uses to clearly communicate with drivers, including Talkdesk,
Textline, and Salesforce.
●
Escalations: Training for support staff regarding best practices and procedures for
escalating operational incidents according to predefined communication protocols.
