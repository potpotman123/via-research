CITY COUNCIL STAFF REPORT Meeting: August 20, 2019 Subject Agreement with Nomad Transit LLC (Via Transportation Inc.) for the 18-month On-Demand Community Shuttle Pilot Program . Recommended Action Authorize the City Manager to execute an agreement with Nomad Transit LLC (Via Transportation Inc.) for the 18-month On-Demand Community Shuttle Pilot Program with a not-to- exceed cost of $1,750,000. Discussion On June 18 , 2019 , as part of the Fiscal Year 2019-2020 Operating Budget, the City Council approved a budget request for implementation of an 18-month on-demand community shuttle with Via . City Council approved elimination of the formal competitive bid process for this contract since Nomad Transit LLC is the only known provider of on-demand, turn-key community shuttle services ( transit vehicles, staffing, and management by one vendor ) . On June 18 , 2019 three alternatives were presented to Council and Alternative A was approved , setting a base fare of $5 , and a not-to-exceed amount of $1,750,000 for the 18-month pilot program . Alternative A includes the use of ten transit vehicles, with hours of operation from 6am to 8pm Monday through Friday, and 9am to 5pm Saturday. Contract Negotiations C ontract negotiations were conducted with Via shortly after Council approval in June 2019 . P rimary negotiation points included indemnification language, limitation of liability , and City insurance requirements . T he City’s project-specific insurance requirements are included as Exhibit D of the agreement, Attachment A. Next Steps Upon authorization of the proposed agreement Via will order the transit vehicles, a process estimated to take between six to eight weeks. Via will also begin vetting and hiring local drivers, and working with City staff on system planning details . City staff will work with Via to finalize the vehicle “ wrap ” , the vehicle branding that will display both the City of Cupertino and Via logos side-by-side . During the planning phase prior to launch, City staff will work with the Via launch team to identify fare packages (weekly/monthly/student /senior ), in addition to other promotional offers at the launch of the program. The data dashboard provided by Via during the pilot program will enable staff to access live ridership data . M onthly reporting will also s how detail such as how promotional fares have changed ridership month-over-month . This will help determine the frequency of promotions and other fare products offered during the pilot program . City Council Updates Q uarterly updates will be presented to Council with ridership reports in addition to making ridership data available to the public on the City website . Staff is working with the GIS Manager and Via to provide current Via Cupertino shuttle data on the City website. Staff will also provide frequent updates on the Cupertino shuttle webpage , www.cupertino.org/shuttle. Community Outreach The September 2019 issue of the mailed Citywide newsletter, The Scene , will feature a n introductory article about the pending shuttle program . A second follow-up article is planned for the October issue which will include more detailed program information such as sign-up information , how to ride information, promotional fares, etc. In the planning phase prior to the system launch, City staff and Via will coordinate with community stakeholders including : Cupertino Chamber of Commerce Apple, Inc. Mid-Peninsula Open Space (Rancho San Antonio Preserve ) De Anza College Cupertino Senior Center Caltrain Cupertino Union School District Kaiser Permanente Santa Clara Main Street Cupertino City staff will engage residents and employees via social media, pop-up events, and all other means at its disposal. Via will also promote the system through its usual system launch protocols , engaging with all potential stakeholder s, including those listed above as well as others . In addition, Via will promote the Cupertino-Via partnership with other media contacts to publicize the system regionally and nationally. Grant Funding Update: Staff will be soliciting grant funds from the Bay Area Air Quality Management District’s (BAAQMD) Vehicle Trip Reduction Grant Program. Approximately $6 million in funding is available in Fiscal Year 2020 and applications are due September 10, 2019. In order to qualify for this grant, the City of Cupertino must enter into an agreement with the Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority (VTA ) and VTA must agree to submit the grant application on the City’s behalf. If awarded to Cupertino, reimbursement payments would be passed through VTA to Cupertino for the period of time or amount funded by the grant. VTA initially indicated a willingness to collaborate with the City and City staff is working towards securing an agreement for the project. Sustainability Impact An on-demand community shuttle service may lead to a reduction in S ingle O ccupant V ehicle (SOV) miles traveled in Cupertino . A reduction in SOV miles traveled would reduce transportation - related greenhouse gas emissions (GHG) , furthering the goals of the City’s Climate Action Plan . A community shuttle will make it easier for residents and employees to ac c ess the growing Bay Area transportation network , enabling more commuters to access Cupertino by public transit . Via v ans will feature bicycle racks to help bicyclists make connections and also provide an alternative mode of transportation during inclement weather. Some of the Via f leet will be ADA vans , making it easier for mobility-impaired individuals to travel freely , and improving their quality of life.
