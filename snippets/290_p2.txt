ANALYSIS
In support of City Council’s "Enhancing Regional Mobility" priority, the City partnered with Via
Transportation, Inc., to launch an on-demand rideshare service on December 11, 2017, to 
offer an additional mobility option for residents, employees, and visitors. City Council 
approved a new contract with Via on November 29, 2022, to continue service through 
December 31, 2024, with the option of up to four two-year renewals. 
The app-based rideshare service currently uses a fleet of six-passenger vehicles, a smart 
phone application and dynamic routing to provide efficient on-demand trips and access to a 
wide range of destinations. Pick-ups and drop-offs take place within a one to two block walk.
There are no fixed routes and no fixed schedules, allowing riders to experience a personalized 
transportation solution that fits their needs. Booking options are available for riders that do 
not have a smartphone, need a cash option, need curb to curb service or need a wheelchair 
accessible vehicle.
The current service area includes the Arlington city limits, the Eastchase area of Fort Worth
and the CentrePort TRE Station. The current rate structure allows riders to access any 
destination within the service area from 6am to 9pm, Monday through Friday and from 9am 
to 9pm on Saturdays for $3.00 to $5.00 per person per trip, depending on distance traveled.

Riders can also purchase a weekly ViaPass for a current cost of $25 for up to four rides per 
day for the week and a monthly ViaPass for a current cost of $80 for unlimited rides for 30 
days.
More information on the service and how to book a ride is available at 
www.arlingtontx.gov/via.
An amendment to the current Via contract is necessary to transition the City’s Handitran 
elderly and disabled service to the Via software platform and to develop a Vietnamese 
language version of the Via smart phone application. In order to provide a unified software 
platform for both the Via rideshare service and the City’s Handitran service for elderly and 
disabled riders, the Handitran service will be transitioned to the Via software platform. A 
unified software platform will allow riders to access both services through one application, 
improve data reporting and analytics and yield efficiencies. The cost of this software service 
provision has a not to exceed amount of $139,984. 
In order to provide a high quality of service to riders, the City has asked Via to provide the 
Via smart phone application in additional languages. Via is in the process of creating a Spanish 
language version of the app, which will be available later this year at no additional charge to 
Arlington. The City will also request that Via create a Vietnamese language version of the app 
for an additional cost of $25,000 for programming work. The Vietnamese language version of 
the app will also be available later this year. 
These amendments to the Contract represent a total of $164,984 and increase the amount of 
the Contract to not to exceed $30,412,626. This not to exceed amount for the two year 
contract includes a maximum of $22,047,916 for the standard Via service, $179,502 for the 
Arlington Student Transportation Program, $4,020,224 for the Arlington RAPID service, and 
$4,000,000 for future intercity services, in addition to the $164,984 for paratransit software 
services and the Vietnamese language app. 
The Texas Attorney General has previously ruled that certain information in the Via contract 
is confidential and cannot be disclosed to the public, and therefore a redacted copy of the 
contract is included in the agenda packet.
