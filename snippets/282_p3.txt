4. 
Contractor’s Proposal Response to the RFP (hereinafter referred to as the 
“Proposal”) 
 
All of the documents referred to above are made a part hereof for all purposes as though 
each were written word for word herein; provided that in the case of conflict in the language 
of any of the documents listed above, priority of interpretation shall be given in the order 
listed above with this Contract taking priority.  All of the above documents are hereinafter 
referred to collectively as the “Contract Documents.” 
 
In addition, the parties agree as follows: 
 
A. 
Data Sharing and Dashboard Services 
THAT the CONTRACTOR and CITY, as outlined in the attached Scope of Services, shall 
co-own all customer data input by end users into the technology solution for purposes of 
the Services and that CONTRACTOR shall make data available to the CITY as set forth 
in the Scope of Services. Notwithstanding the foregoing, CONTRACTOR shall own all 
de-identified, anonymized and/or aggregated data, which it may use for any purpose, 
including to improve its product and services offering, including the Services. For the 
avoidance of doubt, (1) all data shared pursuant to the Contract Documents (including co-
owned data) shall be subject to the confidentiality provisions set forth herein and (2) 
CONTRACTOR agrees that it will not sell any data to third parties that contains personally 
identifiable information. 
 
B. 
Performance Monitoring 
THAT the CONTRACTOR shall, as outlined in the attached Scope of Services, provide 
Weekly Staff Briefings (reporting the service data, performance standards, safety data, and 
customer service data set forth in Section 9 of the Scope of Services on a weekly basis), 
shall provide Monthly Business Reviews (reporting the content set forth in Section 9 of the 
Scope of Services on a monthly basis) within twenty (20) business days of the last day of 
the month for service during the previous month, shall provide Quarterly Business Reviews 
(reporting the content set forth in Section 9 of the Scope of Services on a quarterly basis) 
within twenty (20) business days of the last day of the month after every three (3) months 
of service, and shall provide Annual Business Reviews (reporting the content set forth in 
Section 9 of the Scope of Services on an annual basis) within twenty (20) business days of 
the last day of the month after every twelve (12) months of service. Upon request by CITY, 
CONTRACTOR shall provide daily ridership information. 
 
C. 
Performance Standards 
 
THAT the CONTRACTOR shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the 
following performance standards goals on a monthly basis. CONTRACTOR and the CITY 
may mutually agree to revise the targets for these KPIs depending on the growth of the

service and market conditions, and such KPIs will be jointly reevaluated and updated on a 
regular basis by the parties:  
i. 
Actual pick-up time no more than 3 minutes earlier or later on average than ETA 
indicated to rider 
ii. 
Average technical up time percentage of no less than 98%

Average monthly seat unavailable percentage of no more than 10%

vii. 
Monthly cleaning audit performed by Via illustrates scoring with no less than 90%

viii. 
Average monthly ride experience rating at or above 4.6 
 
Notwithstanding the foregoing, the CITY acknowledges that changes to the area of service, 
hours of service or other parameters of the On-Demand Rideshare Service may result in a 
need to reevaluate and adjust such performance standards goals accordingly. Performance 
standards stated above are conditional on request volume being below 115,000 requests 
per month.  
D. 
Federal Transit Administration (FTA) Drug and Alcohol Testing 
CONTRACTOR has developed and shall maintain an FTA drug and alcohol testing 
program in compliance with 49 CFR Part 40 and 49 CFR Part 655. 
E. 
FTA Maintenance and Required Inspections 
 
THAT the CONTRACTOR shall design and implement an action plan for the maintenance 
and inspection of all vehicles operated as part of the On-Demand Rideshare Service in 
compliance with FTA regulations and in coordination with the CITY and the retained FTA 
expert. CONTRACTOR shall make vehicle inspection reports available upon request by 
CITY and, if required by the FTA, shall use its reasonable best efforts to cause its 
independent contractor driver partners to make their vehicle maintenance records available 
upon request by CITY. CONTRACTOR shall use its reasonable best efforts to cause its 
independent contractor driver partners to make their vehicles available for in-person 
inspection by CITY upon request. 
 
F. 
Insurance 
THAT proof of valid insurance shall be submitted by the CONTRACTOR in accordance 
with the requirements outlined in the underlying RFP.  A new insurance certificate shall be 
provided to CITY within ten (10) days of a request throughout the term of this agreement. 
 
G. 
Service Area 
THAT the CONTRACTOR shall provide an On-Demand Rideshare Service that serves all 
locations located within the city limits, the CentrePort Trinity Railway Express (TRE)

commuter rail station and the Trinity Metro bus stops that may be directly accessed off of 
Eastchase Parkway.   
 
The service area may be revised and/or expanded upon request of the CITY and mutual 
agreement of the parties. If service areas are non-contiguous, riders shall be able to travel 
within (if applicable) and between all service areas.  
 
H. 
Service Parameters 
THAT initial service hours shall match those included in Exhibit “A”, Scope of Services. 
Service hours may change based on demand and resources and will be jointly reevaluated 
and updated on a regular basis by the parties and mutually agreed upon. Service options 
shall be made available for those without access to a smart phone or internet connection. 
Payment options shall be made available for those without access to a credit card.   
I. 
Vehicles 
 
THAT the CONTRACTOR shall use commercially reasonable efforts to monitor its 
independent contractor driver partners to ensure the safe operation of the vehicles and the 
commercially reasonable protection of its passengers and bystanders while operations are 
being conducted.   
 
The CONTRACTOR shall provide service using the vehicle fleet described in the Scope 
of Services. ADA service needs must be met per FTA ADA regulations, but the type and 
number of vehicles used to provide ADA service will be dependent upon demand and 
change over the life of the service. CONTRACTOR shall use commercially reasonable 
efforts to monitor its independent contractor driver partners to ensure all vehicles used for 
the provision of service are cleaned and maintained in accordance with City and FTA 
standards and requirements.  CONTRACTOR shall use commercially reasonable efforts 
for vehicles used for the provision of service to be six (6) years or less in age, have less 
than 150,000 miles and have passed the required inspection. This requirement may be 
adjusted if CITY and CONTRACTOR mutually agree that an inadequate supply of 
vehicles satisfy such criteria. Vehicles shall meet all EPA standards. At least fifty percent 
of the non-ADA fleet shall be hybrid or electric at contract start, and one hundred percent 
of the non-ADA fleet shall be hybrid or electric by the start of the second year of the 
contract term. All vehicles in use in the fleet shall be cleaned, both in the interior and on 
the exterior, at least twice a week.  
      
J. 
Approved Operators 
 
THAT the CONTRACTOR shall on a monthly basis provide CITY with a list of approved 
operators who have been appropriately screened, background checked and drug and 
alcohol tested to comply with all relevant federal, state and local regulations, with the first 
list to be provided to City no less than five (5) days prior to the Rideshare Service start 
date. Operators added to the list after such initial list is provided shall be made subject to 
the same screenings, background checks and drug and alcohol tests referenced above.
