What can microtransit
do for public transit agencies?
VIa
Microtransit is every transit agency's ally in
upgrading the customer experience, growing ridership,
and improving efficiency.
Aggregating riders going from different origins to different destinations in real time - and
routing vehicles dynamically — creates operational possibilities that were unimaginable only
five years ago:
Grow ridership of existing
fixed-route service- bus lines,
bus rapid transit, light rail, and
subways — by solving the first
and last mile problem.
Upgrading legacy dial-a-ride
and paratransit services by
making them on-demand
while, at the same time, using
vehicles more efficiently.
Optimizing the
coverage/frequency trade-off
by replacing underperforming
fixed-route service with
real-time demand response
and using the savings to
increase the frequency of
fixed-route service in
high-demand corridors.
The question, therefore, is how best to integrate microtransit into an agency's existing service
portfolio. Since transit agencies are public organizations that are charged with operating safe,
equitable, and reliable service on behalf of their communities, before changing the status quo
an agency needs to be confident that a new service will strengthen its ability to serve the
public, not weaken it. A transit agency, unlike a private transportation operator, doesn't have
the luxury of experimenting with many different ideas until it finds the right service model; an
agencys decision-makers need to make the most informed decision possible about the right
service before the first vehicle is dispatched.
