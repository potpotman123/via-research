CITY COUNCIL STAFF REPORT 
Meeting: August 20, 2019 
 
Subject 
Agreement with Nomad Transit LLC (Via Transportation Inc.) for the 18-month On-
Demand Community Shuttle Pilot Program. 
 
Recommended Action 
Authorize the City Manager to execute an agreement with Nomad Transit LLC (Via 
Transportation Inc.) for the 18-month On-Demand Community Shuttle Pilot Program 
with a not-to-exceed cost of $1,750,000. 
 
Discussion 
On June 18, 2019, as part of the Fiscal Year 2019-2020 Operating Budget, the City Council 
approved a budget request for implementation of an 18-month on-demand community 
shuttle with Via. City Council approved elimination of the formal competitive bid process 
for this contract since Nomad Transit LLC is the only known provider of on-demand, 
turn-key community shuttle services (transit vehicles, staffing, and management by one 
vendor). 
On June 18, 2019 three alternatives were presented to Council and Alternative A was 
approved, setting a base fare of $5, and a not-to-exceed amount of $1,750,000 for the 18-
month pilot program. Alternative A includes the use of ten transit vehicles, with hours of 
operation from 6am to 8pm Monday through Friday, and 9am to 5pm Saturday.  
 
Contract Negotiations 
Contract negotiations were conducted with Via shortly after Council approval in June 
2019. Primary negotiation points included indemnification language, limitation of 
liability, and City insurance requirements. The City’s project-specific insurance 
requirements are included as Exhibit D of the agreement, Attachment A.  
 
Next Steps 
Upon authorization of the proposed agreement Via will order the transit vehicles, a 
process estimated to take between six to eight weeks. Via will also begin vetting and hiring 
local drivers, and working with City staff on system planning details. City staff will work

with Via to finalize the vehicle “wrap”, the vehicle branding that will display both the 
City of Cupertino and Via logos side-by-side.  
During the planning phase prior to launch, City staff will work with the Via launch team 
to identify fare packages (weekly/monthly/student/senior), in addition to other 
promotional offers at the launch of the program. The data dashboard provided by Via 
during the pilot program will enable staff to access live ridership data. Monthly reporting 
will also show detail such as how promotional fares have changed ridership month-over-
month. This will help determine the frequency of promotions and other fare products 
offered during the pilot program.  
 
City Council Updates 
Quarterly updates will be presented to Council with ridership reports in addition to 
making ridership data available to the public on the City website. Staff is working with 
the GIS Manager and Via to provide current Via Cupertino shuttle data on the City 
website. Staff will also provide frequent updates on the Cupertino shuttle webpage, 
www.cupertino.org/shuttle. 
 
Community Outreach 
The September 2019 issue of the mailed Citywide newsletter, The Scene, will feature an 
introductory article about the pending shuttle program. A second follow-up article is 
planned for the October issue which will include more detailed program information such 
as sign-up information, how to ride information, promotional fares, etc.

City staff will engage residents and employees via social media, pop-up events, and all 
other means at its disposal. Via will also promote the system through its usual system 
launch protocols, engaging with all potential stakeholders, including those listed above 
as well as others. In addition, Via will promote the Cupertino-Via partnership with other 
media contacts to publicize the system regionally and nationally.
