NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN: The City of Spartanburg has moved the due date to May 6, 
2025. Please review the questions and answers below. 
 
1. Will the addendum include a Price Sheet? Phase 1 Development and Phase 2 Operations with a flat 
rate for the platform and variable rate per revenue vehicle service hour? No  
 
2. Please provide a map of the service area for the microtransit service. See Map Attachment 
 
 
3. On the service area map or as a separate map, please indicate where the bus stops and bus routes are 
for the City of Spartanburg’s current bus transit operation.  See Map Attachment 
 
4. Will there be any service zones within the city, or is the entire city effectively a single service zone? 
There is only primary zone but two specific locations outside the current zone which we will allow 
bus riders to travel. The Wal-Mart located at 203 Cedar Spring Rd – Spartanburg & Food Lion 
Grocery Store 95 Garner Rd Spartanburg.  
 
5. Will customers be allowed to take trips outside of the city limits, even a short distance, or will trips 
only be allowed strictly within the city limits? Primary within the zone with some minor exceptions 
 
6. What will be the hours of service initially? How many days per week will the service operate and 
how many hours per day (including differences between weekdays and weekends)? The Service 
will be available Monday – Friday 6:00am to 6:00pm Saturday 10 - 6 
 
 
7. What will be the duration of the pilot program? One year 
 
8. What is the target launch date for the new service? July 1st, 2025 
 
 
9. Does the City expect to have all 3 vehicles in service during all service hours, or a lesser number 
during certain hours of the day and/or days of the week? These vehicles are available at all times. 
Two vehicles will be operational with one vehicle serving as a spare.

e. Existing technology/equipment installed in vehicle, specifically:  
• Do the vehicles currently have any mounting brackets or devices that would allow a tablet computer 
to be installed in them without additional hardware being procured and installed to make possible the 
use of cradles for the tablet computers? No mounting brackets, all technology related items will have 
to be installed by contractor. 
• Do the vehicles have any digital communications capabilities installed in them?  No  
 
11. Are there any specific technology requirements for the microtransit technology platform relative to 
the following: 
a. Integration with existing city systems No 
b. Data reporting formats and frequency We do want the technology that will allow us to see real 
time information about the location and usage of the vehicles 
c. Cybersecurity requirements. We would expect vendor to use industry best practices 
d. Data retention policies. Follow the guidelines of reporting for federal grants 
 
12. What are the City’s expectations for the customer interface of the technology platform relative to 
the following: 
 
a. Language support requirements: Primarily English, but should have the ability to translate into 
Spanish. 
b. ADA compliance specifications (screen reading) Yes 
c. The RFP states that the vendor will provide options for those without smart phones to book rides via 
phone calls. Does the City have internal capabilities, such as with an information phone number for 
its bus transit service, for City staff to handle such customer phone calls and enter trip orders into the 
microtransit service booking system? No the City does not have the capability. The Contractor will 
need to handle phone calls regarding the trips. 
d. Or will the contractor need to arrange for a third party to handle call center agent assisted trip 
booking and to include the costs, including initial training costs, of this external capability in its 
proposal?  Yes this should be included in the response from bidders.  
 
13. Does the City have any specific service level requirements for the new service at this point in time, 
such as target response times, maximum passenger wait times, or similar? Or will those be 
developed in consultation with the technology provider and service operator during the detailed 
planning phase for the new service? We will work with operator to develop the best practices for 
the micro transit industry

14. What are the key performance indicators that the City will use to assess the pilot project, or will 
those be developed in consultation with the technology provider and service operator during the 
detailed planning phase for the new service? Developed with technology provider. 
 
 
15. Are there any specific staffing requirements such as: 
a. Driver qualifications beyond standard licensing – We would prefer they have clean driving 
record and pass a background check with a pre-employment drug screen and on-going random 
drug screening.   
b. Customer service representative staffing – We want someone who will be responsive and 
friendly 
c. Local presence requirements: General Manager/Director  
16. What is the current bus transit fare and what will be the fare for the new microtransit service? 
Current transit fare rates (attached) Micro Transit pricing is $2.00 per ride.   
 
17. If a trip uses both the fixed route bus service and microtransit, what would be the appropriate fare 
for such transfer trips?   No transfers to fixed route. 
18. If a microtransit customer is intending to transfer to or from the fixed route bus service, would this 
be treated the same as a transfer between bus lines currently, with some sort of transfer fare 
payment medium (such as transfer slip that indicates that the passenger has already paid one fare 
and that they should only pay the amount of the transfer fare) that the customer shows to the driver, 
or does the City have another approach in mind to handling microtransit to regular bus service 
transfers? This is a separate system so there will be no transfer fees or discounts. One transaction is 
for microtransit and another for a bus ride.  
 
19. Does the City require a cashless fare system for the microtransit service?Yes If cash fares will be 
accepted, will there be fareboxes installed in the vehicles? If not, how does the City expect the 
contractor to collect cash fares and account for them in its reporting to the City?  Cashless fares 
 
 
20. A digital fare collection system will be an additional implementation cost for the project. The 
question of whether to allow cash fares in addition to digital payments is a policy question that can 
only be addressed by the City. It will also have potential implications for a future digital fare 
collection system for the fixed route bus service. Would the City please provide some guidance to 
the proposers relative to the factors that should be considered for the fare collection system for this 
microtransit service, given that it is a pilot project? The City requires a cashless system for micro-
transit.

damage and for liability coverage for passengers and drivers? Contractor.  If the contractor is
