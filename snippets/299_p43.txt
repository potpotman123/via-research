Technology-Enabled Student Transportation Services 
Via is a trusted provider of safe and consistent student transportation service to school 
districts and educational entities across the United States. Via’s highly experienced 
operations teams ensure students receive superlative service from a high-quality and 
student-centric pool of thoroughly vetted drivers. Meanwhile, our powerful software 
(described in-depth in Microtransit, Paratransit, and Non-Emergency Medical 
Transportation Software) enables our partners to benefit from more efficient and cost 
effective routing, easy-to-access reporting, and seamless communication and information 
transmission between drivers, administrators, and caregivers.  
Rider-Centric Operations 
Via has established rigorous processes for ensuring vehicle cleanliness and student 
safety, including thorough driver recruitment and training, strict vehicle requirements, and 
a comprehensive vehicle inspection plan. These measures, in addition to our experience 
as operators, ensure that we are able to provide industry-leading safety in our services. 
Our operational success is driven by: 
Exceptional Track Record of Recruiting and Retaining Qualified Drivers 
We ensure Via Student Transit drivers undergo drug, alcohol, and TB testing, 
fingerprinting, and DMVR and DOJ checks, and thorough training, such as ADA and 
disability sensitivity training, and also meet standards for professional dress, hygiene, and 
licensing. As transportation providers across the country have struggled to staff their 
services, we have had no driver shortages and have onboarded more than 10,000 drivers 
in the last two years. We deliver these results with a combination of above-market wages, 
driver incentives, high-touch support, targeted driver marketing, and a shift-claim model 
that gives drivers significant input into their own schedules.

Extensive Experience Transporting Passengers from Vulnerable Populations 
We have a decade of experience transporting passengers from vulnerable populations 
and we currently operate a number of large-scale ADA-paratransit services in the US. We 
understand the operational challenges at stake in transporting vulnerable populations, 
including special education students: delivering high on-time performance, ensuring 
compliance on routing and pickups, maintaining clean and reliable vehicles, and sourcing 
and training empathetic drivers. For the New York City Department of Education, we 
power a student transportation solution for both general and special education 
populations and our system is designed to adapt to the diverse routing, trip, and vehicle 
needs of different student populations.

Peace of Mind for Caregivers and Guardians 
We can offer vehicle tracking through a smartphone application that will let caregivers 
and guardians confirm that the student’s ride is on its way, that the student has been 
picked up and dropped off on time, and that they 
have been transported via an efficient route. 
Operational Transparency 
Through the Via Operations Center (VOC), our 
dispatchers can track drivers’ progress and 
intervene when needed. Member staff will also 
have access to the VOC, and will have full 
visibility into bookings and student trip progress 
in real-time. Through the mobile Driver App, installed on an in-vehicle device, drivers can 
receive the latest routing instructions and alerts, and contact dispatch as required. The 
system can also facilitate direct communication between caregivers and drivers to reduce 
calls to the department or the schools requesting updates on routes.  
Comprehensive Transit Network Management 
Via is the trusted operations manager and provider to customers across the globe and has 
deep experience in launching and growing multimodal transit networks. We can act as a 
single vendor to oversee the management of the participating member’s transit system 
and will optimize performance across our own best-in-class operations and our highly 
vetted network of third party subcontractors. 
Via can provide fully turnkey transit management services across fixed routes,

Service Design 
Via’s in-house consulting and service design experts can assess a Member’s existing 
transit network and make forward-looking, holistic recommendations that deliver excellent 
rider experience and cost efficiency. For example, we can determine the optimal mix of 
fixed route and microtransit options for a given use case and geography, or design a 
service that commingles microtransit and paratransit assets (drivers, vehicles, 
cross-dispatched rides) to reduce cost.
