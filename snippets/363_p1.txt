RESOLUTION NO. 2025-46 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BRIGHTON, COLORADO, ACCEPTING 
THE PROPOSAL OF RIVER NORTH TRANSIT, LLC AND APPROVING THE PROCUREMENT OF 
THE MICRO-TRANSIT ON DEMAND SHARED RIDE SERVICE FOR THE CONTRACT AMOUNT OF 
EIGHT HUNDRED THOUSAND DOLLARS ($800,000), AND AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER, 
OR DESIGNEE, TO EXECUTE THE CONTRACT ON BEHALF OF THE CITY 
 
 
WHEREAS, the City of Brighton (the “City”) desires to provide supplemental transit 
services aimed at improving mobility and access for residents and businesses within the 
City (the “Services”); and  
 
 
WHEREAS, the City and the Regional Transportation District (“RTD”) are partnering 
to provide the Services, and RTD has awarded a grant to the City in the total amount of 
$1,800,000 towards the Services, to be disbursed annually to the City through 2027 in the 
amount of $600,000 (the “Grant”) ; and 
 
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to the requirements of the Grant, the City will contribute 
$200,000 annually towards the Services; and 
 
WHEREAS, Article 3-8 of the Brighton Municipal Code sets forth the requirements 
and procedures for purchasing supplies and contracting for professional services; and  
 
WHEREAS, the City published a notice inviting bids through its formal bid process 
for the Services; and 
 
 
WHEREAS, River North Transit, LLC (“River North”) submitted a proposal to complete 
the Services for the contract amount of $800,000 per year (the “Proposal”); and 
 
WHEREAS, sufficient appropriations exist for the Proposal; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City Council believes River North is the most responsive and 
responsible bidder to perform the Services.  
 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BRIGHTON, 
COLORADO, AS FOLLOWS: 
 
Section 1. 
The proposal of River North is hereby accepted.  
 
Section 2. 
The City Manager, or designee, after consultation with the City 
Attorney, is hereby authorized to sign the contract in an amount of $800,000 for the 
Services with River North on behalf of the City. Subject to sufficient appropriation in future 
budget years, the City Manager is further authorized to renew the contract annually in 
an amount not to exceed $800,000. 
 
Section 3. 
This Resolution is effective as of the date of its adoption. 
 
 
RESOLVED this 5th day of August 2025.
