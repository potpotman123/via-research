Peace of Mind for Caregivers and Guardians 
We can offer vehicle tracking through a smartphone application that will let caregivers 
and guardians confirm that the student’s ride is on its way, that the student has been 
picked up and dropped off on time, and that they 
have been transported via an efficient route. 
Operational Transparency 
Through the Via Operations Center (VOC), our 
dispatchers can track drivers’ progress and 
intervene when needed. Member staff will also 
have access to the VOC, and will have full 
visibility into bookings and student trip progress 
in real-time. Through the mobile Driver App, installed on an in-vehicle device, drivers can 
receive the latest routing instructions and alerts, and contact dispatch as required. The 
system can also facilitate direct communication between caregivers and drivers to reduce 
calls to the department or the schools requesting updates on routes.  
Comprehensive Transit Network Management 
Via is the trusted operations manager and provider to customers across the globe and has 
deep experience in launching and growing multimodal transit networks. We can act as a 
single vendor to oversee the management of the participating member’s transit system 
and will optimize performance across our own best-in-class operations and our highly 
vetted network of third party subcontractors. 
Via can provide fully turnkey transit management services across fixed routes, 
paratransit, and microtransit: we will manage every aspect of service design, operations, 
and long-term planning for participating members, and will do so with efficiency and 
transparency. Our Managed Services model includes the following components, as 
needed by each individual member:​
Service Design 
Via’s in-house consulting and service design experts can assess a Member’s existing 
transit network and make forward-looking, holistic recommendations that deliver excellent 
rider experience and cost efficiency. For example, we can determine the optimal mix of 
fixed route and microtransit options for a given use case and geography, or design a 
service that commingles microtransit and paratransit assets (drivers, vehicles, 
cross-dispatched rides) to reduce cost.

Operations Management 
Via’s Operations team can oversee all or a portion of 
operational aspects of fixed routes, paratransit, and 
microtransit depending on the needs of participating 
members. This includes driver recruitment and 
training, fleet acquisition and maintenance, and 
facility procurement and maintenance. A dedicated 
local team will be on the ground to manage 
day-to-day operations. 
Software Infrastructure 
With Via’s turnkey solutio, technology and 
operations will work in tandem to maximize efficiency for participating members. Our 
software design is informed by our experience as operators, and our operations are 
designed around our software. Via will configure our software suite according to the 
participating member’s unique parameters and deliver the following tools: 
●
A fully-accessible, white labeled Rider App that will create a seamless booking
experience across fixed-route, microtransit, and/or paratransit services.
●
The Via Operations Center (VOC), which allows the local operations team to
manage the service highly efficiently and in real-time.
●
An easy-to-use Driver App that ensures drivers have all the information they need
to deliver a safe and outstanding customer experience.
Integrated Transit 
Via’s software also supports seamless integration across the entire public transit network. 
Via’s Integrated Transit features offer riders choices between available transit modes and 
can, based on the participating member’s preference, strategically direct passengers to 
the most efficient and cost-effective mode of travel for the journey.

In June 2021, Via conducted a full transit network redesign study 
for Summit County, Utah as part of the launch of a new transit 
agency, High Valley Transit (HVT). Our team recommended a 
mixed service design plan including 6 high volume fixed routes 
and a new microtransit service commingled with the agency’s 
paratransit services.  
In addition to supporting the new agency’s planning process, Via 
now provides HVT with a fully turnkey service: the technology, 
operations, and management services to run all aspects of the 
HVT network. Since partnering with Via, HVT has improved 
utilization by 60% through the efficient commingling of 
microtransit and paratransit services.
