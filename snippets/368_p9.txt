Resources to Enable Long-Term Success and Scalability
From our experience serving similar region-wide, rural demand response services, we
know the importance of localized, multidisciplinary support to ensure both sustainability
and scalability for our partners’ services. Via's Partner Support program is specifically
designed to support the needs of large regional providers like Bay Transit across every
aspect of the service delivery lifecycle. We will leverage our team’s experience powering
and operating transportation services around the world to ensure that Bay Transit can
meet the nuanced needs of riders throughout the region, both now and in the future. Bay
Transit will have the full support of Via's in-house experts:
●
A dedicated Partner Success Manager,
will act as a key point of
contact to support Bay Transit’s team for the life of the service. In addition to
supporting Bay Transit’s transition from Routematch,
will coordinate regular
business reviews, product updates or expansions, and 24/7 product support for
Bay Transit.
●
Via Strategies, our service design and transportation consultancy, can assist in
optimizing Bay Transit’s services over time, including shifting deviated fixed routes
to demand response, or analyzing ridership patterns to determine how paratransit
can best commingle with general demand response and/or on-demand services to
increase efficiency.
●
Our Growth Marketing and Community Engagement teams can provide
marketing and educational materials to help ensure that riders across services are
comfortable and confident using the new platform. These teams
understand
the importance of utilizing high-touch approaches to establish trust with riders of
specialized services, such as the New Freedom Mobility Management program.
●
Our Grants team can help Bay Transit identify and secure additional funding
sources to support the long-term growth of their services as necessary. They have
