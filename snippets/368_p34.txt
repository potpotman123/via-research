Service Planning and Design: Each community has unique goals and
passenger needs, so our launch process is anchored around a
collaborative input gathering stage that includes simulation studies, a
review of Bay Transit’s technical requirements, and alignment on
performance and reporting goals. The result is a service design plan that is completely
customized to suit Bay Transit’s needs, and which allows Via's implementation specialists
to configure the Via system according to Bay Transit’s goals.
Testing and Training: In advance of launch, Via's launch team will install
Via's Driver App, test with drivers in vehicles, and conduct training
workshops for Bay Transit staff to ensure they are comfortable using Via's
software from day one. Via-provided marketing materials can also support
the Bay Transit’s pre-launch community outreach and marketing efforts to
ensure passengers are aware of the service transition.
Launch Support: We understand how challenging it can be to transition
software providers, and we also recognize the importance of face-to-face
conversations to facilitate trust in the new system. Via is pleased to commit
to on-the-ground support for Bay Transit’s staff during the immediate
launch period. This in-person support will be supplemented by our robust partner support
team, including a dedicated Partner Support Manager and 24/7 technical support.
4.5.2 Data Conversion from Routematch to Via
As part of our launch process, we will migrate all data from Bay Transit’s existing systems
into Via’s platform in order to enable access through the VOC for report and data
generation. Via’s in-house Data Science team will work directly with staff to conduct a
proven, four-step data migration process:
1.
Knowledge sharing & data scoping
2. Data evaluation
3. Initial data migration, quality assurance (QA), and validation
4. Full migration, QA, validation, and field testing
This process will ensure a successful migration of data to the Via Platform, and enable us
to convert Bay Transit’s service with minimal disruptions.

4.5.3 Training Plan
Via has trained tens of thousands of system users and drivers across our global
deployments, and has developed a playbook for helping partners navigate the adoption of
Via’s software. Leveraging this experience, we will develop a customized hands-on
training program for Bay Transit to ensure that all relevant personnel — including
operators, dispatchers, schedulers, and administrative staff — are equipped to use the
Via platform and operate the service successfully. We will supplement the live training
workshops with a suite of written training documents tailored to Bay Transit’s needs.
Sessions will consist of a combination of “classroom” and “hands-on” training. Via will
provide all software necessary and electronic copies of product manuals. There will also
be an online option for those who prefer. We can train as many employees as needed, and
we can adjust our training program outlined below to align with any unique needs.
Overall Systems Training:
Workshops for using the Via Operations Center, Driver App and Rider App.
Accessible Service Scheduling and Dispatch Training:
Detailed workshops for schedulers and dispatchers who are taking
reservations, preparing pre-scheduled manifests, and monitoring and
managing day of service operations.
Advanced Administrative Tools:
Training for advanced administrative functions, such as inputting road
closures and adjusting operational settings / configurations.
Data and Reporting Training:
Workshops for extracting, understanding, and leveraging data through our
various dashboards, reports, and best practices for growing the service.
Driver Training:
Workshop to train drivers. It can be broken up into multiple sessions to
accommodate driver schedules if necessary.
Rider-Centric Tools Training:
Workshops focused on usage of rider centric tools so that staff can help
drive adoption and support rider community questions and needs.
In addition,
, Bay Transit’s Partner Success Manager, will provide additional
follow-up resources and refresher training at any time upon Bay Transit’s request.
