Via Staff Training 
Every member of Via’s Operations team benefits from our robust in-house training 
resources, including a rigorous two-week onboarding program upon joining Via. During 
onboarding, Via employees receive comprehensive instruction on the full range of Via’s 
products and services as well as the functions performed by each team within Via to 
make every deployment a success. Our operational staff in particular receive targeted 
training to ensure excellence in the following areas: 
●
Using the Via Operations Center: An introduction to Via’s proprietary transit
management software, including scheduling, dispatch, and shift management.
●
Field Operations and Service Delivery: In-depth training regarding the needs of
day-to-day field operations, including the execution of daily vehicle and driver
checklists, fleet management, accident reporting, and operational safety.
●
Supply Planning: Extensive training on vehicle supply planning for active services
using Via’s proprietary Supply Forecast Tool and Supply Health Dashboard.
●
Driver Communication: Training modules on the standard operating procedures
and tools that Via uses to clearly communicate with drivers, including Talkdesk,
Textline, and Salesforce.
●
Escalations: Training for support staff regarding best practices and procedures for
escalating operational incidents according to predefined communication protocols.

Customer Staff Training 
Where appropriate, Via conducts tailored training for schedulers, dispatchers, and other 
administrative staff who may be using our platform to manage on-demand transportation 
services. Sessions consist of classroom, hands-on, and virtual training to accommodate a 
wide range of learning styles. Via provides all software necessary, as well as electronic 
copies of product software manuals. We supplement this with training and help tools 
including online content, video instruction, interactive classes, materials such as 
workbooks, “cheat sheets,” training aids and online functional or technical system 
manuals, follow-up “refreshers” as needed at the discretion of our customer. 
Topics covered include the following: 
Introductory Workshop 
Introduction to Via’s scheduling software, including high-level overviews of 
the Via Operations Center (VOC). 
On-Demand Scheduling and Dispatch Training 
Detailed workshops for booking agents who are taking reservations and 
monitoring and managing day of service operations. 
Advanced Administrative Tools 
Training for advanced administrative functions, such as inputting road 
closures and adjusting operational settings/configurations. 
Data and Reporting Training 
Workshops for understanding and leveraging the data through our various 
dashboards, reports, and best practices for marketing and growing the 
service. 
Rider-Centric Tools 
Workshops focused on usage of rider centric tools so that booking agents 
can help drive app adoption and support rider community questions and 
needs.
