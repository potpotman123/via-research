1.1 Via’s Qualifications
Via has extensive experience implementing our paratransit scheduling software for partners
globally. We provide the most innovative technology with user-friendly operator tools, paired with
comprehensive and flexible partner support. When they switch to Via’s platform, our partners see
meaningful improvements in every facet of their service. Our proposal for Hillsborough County is
centered on:
End-to-End Platform
Our cloud-based, software-as-a-service system
addresses all of the County’s key priorities.
Our proprietary algorithms enable advance and
real-time trip reservations and optimize on-time
performance by increasing vehicle utilization while
reducing travel time and vehicle miles traveled ՄVMTՅ.
The Via Operations Console ՄVOCՅ allows operators to seamlessly manage the service at scale and
automatically or manually schedule and dispatch vehicle fleets, drivers, and trips. We also provide
solutions to manage rider eligibility across multiple programs, as well as enable reporting and data
transparency with our comprehensive analytics portal designed for paratransit and Transportation
Disadvantaged operators. We strongly believe in data transparency and that all data collected
within the Via system belongs to our partners. We will provide the County with enhanced access to
their data across the platform, including our analytics portal and enhanced self-serve, raw data
access for any and all of custom reporting needs.
Our rider and driver apps are fully accessible and deliver a high-quality experience that
accelerates user adoption through features such as trip booking, tracking, and access to electronic
bus passes.
For Sarasota County Area Transit’s ՄSCATՅ paratransit service, Via-powered
automated dispatching and booking increased on-time performance by 10%
despite significant driver shortages. Via’s platform streamlined every aspect of
the County’s operations and increased service efficiency and reliability, including
faster responses to day-of unexpected challenges.
Via is committed to meeting Hillsborough County’s technical specifications, and we are pleased to
offer the County a product that meets the requirements outlined in this RFP. Details of our
compliance can be found in Section 3ր Technical Approach.

Improved Rider Experience
Sunshine Line riders will appreciate the enhanced independence and flexibility provided to them by
Via’s Rider App and web booking portal, which empowers paratransit users to quickly and easily
book and take trips on their own. Our experienced in-house Community Engagement team can
create high-touch outreach and in-depth explainer materials (e.g., videos, flyers, signage and user
guides) to help ease the transition for Sunshine Line riders and ensure that they can safely and
securely continue to rely on their paratransit service.
The paratransit service in Hampton Roads, Virginia saw a 44% decrease in
average passenger ride duration and overwhelming rider satisfaction with
Via. Riders will love the updated Sunshine Line trip experience.
Full-Network Connectivity
The Via Platform provides riders with a client-facing rider app that allows riders to discover, plan,
and book trips on fixed-route and demand-response transit in the same app, seamlessly
combining demand-response and fixed-route trips in a single journey.
