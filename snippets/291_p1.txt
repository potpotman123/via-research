Resolution No. __________ 
 
A resolution authorizing the execution of a First Renewal 
and Modification to the On-Demand Rideshare Contract 
with River North Transit, LLC., of New York, New York 
(“Via”) for an amount not to exceed $20,791,648.32 
 
 
WHEREAS, the City partnered with Via Transportation, Inc., to launch an on-demand 
rideshare service on December 11, 2017 to offer an additional mobility 
option for residents, employees and visitors; and 
 
WHEREAS, on November 29, 2022, City Council approved Resolution No. 22-335, 
authorizing the most recent contract for on-demand rideshare service with 
River North Transit, LLC., for an amount not to exceed $30,247,642; and 
 
WHEREAS, on May 23, 2023, City Council approved Resolution No. 23-138, 
authorizing the execution of an Amendment #1 to the On-Demand 
Rideshare Contract with River North Transit, LLC., in an amount not to 
exceed $164,984, to include paratransit software services and creation of a 
Vietnamese language smart phone application; and 
 
WHEREAS, the City desires to renew and modify the contract relative to Handitran 
software services, a dynamic direct route service, the RAPID autonomous 
vehicle service, and the Healthy Arlington Innovative Mobility project 
booking tool; NOW THEREFORE 
 
 
BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF ARLINGTON, 
TEXAS: 
I. 
 
That all of the recitals contained in the preamble of this resolution are found to be 
true and are adopted as findings of fact by this governing body and as part of its official 
record. 
 
II. 
 
 
That the City Manager or his designee is hereby authorized to execute a First 
Renewal and Modification to the On-Demand Rideshare Contract with River North 
Transit, LLC., of New York, New York, for a total amount not to exceed $20,791,648.32.  
The First Renewal and Modification provides for two years of on-demand rideshare 
services and four other related Via services.  The contract renewal costs are as follows:
