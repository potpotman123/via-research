ORDINANCE NO. _______________
Granting to River North Transit, LLC, the authority, right and privilege to 
establish, maintain and operate a public transportation system in The City of Springfield, 
Ohio, for the transportation for hire of passengers only for five years with an option to 
renew for two additional five-year periods, fixing the terms and conditions of such grant, 
and providing for the City's participation in the cost of such operation.

WHEREAS, the City’s Purchasing Division has advertised for and received 
proposals for Transit Management Services for Springfield City Area Transit (SCAT); 
and
WHEREAS, the City Commission determines that a franchise should be granted 
to River North Transit, LLC to provide for a public transportation service in the City 
pursuant to the attached agreement which reflects services negotiated following the bid 
response:  NOW, THEREFORE:
BE IT ORDAINED by the City Commission of The City of Springfield, Ohio:
SECTION 1.  That this Commission hereby adopts the findings set forth in the 
recitals to this Ordinance and makes them a part hereof.
SECTION 2.  That the City hereby grants to River North Transit, LLC, subject to 
the terms and conditions set forth in the Franchise Agreement and Service Order 
attached hereto as Exhibit 1 and incorporated herein by reference, the authority, right 
and privilege to establish, maintain and operate a public transportation system in The 
City of Springfield, Ohio, for the transportation for hire of passengers only for five years 
with an option to renew for two additional five-year periods.
SECTION 3.  This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after 
fourteen (14) days from the date of its passage.
PASSED this __________ day of _________________________, A.D., 2025.
