Staff Report
                
Renewal and Modification of the On-Demand Rideshare Service
Program, Bid Project 17-0132
City Council Meeting Date: 11-30-2021
Document Being Considered:  Resolution
RECOMMENDATION
Approve a resolution authorizing the City Manager or his designee to execute the fourth of four, 
one-year renewal options and modification of an on-demand rideshare project contract with
Via Transportation, Inc., of New York, New York (“VIA”) for an amount not to exceed 
$10,186,523. Via Transportation, Inc. is not an MWBE firm.

contract for a demand response rideshare service with Via Transportation, Inc., of New York,

Inc., of New York, New York (“VIA”) for an amount not to exceed $4,552,510.

ANALYSIS
In support of City Council’s "Enhancing Regional Mobility" priority, the City partnered with Via
Transportation, Inc., to launch an on-demand rideshare service on December 11, 2017 to 
offer an additional mobility option for residents, employees and visitors. The contract was 
renewed for a second year by City Council on December 4, 2018, for a third year on December 
3, 2019, and for a fourth year on October 13, 2020.
The app-based rideshare service currently uses a fleet of 68 six-passenger vehicles, a smart 
phone application and dynamic routing to provide efficient on-demand trips and access to a 
wide range of destinations. Pick-ups and drop-offs take place within a one to two block walk.
There are no fixed routes and no fixed schedules, allowing riders to experience a personalized 
transportation solution that fits their needs. Booking options are available for riders that do 
not have a smartphone or need a cash option.
The current service area covers the entire City limits and the CentrePort TRE Station. The 
current rate structure allows riders to access any destination within the service area from 6am 
to 9pm, Monday through Friday and from 9am to 9pm on Saturdays for $3.00 to $5.00 per

person per trip, depending on distance traveled. Riders can also purchase a ViaPass for a 
current cost of $25 per week and ride up to four times per day all week long. 
The total cost for the fifth year of standard service is $9,555,381. $8,615,579 of this cost will 
come from a mix of Federal Transit Administration (FTA) funding and city funding. The 
remaining $939,802 will come from service revenue that will go directly back into the service. 
The fifth-year contract includes a full twelve months of service with up to 68 six-passenger 
vehicles and citywide coverage.   
In addition to the standard Via service costs listed above, two other related Via services were 
made possible through additional FTA funding.  
The City was awarded $1,698,558 through the FTA Integrated Mobility Innovation (IMI) 
Demonstration Program for the Arlington Rideshare, Automation, and Payment Integration 
Demonstration (RAPID) pilot program, which began in August 2020 and will last through 
November 2022. The City will pay up to $306,000 in the fifth-year contract for services 
performed for deployment of the field demonstration and evaluation of the Arlington RAPID 
project.
The North Central Texas Council of Governments (NCTCOG) also provided an additional 
$350,000 in FTA funds for a two-year pilot of the Arlington Student Transportation Program 
at no additional cost to the City. The program currently provides free Via rides for Tarrant 
County College students.  If approved by City Council, the program will also begin providing 
free Via rides to AISD students who attend the Arlington Collegiate Campus and the Arlington 
College and Career Campus. As of October 31, 2021, $325,142 of the original $350,000 was 
available to cover the cost of free Via rides for eligible students. The City is exploring 
opportunities to offer a similar service to MISD Early College High School Program students.
The Texas Attorney General has previously ruled that certain information in the Via contract 
is confidential and cannot be disclosed to the public, and therefore a redacted copy of the 
contract is included in the agenda packet. 
FINANCIAL IMPACT
The Office of Strategic Initiatives and the Finance Department have determined that it is in 
the City’s best interest to renew the on-demand rideshare contract for a final term of one-
year. A summary of costs is included below. Funds are available in the Office of Strategic 
Initiatives Grant Account No. CC418604-61000-SC0533-GR000018 and Grant Account No. 
CC418604-61000-SC0533-GR000022.
