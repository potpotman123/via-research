iii. Are there different platforms for the products, services, and solutions being
offered? If so, describe in detail the features of your personal and business
plans including how users switch between platforms.
Via’s tech-enabled solutions span three distinct but integrated platforms, offering partners
a comprehensive and continuously evolving tech stack. We have configured our
technology to enable a seamless flow of data between Via’s demand-response software
(including interfaces for riders, drivers, schedulers, dispatchers, and administrators),
Remix’s planning platform (for transit planners), and Citymapper’s intuitive journey
planning app (with a rider-facing app as well as an administrative dashboard),
empowering our customers to create a truly multi- and intermodal network where
fixed-route transit and demand-response services work together to provide
traveler-centric, flexible mobility options. This deep integration between software
platforms significantly expands access and discoverability for riders, while providing
backend users with an unprecedented level of insight into travel behavior.

11. Customer Service
i. Please describe in detail your customer service policies and approach.
From our experience as both operators and software providers for transit services around
the world, we understand that high-quality customer service and technical support is
incredibly important for our on-demand transit riders, drivers, and partners. We provide
full-spectrum customer support, covering all operational and technical assistance needs:
Day-to-Day Rider Support 
In collaboration with our customers, we develop 
hyper-local customer service strategies to directly 
address the needs of riders. Our booking agents within 
our Call Centers are available during all service hours to 
assist riders via phone and email queues in multiple 
languages, and will be available during 100% of 
scheduled service. Agents are able to provide support 
with booking rides, troubleshooting service issues, documenting customer feedback, and 
other rider needs as necessary. Agents are trained to know local points of interest based 
on community needs. Customer Service performance is regularly monitored to ensure 
compliance, and all agents are subject to random quality assurance checks. We have 
developed predefined escalation procedures to ensure that all concerns are addressed 
with the appropriate level of support and oversight, and that all relevant parties are kept 
apprised of updates and resolution timelines. The Via Platform offers a reliable clock 
which is automatically synchronized with the Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) to ensure 
consistent support operations. The Call Center phone number will be published on all 
methods of service-centric communication and any voicemails will be returned within 30 
minutes. 
24/7 Technical Support for Customers 
Our customers have access to 24/7 technical assistance through our support platform. A 
rotating team of support engineers is on call at all times to resolve any problems that may 
arise. Incidents are escalated using a predefined alert protocol, which instantly notifies 
the on-call member of the team. The technical team member will immediately examine the 
issue to begin the process of issue resolution and resource allocation. All issues will be 
resolved based on their level of severity and associated response times, and Via will 
provide the customer with regular status updates on resolution.
