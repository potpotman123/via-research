ii. Describe what options are available to Participating Public Agencies for
booking and using the products, services, and solutions being offered. Offeror’s
response may include details related to turnaround time, on-going schedules,
one-off scheduling, and any other detail associated with accessing the services
included in your proposal.
Participating Public Agencies do not need to book or schedule on-going or one-off use of
Via’s solutions in order to gain access to our on-demand transportation solutions. Rather,
Via’s on-demand transportation turnkey or software solutions will enable Participating
Public Agencies to establish full-scale on-demand transportation services, complete with
self-service tools for ride booking, scheduling, and dispatching.
Riders can easily download the Via Rider application, access the Via 
Web Booking Portal, or call a customer service representative to 
begin booking trips. Our platform makes trip booking easy: riders, or 
reservationists acting on their behalf, can select from favorite 
locations and recently selected destinations, book one-time or 
recurring trips, and more.
