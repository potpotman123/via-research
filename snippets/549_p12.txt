Scenario 3) Upgrade three underperforming Gainesville Connection 
routes 
This service would replace three of the six Gainesville Connection routes. Currently, routes 
20, 41, and 50 (shown on the map below) deliver approximately 220 rides / day and carry 
fewer than 10 passengers per service hour, an opportunity for additional efficiency gain 
through a more flexible microtransit service model. The proposed service area is overlaid on 
the existing routes in the map below.  
Microtransit vehicles required: 7-8 vehicles 
Expected daily ridership: 200-350 rides / day
