4. Branding 
The Deployment will be branded as BRATS On-Demand powered by Via. The "powered by Via" 
banner must be used only in the exact format provided by Via, and will be prominent on all assets 
promoting the Deployment, including (but not limited to) printed collateral, digital materials, 
websites, and any vehicle wraps. The "powered by Via" banner will have equal prominence on all 
marketing materials to any additional partner logos or trademarks. Via may provide pre-approved 
brand assets and guidelines that must be complied with in all marketing communications 
distributed by the Customer.
