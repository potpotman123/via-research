SITE HISTORY
In 1994, a Planning Approval and PUD were approved for the Western portion of the subject site to allow the
addition of day care services to an existing community services facility (YMCA) and to allow multiple buildings on a
single building site. The Planning Approval was amended in 2000, and modifications to the PUD occurred in 1994,
1998, and 2000.
Rezoning of the Western portion of the subject site from R-1, Single-Family Residential District, to B-1, Buffer
Business District, was adopted by City Council at its November 29, 1994 meeting, subject to approval of a Planned
Unit Development (PUD) and compliance with the residential buffer requirements of the Zoning Ordinance.
The Eastern portion of the subject site was before the Planning Commission in June 2007, as part of a one (1) lot
Subdivision application, McGill-Toolen Athletic Complex Subdivision, which was recorded in Probate Court. The
site also was the subject of Planning Approval and Planned Unit Development applications at the same meeting,
which were then amended in February 2008 to allow the development of the existing sports complex. In August
2008, a sidewalk waiver was granted, which waived the construction of a sidewalk along Michael Boulevard.
Subdivision of the Western portion of the subject site to create a single legal lot of record was approved in 1998
and the plat was recorded in 1999. In 2003, subdivision of the lot into three (3) legal lots of record, to include
vacated right-of-way along Downtowner Boulevard, was approved and the plat was subsequently recorded in
Probate Court. Most recently, the Western portion of the subject site was part of a two (2) lot Subdivision, McGill-
Toolen Athletic Complex West Subdivision approved by the Planning Commission at its October 5, 2023 meeting,
which has been recorded in Probate Court.
The 2023 Subdivision application also had Major Modifications to the Planned Unit Development and Planning
Approval to remove the Western portion of the subject site from the associated PUD and PAs.
A Sidewalk Waiver was approved in 1999 to waive construction of a sidewalk along what is now named Marion
Beckham Drive, from Michael Boulevard to Matthews Park.
There have been no Board of Adjustment cases associated with the site.
