ICTC was also able to secure an additional grant from the CMO program for a total of $300,000 and a secondary 
grant from the Low Carbon Transit Operations Program (LCTOP) in the amount of $520,353 to further fund 
operations of the service. ICTC also recently applied for an additional grant from the LCTOP program in the 
amount of $516,782 to further fund operations of the service. The total grant funding received between the two 
programs is $2,835,010.  
 
Due to significant demand, it is ICTC’s intention to utilize the additional funding to cover the cost of operations 
through December 2025 and beyond. ICTC is currently operating approximately 800 hours of service on a 
monthly basis which equates to a dollar value of approximately $72,000 per month. To date, ICTC has spent 
approximately $1.7M on the service.  
 
The existing contract value with VIA which includes the initial contract, and two separate amendments totals 
$1,998,875.00. ICTC is seeking to increase the contract with VIA by $500,000, bringing the total contract value 
to $2,498,875.00. 
 
ICTC will continue to search and attempt to secure additional funding to continue to operate the service beyond 
the required CMO grant requirement of 5 total years. To date ICTC has completed 1 year of development and 
2 plus years of operations.  
 
The ICTC Management Committee met on May 14, 2025, and forwards this item to the Commission for their 
review and approval after public comment, if any: 
 
1. Approve the Third Modification to the agreement between ICTC and Nomad Transit, LLC (VIA) 
for the Calexico On Demand service in the amount of $500,000 modifying the total contract value 
to $2,498,875. The modification is contingent upon receipt of said additional funding. 
2. Authorize the Executive Director to sign Modification No. 3.

MODIFICATION #3 TO AGREEMENT FOR CALEXICO ON DEMAND TRANSIT 
SERVICES 
 
 
THIS THIRD MODIFICATION TO AGREEMENT FOR SERVICES (“Third Modification”), made 
and entered into this _____ day of ___________________, 2025, is by and between the IMPERIAL 
COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION (“ICTC”) and NOMAD TRANSIT LLC, a 
Delaware limited liability company, and registered as a foreign limited liability company in California 
(“CONSULTANT”) (individually, “Party;” collectively, “Parties”).   
RECITALS 
 
WHEREAS, ICTC and CONSULTANT entered into that certain Agreement for Services on January 
26, 2022 through Minute Order No. 7C, as amended by a First Modification on June 28, 2023 through Minute 
Order No. 5C, as amended by a Second Modification on October 23, 2024 through Minute Order No. 7B 
(“Agreement”), incorporated by this reference as though fully set forth herein;  and  
 
WHEREAS, section 9.1 of the Agreement contains two (2) single year mutual options to extend and 
parties have exercised one (1) option period to extend through December 31, 2025;  and 
 
WHEREAS, to pay for additional necessary services, additional funding has been allocated, and the 
Parties now wish to amend the total not to exceed compensation amount.   
 
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of their mutual covenants, ICTC and CONSULTANT have 
and hereby agree to the following:  
1. 
Section 6 of the Agreement shall be amended to read as follows:   
“Additional funds have been allocated in a total amount of $500,000 = $500,000.  Therefore, the 
total compensation payable under this Agreement shall not exceed $1,998,875 + $500,000 = $2,498,875 
unless otherwise previously agreed to by ICTC.  However, this Third Modification is contingent upon 
receipt of said additional funds.”   
2. 
Section 31 of the Agreement regarding Non-appropriation is reiterated.   
3. 
All other terms and conditions of the Agreement are and will remain in full force and effect.  
There are no other modifications, express or implied except as herein provided.
