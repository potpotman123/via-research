EXHIBIT B 
Method of Payment 
 
The Fees for the Service are outlined below. The Contractor will require the County to issue an 
upfront fee, ongoing monthly fees based on vehicle hours. All regulatory fees related to the service 
will be charged as a pass-through cost. The Project shall include a fleet of vehicles as described in 
Exhibit A, Service Parameters, subject to extension by mutual agreement of the parties on terms 
to be agreed, including any change in fees. The total Contract amount shall not exceed the two 
million nine hundred one thousand four hundred ten dollars and zero cents ($2,901,410.00) 
Contract Section 4 Maximum Contract Expenditure for the 12-month Contract Section 5 Term.   
  
 
 
Fees assume Launch Date occurs within four months of contract signing. If the Launch Date is 
delayed beyond four months of contract signing, the parties have the option to renegotiate the fees. 
Ongoing Invoice Fees will be billed payable in advance on the first of the month and as further 
specified below.  
 
Fixed Upfront Costs  
The County shall pay to the Contractor a one-time non-refundable installation fee of one hundred 
sixty-four thousand dollars and zero cents ($164,000.00), which will be payable in full upon 
signing of the Contract. In addition to technology localization costs, installation fee includes 
Wheelchair Access Vehicle (WAV) retrofits at a cost of seventeen thousand dollars and zero cents 
($17,000.00) each and vehicle wraps for the fleet at a cost of three thousand dollars and zero cents 
($3,000.00) each. If the County requires additional WAV retrofits or vehicle wraps, the Contractor 
will bill the County as incurred.  
 
 
Ongoing Invoice Fees  
The County shall be responsible for monthly ongoing fees of seventy dollars and nineteen cents 
($70.19) per vehicle hour (the “Monthly Subscription Fees”). A vehicle hour is defined as each 
hour during which a driver is paid on the Via platform. Notwithstanding the number of vehicle

hours incurred per month, the County shall be responsible for a minimum Monthly Subscription 
Fee of fifty-five thousand dollars and zero cents ($55,000) per month (the “Minimum Monthly

The Contractor shall provide to the County an invoice statement showing the calculation of the 
Monthly Subscription Fees incurred based on the actual number of vehicle hours that Driver 
Partners performed during that month in the form included at the bottom of Exhibit B. The County

format, and sufficient to process invoicing such that questions regarding invoice or invoice support 
format will not delay payment to the Contractor. If the County requires additional invoice support, 
the Contractor will use best efforts to provide it and additional costs may apply.   
 
If changes in applicable federal, state, or local law result in a significant change in the Contractor’s 
costs, the parties may opt to renegotiate the ongoing monthly fees. Should changes in applicable 
market dynamics result in an increase in the Contractor’s cost per hour of ten percent (10%) or 
more to any of the following categories, compared to costs at the time of contract signing, the 
parties may amend the ongoing on-demand vehicle hour rate to cover labor (driver pay), vehicle 
rental, and fuel.  
 
Should the Contractor elect to adjust the per vehicle hour rate, the Contractor shall provide 
supporting documentation of the relevant cost increases to the County, and the Contract and/or its 
exhibits will be amended to reflect the new Fee structure and per vehicle hour rate so that, at the 
discretion of the County, either:  
1. The total not-to-exceed contract value is adjusted such that the total not-to-exceed number 
of vehicle hours remains unchanged, or  
2. The total not-to-exceed number of vehicle hours is adjusted such that the total not-to-
exceed contract value remains unchanged.  
 
 
Launch Delay  
If the Launch Date is delayed for more than thirty (30) days by the County for any reason, the 
County shall be responsible for paying the Contractor for any actual out of pocket costs incurred 
by the Contractor  in preparation for launch (the “Upfront Costs”) and a two thousand dollars and 
zero cents ($2,000.00) technology fee for the cost of maintaining the technology infrastructure for 
the County’s deployment during the period of delay (the “Technology Fee”). The Upfront Costs 
shall be payable within fifteen days from the date the County notifies the Contractor of the delay 
and the Technology Fee shall be payable monthly at the beginning of the month in which it is 
incurred.
