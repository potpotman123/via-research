Experts from our Partner Operations team will continuously analyze performance data,

benchmark Commerce’s service against deployments of similar size and scope. These 
data-driven insights will equip Commerce with a comparative understanding of 
performance and strategies to grow the service. 
 
Performance Evaluation 
Via will give Commerce comprehensive visibility into all aspects of service delivery, 
enabling the City to track agreed-upon milestones and performance standards. 
Speciﬁcally, Via will provide: 
 
• In-depth reporting. Via will provide comprehensive data reports to evaluate the 
performance of Commerce’s microtransit program on a daily, weekly, and/or 
monthly basis as requested by Commerce. Authorized users will be able access 
data on a 24/7 basis directly through the Via Operation Center (VOC), our web-
based administrative console, using their assigned credentials. All data from the 
VOC can be exported into CSV and Excel formats for further analysis or to easily 
upload data into separate systems as needed. Please ﬁnd additional information 
regarding Via’s reporting suite in proposal section Appendix 5.A. Software 
Overview. 
• Quarterly business reviews. We will conduct in-depth, data-driven Quarterly 
Business Reviews (QBRs) that analyze the service’s long-term performance along 
speciﬁc, mutually-deﬁned key performance indicators (“KPIs”). Supported by Via’s 
in-house Operations and Data Science teams, the Partner Success Manager will 
schedule these quarterly review meetings with City stakeholders to assess system 
performance and trends. 
• Partner success services. In addition to regular reports and QBRs, Via will provide 
performance evaluation on an ongoing basis through the Partner Success services 
described in proposal section 3.A.2. Professional and Technical Qualiﬁcations.
