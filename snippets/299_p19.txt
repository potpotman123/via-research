Not to Exceed Pricing
Region 4 ESC requests pricing be submitted as not to exceed pricing. Unlike fixed pricing, the Contractor can adjust
submitted pricing lower if needed but, cannot exceed original pricing submitted. Contractor must allow for lower
pricing to be available for similar product and service purchases. Cost plus pricing as a primary pricing structure is
not acceptable.

Provide a detailed ninety-day plan beginning from award date of the Master Agreement describing the
strategy to immediately implement the Master Agreement as supplier’s primary go to market strategy for
Public Agencies to supplier’s teams nationwide,
to include, but not limited to:
    i. Executive leadership endorsement and sponsorship of the award as the public sector go-to-market strategy
within first 10 days
   ii. Training and education of Supplier’s national sales force with participation from the Supplier’s executive
leadership, along with the OMNIA               Partners team within first 90 days
Phase 1: Initial Endorsement and Strategy Alignment (Days 1-10)​ ​ Objective: Secure executive leadership
endorsement and align the organizational strategy with the Master Agreement.​ ​ Actions:​ Executive Meeting and
Endorsement: Within the first three days, organize a meeting with Via’s executive leadership to present the Master
Agreement's benefits, emphasizing its significance for public sector engagements. Secure formal endorsement
and sponsorship from executive stakeholders.​ Internal Announcement: Following executive endorsement, issue an
internal announcement to all employees, highlighting the executive leadership's commitment to and the strategic
importance of the Master Agreement for public sector sales.​ Strategy Workshop: Host a strategy workshop with
key stakeholders, including sales, marketing, product teams, and executive leadership, to align the organization's
go-to-market strategy with the Master Agreement. Identify key markets, products, and services that will be
prioritized under this agreement.​ Communication Plan: Develop a comprehensive communication plan to keep all
stakeholders informed about the Master Agreement's implementation progress and successes.​ ​ ​ Phase 2: Training
and Education (Days 11-60)​ ​ Objective: Educate and train the national sales force on the Master Agreement, with
participation from executive leadership and the OMNIA Partners team.​ ​ Actions:​ Training Program Development: In
collaboration with the OMNIA Partners team, develop a detailed training program covering the Master Agreement's
scope, benefits, and how to effectively sell to public agencies.​ Executive Participation in Training: Schedule
sessions where executive leaders share their vision and support for the Master Agreement, reinforcing its
importance to the sales team.​ Nationwide Training Sessions: Roll out the training program across all national
teams through a mix of virtual and in-person sessions, ensuring comprehensive coverage and understanding.​
Training Materials: Create and distribute detailed training materials, including FAQs, best practices, and case
studies demonstrating successful public sector engagements under similar agreements.​ ​ ​ Phase 3: Execution and
Feedback (Days 61-90)​ ​ Objective: Implement the go-to-market strategy nationwide, continuously monitor
performance, and collect feedback for improvements.​ ​ Actions:​ Sales Execution: Equip sales teams with the
necessary tools and information to begin targeting public agencies, applying the training and strategies discussed.​
Performance Monitoring: Establish key performance indicators (KPIs) related to the Master Agreement's
implementation, such as number of public agency engagements, sales cycle time for public sector deals, and
customer satisfaction.​ Feedback: Create mechanisms for sales teams to provide feedback on the training,
materials, and any barriers they encounter in the field. Use this feedback to make real-time adjustments.​ Review
and Adjust Strategy: At the end of 90 days, conduct a comprehensive review of the implementation's success,
analyzing performance data and feedback. Adjust the go-to-market strategy as necessary to optimize for future
success.
