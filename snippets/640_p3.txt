During the planning phase prior to launch, City staff will work with the Via launch team 
to identify fare packages (weekly/monthly/student/senior), in addition to other 
promotional offers at the launch of the program. The data dashboard provided by Via 
during the pilot program will enable staff to access live ridership data. Monthly reporting 
will also show detail such as how promotional fares have changed ridership month-over-
month. This will help determine the frequency of promotions and other fare products 
offered during the pilot program.  
 
City Council Updates 
Quarterly updates will be presented to Council with ridership reports in addition to 
making ridership data available to the public on the City website. Staff is working with 
the GIS Manager and Via to provide current Via Cupertino shuttle data on the City 
website. Staff will also provide frequent updates on the Cupertino shuttle webpage, 
www.cupertino.org/shuttle. 
 
Community Outreach 
The September 2019 issue of the mailed Citywide newsletter, The Scene, will feature an 
introductory article about the pending shuttle program. A second follow-up article is 
planned for the October issue which will include more detailed program information such 
as sign-up information, how to ride information, promotional fares, etc.

Grant Funding Update: 
Staff will be soliciting grant funds from the Bay Area Air Quality Management District’s 
(BAAQMD) Vehicle Trip Reduction Grant Program. Approximately $6 million in funding 
is available in Fiscal Year 2020 and applications are due September 10, 2019. In order to 
qualify for this grant, the City of Cupertino must enter into an agreement with the Santa 
Clara Valley Transportation Authority (VTA) and VTA must agree to submit the grant

Sustainability Impact 
An on-demand community shuttle service may lead to a reduction in Single Occupant 
Vehicle (SOV) miles traveled in Cupertino. A reduction in SOV miles traveled would 
reduce transportation-related greenhouse gas emissions (GHG), furthering the goals of 
the City’s Climate Action Plan. A community shuttle will make it easier for residents and 
employees to access the growing Bay Area transportation network, enabling more 
commuters to access Cupertino by public transit. Via vans will feature bicycle racks to 
help bicyclists make connections and also provide an alternative mode of transportation 
during inclement weather. Some of the Via fleet will be ADA vans, making it easier for 
mobility-impaired individuals to travel freely, and improving their quality of life.  
 
Fiscal Impact 
A Budget Adjustment of $1,750,000 for this program was approved with the Operating 
Budget for Fiscal Year 2019-2020.  No additional funding is needed.
