Operations Management 
Via’s Operations team can oversee all or a portion of 
operational aspects of fixed routes, paratransit, and 
microtransit depending on the needs of participating 
members. This includes driver recruitment and 
training, fleet acquisition and maintenance, and 
facility procurement and maintenance. A dedicated 
local team will be on the ground to manage 
day-to-day operations. 
Software Infrastructure 
With Via’s turnkey solutio, technology and 
operations will work in tandem to maximize efficiency for participating members. Our 
software design is informed by our experience as operators, and our operations are 
designed around our software. Via will configure our software suite according to the 
participating member’s unique parameters and deliver the following tools: 
●
A fully-accessible, white labeled Rider App that will create a seamless booking
experience across fixed-route, microtransit, and/or paratransit services.
●
The Via Operations Center (VOC), which allows the local operations team to
manage the service highly efficiently and in real-time.
●
An easy-to-use Driver App that ensures drivers have all the information they need
to deliver a safe and outstanding customer experience.
Integrated Transit 
Via’s software also supports seamless integration across the entire public transit network. 
Via’s Integrated Transit features offer riders choices between available transit modes and 
can, based on the participating member’s preference, strategically direct passengers to 
the most efficient and cost-effective mode of travel for the journey. 
Marketing and Community Engagement 
Via’s in-house Marketing team can work with participating members to design bespoke 
and multi-channel brand and marketing programs to generate excitement and awareness 
for the member’s services. Via has extensive expertise on how to drive rider engagement, 
design and launch customer surveys, manage public relations, and develop creative 
assets unique to our partner’s preferred branding. A dedicated Community Engagement

In June 2021, Via conducted a full transit network redesign study 
for Summit County, Utah as part of the launch of a new transit 
agency, High Valley Transit (HVT). Our team recommended a 
mixed service design plan including 6 high volume fixed routes 
and a new microtransit service commingled with the agency’s 
paratransit services.  
In addition to supporting the new agency’s planning process, Via 
now provides HVT with a fully turnkey service: the technology, 
operations, and management services to run all aspects of the 
HVT network. Since partnering with Via, HVT has improved 
utilization by 60% through the efficient commingling of 
microtransit and paratransit services.
