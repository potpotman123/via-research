Looking Forward:  Scope of Services 
The City is seeking one prime contractor to provide day-to-day administration and operation of its 
public transit/transportation services.  Teaming is encouraged, provided one firm serves as the 
prime contractor and others as subcontractors. 
 
 
1.3 
General Public Fixed-Route Service 
The City’s fixed-route transit service includes the following elements:  three routes (Routes 1, 2, 
and 3) operating within Lompoc city limits; one route (Route 4) serving Vandenberg Village and 
Mission Hills; a limited-stop, high-frequency shuttle (Route 5/H Street Shuttle) traveling chiefly 
along H Street; an inter-community service (Wine Country Express) linking Lompoc with the 
neighboring Santa Ynez Valley; and a twice-weekly service (Santa Barbara Shuttle) linking 
Lompoc with healthcare centers in Santa Barbara and Goleta. 
 
Routes 1, 2, and 3 each operate on 40-minute service frequency using one vehicle for each 
route. These routes will operate Monday through Friday, from 6:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and will focus 
on east-west travel in south Lompoc, central Lompoc, and north Lompoc, offering connections 
with the H Street Shuttle at multiple locations. The proposed route alignments and schedules are 
provided in Attachment 15. 
 
Route 4 will operate from approximately 6:30 am to 5:00 pm, Monday through Friday.  The 
Contractor will deploy one vehicle to operate this service. The proposed route alignment and 
schedule are provided in Attachment 15. 
 
Route 5 (the H Street Shuttle) will provide 20-minute bi-directional service from 6:00 am to 5:00 
pm Monday through Friday north and south on H Street between the Transit Hub and Mission 
Plaza. The Contractor will deploy one vehicle to operate this service.  The proposed route 
alignment and schedule are provided in Attachment 15. 
 
The Wine Country Express will continue to provide three round trips per day, Monday through 
Saturday, as currently scheduled.  One vehicle will be utilized. A link to the service brochure is 
provided in Attachment 15. 
 
The Santa Barbara Shuttle will continue to provide service between Lompoc and Santa Barbara 
on Tuesday and Thursday as currently scheduled. The shuttle currently picks up Dial-A-Ride 
passengers in Lompoc first, then picks up other riders at the Transit Hub at 8:30 a.m.  Riders are 
picked up at 1206 W. Chapala Street in Santa Barbara (next to China Pavilion) at 3:30 p.m., after 
which the shuttle picks up Dial-A-Ride passengers before returning to Lompoc. The vehicle and 
driver remain in Santa Barbara during this period. One vehicle will be assigned. 
 
The services discussed above will not operate on Saturday (except the Wine Country Express), 
Sunday or City-designated holidays.  These include  New Year’s Day, MLK Jr. Day, Presidents’ 
Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving

1.4 
ADA Complementary Paratransit Service 
The City’s ADA complementary paratransit service (Dial-A-Ride) is a curb-to-curb, shared-ride 
service provided for residents of the Lompoc Valley possessing an ADA recognized disability.  
Physician certification is required in order to be eligible to use the service. 
 
The service will operate Monday through Friday, 6:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.  Service will initially be 
provided with one vehicle, with the potential for more to be added should demand warrant. The 
service span mirrors that of the fixed-route service. 
 
The service will not operate on Saturday, Sunday, or City-designated holidays:  New Year’s Day, 
MLK Jr. Day, Presidents’ Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, 
Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. 
 
The City will continue to be responsible for all eligibility and registration for the ADA Paratransit 
service. Registration applications and waivers must be submitted to the City for review and 
approval.  Approval is required before the rider can use this service. Applications are typically 
processed within three business days of receipt, and notification of approval is provided by mail.  
 
A personal care attendant (PCA) may accompany a fare-paying  rider for no additional fare.  By 
contrast, a person traveling as a companion must pay the same fare as the rider they are 
accompanying. 
 
ADA Paratransit reservations shall be supported by the Contractor’s website and mobile customer 
service portal, as detailed in the Scope of Work. User-friendly features shall enable customers to 
quickly and easily schedule, purchase, and manager their trips and accounts. These tools will 
support both on-demand and scheduled trip booking.  Riders must also be able to book a trip via 
telephone. Bidders are to discuss their proposed ride scheduling terms and policies within their 
respective proposals. 
 
A late cancellation is defined as one that is received less than two hours before the requested 
pick-up time. A no-show is defined as a trip that is not cancelled or one that is cancelled after the 
vehicle has arrived at the pick-up location. Drivers will wait three (3) minutes after the scheduled 
pick-up time.  Excessive late cancellations or no-shows may result in suspension or loss of riding 
privileges. 
 
1.5 
General Public On-Demand Service 
The Contractor will operate a general public on-demand service within Lompoc city limits as well 
as in Mission Hills and Vandenberg Village.  This service will utilize a “corner-to-corner” service 
approach focusing on safe rider pick-up and drop-off at nearby intersections in close proximity to 
the origin and destination points. The locations of “corner-to-corner” virtual stops will be 
determined by the successful bidder’s dispatching algorithm, which will identify safe locations 
(such as nearby intersections or side streets) for the vehicle to stop. ADA-certified riders will be 
eligible to receive curb-to-curb service.
