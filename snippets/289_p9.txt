During the Contract term, the Contractor shall use commercially reasonable efforts so that the service 
scope parameters be as follows: 
  
1. Service Days/Hours: Hours in which the Dynamic Direct Route service will operate will be 
mutually agreed upon in writing (email being sufficient). Service Days and Hours may be 
changed or extended at City’s request and mutual agreement between the City and Contractor. 
For all other services, Monday through Saturday excluding holidays, for which a mutual decision 
whether the service should operate during such days will be made between the City and 
Contractor. The service will operate from 6am to 9pm, Monday through Friday, and 9am to 9pm 
on Saturdays. Service Days and Hours may be changed or extended at City's request and mutual 
agreement between the City and Contractor. 
  
2. Geographic Coverage: The service area for the Contract term will be the area noted as the Service 
Area in Section I(G) of the Contract. The service area may be revised upon request of the City 
and mutual agreement between the City and Contractor. The service area for the Dynamic Direct 
Route services will be designated by two core anchor points within the City’s on-demand 
rideshare service, including the Centreport Station and the Downtown/UTA hub. The service area 
may be revised upon request of the City and mutual agreement between the City and Contractor. 
  
3. Ride Pricing: Ride Cost will be $3 to $5 per passenger, based on distance traveled, with discounts 
and/or promotions made available to users occasionally. Based on data collected during service 
operations, the Contractor may request a pricing change. All pricing decisions will be made based 
on mutual discussion and agreement between the City and Contractor. With respect to the 
Dynamic Direct Route services, ride cost will be $3 to $5 per passenger based on distance 
traveled, with discounts and/or promotions made available to users occasionally. Ride Cost may 
change but will not exceed $5 per passenger. Based on data collected during service operations, 
the Contractor may request a pricing change beyond the $5 per passenger maximum. All pricing 
decisions will be made based on mutual discussion and agreement between the City and 
Contractor 
  
The Service Scope Parameters defined above may be re-defined or adjusted by mutual agreement between 
the City and Contractor at any time based on additional data or new information collected. 
6. Fleet 
A. General Fleet Plan 
  
Contractor will cause a sufficient fleet of vans to be deployed for the Service, and to be custom branded 
using magnets or vehicle decals so as to be easily recognizable. The number of vans included in the fleet 
may be increased or decreased upon request by the City and agreement of both Parties.  Vehicle types 
must be approved by the City prior to being added to the fleet. Qualified and accredited independent

contractor drivers will be able to gain access to these vehicles after being registered onto the Via system, 
enabling these individuals to sign up for specific daily driving sessions or longer periods of work. 
As necessary, Contractor may also establish a means for independent contractor drivers to drive on the 
Via platform using their own qualified vehicles, for instance, during times of high demand when the fleet

For all drivers on the Service, Contractor shall ensure that driver registration and certification protocols

With respect to autonomous vehicles, Contractor will ensure that any operators, whether in person or 
remote, are qualified and accredited, and comply with all relevant City and FTA guidelines, including any 
drug & alcohol regulations. All autonomous vehicles added to the fleet must meet at least the same 
standards as non-autonomous vehicles in the current Via fleet, to the extent applicable taking into 
consideration the nature of such autonomous vehicles. 
 
B. Wheelchair Accessible Vehicle (WAV) Fleet 
  
Contractor will contract with a vehicle provider that shall develop and maintain a WAV fleet plan that 
satisfies relevant FTA ADA requirements - including the equal provision of service to potential riders of 
all physical means. Contractor shall procure through its partnership with May Mobility at least one (1) 
autonomous vehicle to its WAV Fleet, which will operate under the Arlington RAPID program, as 
described in Section 11.  
  
To fulfill this goal, Contractor shall cause to be deployed for the Service appropriately-qualified drivers 
operating existing City vehicles, taxi or livery vehicles, or dedicated vehicles specifically deployed for the 
Service.  
 
7. Customer and Driver Support 
While its platform is highly automated, and requires minimal human intervention to operate, Contractor 
shall provide high-quality customer service to both customers and driver partners during the course of the 
Service. If an issue arises for a customer or driver before, during, or after a ride, these parties will be able 
to reach customer service staff by in-app form submission, by submitting an email ticket, or by calling in, 
which will be replied to promptly by such staff. 
  
Upon request by the City, the Contractor shall provide to the City a periodic reporting system of customer 
service events, in order to maximize the City’s learnings about the Service.

8. Marketing & Promotions 
Contractor shall work closely with the City to create a unified marketing and promotional program that 
increases community awareness of the Service and maximizes its success. The City will cooperate with 
Contractor in all respects and support Contractor’s team by providing useful local insights and leveraging 
existing marketing platforms to amplify the Service. 
9. Data Sharing & Reporting 
Contractor shall regularly share with the City a collection of data generated by the Service, in order to 
provide the City with useful insights about mobility in Arlington, and the operation of a flexible 
transportation system.
