Addendum  
to the 
791 PURCHASING COOPERATIVE AND VENDOR AGREEMENT 
 
Via Transportation, Inc. (“Via”) and 791 Purchasing Cooperative (“Cooperative Agency” and, together 
with Via, the “Parties”) have entered into that certain agreement titled 791 PURCHASING 
COOPERATIVE AND VENDOR AGREEMENT for RFP #791_2023_09_001 Mobility Software and 
Solutions dated December 13, 2023 (the “Agreement”). The Parties wish to add the following 
clarifications to the Agreement reflecting their original understanding: 
 
1. Clarifications. 
a. The Agreement with Cooperative Agency was signed by Via Transportation, Inc. on 
behalf of its subsidiaries and affiliates.  
a. It is the Parties' intent that each contract with a 791 Purchasing Cooperative Participant, 
pursuant to the Agreement, shall be entered into by the applicable Via subsidiary. 
b. The Via subsidiaries mentioned in the Agreement refer to the following entities:  
 
Via Mobility, LLC 
 
Flatiron Transit, LLC 
 
Nomad Transit, LLC 
 
Greenpoint Transit, LLC 
 
River North Transit, LLC  
 
Tidewater Transit, LLC  
 
Sioux Falls Transit LLC 
 
Crosby Street Transit LLC 
 
Remix Technologies LLC 
 
Citymapper Ltd. 
 
c. Via Transportation, Inc. is the parent entity of the wholly owned subsidiaries listed 
above. Each of the above-listed entities may enter into a contract with a 791 Purchasing 
Cooperative Participant for the purchase of products and services, pursuant to the 
Agreement.  
d. The foregoing list of entities may be updated by the parties in writing at any time.  
 
 
2. Use of Terms, Governing Law. Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the meanings 
set forth in the Agreement. Except as expressly provided herein, the terms and conditions of the 
Agreement remain unchanged. In the event of a conflict between this Addendum and the 
Agreement or any part thereto, this Addendum shall control. This Addendum will be governed by 
the same law as the Agreement and is effective as of October 1, 2024. 
 
VIA TRANSPORTATION, INC.
