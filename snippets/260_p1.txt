DESCRIPTION:
2026 Public Contract for Services with River North Transit LLC For Regional 
Rideshare Services in the Amount Of $4,404,615.00.
SUMMARY:
Douglas County intends to continue providing Link on Demand (Link) regional 
rideshare service in Highlands Ranch and expand east through portions of 
unincorporated Douglas County into the Town of Parker in 2026. The request 
is for approval of the 2026 Public Contract of Service (PCS) between the 
Douglas County Board of County Commissioners (BCC) and River North 
Transit LLC, a subsidiary of Via Transportation Inc. (Via), to provide regional 
rideshare in the amount of $4,404,615.
RECOMMENDED
ACTION:
The County Attorney’s Office has reviewed the Contract, and it complies with 
all State and local requirements. Staff recommends approval of the 2026 PCS 
between the Board of County Commissioners and River North Transit LLC in 
the amount of $4,404,615.
