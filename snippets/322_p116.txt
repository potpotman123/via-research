Partner Challenges
Metrobus faced recurring challenges as they utilized various 
legacy technology providers, including Routematch and 
Tripspark, to power their paratransit service. Facing significant 
operational inefficiencies paired with higher than usual 
ridership demands, the agency sought to upgrade their 
technology.
Via Solution
Since launching Via’s technology in St. John’s in November 
2021, we have promoted greater efficiency and high quality of 
service for Metrobus. Our system utilizes ViaAlgo to optimize 
routing for the agency’s fleet of 18 vehicles, as well as two 
unique solutions for utilizing taxi partners for overflow trips. 
Our algorithm enables the Agency to better account for 
detours, boarding times, and dynamic capacity for passengers’ 
accessibility devices. Given the success of the service, 
Metrobus is working to expand their options and launch a 
general microtransit service in the near future.

3. Technical Approach
We are excited to leverage a combination of our best-in-class software solution and expert
support to help enhance mobility and access for Hillsborough County’s Sunshine Line. For this
project, Via proposes our Software-as-a-Service ՄSaaSՅ solution: a comprehensive technology
system to power intuitive and user-friendly mobility services for riders. Our technology, in tandem
with our deeply involved partner support services and in-depth experience replacing
Routematch-powered services, will help seamlessly expand mobility across the county while
maintaining manageable operational and administrative costs.

the needs of the County (as well as the technical requirements and specifications listed in the RFPՅ
to deliver a highly efficient, best-in-class mobility service to the County. In response to the
County’s key technical priorities, we’ve highlighted our platform’s capabilities to provide:
User-friendly reservations for advance, real-time, and subscription / recurring
trips paired with manual, computer-assisted, and fully automated trip scheduling.
Comprehensive client record and eligibility applications management features to
accommodate the County’s current application process logic and flow.
An extensive selection of service reports, as well as reporting configurability and
the complete data transparency of Direct Data Access ՄDDAՅ.
A custom-designed bus pass distribution solution that enables staff to continue to
provide a high volume of passes monthly to clients within the Via system.
High quality in-vehicle Mobile Data Computers ՄMDCՅ, along with all the necessary
installation, wireless service, software applications, and support.
