Third-Party Agreements
To facilitate the provision of our services, Via shares data with third-party contractors based on a
strict business need-to-know basis. When transferring user information to a third party, we ensure
that the third party collects, processes, and discloses personal information only for limited and
specified purposes. This includes, for example, information shared in order to process card
payments, perform data analyses, or send or receive automated SMS text messages.
Via does not rent, sell, or share user information with other people or non-affiliated companies for
their direct marketing purposes unless we have explicit user permission.
Before sharing any user information with third parties, Via will perform due diligence to assess
information security risks arising from the engagement, and establish an agreement that includes:
●
A list of all data the provider may process, its purpose, and where it will be stored
●
A commitment from the third party to protect the confidentiality of all information
●
A requirement for the third party to develop an annual report on their implementation of
necessary privacy regulations
●
A requirement for the third party to notify Via in case of a security incident
●
A process for how, at the conclusion of the agreement, the third party will return and/or
destroy the data in their possession and report this to the database controller
Data Lifecycle Management / Retention
New data is submitted into the system through our user-facing applications — the Rider and Driver
Apps. Once received, the data is encrypted and stored inside the AWS infrastructure, and analyzed
and processed by the appropriate Via teams.
Via’s data is archived by means of Amazon Web Services ՄAWSՅ S3 backups. All data is eventually
expired and removed according to the Data Retention Policy. During the data retention period, our
partners can access and restore any archived information in real time and without the need to shut
down the database. The stored data will be time and date stamped, and contain all the information
our partners need for selective sorting and retrieval.
After the retention period expires, Via can provide the County all ridership data for their
organizational use and data is completely deleted from Via’s cloud database and any associated
backups. For security purposes, some data is anonymized with no traceback possibility and is
stored in the database.

4. Cost
While Via offers Hillsborough County the most advanced paratransit scheduling software system
and robust partner support services, we also acutely understand the financial consideration that
the County must take when choosing software providers and are fully committed to providing the
best value for money to the County. As such, we are offering a discounted proposal within the
County’s Year 1 budget at a total price of $247,200. Our Software-as-a-Service solution includes
two fee categories:
●
One-Time Fees:
○
Installation Fee: This one-time fee covers all technical tasks and start-up activities
required to launch a successful paratransit scheduling software system, including
training, technology configuration, testing, and quality assurance ՄQAՅ. Note that
Via has included a web booking portal and Direct Data Access ՄDDAՅ in the base
installation fee at no additional cost to the County. Pricing for GTFS integration is
included in Via’s offer, and pricing for SSO integration can be provided upon
request.
○
Mobile Data Computer ՄMDCՅ Fee: This one-time fee covers the entire MDC
package for Year 1, including purchase of Samsung tablets, RAM mounts, power
cords, rugged cases, shipping, and installation costs, as well as twelve months of
data plans for the 68-vehicle fleet. Please note that we have priced two options for
MDCs: one for 8” tablets, which are more cost effective and can also be suitable for
this use case based on our prior experience, and another with ֜10” tablets as
requested by the RFP.
●
Recurring Fees:
○
Monthly Per Vehicle Fees: After launch, our solution is priced as an all-inclusive
monthly per-active vehicle fee, granting ongoing access to Via’s cloud-based
software. This means that the County will only be charged for active vehicles
during service hours, and not for every vehicle in their fleet. The fee also covers
our reporting suite, Amazon Web Services cloud hosting, marketing support,
technical support, and continuous software upgrades.
○
Ongoing MDC Fee ՄYears 2֡ Only): After the purchase of MDCs has been
completed in Year 1, a $31,500 annual cost would apply in subsequent years to
cover data plans and ongoing support & standard replacement needs for 68
devices.
