This Data Sharing Agreement TSD-24-146 (DSA) by and between Tacoma School District No. 10 
(District) and Nomad Transit (collectively identified and referred to herein as the “Party” and/or the 
“Parties”), is entered into for the (1) establishment of a working relationship between the District and 
Nomad Transit and (2) establishment of Nomad Transit scope of access to the District’s educational 
records for students served by Nomad Transit.  
The general objective of this DSA is to support the safe and reliable on-demand alternative vehicle 
transportation services for District students. The District may only release confidential information and 
data to Nomad Transit in accordance with the terms of this DSA, including all attachments and 
appendences, which are incorporated herein by reference.   
RESPONSIBILITIES OF NOMAD TRANSIT 
1. In order to accomplish the general objective(s) of this DSA, Nomad Transit shall perform the specific
duties as outlined below to the satisfaction of the District’s designee, Director of Transportation
Services.
2. Nomad Transit will only request data as identified within this DSA, and will follow the procedure for
making such request(s) within Attachment A.

c.

agreements.
d. Upon any request for confidential information, Nomad Transit will present to the District the

RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DISTRICT 
A. Upon Nomad Transit direct request to the District, and upon demonstrated completion of any 
potential provision of consent forms, the District may share the specific confidential information as 
identified in Attachment A with Nomad Transit, per the terms of this DSA.  
GENERAL TERMS 
A. CONFIDENTIALITY.  The term “confidential information" as used in this DSA means any and all 
information provided by the District to Nomad Transit which is protected by the FERPA, 20 U.S.C. 
Sec 1232g, its corresponding regulations found in 34 C.F.R. Part 99, and all other similar federal, 
state, and local LORs. Such personal information is also exempt from mandatory disclosure by a 
school district under the terms of the state public disclosure laws codified at chapter 42.56 RCW.
