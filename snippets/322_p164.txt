G7. What is your privacy policy? (Please add comments)
Via's privacy policy is focused around confidentiality, physical and digital 
material security, incident management protocols, and primary points of 
contact for security and privacy questions. These guidelines and specific 
processes meet the highest standards and can be tailored to meet the 
County's specific needs and requirements. Furthermore, Via complies 
with all applicable data protection laws in the geographies in which we 
operate, including the state of Florida. Our detailed privacy policy can be 
found here: https://ridewithvia.com/privacy-policy/
G8. Can you provide your physical security policies?
X
Via's physical security policies include ISO 27001-compliant policies 
relating to physically protecting the work environment inside and outside 
of the office, including working in secure areas, a "Clean Desk and Clear 
Screen" policy, and guidance for handling physical documents. 
Additionally, our infrastructure is based in Amazon Web Services (AWS), 
which maintains strict physical security policies based on perimeter, 
infrastructure, data, and environmental layers of protection. We look 
forward to providing the County additional details on Via's physical 
security policies as necessary.

G16.  How do you protect sensitive data in storage infrastructure?
Via uses appropriate encryption technologies to protect data stored on 
our corporate and production servers, including sensitive data. Via also 
encrypts all data transmissions containing sensitive data.
G17.  What audit logs are available?
Via utilizes cloud environment logs to track and store human/machine 
actions performed on the cloud infrastructure, as well as traffic logs to 
track all partner connections to the API, including connection time, IP, 
action performed, and success/failure code. We our open to further 
discussing with the County how we can provide access to these logs as 
needed.
G18.  How is the data encrypted? Is it encrypted at rest, in transition, and for disposition?
Via's data is encrypted at rest through AWS Keys Management.
G19.  What type of encryption do you use for the transfer of information (via emails, etc.)
Please refer to Section 3.8.4: Data Access under Encryption Protocols for 
details on the types of encryption we use for the transfer of information.
G20.  What are your layers of security (including network, application, etc.)?
Partners connect to the Via system through a single user login that 
connects them to all tools and resources appropriate to their role. Our 
system uses AWS Cognito to authenticate, authorize, and audit each 
user through a multi-factor authentication process. The system relies on 
multi-tier architecture to separate the network into private and public 
layers. Partners connect to the web-based Via system over RestAPI on 
the public internet using secure https connection, going through a 
Reblaze Web Application Firewall (WAF), which examines each 
request. Incoming partner traffic flows through at least two load-
balancers (ELB), which mask internal IP address allocation.
