4. Scope of Work
We are excited to leverage our best-in-class software solution and the expert support of
the Via team to help Bay Transit transition their demand response services from
Routematch to the Via platform. Unifying demand response and on-demand services onto
the Via platform will enhance both the user experience and efficiency of the services in
the short term, while also offering long-term sustainability and scalability as needs evolve.
We will leverage a dedicated project team to ensure that Bay Transit receives prompt
support and excellent guidance in developing their services. In particular, our platform
and project team will accomplish the RFP’s stated needs and goals, including:
Scalable: Meeting Bay Transit’s evolving service needs by providing a
software platform with scalable infrastructure, capabilities, and features.
Flexible: Matching the nuanced local needs of Bay Transit riders, drivers,
and staff by offering flexible platform configurations.
Easy to Use: Improving Bay Transit’s rider, driver, and provider experience
through a user-friendly, integrated, and automated software suite.
Interoperable: Accommodating Bay Transit’s existing and future technology,
hardware, and software systems through compatible integration capabilities.
Secure: Protecting data, systems, and users from unauthorized access,
malicious attacks, and vulnerabilities through robust security standards.
Safe: Prioritizing user safety through a combination of features relating to
data security, incident response, and safe transportation operations.
Reliable and Available: Ensuring the delivery of consistent performance
through constant system monitoring and prompt incident response.
We will work closely with Bay Transit to meet and exceed the requirements defined in
Section 2.3 of the RFP. Below, we have outlined our approach for each group of
specifications, and welcome further discussion on how we can best support Bay Transit.

Via’s Rider App has been downloaded by millions of passengers and consistently receives
glowing reviews, and is the only mobile booking app that has consistently demonstrated
an exceptional user experience. As part of Bay Transit’s evaluation process, we suggest
downloading and using various proposers’ apps to get a direct sense of their functionality
and the user experience. Poor app performance — whether due to a poor User Interface
(UI) or an unreliable booking process — dissuades riders from sticking with a service, or
even booking their very first trip.
The app will be white-labeled to reflect Bay Transit’s preferred branding. Should Bay
Transit prefer, we are also happy to adjust the branding to reflect the consolidated
services. Our rider app is designed with accessibility at the forefront and includes a host
of features for those in need of visual, audio, cognitive, or mobility accommodations. The
app is also available in many languages, including Spanish, and will use the device’s
primary language as the default language setting. Riders
without internet access will be able to create accounts and
book rides by calling a customer support representative.
Rider Registration
A new rider creates an account by entering an email address,
phone number, and password. They can then indicate
specific preferences in configurable profile fields, such as for
fare payment methods and vehicle types (such as a
wheelchair-accessible vehicle), or note if they require a lift,
walker, scooter, or other mobility device. Passengers can also
note if they will be traveling with a personal care attendant.
Riders who would prefer to call a customer support
representative can create an account over the phone. Upon account creation, our system
will automatically populate a Rider Profile where administrators can review customer
account details. Riders can track things such as payment and ride history through their
Rider App profiles, and will have password or username recovery options.
Ride Booking
Upon opening the rider app and creating an account, users will clearly see the geofenced
zones in which services are offered and will be invited to set a pick-up location. Riders will
be able to seamlessly transition between services that they are eligible for, even when
