Our demand response software delivers an exceptional experience for all users — riders,
drivers, and backend staff — and the necessary flexibility to meet Bay Transit’s evolving
transit needs. Our dedicated project team and expert advisors will continually work with
Bay Transit to achieve its goal of streamlining transit operations and enhancing the user
experience of Bay Transit drivers, dispatchers, and riders. We will leverage our in-house
service design, marketing, and fundraising teams to ensure a seamless demand response
service transition and long-term scalability across Bay Transit’s services.
Our proposal for Bay Transit is centered on:
A Single End-to-End Platform for On-Demand and Demand Response Services
Via’s suite of familiar and user-friendly tools will provide Bay Transit with a single unified
interface to manage all of its on-demand and demand response services, eliminating the
​need for Bay Transit administrators to learn an entirely new system. Our software suite,
which currently powers Bay Transit Express in the Gloucester Courthouse area, includes
three core components: our white-labeled, fully automated Rider App, designed with user
experience and accessibility at the forefront; our Driver App, which provides real-time
turn-by-turn navigation and a hands-free display; and the Via Operations Center, a
highly automated administrative platform that Bay Transit staff will use to manage
services in real time. Through Via’s software, administrators will be able to seamlessly
visualize and analyze the services thanks to a suite of fully integrated dashboards,
reports and data tables.

Algorithms to Maximize Service Value
Our partners provide services that are
essential to the overall mobility of their
communities, and Via’s technology is proven to
maximize the public benefit that they can
provide within a fixed budget. Our proprietary
algorithms and mapping infrastructure are
designed to help agencies provide their riders
with essential services that offer high service
quality, increased vehicle utilization, and
reduced vehicle miles traveled. Our software
uses machine learning, artificial intelligence,
and leverages the data of the over 145 million
trips we have powered. This allows our
platform to make routing decisions based on
historic and real-time traffic speeds, travel times, and customer demand data on a
second-by-second basis, allowing vehicles to avoid congested areas and slower roads
and provide more accurate wait times and ETAs to riders. As an example, King County
Metro in Seattle, WA ran microtransit pilot programs with three vendors over four years,
and found that they were able to save more than $53 per ride using Via’s technology
compared to competitors’, allowing them to serve more trips with the same budget (as
shown in the graphic above).

Local Experience Powering Bay Transit Express

unique physical environment of the rural region, and the needs of
the local community through our history of successful partnership
with Bay Transit. In particular, our experience powering Bay Transit
Express since 2021 has given us a keen awareness of unique rider
preferences, demand patterns, and other metrics critical to
delivering a successful service in Bay Transit’s service area. In addition, we have
