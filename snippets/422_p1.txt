Exhibit A - UTA On Demand Microtransit and Other Innovative Mobility Services Scope of Work CONTINUING SERVICE: TRANSPORATION AS A SERVICE (TaaS) ON-DEMAND AND MICROTRANSIT The Contractor must plan, design, operate, and continuously improve on-demand and microtransit services utilizing TaaS models and other on-demand services as determined by UTA to support UTA’s Five-Year Service Plan . TaaS solutions must include a full turn-key solution which supports all aspects of a services including, but not limited to, contractor provided technology solutions (mobile app called UTA On Demand App), vehicles, vehicle maintenance and cleaning, drivers, and customer service capabilities. A service may be implemented as a pilot for testing concepts and various aspects of the service. The Contractor must provide the following services as part of a TaaS solution. Personnel The Contractor must provide all necessary personnel, including but not limited to, drivers, mechanics, administrative, customer service agents, service managers, and others to successfully operate a TaaS service and that meet the requirements itemized below. The Contractor must provide qualified drivers with good customer service skills, who are experienced in serving people with disabilities whether or not they use mobility devices, and who are trained as required by law for safe vehicle operations. The Contractor’s qualified and trained drivers must instill in customers confidence in the quality and safety of the service. The Contractor must establish minimum standards and driver qualification requirements. The Contractor must conduct a Utah Bureau of Criminal Identification (BCI) background check on all drivers, who must pass such check prior to their performing work on UTA contract services. The Contractor must implement a comprehensive drug and alcohol testing program for all drivers. This program must cover recruitment, hiring, and ongoing employment, ensuring full compliance with all applicable Federal Transit Administration (FTA) requirements. Key elements include but are not limited to adoption of clear policies and procedures, mandatory training for relevant personnel, regular random drug and alcohol testing, post-incident/accident drug and alcohol testing, and reasonable suspicion drug and alcohol testing. The Contractor is solely responsible for the independent implementation and management of its drug and alcohol program. UTA requires the ability to contact appropriate Contractor personnel regarding the drug and alcohol program. The Contractor must identify a Designated Employer Representative (DER) and/or a Drug &amp; Alcohol Program Manager (DAPM) for effective communication and service oversight. The Contractor must provide all technical training and support required for the provision of service that meets the requirements and standards of this contract, as well as any training required by UTA, and state and federal laws and regulations. The Contractor may provide some services with subcontractors. E.g., maintenance contracts. Such subcontractors and their qualifications must be included in this proposal, except that a subcontractor may be proposed during the contract term for UTA review and approval consistent with the section governing Addition of Subcontractors. The Contractor agrees to provide sufficient access to UTA and its contractors to inspect and audit records and information related to performance of the Contract as reasonably may be required. Vehicles The Contractor must provide all vehicles that are necessary to perform the requirements of this contract, such vehicles must meet all legal requirements, including but not limited to registration and emission inspections. The Contractor must be able to supply vehicles with specific fuel requirements as specified for each service order by UTA, including gasoline fueled vehicles, hybrid vehicles, and electric vehicles. Contractor must provide vehicles that meet ridership demand, service parameters, and that perform safely in weather conditions common to the Wasatch Front Region. The Contractor must be able to increase or reduce the number of vehicles to perform contract services as UTA determines requirements to meet changes in service demand or expansion. UTA will provide reasonable notice of such changes whenever possible. The Contractor must be able to temporarily shift vehicles across zones as needed to balance supply. All vehicles dedicated exclusively to UTA’s services must be Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) compliant wheelchair accessible vehicles (WAV). The Contractor is solely responsible for the maintenance of the service vehicles. Contractor must maintain service vehicles in a state of good repair that enables contractor to provide highly reliable service. The Contractor must regularly clean the vehicles to provide a pleasant environment for customers that is free of offensive odors and substances. The Contractor must register all permanent vehicles in Utah, pay Utah state registration fees, and affix Utah plates. As needed, the Contractor to provide enhanced cleaning procedures to ensure customer health and safety (e.g., support COVID-19 cleaning, and safety protocols as recommended by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC). Parking / Vehicle Storage Facilities UTA will collaborate with the Contractor to identify appropriate parking lots or vehicle storage locations which UTA may own or lease for contractors use for this contract. In the event UTA and the Contractor are unable to identify an appropriate UTA controlled vehicle storage location, the Contractor will have sole responsibility to identify and lease or otherwise obtain use of an appropriate vehicle storage facility. Fuel The Contractor must include fuel costs in any TaaS pricing models. The Contractor must provide all fuel and charge the cost of fuel to UTA as a passthrough cost. The Contractor must report on fuel purchase and use by zone to enable National Transit Database (NTD) reporting and to fulfill UTA zone-specific billing requirements including but not limited to cost, gallons used, sales tax, and fuel spend without sales tax. Electric Vehicle Charging If the Contractor operates a service under this contract using an electric vehicle or fleet, UTA will collaborate with the Contractor to identify appropriate vehicle charging locations. Expense Responsibility: UTA is currently covering all electric vehicle charging costs because all designated charging locations utilize UTA-owned infrastructure. Contractors should not include electric charging expenses in their bids. The responsibility for payment of electric vehicle charging expenses will be determined on a case-by-case basis for any charging that occurs outside of UTA's designated infrastructure. Ride Booking Technologies The Contractor must provide a single customer-facing smartphone application (UTA On Demand Customer App) for a fully automated scheduling, dispatching and reserving demand responsive transit service as described in the section governing Software as a Service (SaaS) Solutions. All TaaS solutions must be managed through the UTA On Demand App that integrates all service options. This platform must be connected to UTA’s preferred trip planner, Transit App, for trip discovery. Customer Support Services The Contractor is required to develop and implement comprehensive Customer Support Services that provide to On Demand customers timely and effective information and actions such as may reasonably expected for the criticality and urgency of the services being provided. On Demand customers include IMZ clients such as Tooele County. The Contractor must provide Customer Support Services to respond to and to document customer feedback through a ticketing system. The Contractor must have a regular Customer Support Services Plan. The regular support plan must include: A critical issue escalation path and procedure for ad-hoc UTA support through the UTA Customer Service Department with processes for working with UTA for prompt and effective resolution when such coordination is required. A complaint resolution target timeline (e.g., 4 working days). Management of lost and found services. The ability to collect granular data regarding customer experience and satisfaction. This includes trend analysis of customer-provided quality ratings for the support they receive. The ability to audio record and review customer support calls. The Contractor must maintain ADA compliance and ability to communicate with, book on-demand, and pay for ride services for customers without a smartphone through telephone. The Contractor must provide customers access to the Contractor’s customer support services to book on-demand and to pay for ride services through telephone or web-based services for customers without a smartphone. Any additional relevant regulation or requirements from the FTA Contractor Manual, 2025 (e.g. FTA Safety Management System (SMS), NTD Safety &amp; Security (SS40, SS50), Transit Asset Management (TAM), etc. as needed). Marketing and Communications UTA marketing personnel and marketing consultants will generally lead the creation of a marketing strategy and plan, customer acquisition plan, and any related marketing collateral. POTENTIAL NEW SERVICES Transportation as a Service (TaaS) Solutions – Transportation Network Company (TNC) On Demand Services 1 .1 Operations TNC Operations will allow UTA to test new and innovative transportation solutions by offering riders on-demand transit services through a Transportation-as-a-Service (TaaS) model. This service is designed to enhance transit operations and improve the customer experience by providing partially subsidized trips to passengers. Customers will be able to travel anywhere within a service area and to limited service points outside of the service area during specified service hours. The goal is to deliver high-quality, flexible, and immediate transit solutions when and where demand is lower than the microtransit threshold (between 2-5 riders per vehicle per hour). Testing this type of TNC coverage is intended to strengthen the reliability and adaptability of UTA’s transit network in support of the agency’s Five-Year Service Plan. 1 . 2 Contractor Requirement s As part of the TaaS solution, the Contractor must provide the following capabilities: Ability to set days and hours of operation. Geographic service area restrictions. Budget control features, including a maximum per-ride subsidy amount (e.g., $10.00). Transit voucher issuance and management. Use of non-dedicated vehicles - provided that FTA-defined ADA-equivalent service is available. Rider payment of the standard UTA fare upfront. Ability to set subsidy limits on a per-person, per-trip basis, or by time window. Multiple options for program and voucher distribution. Data reporting capabilities: ridership data, NTD and other FTA mandated data, compliance data, etc. as needed. Compliance with all other applicable TaaS program requirements. 2. Transportation as a Service (TaaS) Solutions – Low Ridership Shuttles The Contractor must plan, design, operate, and continuously improve shuttle services serving low-ridership markets (under five riders per hour). These services are designed to serve low-demand areas, including outlying rural locations that are farther from UTA service garages and where operating fixed routes is challenging for UTA. They may also be piloted during low-demand times of day or on certain days of the week to assess potential interest from new customer markets. They may also be tested in areas where travel demand is highly structured—with consistent but often spread-out origins and destinations—and where ridership remains low, as measured by passengers per vehicle hour falling below UTA’s threshold for introducing bus service. The contractor must provide the following services as part of a low-demand shuttle solution. Fixed routes with fixed shuttle stops. Consistent operating schedule. Scheduled timepoints or headways. Vehicles: Operating ADA compliant vehicles sized for specific shuttle route needs. Acceptable vehicle sizes and examples include small, medium (such as cutaways, minibuses, midi-buses, and up to 16-passenger vehicles), and full size. Complying with all applicable public transit regulations, including but not limited to FTA fixed-route ADA accessibility requirements—such as making internal and external stop announcements. UTA is open to hybrid shuttle service designs that also serve limited on demand requests. The Contractor may propose flexible solutions for low-ridership areas or times. This could include, but is not limited to, a hybrid service model that combines scheduled shuttles with an on-demand component. These services are designed to complement existing UTA operations. Shuttle operations shall be delivered as a full turnkey solution, consistent with standards as outlined in this Scope. Specific shuttle services will be authorized under this contract with service orders. The detailed requirements for these services are in the Appendix A : Transportation as a Service (TaaS) Solutions – Low Ridership Shuttles . At present, this includes Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. Ability to set subsidy limits on a per-person, per-trip basis, or by time window. Multiple options for program and voucher distribution. Data reporting capabilities. Compliance with all other applicable TaaS program requirements. 4 .
