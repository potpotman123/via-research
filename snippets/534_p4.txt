SCOPE OF WORK 
PUBLIC TRANSIT CONSULTANT SERVICES 
 
October 10, 2022 
 
 
I. 
BACKGROUND: 
 
The VTrans Public Transit Section is managed by a Program Manager, and currently consists 
of three regional public transit coordinators and a financial administrator. Funding is 
primarily through the Federal Transit Administration (FTA), Federal Highway Administration 
(FHWA), and the state transportation fund. The Public Transit Section is a collaborative team 
with a diverse range of individual skills and experience, whose work involves extensive 
consultation with staff and leadership from a wide variety of offices and organizations – 
including local transit providers and their state association, regional planning bodies, other State 
agencies, private sector entities, and the federal government. Typical activities include: 
 
x Preparing statewide public transit plans, such as the Public Policy Transit Plan, and the 
Human Service Transportation Coordination Plan, and undertaking special studies as 
needed by legislative bodies.  
 
x Implementing federal rulemaking, such as the Public Transit Asset Management Plan, and 
the Public Transit Safety Plan, as well as complying with all FTA rules, requirements, and 
circulars. 
 
x Providing technical assistance to Vermont’s public transit providers, including route and 
service development, civil rights and maintenance compliance. 
 
x Helping Public Transit Program Coordinators with Subrecipient Management Reviews. 
Assistance will include but not be limited to reviewing and providing guidance on the eight 
(8) primary areas of focus: ADA, DBE, Financial Capacity, Financial Management, 
Maintenance, Procurement, Technical Capacity and Title VI as outlined in VTrans’ 
Subrecipient Management Review Compliance Workbook. Aiding with site visits that may 
include but not be limited to in depth review/inspection of fleet vehicles, facilities, ADA 
compliance, labor and public notice compliance, financial records, procurement files and 
safety and security procedures.  
 
x 
Assisting the Secretary's office in the development and analysis of public transit policy, 
participating in agency-wide internal strategic planning efforts, and providing 
background and support for Agency legislative activities at the state and federal level. 
 
x Preparation of federal discretionary grant applications for FTA and other federal 
competitive grant programs, such as State of Good Repair, Bus and Bus Facilities and 
Low or No Emissions Vehicles, and information technologies projects.
