SUBJECT: 
Remix Technologies, LLC - Request for Approval of Liability Terms 
 
The City acting through the Managing Director
desires to enter into a micro-purchase
service agreement with Remix Technologies, LLC (Remix) for a software data platform.  The 
software platform will allow the City to both better engage with residents related to street design 
as well as collaborate with partner agencies such as SEPTA and DVRPC while saving the city 
consulting fees for concept design development.  The contract would be for a term of one (1) 
year, effective June 30, 2021, at a cost to the City of $34,000.  This pricing is being offered
exclusively for the City of Philadelphia as a part of a regional license with SEPTA and DVRPC.  
 
Because the limitation of liability provision of the contract differs from the City s standard 
provisions applicable to intellectual property contracts, this provision requires approval from the 
Director of Finance, the City Solicitor, and the Risk Mana
 The proposed 
limitation of liability is attached as Exhibit A, 
 
Discussion 
 
Remix is the only company that brings together the complete transportation picture in a single 
software platform.  The software platform is called Remix Streets and Explore.  The use of this 
platform helps cities develop the best transportation outcomes for their communities. The 
technology enables the City to see all of the data in one place, plan a holistic network, and build 
consensus across stakeholders.  
 
Remix will not agree to unlimited liability for this project.  The proposed limitation of liability 
has been reviewed by attorneys in the Law Department s Commercial Law Unit and Intellectual 
Property Division and has been revised to increase the liability limit to twice what originally 
proposed by Remix based on a recommendation from the Division of Risk Management. 
Acceptance of the proposed terms appears to be a legitimate business decision in this particular 
case.
