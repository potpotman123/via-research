Customer will be billed for the above fees as described below. Fees assume Launch Date occurs
within six months of contract signing. River North Customer shall pay the following fees to River
North:
Fixed (Upfront) Costs
Customer shall pay to River North a non-refundable installation fee of $239,120, which will be
payable in full upon signing of this Order. In addition to technology localization costs, installation
fee includes 7 rear-entry WAV retrofits at a cost of $22,000 each, 16 vehicle wraps at a cost of
$3,000 each, and 16 in-vehicle fareboxes at a cost of $1,070 each, which will be treated as a
pass-through. Should Customer require additional WAV retrofits or vehicle wraps, River North will
bill Customer as incurred as a pass-through.
Ongoing Invoice Fees
Customer shall be responsible for monthly Variable Fee per Revenue Hour in the table above
(the "Monthly Subscription Fees"). A revenue hour is defined as each hour during which a driver
is paid on the Via platform. All fees set forth herein shall be payable by Customer on a net thirty
basis. River North
River North will provide Customer with an invoice statement showing the calculation of the
Monthly Subscription Fees incurred based on the actual number of revenue hours that Driver
Partners performed during that month in the form attached hereto as Exhibit A. The Customer
hereby agrees that the sample invoice form set forth on Exhibit A is satisfactory to Customer, both
in substance and format, and sufficient to process invoicing such that questions regarding invoice
or invoice support format will not delay payment to River North. If Customer requires additional
invoice support, River North can use best efforts to provide it, and additional costs of $1,500 per
custom request will apply.
Should changes in applicable federal, state or local law result in a significant change in River
North's costs, River North or Customer may opt to renegotiate the ongoing monthly fees. Should
changes in applicable market dynamics result in an increase in River North's cost per hour of 10%
or more to any of the following categories, compared to costs at the time of contract signing, River
North may propose an amendment to the ongoing on-demand revenue hour rate accordingly,
subject to written approval by the Customer, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Any such
proposed rate adjustment shall occur no more than once annually and must include supporting
documentation of the relevant cost increases. The Customer shall have thirty (30) days to review,
validate, or dispute such adjustment prior to implementation for the following line items:
• Labor (driver pay)
• Vehicle rental
• Fuel

4. Service Parameters
River North will provide access to a platform service (the "Platform") through which Riders will be
able to book and pay for rides on a shared and on-demand basis; and dedicated vehicles will be
offered by the Vehicle Provider to Driver Partners on a rental basis.
• Geographic Coverage Zone: Approximately 25 square miles coverage zone in
Springfield, Ohio.
• Service Days/Hours: To be determined during planning and design phase and agreed
upon by both parties
• Rider Fare: The rider fare will be determined during planning and design phase and
agreed upon by both parties. During the duration of the Service, rider fare may be
amended by mutual agreement between Customer and River North which may require
Commission approval.
• Payment: River North will ensure acceptance of Rider payment through the app via credit
cards, cash and pre-paid debit cards.
• Vehicle Fleet: The Vehicle Provider will offer a fleet of up to 16 branded, licensed and
insured vehicles, including 7 Wheelchair Accessible Vehicles (WAV) to be made available
to independent contractor Driver Partners, who will be able to gain access to these
vehicles after being registered onto the Platform. Three vehicles within the fleet will be
treated as spare vehicles. If the branded vehicle fleet is not sufficient to meet service
requirements for any reason, River North has the flexibility to (1) supplement the fleet with
a temporary fleet that would include other makes and models, and/or (2) allow for Driver
Partners to drive their own personal vehicles, to be deployed for temporary short term
periods, with prior written approval from the Customer on acceptable vehicle
specifications, including safety standards, insurance coverage, and branding
requirements. River North shall provide documentation verifying compliance with these
requirements for each such vehicle prior to deployment..
• Parking: Parties shall mutually agree to lease terms for the facility located at 100 W
Jefferson St, Springfield, OH 45506. Terms shall be specified in a separate Facility Lease
Agreement.
