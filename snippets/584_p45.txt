SCOPE OF SERVICES 
Transit, Streets, and On-Demand Planning software: Scope of Services 
BACKGROUND 
The Capital Metro Planning Department needs to continue to use the Remix Transit, Streets, and On-Demand 
software as a service platform that can be utilized by existing staff to help aid in the route design, census data 
analysis and public engagement process as well as fulfilling federal requirements for transit service changes 
such as conducting a Title VI analysis. 
Every five years, Capital Metro undergoes a holistic update to their 10-year service plan in order to address 
changing demographic markets, new development, and a variety of other factors. The development of this plan 
involves the extraction and analysis of loads of data for every route in the Capital Metro system along with 
demographic and accessibility analyses of all areas within Capital Metro’s service area (whether they are 
served by a route or not). The creation of this plan involves the hiring of a team of consultants and is only 
carried out once every five years. The planning and public engagement required for the implementation of 
these service plans requires exhaustive and extensive efforts by internal planning, public engagement, and 
communications staff for each and every system or route change. 
PRIMARY WORK TASKS 
The software as a service platform should address each of the following: 
1)
Route Design – Ability to quickly and easily draw transit routes and stops on existing street network
and alter spans of service, travel speeds, and vehicle headway. Ability to import existing Capital Metro
routes and stops for individual or system-wide analysis using GTFS feed or other data format.
2)
Demographic Analysis – Ability to instantly view affected demographic populations within defined
distance from bus stop placement along selected transit route. Including the ability to export
demographic data.
3)
Operations Costing – Ability to instantly view estimated annual operations costs and bus requirements
for selected transit route, based on editable user inputs (e.g. span of service, travel speed, vehicle
headway, etc.)
4)
Title VI Reporting – Ability to generate federally-mandated reports in a format and using a methodology
accepted by the Federal Transportation Agency (FTA), pertaining to Low-Income and Minority access
to transit, based on a proposed route/system change drawn or created within the software as a service
platform. Title VI reporting functionality will be updated as FTA regulations or other governmental
requirements change.
5)
Ability to integrate NearMap to ensure the most up to date aerials are being viewed.
6)
Ability to add custom agency data such as ridership, active transportation networks, bike share
locations, trail networks, and more.
7)
Public Outreach – Ability to display and interact with multiple route options while instantly updating
corresponding demographic and operations cost analyses. Ability to share interactive web maps to
interested parties outside of Capital Metro. Ability to share maps and comment and text annotations
with internal and external stakeholders via a hyperlink.
8)
System Accessibility – Ability to analyze locational travel sheds - how far a potential customer could
travel in a given amount of time (e.g. 10, 15, 30, 60 minutes) from a particular location – using proposed
(or existing) route or route network by time of day and day of week.

PROJECT PLAN & MILESTONES: 
- 
Within first two weeks following contract execution: 
o
Establish login information and accounts for all relevant CMTA Staff
o
Upload Current Street Network, updated GTFS feed and relevant data
o
Provide on-boarding/training sessions as described in “Other Work Tasks/Services” section
below.
OTHER WORK TASKS/SERVICES 
- 
Vendor will provide on-boarding/Training Sessions for all staff who plan to actively use the program 
and maintain a user account either on-site or via webinar/video conference. On-boarding/Training 
sessions will be provided upon request for new staff. Full user documentation will be provided by the 
vendor. 
- 
Vendor will aid in the updating of underlying demographic data and transit route/stop data within the 
software as a service platform. Demographic data, drawing from most up-to-date annual American 
Community Survey (ACS) data, should be updated by vendor automatically as the data becomes 
available online. GTFS or other data format feeding the transit route/stop data should be updated with 
each triennial service change (January, June and August), by request of Capital Metro staff. 
- 
Vendor will aid in the uploading of custom data layers to the software as a service platform 
- 
Vendor will be available to help troubleshoot problems with the platform and/or data outputs throughout 
the time that Capital Metro maintains a subscription license during business hours (Monday-Friday, 
9am-5pm CST). Service response and resolution time during these hours should be less than 48 
hours. 
- 
Vendor will consider custom software changes or updates based on customer needs/requests. 
- 
For setting user logins, Capital Metro prefers an option to have user authentication integration with 
Microsoft Active Directory. Capital Metro will evaluate if this implementation is feasible with given 
security needs. 
- 
Vendor will provide secure web access to hosted system 24x7x365 except for agreed upon periods of 
maintenance. 
- 
Describe the safeguards in place to prevent unauthorized access to the domain, software, and data. 
- 
Vendor might perform interviews of Capital Metro IT personnel to determine business requirements 
and address all business requirements in the proposed specifications. 
- 
Describe the process and procedures for regular backups of analysis/maps/data output created by 
Capital Metro accounts.
