SECTION I 
 
GENERAL INFORMATION 
 
1.0. 
Introduction 
Tacoma School District (District) prepared this BID document to procure safe and reliable 
transportation services with vehicles for District students to/from District locations.  
This BID contains the instructions governing the proposals to be submitted by interested 
proposers. All proposers waive any right to claim damages of any nature, whatsoever, 
based upon the selection process.  Proposers who submit proposals that do not follow the 
instructions or do not provide the information requested within this BID may be subject to 
immediate rejection.  All proposals submitted shall be complete with pricing guaranteed 
for three (3) years from the date of the contract.  
 
1.1. 
Background/Rationale: 
This BID is for a three (3)-year contract term, with an estimated usage of one hundred and 
ten (110) trips daily, during the regular school year. Multiple vendors may be awarded; 
however, the District reserves the right to not award an item based on the best interests of 
the District relative to cost and contract administration.  
1.2. 
Invitation to Provide Bid 
 
This BID is for the procurement of Alternate Transportation with Vehicles for District 
Students.  This BID will be managed electronically using the Bonfire on-line bid site (see
