ON-DEMAND RIDESHARE CONTRACT 
 
 
River North Transit LLC  (“Contractor” or “Via”) and City of Arlington, TX (“Customer” or “City” and, 
together with Via, the “Parties”) have entered into that certain agreement titled On-Demand Rideshare 
Contract, dated November 29, 2022 (the “Agreement”). This Amendment #1 to the On-Demand Rideshare 
Contract (the “Amendment”) is entered into on this 23rd day of May, 2023.  Upon execution of this 
Amendment #1 (the “Amendment”), the Parties agree to modify the Agreement as follows: 
 
1. 
Amendment.  
 
Section II. A. of the Agreement is modified to read as follows: 
 
CITY agrees to pay CONTRACTOR the amounts set forth in the Scope of Services, not to 
exceed $30,412,626 (i.e., the total amount of funding obtained by the City as of the date hereof) 
in the aggregate for the initial two-year period of this Contract, subject to the following 
provisions. This total of $26,367,762 includes a maximum of $22,047,916 for the standard Via 
service cost, $179,502 for the Arlington Student Transportation Program, $4,020,224 for the 
Arlington RAPID service, and $139,984 for the Handitran paratransit software, and $25,000 for 
the translation of the rider app in Vietnamese.   
 
In addition, all other references to the “not to exceed” amount shall be increased accordingly to 
$30,412,626,

related services for City’s Handitran paratransit services.   
 
3. Conflicts, Use of Terms, Governing Law. Capitalized terms used but not defined herein have the 
meanings set forth in the Agreement. Except as expressly provided herein, the terms and conditions of the 
Agreement remain unchanged. This Amendment #1 will be governed by the same law as the Agreement 
and is effective as of May 23, 2023.

EXHIBIT E 
Paratransit Software DEPLOYMENT SERVICE ORDER 
 
By this service order (the “Order”), River North Transit, LLC, a Delaware company with its principal office located at 10 Crosby Street, 
Floor 2, New York, New York 10013 (“Via”), and the customer identified below (“Customer”) agree to collaborate towards the 
deployment of the Via software and related services described below for use in Customer operated Handitran paratransit services(the 
“Deployment”) in Arlington, TX.  
 
Customer Name: City of Arlington, Texas  
Customer Entity Type / State of Incorporation: City, State of 
Texas 
Customer Notice Address: 101 W. Abram Street, 
Arlington, TX 76010 
Customer Email: alicia.winkelblech@arlingtontx.gov 
Term: The duration of the Deployment shall last until December 31, 2024, subject to extension by mutual agreement of the 
parties on terms to be agreed (including any increase in monthly fees for additional months) or earlier termination under the 
Terms of the On-Demand Rideshare Contract dated November 29, 2022.   
Services: Customer will receive access to the standard Via Solution and automatic software updates, comprised of: 
(a) Fully localized proprietary routing and matching algorithms that analyze all trip requests, assign riders dynamically 
to the best-suited vehicle, and group passengers headed in the same direction into efficient shared rides powered by 
Via’s patented technology; 
(b) Downloadable iOS and Android rider apps that allow customers to book rides, track vehicles in real time, and 
troubleshoot any issues; 
(c) Drivers supporting the service, employed by the Customer or taxi, will use a downloadable driver app that provides 
efficient turn-by-turn directions; the app allows drivers to start and end driving time, access any rider notes and 
contact live support via SMS; and 
(d) Access to the Via Operations Console (“VOC”), which allows administrators to perform a variety of functions, 
including booking trips, checking trip details, creating and editing rider accounts, update rider eligibility, and 
managing live service; 
(e) Access to the data reporting  set out in Appendix 1, made available via the VOC. Ability to run custom queries 
through access to Via’s Snowflake Sharing Solution which allows partners to access their Via data through a state- 
of-the-art machine-to-machine sharing solution.  
(f) Via will translate the rider app into Vietnamese and Spanish.  
 
Once the first phase of the launch process is complete and all inputs are received from the Customer, Via will deliver a 
service design document to be reviewed by the Customer and mutually agreed to by the parties in writing. The target launch 
date will be set in the service design document.   
Support Services – Installation. Via provides installation support for up to four weeks after the Deployment begins 
serving Riders, consisting of:  
(a) Localization for the Deployment Zone (defined below), including optimizing the algorithm, configuring back-end, 
and defining acceptable pick-up and drop off points; 
(b) Testing and quality assurance;  
(c) Helping Customer to build a launch plan; and 
(d) Instruction for drivers, dispatchers, and managers on Via’s best practices as launch support  
Support Services – Ongoing. The following services are included in the fees up to the number of hours per month 
identified below:  
(a) Operational support and system adjustments: up to 10 hours per month 
(b) Expert consulting: up to 5 hours per month 
● 
Community Engagement and growth: support with engaging existing Rider community to ensure 
smooth transition to new technology  
● 
Operations: Including supply optimization analysis, payment & fraud investigation, and business 
case/unit economics analysis 
● 
Service expansion: Including feasibility analysis for service expansions or additional projects 
(c) Tech Support: Dedicated Via point of contact will use commercially reasonable efforts to respond within one 
business day for non-critical issues (upon receipt of a detailed description of the issue as requested by Via) and to 
ensure that assistance is provided within a reasonable time frame. Via will also provide Customer with an 
appropriate channel for alerting Via to system outages or other critical issues, with respect to which Via will 
provide emergency assistance. 
 
Via will notify Customer if Customer is within 1 hour of exceeding the capped hourly limit on Operational Support and 
System Adjustments and/or Consulting Services. If requested, hours beyond those set out above will be charged on an 
hourly rate.
