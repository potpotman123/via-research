EXHIBIT A 
 
Transit Provider Services 
Mobile, Alabama 
Reference # 5963 
 
SCOPE OF SERVICES 
 
SERVICE PROVIDER’S NAME will provide the following services, which will be set forth in greater detail 
in one or more of the below sections: 
1) Full and comprehensive “turnkey” management, operations, logistics, maintenance, promotion, 
and innovation implementation(s), including end-to-end technology platform(s), for the Mobile 
Transit System, integrated with an expanded, City-funded equivalent version of Mobile Area 
Chamber of Commerce’s pilot ride-share program “MoGo;”  
2) Fixed route, paratransit, and Microtransit services, to the greatest degree possible, within all 
annexed areas of City of Mobile; 
3) Advisory and technical assistance as may be reasonably required in the management of the Mobile 
Transit System.   
 
 
Provision 1- Service Provider shall assume control of the existing transit system and operate it mostly  as-
is from execution date of the contract through month nine of the Agreement.  
Provision 2- Service Provider shall be prepared to start operations of the re-branded and fully integrated 
system, including the new and expanded on demand rideshare service, which is then operated more in line 
with the VIA proposal for services from the start of month ten and for the next four annual terms of the 
contract.   
Provision 3- The SERVICE PROVIDER reserves the right and has an obligation to adjust services to a level 
that coincides with the total funding amount for any fiscal year, but shall consult the OWNER prior to 
implementing said adjustments to the system. Any adjustments to the system shall be communicated to the 
public by and through the SERVICE PROVIDER.
