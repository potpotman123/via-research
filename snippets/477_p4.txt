MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING OF THE BOARD OF RIVER NORTH TRANSIT LLC 
 
 
A special meeting of the management and officers (the “Board”) of River North Transit LLC (the 
“Company”) was held virtually on December 1, 2021 at 11:05 AM EST. Erin Abrams presided as Chair. 
 
 
Present: 
Erin Abrams, Manager 
 
Alex Lavoie, Manager 
 
Dillon Twombly, Manager-Elect 
 
Erin Abrams called the meeting to order and said the first matter to come before the Board was the 
Company’s restructuring of its officers, to remove Ryan Sullivan and John Criezis as managers (as they are 
no longer employed by the Parent Company, Via Transportation, Inc. (“Via”)), and add Dillon Twombly 
as a manager. Erin Abrams presented a brief summary of the business and operational reasons for the 
proposal, which included the desirability of having at least three managers of the Company and granting 
Dillon Twombly, Chief Revenue Officer of  Via, with the authority to approve and sign contracts on behalf 
of the Company. 
 
 
The current managers for the Company would be as follows: 
 
 
Erin Abrams, Manager 
 
Alex Lavoie, Manager 
 
Dillon Twombly, Manager 
 
 
The board voted to appoint Dillon Twombly to the Board effective December 3, 2021. 
 
 
After a full discussion, the Board agreed that the appointments were in the best interests of the 
Company. Upon motion made by Erin Abrams to approve the appointments, and seconded by Alex Lavoie, 
the Board unanimously approved the appointments. 
 
The meeting was adjourned at 11:10 AM EST.
