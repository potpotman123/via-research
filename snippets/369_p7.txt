Service Design and Operations Details
• One year pilot, launch Fall 2022
• Curb to curb service design 
• ~2.5 square miles 
• Service hours to cover all or most of high frequency fixed route in the 
service area (5am-12am) 
• Max 5 vehicles in service/2 spares
• Fares: Same as bus fare, normal transfer rules 
• TAP eligible and student discounts accepted
