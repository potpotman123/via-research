Microtransit Services Overview – Clayton Popik and Latrice Virola 
o You can find the Microtransit contract awarded to Via in Resolution #72 in 2023. 
o Via will provide software, vehicles, and drivers as a turnkey solution in Stark County. 
o SARTA Connect will add to transit-dependent residents and the surrounding areas: 
1. More Flexibility 
2. Improved Access 
3. Always Affordable 
4. Seamless Travel Planning 
o The objectives of SARTA Connect: 
1. Convenient app-based booking.  
2. Work trips, fill service gaps.  
3. Create a solution for first-mile/last-mile obstacles. 
4. The SARTA Connect will be a feeder service to SARTA’s fixed routes. 
5. Countywide rural public transportation.

o Pilot launch July 2024. There will be a gradual launch across the county. Transit 
Centers will be anchors for each pilot area. After each pilot launch, SARTA will 
evaluate each stage. 
o The SARTA Connect one-way ride cost will be the same as SARTA’s fixed route cost 
of $1.50.  
 
Pilot Areas and Timing of Launch 
Approximately six (6) to eight (8) vehicles will begin at the pilot launch in the SW Stark 
Massillon area, with the expectation that twelve (12) to fifteen (15) vehicles will be in use by 
the final launch stage.  
 SW Stark/Massillon Transit Center – July 22, 2024 (Approx. 6-8 vehicles) 
 NE Stark/Alliance Transit Center – September 2024 
 SE Stark/Cornerstone Transit Center – November 2024 
 NW Stark/Belden Village Transit Center – January 2025  
 Final evaluation of countywide service – March 2025 
 
Mobil Application Branding and Logo 
 The logo and vehicle wrap will be relative to our current one, with just enough 
modifications to distinguish between SARTA regular services and Via services. 
 
Sunday Service 
 Reintroduce Sunday service to Stark County. 
 Attentive restart in April 2025. 
 Start as a pre-booked Demand Response service to determine usage. 
 Introduce an on-demand service if ridership demand can support it.
