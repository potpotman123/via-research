2. Relevant Experience
At Via, we believe that access to mobility promotes human connections, creates
economic opportunity, and fosters more equitable communities. To advance this vision,
we build innovative software enabling our partners to transform legacy transportation
systems into user-friendly, technology-enabled networks. Using our software, our
partners achieve an enhanced level of visibility over their operations, simultaneously
lowering operating costs and increasing quality of service for riders. We offer innovative
solutions for microtransit, ADA paratransit, workforce enablement, senior transportation,
integrated trip planning and payment systems, autonomous vehicle (AV) fleet
management, and school bus routing.
We are also more than a technology company:
we operate many demand-response services
ourselves, which means we understand the
day-to-day realities of delivering equitable and
accessible public transit.
Our
response to this RFP is informed by our deep
experience as both operators and software
providers of demand-response services.
2.1 Via and Bay Transit
Via is deeply committed to supporting Bay Transit’s vision of
expanding transit access across the Northern Neck and
Middle Peninsula regions. Since June of 2021, we have
partnered with Bay Transit to power Bay Transit Express in
the Gloucester Courthouse Area, the region’s first on
demand service.
