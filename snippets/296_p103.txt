In October 2020, Via partnered with Miami-Dade County
to launch MetroConnect, an on-demand microtransit
service that complements the existing transit
infrastructure and encourages wider transit usage.
MetroConnect provides shared, wheelchair accessible,
first-and-last mile rides to and from Metrorail and bus
stations within the service zones.
In 2023, the County leveraged Via's Remix planning suite
to conceptualize, solicit feedback on, and implement the
Better Bus Network (BBN) - the first redesign of the
Miami network of bus routes in 40 years. With more than
130+ routes designed on Via's Remix platform, the County
continues to improve transit outcomes for residents,
growing ridership while lowering cost per ride.
Via and Miami Dade County continue to innovate with the
launch of autonomous vehicle shuttle service in 2024 and
the roll out of Citymapper MaaS journey planning
solutions in early 2025.

In August 2023, Detroit Public Schools Community
District faced driver shortages that impacted the delivery
of its yellow school bus routes. Fortunately, DPSCD
already had a robust small vehicle transportation program
for students with complex needs, including nearly 500
students experiencing housing instability. By partnering
with Via, among other vendors, the District was able to
expand the small vehicle program to ensure that students
in need — particularly students with disabilities and those
accommodated under the McKinney Vento Act — could
be transported despite limited yellow bus resources.
Now, DPSCD's small vehicle program has the capacity to
offer safe and reliable transportation to every student in
need, even as this population grows or fluctuates. DPSCD
looked for vendors that not only had experience
transporting sensitive populations, but also could
accommodate the flexibility required to deliver routes that
change with student circumstances and schedules. For
some students, the small vehicle program is a way to get
to school day-to-day; for others, it can meet a specific
need, like an after-school program or special event.
Choosing the right suppliers, and growing the District's
roster of vendors, has been critical in building a strong
program that delivers quality transportation throughout
the year.
