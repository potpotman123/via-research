The most accurate trip plans, delivered in an intuitive app that customers love 
Citymapper integrates real-time trip planning and nearby transport options into a single, 
user-friendly interface. Riders can compare transit options, 
including microtransit, shared mobility, and active travel, with live 
departure times, fare details, and filtering options for 
accessibility needs. Our intermodal routing combines transit with 
bike, ride-hail, and more, providing step-by-step itineraries with 
precise travel times, entry and exit guidance, and live bus 
locations. 
Our platform’s high data accuracy, powered by 40+ custom tools 
and a team of expert analysts, ensures that users aren’t waiting 
on "phantom" buses or relying on outdated info. Custom 
algorithms make arrival predictions 15% more accurate than 
industry norms, while natural language parsing of disruption 
alerts keeps users informed and rerouted around delays. With 
over 150 mobility partners integrated—spanning bikes, cabs, 
scooters, and carshare—Citymapper delivers hyper-accurate, 
up-to-date trip plans that inspire user confidence. 
By leveraging machine learning, Citymapper ranks routes based 
on observed preferences, allowing for localized route 
recommendations tailored to OMNIA members’ riders. A dynamic 
map view also shows nearby bus stops, rail stations, and bike 
rentals, providing real-time departures and vehicle availability. 
Citymapper empowers OMNIA members to offer trusted, engaging, and accurate MaaS 
experiences, setting a new standard in public transit accessibility and satisfaction.
