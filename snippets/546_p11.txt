The City currently operates a turnkey model, in which NoMad is contracted to provide all elements of 
the service such as the vehicles, drivers, and technology. The City does provide a site for vehicles to be 
stored overnight. NoMad provides the app that connects drivers and vehicles with passengers who want 
rides. NoMad does not employ drivers, and they do not own vehicles. Drivers are independent 
contractors who can self-select shifts and vehicles are currently owned by VGM and are rented by the 
drivers for the duration of each shift. NoMad also supplies a rider support line to assist with booking 
rides and issues including account support, fees or charges, and lost items. The service currently 
operates from 6AM – 11PM Monday-Friday, 9AM – 11PM Saturday, and 8AM – 8PM Sunday. The service 
is supported by up to 15 vehicles (inclusive of 4 wheelchair assessable vehicles), dependent upon 
demand. Riders can book a ride through the Via app or over the phone and payment can be made 
through a credit/debit card or pre-paid credit card. Currently, the City’s On-Demand Rideshare Program 
does not allow a pre-scheduled booking option, but the City is open to considering the feature. 
Passenger fares are $3.50 per ride, $1.75 for seniors and people with qualifying disabilities. Frequent 
riders may consider ViaPass, which offers a $15 weekly pass allowing you to ride up to 4 times a day, 
every day of the week. Riders are typically asked to walk less than 500 feet to their pick-up/drop-off 
location in order to optimize routes for greater efficiency, however door-to-door service is available to 
users with limited mobility subject to proof of eligibility.  
Since launch through September 2023, Via has provided a total of over 728,000 rides throughout West 
Sacramento for over 13,600 unique accounts. Of the 13,600+ accounts, more than 560, or 
approximately 5 percent, are senior or disabled account holders. On average, pick-up estimated time of 
arrival is approximately 12.8 minutes, deviation between estimated time of arrival and actual pick-up 
time is 0.9 minutes, and 86 percent of vehicles are within 5 minutes of estimated pick up time. Riders 
walk an average of 318.5 feet to be picked up and trips are an average of 3.3 miles and 10.2 minutes per 
ride.  
On June 15, 2022, the City Council approved a contract of $2,741,254 – comprised of $2,395,191 of 
Transportation Development Act funding, $69,014 Fiscal Year (FY) 2021-22 fare revenues, and $277,049 
of anticipated FY 2022-23 fare revenue reinvestment – with NoMad Transit LLC to support the On-
Demand Rideshare Program operations and allocated $115,000 to fund staff time and materials for 
fiscal year 2022-23.  
Between July 2022 – June 2023, the service has completed over 188,000 rides - averaging of 3,622 
weekly rides, 595 weekday daily rides, and 322 weekend daily rides. Currently, over 600 unique riders 
are using the service weekly. Senior/Disabled ridership comprises an average of approximately 379 
weekly rides. Average pick-up time for FY 2022-23 is 14 minutes, deviation between estimated time of 
arrival and actual time of arrival is approximately 1.3 minutes, and 87 percent of vehicles are within 5 
minutes of estimated pick up time. For Fiscal Year 2022-23, the average pick up walking distance was 
320.7 feet. With recent ridership trends, Via currently targets running 1,000 - 1,050 driver hours per 
week.

Given the success of this program, the City is interested in continuing the On-Demand Rideshare 
Program. The City is interested in maintaining similar service parameters and system to the existing 
program, but is open to considering transit model approaches ranging from nearly fixed route to entirely 
demand-responsive and applying flexibility in any combination to routes, stops, and schedules. The City 
is also interested in understanding costs to expand the service into a zone or select locations in 
Downtown Sacramento. The City is also open to considering changes to the program that can provide 
cost-savings without significant impact to ridership or considering changes that can provide a more 
equitable program. This RFP is designed to provide flexibility in soliciting responses from a broad pool of 
experts spanning transit services and operations, technology providers, transportation network 
companies, startups, transit vehicle vendors, and marketing and promotions specialist, and other 
services necessary to continue the City’s On-Demand Rideshare Program. The current On-Demand 
Rideshare Program operates as detailed:

2.3. Scope of Work 
The following is a list of tasks that are intended to be the responsibility of the Contractor pursuant to 
this Request for Proposals ("Scope of Work "). Submittals are encouraged (but not required) to propose 
a comprehensive service approach, describing a specific operational model, technology platform, service 
territory, vehicle acquisition scheme, and marketing and performance monitoring plan. 
Task 1: Project Management 
All communications between the City and the Contractor shall be coordinated through their respective 
Project Managers. In the area of Project Management, the Contractor shall: 
• 
Schedule and facilitate Project Development Team (PDT) meetings, including a kick-off meeting 
and meetings at key milestones interagency meetings, field reviews, advisory and/or 
stakeholder group meetings, and other project related meetings. 
• 
Contractor shall prepare agendas, minutes, and sign in sheets for all meetings. 
• 
The City Project Manager must be kept abreast of all coordination with outside agencies, prior 
to any meeting with an outside agency or organization. 
• 
The Contractor will prepare and maintain a detailed work plan in accordance with any major 
milestones and deadlines to launch, expand, and/or make changes to the service. The 
Contractor will maintain and update the work plan as approved by the City Project Manager. 
• 
Coordinate project resources and work so that milestones are met in an efficient manner. Tasks 
will be laid out so as to minimize implementation time and cost while taking into consideration 
resource and time constraints such as City staff availability.
