Open Session Agenda Item 
April 18, 2025 
From:  
Taulby Roach, President and Chief Executive Officer   
Subject:   
Contract Award – Stockless Office Supplies  
Disposition: Approval 
Presentation: Thomas Curran, Executive Vice President – Administration  
 
William A. Dee, Vice President, Procurement and Inventory Management  
 
Objective:   
To present to the Board of Commissioners for approval, a request for authorization to enter into a 
contract with Staples Contract & Commercial LLC to provide stockless office supplies, copier paper, 
office furniture and possibly janitorial supplies for a five-year period.   
 
Background: 
In October 2008, Bi-State Development (BSD) solicited proposals, under solicitation 09-RFP-5460-
DR – Stockless Office Supplies, to obtain a qualified vendor to satisfy the requirements associated 
with providing stockless office supplies to meet its office supply requirements on an as-needed basis.  
Following the evaluation process, BSD awarded the Stockless Office Supplies contract to Staples.    
 
In December 2011, the then President & CEO of BSD signed a contract modification which allowed 
BSD to continue using Staples as BSD’s office supply vendor through joining a purchasing alliance 
called National Joint Powers Alliance (NJPA).  On June 6, 2018, the National Joint Powers Alliance 
(NJPA) changed its name to Sourcewell. The name change was intended to better reflect the 
organization's purpose and mission.  Sourcewell is a national public service agency; with national 
contract offerings available since 1995 and serves over 70,000 members throughout the nation with 
competitively-awarded contract purchasing solutions through industry-leading vendors for schools, 
state and local government, and other municipal government agencies, as well as non-profit 
organizations. 
 
In February 2020, BSD’s Internal Audit conducted an audit of the stockless office supplies NJPA 
agreement and recommended Procurement to submit a briefing paper to obtain Committee and 
Board Approval every five (5) years to remain a member of the National Joint Powers Alliance 
(NJPA), now Sourcewell.   
 
Analysis:  
Sourcewell was created by state law as a service cooperative to provide programs and services to 
education and government agencies. Sourcewell was established with the statutory purpose of 
assisting public agencies in meeting specific needs that are more efficiently delivered cooperatively 
than by an entity individually. 
 
Sourcewell has nationally solicited, evaluated and awarded contracts through a competitive bidding 
process on behalf of its members.  It was the responsibility of BSD to interpret local purchasing laws 
to determine if we (BSD) would be able to participate in NJPA, now Sourcewell, contracts.  The 
Sourcewell procurement process consists of the following:

 Develop Requests for Proposal – Study clients’ needs and conduct industry search  
 Public Notice & Advertisement – Advertise RFP notice, and host a pre-proposal conference 
 RFP Opening – Official public RFP opening of properly submitted proposals  
 RFP Evaluation – Review proposals and evaluate based on established scoring criteria. 
 Contracts Awarded – Contracts awarded to the most responsive and responsible supplier(s), 
and award notices sent. 
 
As a result of the procurement process described above, Staples was awarded the contract which 
consists of several business essentials such as office and janitorial supplies, office furniture, 
technology, print; and promotional and logo items.   
 
 
Stockless office needs are ordered via authorized BSD employees through the Staples Advantage 
ordering platform. BSD’s past spending from 2022 through 2024 has been approximately $100,000 
to $122,000 per year ($122,000 x 5 = $610,000); therefore, BSD anticipates the next five-year total 
spend to not exceed $1 million. 
 
Previous Action: 
The Audit, Finance and Administration Committee approved this item at the March 21, 2025 
meeting. 
 
Board Action Requested: 
The Audit, Finance and Administration Committee recommends that the Board of Commissioners 
authorize the President & CEO to renew the Sourcewell Participating Agreement with Staples 
Contract & Commercial LLC, who was awarded the contract as a result of a competitive 
procurement process and is most advantageous to BSD with price and other factors considered, for 
a five-year (2025-2029) timeframe in a not-to-exceed amount of $1 million.  
 
Funding Source:  
The funding source is provided via each of BSD’s Cost Centers that has been allotted in their budget 
for office supplies.
