Appendix B: 
Deployment Parameters 
 
The Parties agree to the following Deployment Parameters. Upon mutual agreement, the Parties 
may agree to subsequent modifications of these Parameters during the term of this Agreement. 
 
 Deployment Service Zone: Approximately 28.2 square miles coverage zone in Southern 
Davis County, Utah. Approximately 71.1 square miles coverage zone in Salt Lake County, 
Utah. See Appendix A for a map of the service zone boundary. 
 Service Days/Hours: The Deployment’s operating hours shall be Monday through Friday, 
6:00am to 9:00pm in South Davis County. In South Salt Lake County, the Deployment’s 
operating hours shall be Monday through Friday, 4:00am to 12:15am, and Saturdays, 
6:00am to 9:00pm. 
 Vehicle Fleet: For South Davis County, Via’s Vehicle Partner will offer a fleet of up to 
nine (9) branded, licensed, and insured vehicles, including four (4) Americans with 
Disabilities Act (ADA)-compliant Wheelchair Accessible Vehicles (WAV) to be made 
available to independent contractor Driver Partners, who will be able to gain access to these 
vehicles after being registered onto the Platform. Of the nine total vehicles, three (3) 
vehicles shall be treated by Via as spares in the event that a regular service vehicle requires 
maintenance or replacement. Fleet size and composition of WAVs and non-WAVs may be 
adjusted based upon mutual agreement of the Parties as the service grows in 2025 and 
2026.  
For South Salt Lake County, Via’s Vehicle Partner will offer a fleet of up to twenty six 
(26) branded, licensed, and insured vehicles, including nine (9) Americans with Disabilities 
Act (ADA)-compliant Wheelchair Accessible Vehicles (WAV) to be made available to 
independent contractor Driver Partners, who will be able to gain access to these vehicles 
after being registered onto the Platform. Of the twenty six total vehicles, six (6) vehicles 
shall be treated by Via as spares in the event that a regular service vehicle requires 
maintenance or replacement. Fleet size and composition of WAVs and non-WAVs may be 
adjusted based upon mutual agreement of the Parties as the service grows in 2025 and 
2026.

Appendix C: 
Service Fees 
 
The table below outlines the payment structure, in which UTA would be charged a price per driver 
hour. Any new regulatory fees imposed by a governmental entity related to the service will be 
charged as a pass-through cost contingent on UTA advance agreement provided such agreement 
will not be unreasonably withheld. UTA will have the option of decreasing other services or 
expenses in order to off-set these additional fees. The Deployment shall include a fleet of vehicles

be agreed (including any change in fees). The total contract value shall not exceed that listed in 
this Appendix C, “Not-to-Exceed Fees,” of $21,793,653 for the 36-month duration of this Service 
Order (through December 31, 2026).

Ongoing Invoice Fees

Year 3 (January 1, 2026 – December 31, 2026): $59.74 per driver hour in ongoing project costs, 
to include driver partner pay, vehicle rental and wheelchair-accessible vehicle (WAV) costs, live 
customer support, and project operations

network at no additional cost to Via for the UTA On Demand vehicle fleet as set forth within

meet the vehicle requirements set forth herein.  Such cost equals a rate of $6 per vehicle hour. For

responsible for the provision of vehicles on a rental basis to Driver Partners.
