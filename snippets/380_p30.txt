This Data Sharing Agreement TSD-24-146 (DSA) by and between Tacoma School District No. 10 
(District) and Nomad Transit (collectively identified and referred to herein as the “Party” and/or the 
“Parties”), is entered into for the (1) establishment of a working relationship between the District and 
Nomad Transit and (2) establishment of Nomad Transit scope of access to the District’s educational 
records for students served by Nomad Transit.  
The general objective of this DSA is to support the safe and reliable on-demand alternative vehicle 
transportation services for District students. The District may only release confidential information and 
data to Nomad Transit in accordance with the terms of this DSA, including all attachments and 
appendences, which are incorporated herein by reference.   
RESPONSIBILITIES OF NOMAD TRANSIT 
1. In order to accomplish the general objective(s) of this DSA, Nomad Transit shall perform the specific
duties as outlined below to the satisfaction of the District’s designee, Director of Transportation
Services.
2. Nomad Transit will only request data as identified within this DSA, and will follow the procedure for
making such request(s) within Attachment A.

3. Nomad Transit will share the results of any data analysis completed as a result of this DSA with the
District within thirty (30) days of completion and/or upon request.
4. Nomad Transit must provide all data analyses to the District prior to publishing or making that
information publicly available; to ensure that no publication, despite de-identified, may lead to an
identifiable student.
a.
Nomad Transit may only publish or make information publicly available after having received
written approval from the District, upon review of the provided data analysis.
5. Nomad Transit certifies by signing this DSA, only those employees and/or volunteers directly
involved in the efforts covered per this DSA will have access to the data shared, and all will comply
with all applicable LORs and duties and obligations contained in this DSA.
6. Nomad Transit agrees that some instances may require the need to obtain signed parent/guardian
consent forms for the sharing of confidential information. If/when such instance arises:
a.
The District will notify Nomad Transit of such need, and it will be the responsibility of Nomad
Transit to obtain such.
b. Nomad Transit may utilize a template provided by the District, or may utilize a District
approved template.
c.
Nomad Transit will maintain record of the consent forms and associated confidentiality
agreements.
d. Upon any request for confidential information, Nomad Transit will present to the District the
signed parent/guardian consent form(s).
RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE DISTRICT 
A. Upon Nomad Transit direct request to the District, and upon demonstrated completion of any 
potential provision of consent forms, the District may share the specific confidential information as 
identified in Attachment A with Nomad Transit, per the terms of this DSA.  
GENERAL TERMS 
A. CONFIDENTIALITY.  The term “confidential information" as used in this DSA means any and all 
information provided by the District to Nomad Transit which is protected by the FERPA, 20 U.S.C. 
Sec 1232g, its corresponding regulations found in 34 C.F.R. Part 99, and all other similar federal, 
state, and local LORs. Such personal information is also exempt from mandatory disclosure by a 
school district under the terms of the state public disclosure laws codified at chapter 42.56 RCW.

compliance with 34 C.F.R. 99.31(a) (6): 
a.
In all respects, Nomad Transit will comply with the provisions of FERPA.  Nothing in
this DSA may be construed to allow either party to maintain, use, disclose, or share
student record information without appropriate permission or in a manner not allowed

b.
Nomad Transit will use confidential information shared under this DSA for no purpose
other than the goals outlined in this DSA. Nothing in the Agreement shall be construed
to authorize Nomad Transit to have access to additional confidential information from
the District that is not included in Attachment A. By entering this DSA, Nomad Transit
understands that this DSA does not convey ownership of confidential information to
Nomad Transit.
c.
Nomad Transit will require all employees to comply with this DSA and all applicable
provisions of FERPA and all other applicable LORs with respect to the confidential
information shared under this DSA.  Nomad Transit agrees to require and maintain an
appropriate confidentiality agreement from each Nomad Transit employee or volunteer
with access to confidential information pursuant to this Agreement. Nothing in this
section authorizes Nomad Transit to share confidential information provided under this
Agreement.
d. Nomad Transit will not disclose confidential information produced to it under this DSA
in any manner that could identify any individual student or teacher, except as authorized
by FERPA, to any entity other than the District or employees and volunteers of  Nomad
Transit also working for and on behalf of the District pursuant to the terms of this DSA
and if deemed necessary by the District as specifically identified in each District
student’s District approved consent form.
e.
Nomad Transit will not provide any confidential information obtained under this
Agreement to any individual, agency or entity outside of this Agreement without the
prior written consent of the District, unless required to make such disclosure under an
applicable law or court order.
f.
Nomad Transit agrees that disclosure of confidential student information, without
permission of the District is just cause for the District to immediately terminate the DSA.
g. Any violation of this Agreement and/or the provisions of FERPA or accompanying
regulations related to the nondisclosure of protected student information may result in a
determination by the Department of Education that the violating party is prohibited from
accessing student education records for up to five (5) years, pursuant to 34 CFR
Sec.99.31(a)(6)(iv).
