UNFINISHED BUSINESS 
 
Commissioner Nash addressed the board regarding the possibility of a motion to have all expenditures 
approved by the board for the expenditures that go through for that grant funding on the $1M grant.  
Administrator Eisenbarth recommended that a report come forth to the Ways & Means committee regarding 
the monies spent.  Corporate Counsel responded about the scope of the spending.  He listed many scenarios 
including operational expenses that money would be able to be spent on.  He stated that the grant is very 
broad.  The funds were allocated on a variety of factors to all airports.   
 
Administrator Mark Eisenbarth addressed the board regarding the agreement with the tenant at the airport.  He 
believes they are very close to an agreement and informed the board he would like to bring this under his 
report at full board.
