EXHIBIT A 
RIDESHARE SERVICES SCOPE OF SERVICES 
 
1. Purpose; Scope 
Contractor will provide rideshare services as described below (the “Service”) for the City and its residents 
and visitors. Contractor's Services include: 
 Access to Via's fully localized dynamic vehicle routing and real-time passenger aggregation 
system; 
 Establishing relationship with vehicle rental company ("Vehicle Partner") to provide access to 
Vehicles on a rental basis to independent contractor driver partners; 
 Accompanying technical and operational support services in order to provide a turnkey solution; 
 A series of service development meetings to refine service scope for a supplemental microtransit 
service that operates during peak hours to maximize efficiency across modes and improve quality 
of service (the “Dynamic Direct Route”); 
 Data sharing and reporting as described under Section 9 below; 
 Access to the Via mobile application for users to book rides from a smartphone; 
 Integration of the Arlington Student Transportation Program as described under Section 10 
below; 
 Integration of the Arlington RAPID service as described under Section 11 below; and 
 Advertising services as described under Section 12 
 
2. Duration 
The Project Scope will begin on January 1, 2025, unless otherwise mutually agreed by both parties. The 
Service shall operate through December 31, 2026, with the option to renew in accordance with the 
Contract. 
3. Fees 
A. Contractor Fees 
 
The City agrees to pay Contractor the amounts below, not to exceed $20,791,648.32 in the aggregate for 
the term of this Contract.
