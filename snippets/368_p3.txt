Our demand response software delivers an exceptional experience for all users — riders,
drivers, and backend staff — and the necessary flexibility to meet Bay Transit’s evolving
transit needs. Our dedicated project team and expert advisors will continually work with
Bay Transit to achieve its goal of streamlining transit operations and enhancing the user
experience of Bay Transit drivers, dispatchers, and riders. We will leverage our in-house
service design, marketing, and fundraising teams to ensure a seamless demand response
service transition and long-term scalability across Bay Transit’s services.
Our proposal for Bay Transit is centered on:
A Single End-to-End Platform for On-Demand and Demand Response Services
Via’s suite of familiar and user-friendly tools will provide Bay Transit with a single unified
interface to manage all of its on-demand and demand response services, eliminating the
​need for Bay Transit administrators to learn an entirely new system. Our software suite,
which currently powers Bay Transit Express in the Gloucester Courthouse area, includes
three core components: our white-labeled, fully automated Rider App, designed with user
experience and accessibility at the forefront; our Driver App, which provides real-time
turn-by-turn navigation and a hands-free display; and the Via Operations Center, a
highly automated administrative platform that Bay Transit staff will use to manage
services in real time. Through Via’s software, administrators will be able to seamlessly
visualize and analyze the services thanks to a suite of fully integrated dashboards,
reports and data tables.
