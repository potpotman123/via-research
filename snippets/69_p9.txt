requirements provided in Exhibit C, attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference. The

of the County.

FORCE MAJEURE: No Party shall be liable for failure to perform hereunder if the 
failure is the result of force majeure. Any time limit shall be extended for the period of any delay 
resulting from any force majeure, or this Contract may be terminated if such delay makes performance 
of the Contract impossible or impracticable. Force majeure shall mean causes beyond the reasonable 
control of a Party such as, but not limited to, weather conditions, acts of God, strikes, work stoppages, 
unavailability of or delay in receiving labor or materials, faults by contractors, subcontractors, utility 
companies or third parties, fire or other casualty or actions of government authorities.
