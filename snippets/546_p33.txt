7. 
Consultant Selection and Contract Award 
7.1. Proposal Evaluation Process 
The Proposal review and evaluation process will comply with the following processes ("Proposal 
Evaluation Process"): 
A. The Proposal Evaluation Process is designed to establish a list of qualified Proposers with the 
best combination of attributes based upon the Evaluation Criteria. 
B. The City's Proposal Evaluation Process is intended to identify the Proposer with the best 
combination of attributes. Cost is not the exclusive attribute and the lowest Proposer is not 
guaranteed to be offered a Contract. 
C. The Project Contact, identified in the "Contact Information" of this RFP, shall manage the 
Proposal Evaluation Process and maintain all records related to the process. 
D. The Project Contact shall conduct an initial review of all Proposals and determine if each 
Proposal is in compliance with the requirement specified in this RFP and, thereby, responsive. 
E. The Project Contact shall work with selected City staff whose specialties and responsibilities are 
relevant to this RFP (collectively, "Evaluation Team") to complete the evaluation process. 
F. The Evaluation Team shall evaluate all responsive Proposals. Each Evaluation Team member 
shall score each responsive Proposal based on the Evaluation Criteria. 
G. The City reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to request clarification of any Proposal. Any 
such communication shall only be after initial evaluation by the Evaluation Team and only for 
the purpose of ensuring full understanding of the Proposal. Any clarification shall be made by 
the Proposer in writing. 
H. Proposers with the highest scores pursuant to the Evaluation Criteria may be asked to complete 
an interview. The City reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to request an interview. If 
requested, interviews shall be conducted as provided in this RFP. 
7.2. Proposer Interviews 
The top-rated Proposers with the highest score from the Evaluation Criteria may be interviewed and 
rated. The City reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to request interviews. If interviews are held, they 
will occur at a time and location to be specified or virtually via a City internet platform. 
A. Interviews will be conducted by the Evaluation Team and in a format determined by the Team, 
generally consisting of Team questions and Proposer presentations. The interviews will be 
limited to one (1) hour. The Evaluation Team shall use the Evaluation Criteria to rate Proposers 
during the interview.

B. Interviews shall be attended by the individual identified in the Proposal as the primary contact 
to the City on a day-to-day basis and members of the proposed team identified in the 
"Personnel" section of the Mandatory Qualifications and Experience. 
C. Following all interviews, the Evaluation Team will re-rank the Proposers to determine the 
highest ranked Proposers. Then, the Project Contact will open the sealed Cost Proposals for the 
highest ranked Proposers and re-rank the highest ranked Proposers based on overall ranking 
and Cost Proposal. 
7.3. Contract Award Process 
The contract selection and award process shall comply with the following process ("Contract Award 
Process"): 
A. The Project Contact shall invite the Proposer with the highest score from the Proposal 
Evaluation Process to participate in contract negotiations with the City. 
B. Proposer and City shall negotiate the final terms and conditions of the Contract, pursuant to this 
RFP. The Contact shall contain the same, or substantially the same, terms and conditions as 
portrayed in this RFP, the Scope of Work, the Proposal, and the City's Standard Contract. 
C. Following negotiations, the City shall draft a contract for execution by all parties. If the Proposer 
fails to sign and return the Contact within seven (7) working days of its delivery to the Proposer, 
the City may determine, at its sole discretion, that the Proposer is nonresponsive to the terms of 
this RFP and initiate further Proposal Evaluation Processes. 
D. City records related to this RFP shall be made available for public inspection, as permitted by 
law, following Contract execution, or rejection of all Proposals. 
E. The City reserves the right to accept or reject any or all Proposals.
