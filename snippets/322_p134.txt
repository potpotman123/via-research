For a seamless transition from their legacy paratransit service, we can import data from the
County’s existing Routematch databases into our system. Via’s in-house Data Science team will
work directly with staff to conduct the following four-step data migration process:
●
Step 1 - Knowledge sharing & data scoping. We will perform extensive scoping to assess
existing datasets to determine what data needs to be migrated. Via’s team is highly familiar
with the process of obtaining data from the Routematch platform.
●
Step 2 - Data evaluation. We will evaluate data formatting, the speed of data imports, and
the need for additional data fields and product features to support the migration. We have
a fully-built internal tool to clean and convert Routematch data into a format compatible
with Via’s system.
●
Step 3 - Initial data migration, quality assurance ՄQAՅ, and validation. We will upload an
initial data set, and perform extensive QA to evaluate the success of the migration and
confirm the system’s readiness for bulk uploads. Our team will also perform data validation
to ensure the data makes sense (e.g., the geographic location of pickups).
●
Step 4 - Full migration, QA, validation, and field testing. We will upload the entire data set,
perform QA and validation, as well as extensive live field testing.
●
Step 5 - Change Management and Training. We recognize that County staff may be used
to working with data in a particular way within the Routematch system, such as manually
geocoding new addresses or managing paper manifests. We offer change management
workshops to train staff in Via’s automated systems and build trust in our various tools.
This process will ensure a successful migration of data to the Via Platform, and enable us to
convert the County’s service with minimal disruptions.
