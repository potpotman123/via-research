RECESS AND RECONVENE 
At 4:08 p.m., the meeting was recessed for members to participate in a group photo 
commemorating Pierce Transit’s 45 years of service.

PRESENTATIONS 
 
1. Honoring Greg Snyder for Operator of the Month for March 2025 
 
Assistant Transportation Manager Scott Gaines honored Greg Snyder for being 
selected Operator of the Month for March 2025.  He reported that Mr. Snyder has 
been an operator since 2016 and this is his second time being selected Operator of 
Month. He reviewed his accomplishments, highlighting his commitment to safety and 
providing outstanding customer service.  
 
Mr. Snyder was presented with a certificate and congratulatory letter from Pierce 
Transit CEO.  
 
On behalf of the Board, Chair Walker congratulated Mr. Snyder and thanked him for 
his service and dedication.  
 
PUBLIC COMMENT 
 
Chair Walker provided participation instructions to the public and opened public comment.   
 
No public comment was provided, and public comment was closed.  
 
PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Title VI Equity Analysis for the Route 101 - Gig Harbor Trolley Route Title VI  
 
Planning Manager Tina Lee (Commissioner Mello temporarily left the meeting room at 4:22 
p.m.) kicked off the presentation and gave an overview of the Gig Harbor trolley service, 
which began in 2013. She reviewed the route that is being proposed and noted that the change 
in the route triggered the need for an equity analysis. She noted that the proposed route will 
be called the Waterfront Connector. Ms. Lee reported that the service will be provided by an 
ADA compliant bus and the service will support economic development and improve access 
to event in the corridor. She advised the Waterfront Connector will run on a 30-minute 
frequency June 7 through August 28 on Thursdays, Saturdays, and Sundays.

1. FS 2025-016, Authorize the Chief Executive Officer to Increase the Contract 
Amount with Via Transportation, Inc., (Contract No. 1750) in the Amount of 
$3,200,000 to provide Runner Services in Puyallup, Spanaway, Tideflats, 
Ruston, and Gig Harbor for a new total not to exceed contract spending 
authority amount of $7,100,000 
 
Specialized Transportation Administrator Sarah Streiffert provided an overview of 
how the Runner Service is going, reporting that ridership has grown since inception 
of the service and ridership has stabilized since Q4 of 2024. She reviewed the 
Runner zones and advised the commissioners that Pierce Transit will be 
restructuring its Runner operations to make the system more efficient and to reduce 
wait times. This change may mean that customers may not necessarily be taken to 
point to point locations, but instead be taken to or connected with the closest fixed 
route location.

2. Update on Hardship Assistance Response Team (HART) Program 
 
Emergency Management Coordinator Rodney Chandler provided a status update on 
the roll out of the HART program. He advised that Pierce Transit will be utilizing a 
Designated Crisis Responder (DCR) contracted through Multicare to assist those 
experiencing a crisis in the Pierce Transit system. He advised the DCR is in the 
process of being onboarded. In addition to working out in the Pierce Transit system, 
the DCR will provide training to Pierce Transit staff so they can recognize the signs 
of a person in crisis and teach de-escalation skills.  
 
Mr. Chandler advised that the HART vehicle is operationalized and that the program 
will not serve the City of Tacoma as they have their own behavioral health tax. He 
advised this program is a new concept for transit and staff is working hard to 
operationalize it.
