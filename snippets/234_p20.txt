with changed or new County system, and if so, shall provide implementation

22.4.

Enterprise Resource Planning system (“ERP”), and determines, at County’s sole

County written request, work with County to determine if integration is possible with 
the new ERP, and if so, shall provide its services to County to ensure employee data

provide to County any data, information, and technical support County requests prior 
to selection of a new ERP to ensure integration is possible. Such professional 
services shall be provided at the applicable rates specified in Exhibit A.
