ITEM TITLE
Agreement with Spare Labs, Inc., through Stripe Payment Processing, for Online Digital Credit /
Debit Card Payment Processing for BRATS
STAFF RECOMMENDATION
Related to the authorization to replace BRATS’ current credit / debit card payment processor,
Braintree, with Spare Labs, Inc., through Stripe Payment Processing, take the following actions:
1) Approve the following agreements and documents:
• Stripe Connected Account Agreement
• Stripe Services Agreement - United States
• Stripe User Bank Account Debit Authorizations
• Stripe Privacy Policy; and
• Stripe E-Sign Disclosure
2) Authorize the Baldwin County Commission Finance / Accounting Department to electronically
accept the above referenced agreements and related documents and provide all other information
necessary and permitted by law, as part of the Stripe Payment Processing account setup process.
BACKGROUND INFORMATION
Background:  Since 2020, through an agreement with Via Transportation, BRATS has utilized
Braintree to process passenger credit / debit card payments. BRATS is transitioning from Via
Transportation to a new micro-transit software provider, Spare Labs, Inc (Spare). Spare is integrated
with Stripe Payment Processing (Stripe) for online digital payment processing.
There are no set-up fees associated with establishing a Stripe Payment Services account. Stripe will
deduct the payment processing fees from the fares paid by the passenger and remit the remaining
fare balance to BCC / BRATS. Stripe is Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)
compliant, to protect cardholder data, ensuring secure processing, storage, and transmission of
credit card information.
