Remix July 2026: New stop updates, Presentation Studio tools, and Saved Views

August 3, 2026

Keep your stops in sync with the latest GTFS.

Refresh any Remix map with the latest stop data from your GTFS, current service map, or a previous GTFS version—keeping maps up to date and making clean GTFS exports easier than ever.

Learn more

Add more focus to your map exports.

De-emphasize individual or multiple lines in Presentation Studio exports to create cleaner, more focused maps—while automatically dimming associated stops for a polished presentation.

Quickly access and organize Saved Views.

Quickly create, rename, organize, and switch between Saved Views with a new visual menu in Presentation Studio, making it easier to prepare multiple map views from the same project.

Export entire point layers in one click.

Skip the repetitive work—now you can export an entire point layer at once, using the same export options available for individual points.
