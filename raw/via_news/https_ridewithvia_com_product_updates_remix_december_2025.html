Remix December 2025: Celebrating this year's favorite features.

December 16, 2025

Stickers: a delightful visual upgrade for clearer communications.

Add map annotations like arrows, construction cones, or bus stop icons using our new Stickers feature—ideal for highlighting detours, service changes, or key destinations in Presentation Studio. Or make a fun holiday map for your team!

Learn more

It’s contagious! Teams are working together thanks to Multi-user editing.

Collaborate on projects in real time, give live feedback, and keep everyone in sync with Multi-user editing. Over 4,300 collaborative plans were created in Remix this year, and over 2,000 had 10+ collaborators. Keep it up!

Explore more

What happens if we change these routes? Predict how riders will move with Ridership Modeling.

Model demand, predict ridership, and design better networks—using real, current trip data. No PhD required. *Coming soon

Check it out

 

Manage all of your stop info, from end-to-end. 

Finally get your stop amenities info up to date! You can upload, create, update, visualize and export any information about stops in Remix.

Learn more

Multi-modal planning? Now you can schedule it. 

You can now plan driver shifts for demand-responsive services directly within Remix Scheduling. View on-demand and fixed-route shifts side-by-side to build a unified driver schedule—and reduce costly gaps or overages.

*Coming soon

Arrival and departure times in the timetable include Dwell Times.

You can now set dwell times at individual stops. Arrival and departure times update automatically—along with any downstream trips. Perfect for modeling timed connections or real-world operations.

Learn more

Carpool relief trips in clicks = fewer vehicles on the road.

Remix now finds smart ways to pool driver relief trips—based on time, location, and vehicle space. Just toggle it on to cut vehicle hours, fuel costs, and manual edits. Driver paddles update automatically, too.

Learn more

Manually assign garages—when you need to.

Override garage assignments in Remix Scheduling when you need to. Pick from garages serving a block’s start or end lines, and Remix will update pull-in/out chores for you. Want to go back to automatic? Just hit “Reset garages.”

Learn more

Add details that matter with custom line attributes.

Create, update, and export your own route info—from contract type to service status—all in one place. Group lines however you need to compare easily: Proposed vs. Existing, Local vs. Express, and more.
