Via February 2025: Live disruption management, a new booking page, and WebApp gets a makeover

February 12, 2025

Breakdown Bundle: Manage live disruptions in just a few clicks

Breakdown? No problem. Three new features make it easy to fix in a flash. First, edit your old shift to reflect the breakdown, then duplicate the shift and swap in a new vehicle, and finally reassign the rides to your new shift in bulk. Coming soon!

Hello, new Booking Page

​​​​​Quickly view all of your rides from one central page and take bulk actions. Users favorite features: new filters, exporting rides over multiple days, and bulk canceling.

Learn more

It’s driver’s choice: introducing Locationless Breaks

​​​​​Give your drivers flexibility to take breaks wherever they want to, instead of pre-setting locations for them.

Learn more

A WebApp makeover, designed for your riders

​​​​​WebApp has a new look and feel so your riders get an easy, consistent (dare we say delightful) experience across both the rider and web apps.

Learn more

More new features:

  • Duplicate Shifts: Need to create a new shift quickly? You can now duplicate shifts in the Ride Plan and Shift Manager without starting from scratch.

  • New Driver and Fleet Management Custom Fields: Want to capture more info about your drivers or vehicles? You can now request custom info fields, so you have the data you need right at your fingertips!
